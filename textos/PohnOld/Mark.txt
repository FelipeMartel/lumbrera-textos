<b>Marcos 1</b><br><b>1</b> TAPIN rongamau en Iesus Kristus, Sapwilim en Kot. <b>2</b> Duen a intingidi ren saukop Iesaia: Kilang, i kadarala mo ’mui ai men kadar me pan kaonopada al omui! <b>3</b> Ngil eu likelikwir sili nan sap tan: Komail kaonopada al en Kaun o kaineneda al a! <b>4</b> Ioanes kotido wia paptais nin sap tan o padapadak duen paptais en kalula ong lapwa dip akan. <b>5</b> Toun sap en Sus akan o toun Ierusalem karos pokon dong re a; irail paptaisela re a nan pilap Iordan, weokada dip arail akan. 
<b>6</b> Ioanes kin likauwi wun en kamel, o perapirki kil en man, o kin kangkang mansiek o oni en nan wel. <b>7</b> O padapadak masani: Meamen laud sang ia kotido mur i. Sal en a sut i sota war ong idiokidi lapwada. <b>8</b> I paptais kilar komail pil. A a pan paptais kin komail Ngen saraui! <b>9</b> Kadekadeo ni ran oko Iesus ap kotido sang Nasaret en Kaliläa ap paptaisela ren Ioanes nan Iordan. <b>10</b> A madang kotida sang nan pil o, ap kotin masani nanlang kan ar ritida, o Ngen mom en muroi piridi dong po a. 
<b>11</b> Ngil eu ap peidido sang nanlang kan: Nai kompok komui, me I perenki! <b>12</b> A madang Ngen kalua wong i sap tan. <b>13</b> A ap kotikoteki sap tan ran pangaul wia songesong pan Satan, ieiang man laualo; a tounlang kan me papa ong i. <b>14</b> Murin en Ioanes a pangalar, Iesus ap koti dong Kaliläa, padapadak rongamau en wein Kot. <b>15</b> Masani: Ansau o leler wein Kot mier! Komail kalula! Kamelele rongamau! <b>16</b> Ni a kotikot sili liman sed en Kaliläa, a ap masani Simon o ri a ol Andreas ara lalaid sili, pwe ira saused. 
<b>17</b> Iesus ap kotin masani ong ira: Koma idauen ia do! Pwe I pan wia kin koma la saused en aramas! <b>18</b> Ira ari madang kasedi ara uk eko, ap idauenla i. <b>19</b> Ari, ni a kotila sang wasa o, a ap kotin masani Iakopus, nain Sepedäus o ri a ol Ioanes mimi pon sop pot wonewone ara uk oko. <b>20</b> I ari kotin molipe ira madang, ira ari pededi sam ara Sepedäus nin sop o ren toun dodok ko o idauen la i. <b>21</b> Irail ap koti dong Kapernaum. A madang kotilong ong nan sinakoke ni ran en sapat o kawewe. 
<b>22</b> Re ap puriamuiki a padak, pwe a kotin kawewe ong irail ni manaman sota dueta saunkawewe kan. <b>23</b> Aramas amen iang mi nan sinakoke o, me ngen saut mi lole, ap weriwer, <b>24</b> Indada: Ko sang kit! Menda re omui kit, Iesus en Nasaret? Komui kotido en kawe kit ala? I asa komui, Saraui en Kot. <b>25</b> Iesus ari ongiongida, masani: Nenenla o kowei sang i! <b>26</b> Ngen saut lao kapururada ap weriwer laudeda, a ap koiei sang i. 
<b>27</b> Irail karos puriamui, pepeidok nan pung arail indada: Dakot met? Padak kap, dakot met? Pwe ni manaman a masani ong ngen saut akan, irail ari peiki ong i. <b>28</b> I ari madang indand sili nin sap karos impan Kaliläa. <b>29</b> Irail ari madang koti sang nan sinakoke, ap iang Iakopus o Ioanes kotilong ong nan im en Simon o Andreas, <b>30</b> Saulap en Simon wononeki somau karakar. Irail ap kaireki i madang duen i. <b>31</b> I ari koti wong ale pa a, kauada. Somau karakar ap madang ko sang; i ari papa irail. 
<b>32</b> A lao sautik penaer, ni katipin kirila, re ap wa pena ong i karos me somau o me tewil ti po arail. <b>33</b> O toun kanim o pokon dong ni im o. <b>34</b> A ap kotin kakelailada me toto me somauki song en somau toto, kausa sang tewil toto, ap kulikedi tewil akan, ren der lokaia, pwe re asa i. <b>35</b> A kotin kipada nin soran, kotilang sap tan, pwen laolao wasa o. <b>36</b> Simon o me iang ko ap idauenla i. <b>37</b> Ni arail diar i, re ap potoan ong i: Irail karos raparapa kin komui. 
<b>38</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Kitail daulul wong sap tei ko, pwe I en pil padak wasa o, pwe i me I kokido. <b>39</b> A ap kotin padapadak sili nan arail sinakoke kan nan sap en Kaliläa karos o kakaus tewil akan. <b>40</b> Ol tuketuk amen ap poto dong i kelepuki poeki potoan ong i: Ma re kotin kupura, re kotin kamakeke ia da! <b>41</b> Iesus ari kupuro kila, kapawei lim a, sair i masani ong i: I pan, makelekelada! <b>42</b> A ni a masani eta, ari a tuketuk ko sang madang, a ap makelekel lar. <b>43</b> I ari kalikedi i, ap madang kadarala. 
<b>44</b> Masani ong i: Kalaka! Koe depa indai ong meamen; a kowei kasale ong koe samero o, o kida, pweki om makelekelada, me Moses masani, pwen kadede ong irail. <b>45</b> Ari, a kolata kalokisili, suaiki sili o ari, kareda en Iesus a sola sansal koti dong kanim o, kotikot eta liki nan sap tan, irail ap pokon dong re a sang pali karos. 
<b>Marcos 2</b><br><b>1</b> MURIN ran akai a ap pil kotilong ong Kapernaum, ap loki sili me a kotikot nan im o. <b>2</b> Me toto ap madanga pena, im o lao koidala o pil likin im o. A ap kotin kawewe ong ir masan o. <b>3</b> Irail ari poto dong re a, wa kodo me somau rerer men, roan pa men. <b>4</b> Irail sota kak kai dong impa pweki pokon o. Re ap palang pasang pon im o, wasa a kotikot ia; irail lao palang pasang ap kakiredi ro o, me somau o mi poa. <b>5</b> Iesus lao mangi ar poson, ap masani ong me somau rerer o: Nai, dip om akan lapwa sang uk er! 
<b>6</b> Ari, saunkawewe kai momod wasa o, mongemongloleki: <b>7</b> Da me men et lokelokaia? A lalaue. Is me kak lapwada dip akan, pwe amen ta, Kot? <b>8</b> Iesus ari madang angiada nan kupur a duen ar mongemonglole, ap kotin masani ong irail: Da me komail mongemongloleki? <b>9</b> Ia me mangai ren indai ong me somau rerer: Dip om akan lapwa sang uk er, de inda: Uda, ale ki om, kowei? <b>10</b> A pwe komail en asa, me Nain aramas me manaman ong lapwada dip akan nin sappa, iei me a kotin masanieki ong me somau rerer: 
<b>11</b> I indai ong uk: Uda! Al eki om! U ko ong ni im om! <b>12</b> I ari madang uda, ale ki, aluwei mo ’rail karos. Irail karos ari malamalaua kila, kapinga Kot inda: Se saikenta man kilang meakot et! <b>13</b> I ari pil kotila liman sed o. Irail karos ap pokon dong i, i ari kotin kawewe ong irail. <b>14</b> A lao kotin daulul wasa o, ap masani Lewi, nain Alpäus, a momod ni wasan nopwei, ap masani ong i: Idauen ia do! I ari uda idauenla i. 
<b>15</b> Kadekadeo a lao kaipokedi konot nan im a, saunopwei o me dipan akan me toto iang Iesus o sapwilim a tounpadak kan konokonot; pwe irail me toto, me idauen i. <b>16</b> Ari, ni en saunkawewe o Parisär akan ar kilang a kotin iang me dipan o saunopwei kan konokonot, irail ap indai ong sapwilim a tounpadak kan: Da me a iangki saunopwei o me dipan akan konot o kaker eki? <b>17</b> Ni en Iesus angiada, ap kotin masangi ong irail: Me kelail akan sota mau ong saunwini pwe me somau ta. I sota kodon ekeredo me pung kan, a me dipan akan. 
<b>18</b> En Ioanes a tounpadak kan o Parisär akan kin kaisesol. Irail ari kodo potoan ong i: Da me en Ioanes a tounpadak kan kin kaisesol, o en Parisär ar tounpadak kan, a sapwilim omui tounpadak kan sota kin kaisesol? <b>19</b> Iesus ap kotin masani ong irail: Da, toun kamadip en kapapaud pan kak kaisesol ni en ol kamod a iang irail? Ni en ol kamod a iang irail, re sota pan kak kaisesol. <b>20</b> A ansau kokodo, me ol kamod pan wisike sang irail, iei ran o, me re pan kaisesol. 
<b>21</b> Sota amen me pan deik ong likau kap ni likau maring, pwe likau kap pan dei sang likau maring, por o ap pan laudela. <b>22</b> Pil sota amen me kin deuke kida ed maring wain kap, pwe wain o de ter pasang ed o, wain o ari wudokila, ed o ola. A wain kap mau ong nan ed kap. <b>23</b> Kadekadeo a kotin weid nan mat en korn ni ran en sapat. Sapwilim a tounpadak kan ap dondolung kowei wan korn. <b>24</b> Parisär oko ap indai ong i: Kilang, da me irail wia ni sapat me sota pung, en wiaui? 
<b>25</b> A kotin masani ong irail: Komail sota wadoker, me Dawid wiadar, ni a men kaualap, o a men manga, i o me iang i? <b>26</b> Duen a pedelong ong nan tanpas en Kot ni muein Apiatar samero lapalap, o kangala prot mairong o nek ong irail, me iang i, me sota pung aramas en tungole, samero kan eta? <b>27</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Sapat me wiaui ong aramas a kaidik aramas ong sapat. <b>28</b> I me Nain aramas pil udan kaun en sapat. 
<b>Marcos 3</b><br><b>1</b> KADEKADEO a pil kotin pur ong nan sinakoke. Ari, aramas amen mia, me pa a motor. <b>2</b> Irail ari masamasaneki, ma a pan kakelada i ni ran en sapat, pwe irail en karaun i. <b>3</b> I ari kotin masani ong aramas o, me pa a motor: Uda kodo met! <b>4</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Me pung, wia mau ni ran en sapat de wia sued? Kamaur de kamela? Ir ari nenenla. <b>5</b> I ari angiang aupwili sili po ’rail, kupurokila ar kapitakai, ap kotin masani ong aramas o: Kapawei pa om! I ari kapawei. Pa a ap kelailada. 
<b>6</b> Parisär oko ap madangalang ren iang en Erodes. Re ap kapakaparok duen ar pan kamatala i. <b>7</b> A Iesus kotin kapitawei sang pein i o sapwilim a tounpadak kan, koti wong liman sed. Pokon kalaimun ap idauen i sang Kaliläa, o Iudäa. <b>8</b> O sang Ierusalem, o sang Idumäa, o sang palilan Iordan, o toun liman Tirus o Sidon, pokon kalaimun, re rongada duen a kotin wiada manaman kaualap, re ap pokon dong re a. <b>9</b> A kotin masani ong sapwilim a tounpadak kan, kisin sop kis en kaonop ong i, pweki pokon o, pwe re de koi dong i. 
<b>10</b> Pwe a kotin kakelada me toto, irail ari pakoideda, pwe me somau ko me sair i. <b>11</b> O ni en ngen saut akan ngar i, re pupedi mo i, weriwer, indada: Sapwilim en Kot komui! <b>12</b> I ari kotin kalik irail edi, ren der kaireki i. <b>13</b> I ari kotidala nan nana o, ap molipei dong i, me a kotin kupura, re ap poto dong re a. <b>14</b> I ari kotin kileledi ekriamen, pwe re en ieiang i, pwen poroporone ir sili padapadak. <b>15</b> O manamanla kakelada me somau kan o kakaus tewil akan; <b>16</b> Simon a kotiki ong ad a Petrus. 
<b>17</b> O Iakopus nain Iepedäus, o Ioanes ri en Iakopus, a kotiki ong ad ara Poanerkes. Iet wewe: Nain nansapwe. <b>18</b> O Andreas, o Pilipus, o Partolomäus, o Matäus, o Tomas, o Iakopus, nain Alpäus, o Tadäus, o Simon men Kana, <b>19</b> O Iudas Iskariot, me pangala i. <b>20</b> Irail ari ko dong nan im eu. Pil pokon eu pokon pena. Irail ari sola kak manga. <b>21</b> Ni kompokepa ko rongada, re kodo, pwen wala sang i; pwe re inda, me a iakala. 
<b>22</b> A saunkawewe ko, me kodido sang Ierusalem inda: A katia Peelsepup o saumas en tewil akan; iei me a kakauseki tewil akan. <b>23</b> I ari molipe ir do, masani ong irail ni karaseras akan: Ia pan duen Satan a kausawei Satan? <b>24</b> O ma toun wei eu u pena nan pung ar, wei o sota pan kak pwaida. <b>25</b> O ma penainai eu u pena nan pung ar, penainai o sota pan kak pwaida. <b>26</b> O ma Satan u ong pein i, a sota pan kak pwaida, a pan imwisokela. 
<b>27</b> Sota amen me pan kak ko ong nan im en kelail amen kulia a kapwa, ma a so saliedi mas me kelail o. A ap pan kak kawela im a. <b>28</b> Melel I indai ong komail: Dip akan karos pan lapwa sang nain aramas akan, o laue, me re lalaueki. <b>29</b> A meamen lalaue Ngen saraui sota pan lapwada, a a pan puk ong kamekam soutuk. <b>30</b> Pweki ar inda: A katia ngen saut. <b>31</b> Ari in a, o ri a ol akan ap kodo, mi liki o iloke dong. <b>32</b> A pokon me momod impa o ap potoan ong i: Re mangi, in ar o ri ar ol akan mimi liki kainoma komui. 
<b>33</b> A ap kotin sapeng irail masani: Is in ai de ri ai kan? <b>34</b> A ap kotin aupwil sili po ’rail, me mod impa ko, masani: Kilang, in ai o ri ai ol akan! <b>35</b> Pwe meamen wiawia kupur en Kot, iei me ri ai ol o ri ai li o in ai. 
<b>Marcos 4</b><br><b>1</b> KADEKADEO a pil kotin tapiada kawewe liman sed o. Pokon kalaimun eu ap ko pena re a. A ap kotidang pon sop pot kotikot pon sed o. A pokon o mi nan sap pon oror. <b>2</b> A kotin kawewe ong irail me toto ni i karaseras akan, masani ong irail ni a padak: <b>3</b> Rong! Kilang, saunkamor o kolan kamorok. <b>4</b> Ni a kamokamorok akai ap moridi ong pon kailan al, manpir ap pirido nam ir ala. <b>5</b> A akai moredi ong wasa takain, wasa pwel petepet; irail ari madang wosada, pweki sota pwel lole. 
<b>6</b> Ni en katipin dakarada, re ap mongela o mela pweki sota kalau ar. <b>7</b> A akai moredi nan tuka teketek; o tuka teketek wosada, kasokiala, re ap sota wa. <b>8</b> A akai moredi nan pwel mau o pwaida wa; akai pan me sileisok; a akai me woneisok, a akai me epuki. <b>9</b> A kotin masani: Me salong a mia men rong, i en rong! <b>10</b> A lao kotin kalapukela, men imp a iangaki ekriamen ap kalelapok re a duen karaseras pukat. 
<b>11</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: A muei ong komail, en asa duen me rir en wein Kot. A men liki re pan asa meakaros sang ni karaseras akan. <b>12</b> Pwe re kilang, ari sota kilang, o rongerong, ap sota dedeki; pwe re de pan wukila o dip ar lapwa sang irail. <b>13</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Komail sota dedeki karaseras pot et? A iaduen, omail pan asa karaseras tei ko? <b>14</b> Saunkamor me kamorok sili masan. 
<b>15</b> A mepukat me mi pon kailan al, wasa masan moredi ong, a re lao rongadar, Satan ap madangado, ki sang masan, me kamoremoredi ong nan mongiong arail. <b>16</b> O mepukat me moredi ong wasan takain, re lao rongadar masan o ap madang peren kida ale. <b>17</b> A sota kalaua re ’rail ap dadaur eta ekis, lao songesong o kamekam wiaui pweki masan o, re ap madang makar kida. <b>18</b> A mepukat me moredi ong nan tuka teketek, me rongada masan. <b>19</b> A anan en pai en sappa o inong kapwa, o norok en okotme pwaida, kasokela masan, ap sota wa. 
<b>20</b> A mepukat irail, me moredi ong nan pwel mau, me rongada masan o, ale o wa, akai pan me sileisok, a akai me woneisok, a akai me epuki. <b>21</b> A kotin masani ong irail: Kandel pan wisikedo, pwen ko ong pan kasak de pan men wendi? A sote koda wong nin deun kandel? <b>22</b> Pwe sota me rir kot, me so pan sansalada, pil sota me okiok kot, me so pan lok sili. <b>23</b> Meamen me salong a mia men rong, i en rong! 
<b>24</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Komail kalaka, me komail rong! Pwe men song, me komail songki iduen a pil pan song, ong komail. O omail tungol pan laud. <b>25</b> Pwe me a mia, ekis pan ko ong; a me sota a mia, me ekis mi re a, pan katia sang. <b>26</b> A kotin masani: Iet duen wein Kot, ras ong aramas amen, me kamoredi ong nan pwel war en tuka. <b>27</b> Ap mairela, pirida, pong ran; a war o wosada ap kakairida a ap sota asa due. <b>28</b> Pwe pwel kin pein kawosada tuka. Mas katoka, mur mpok, mur war en korn nan mpok ap pwarada. 
<b>29</b> A ni wa ma, a ap madang ki ong men lepukedi, pwe rak mier. <b>30</b> A pil kotin masani: Da me kitail en karasai ong wein Kot? De da me kitail en kasosong ong? <b>31</b> A rasong wan tuka, me pan kamoremoredi ong nan pwel, me tikitik sang war karos, me mi nan sap. <b>32</b> A lao kamoredi, ap wosada laude sang dip karos, ap kasang pasang laud; manpir en pan lang ap kin wendi pan mot a. <b>33</b> I song en karaseras akan toto a kotiki ong irail ni a kawewe ong irail masan o duen ar weweki. 
<b>34</b> Sota a masan, ma so karaseras. Ni arail kelepala, a ap kotin kadede ong sapwilim a tounpadak kan meakan. <b>35</b> Ni ran ota, lao sautik penaer, a kotin masani ong irail: Kitail daulul wong palio! <b>36</b> Ari, ni ar kadar pasang pokon o, re kamasalada nin sop o. Pil akai kisin sop mi re a. <b>37</b> Melimel ap koda, iluk ap pungedi ong nan sop o kadirela. <b>38</b> A a kotikot nan murin sop o, saisaimok pon ulunga. Irail ap kaopada potoan ong i: Saunpadak, kom sota kupurai kit, pweki at pan mela? 
<b>39</b> I ari kotin kipada, angiangida melimel, o kotin masani ong sed o: Moledi! Ang ap dukedi o molela kaualap. <b>40</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Da me komail masapwekeki? Iadue, sota omail poson? <b>41</b> Irail masapwekada kaualap, ap indada nan pung arail: Iaduen ol men et, me ang o sed peiki ong? 
<b>Marcos 5</b><br><b>1</b> IRAIL ari kotela sed o koti dong nan wein Katara; <b>2</b> A lao kotidi sang nin sop, aramas me ngen saut ti poa, madang ap tang dong i sang nin sousou kan. <b>3</b> Pwe a kin kaukauson nin sousou kan, sota amen, me kak saliedi i, sal mata sota pil kak. <b>4</b> Pwe pan toto a salikidi sal en nä o sal mata, ari a kin kamueit pasang sal mata o sal en nä; sota amen me kak kamandala i. <b>5</b> Ansau karos, pong o ran, a kin mimi nan sousou kan o nana, weriwer o pein lokeki i takai. 
<b>6</b> A ni a kilang Iesus wasa doo, a ap tangalang dairukedi. <b>7</b> Weriwer laudeda indada: Menda re omui ngai Iesus, Sapwilim en Kot lapalap? I kaukilang komui Kot, komui ender kawairok ia! <b>8</b> Pwe a kotin masani ong i: Koti sang aramas en, ngen saut koe! <b>9</b> I ari kotin kainoma re a: Is ad om? A ap sapeng potoan ong i: Iet ad ai lekion, pwe kit me toto. <b>10</b> A ap poeki, ender kotin kaus ir ala nin sap o. <b>11</b> Ari, pwin pwik kalaimun eu mimi impan nana o, mangamanga sili. 
<b>12</b> Irail ap poekipoeki potoan ong: Re kotin kadar kit ala, pwe sen til ong nan pwik oko. <b>13</b> Iesus ari kotin mueid ong. Ngen saut akan ap koiei sang, ko ong nan pwik oko. Pwik oko ap tangedi wei ni aul ong nan sed ap mopila nan sed. Irail impan riakid. <b>14</b> Silepa kan ap tangdaui, kaireki sili nan kanim o nan sap; re ap koieila pwen kilang duen me wiaui; <b>15</b> Irail ari poto dong ren Iesus, kilang, me tanwar en tewil o a momod, o likauer, o lolekonglar; iei i me lekion mier re a. Re ap masapwekada, 
<b>16</b> Irail me kilanger ap indai ong irail duen me wiaui ong me tanwar en tewil o, o duen pwik akan. <b>17</b> Re ap poekipoeki, a en kotiwei sang sap arail. <b>18</b> Ni a koti dong nin sop o, me tanwar en tewil o poeki re a en iang i. <b>19</b> A i sota mueid ong i, ap masani ong i: Ko ong ni im om ren sau om, kaire kin ir duen manaman lapalap, me Kaun o wiai ong uk er o poke uk alar. <b>20</b> I ari kola, kaloki sili nan Dekapolis duen manaman lapalap, me Iesus kotin wiai ong i; irail karos ap puriamui kila. 
<b>21</b> Iesus lao pil kotela sed o ong palio, pokon kalaimun eu ap ko pena re a. A kotikot pon oror. <b>22</b> Ari, saumas en sinakoke amen, me ad a Iairus kotido. Ni a tu ong i, ap dairukedi sang. <b>23</b> Poekipoeki kaualap, potoan ong: Nai kisin seripein kis koren iong mela; i poeki, komui en kotido en pwil lim omui poa, pwen kelailada, ap pan maurela. <b>24</b> I ap kotin iangala, aramas toto ap idauenla, koi dong pena. <b>25</b> Li amen, me nta pwilipwil par ngaulriau. 
<b>26</b> O lokolokki me toto ren saunwini toto o kida a kapwa karos, ap sota katepe, pwe a totorela eta. <b>27</b> Ni a rongada duen Iesus, a ap dilongedo muri nan pokon o, o sair sapwilim a likau. <b>28</b> Pwe a inda: Ma i sair sapwilim a likau eta, i pan kelailada. <b>29</b> Ari, pwilipwil en nta madang matata. I ari pamada nan war a, me a kelada sanger somau o. <b>30</b> Iesus ap madang kotin kaanda, me roson kis ko sanger. A ap saupei dong pokon o masani: Is me sair ai likau? 
<b>31</b> A sapwilim a tounpadak kan potoan ong: Komui masani, pokon et koi dong komui penaer; a komui masani: Is me sair ia? <b>32</b> I ari kotin aupwil sili, pwe a men masani, me wia me o. <b>33</b> Li o ari masapwekada, rerer, asaer me wiaui ong i ap potodo, dairukedi mo a, potoan ong duen melel en me wiaui o. <b>34</b> A ap kotin masani ong i: Nai om poson me kakelail uk adar. Ukowei popol, o kelada sang om somau! <b>35</b> Ni a kotin mamasan, irail kodo sang ren saumas en sinakoke indada: Noum serepein o melar. Menda kom kangiri saunpadak en? 
<b>36</b> Ni en Iesus a angi kair o, ap masani ong saumas en sinokoke o: Kom der masak poson eta! <b>37</b> A ap sota kotin mueid ong meamen en idauenla i, Petrus eta, o Iakopus o Ioanes ri en Iakopus. <b>38</b> Irail koti dong ni im en saumas en sinakoke o, ap masani arail moromorong o mamaiei kaualap. <b>39</b> A ni a kotilong, a masani ong irail: Da me komail moromorongki o mamaukeki? Seri en sota melar, a mamair eta. 
<b>40</b> Re ap kakaurureki i. I ari kotikiiei sang irail karos, ap kotin ukada sam o in en seri o me iang i ko; a ap pedelong ong wasa, me seri o wonon ia; <b>41</b> I ari kotin ale pa en seri o masani ong i: Talita kumi! Iet wewe: Seripein i indai ong uk, paurida! <b>42</b> Seripein ap madang paurida, alu; pwe a saunpar eisokriau; irail ap malamalaua kila kaualap. <b>43</b> I ari kotin kalik irail edi, pwe meamen de asada, ap masani, kisin manga en ko ong. 
<b>Marcos 6</b><br><b>1</b> A AP kotila sang wasa o, ap koti dong ni udan sap we, o sapwilim a tounpadak kan idauenla i. <b>2</b> Lao lel ran en sapat a kotin tapiada kawewe nan sinakoke; me toto, me rong puriamui kila, inda: Ia me lap en ale mepukat? O lolekong da eu, me mi re a, me a kak wiaki manaman pukat? <b>3</b> Kaidin i kapenta o, nain Maria, o ri en Iakopus, o Ioses, o Iudas, o Simon? A kaidin ri a li akan kin mi re atail? Re ap makar kida i. 
<b>4</b> Iesus ap kotin masani ong irail: Sota wasa, me saukop sota wau ia; nan sap we ta o ren sau i ko o toun im a. <b>5</b> A ap sota kak wia manaman eu wasa o, me somau kai ta, me a kotin pwil po ar lim a ko o kakelail ir ada. <b>6</b> I ari pone kida ar soposon, ap kotin paang sili kanim akan, kaukawewe. <b>7</b> A ap kotin molipe dong re a ekriamen oko, o kotin kadar ir pasang riamen me eu o kotiki ong irail manaman, en poedi ngen saut akan. 
<b>8</b> Ap masani ong irail, re depa wa meakot ni ar koko sili, sokon eta pot; iermen ed, de prot, pil sota moni nan ed. <b>9</b> A re ko ong eta nan sut, o der mi nan sakit riau. <b>10</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Wasa kis, me komail pedelong onger ni im eu, komail mimieta, komail lao kowei sang wasa o. <b>11</b> A wasa eu, me kang kasamo o rong komail, komail sipede sang pwel ni nä omail, ni omail ko sang, pwen kadede ong irail. <b>12</b> Irail ap kola o padapadaki me ren kalula. 
<b>13</b> Re pil kause sang tewil toto o keie me somau toto, kakela ir ada. <b>14</b> Erodes nanmarki mangiada pwe mar a indandsili, a ap masani: Iei Ioanes saunpaptais o, me mauredar sang ren me melar akan; iei i me a wia manaman pukat. <b>15</b> Akai inda, me i Elias; a akai, me i saukop amen de amen me due saukop akan. <b>16</b> Ni en Erodes mangiada, a ap masani: Ioanes, me i lepuke sang mong a; ari, a mauredar. 
<b>17</b> Pwe pein Erodes me poron wong, saikidi Ioanes, o saliedi ong nan imateng pweki Erodias, en ri a ol Pilip a paud, pwe a id paude kida i. <b>18</b> Pwe Ioanes me kapung ong Erodes: Me sota pung omui atia sang en ri omui ol a paud! <b>19</b> Iei, me Erodias kailong kila i, ap men kamela i; ari, a sota kak. <b>20</b> Pwe Erodes laan Ioanes, pwe a asa, me i ol pung o saraui amen, i ari tataakata i, ap peiki ong o mamangi pereneki. 
<b>21</b> Ari ran mau eu, ran en Erodes a ipwidi, a ap kotin kamadipa a saupedi ko, o a saumas oko, o na me lapalap en wein Kaliläa kan. <b>22</b> Ari, nain Erodias seripein ap kodo kakalek, kamakamait Erodes o me momod re a ko ni tepel. Nanmarki ap masani ong seripein o: Poeki re i meakot, me koe mauki; i pan ki ong uk. <b>23</b> I ari kotin kaula: Meakot koe poeki re i, i pan ki ong uk, lao lel apali en ai wei. <b>24</b> A ap pedoi wei indang in a: Da me i pan poeki? I ari inda: Monga en Ioanes saunpaptais o. 
<b>25</b> I ari puredo, madang dong ren nanmarki o, poeki, inda: I men, komui en madang kotikido monga en Ioanes saunpaptais nan eu dal en! <b>26</b> Nanmarki ap topwaila kaualap, a pweki a kotin kaula o pweki ir, me iang i ni tepel ko a sota men kapapalawei. <b>27</b> Nanmarki o ap madang poronela saunpei amen, masani, monga en wisikedo. <b>28</b> A ap kola, lepuke sang monga nan imateng, ap wa kodo monga o nan dal eu ap ki ong seripein o; seripein o ap kiong in a. <b>29</b> A tounpadak kan lao rongadar, re ap kodo ale kalep a, nekidala nan sousou o. 
<b>30</b> Wanporon oko kapokon irail pena ren Iesus, potoan ong i meakaros, me re wia o me re kawewedar. <b>31</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Komail kelepe dong sap tan; kamol mas. Pwe me toto purepure ir; irail ari sota pisokki ong manga. <b>32</b> Re ap takeda sop kelep kolang sap tan. <b>33</b> A me toto kilang ar masalwei; me toto asa irail. Ir ari sapal sang kanim karos audi mo a ap pokon pena re a. 
<b>34</b> A Iesus lao keredi sang nan sop o, ap masani aramas toto, ap kupura ir ala; pwe likamata irail sip, me sota sile parail. A ap kotin tapiada kawewe ong irail me toto. <b>35</b> Wasa lao sautik penaer, sapwilim a tounpadak kan ap poto dong re a, potoan ong: Sap tan eu met, ap wasa sautiker; <b>36</b> Re kotin kadar ir pasang; pwe re en paangewei sap koren kan o kisin kanim akan o pein netiada kan ar prot, pwe sota man ar tungole meakot. 
<b>37</b> A ap kotin sapeng masani ong irail: Komail katungole ir! Re ap potoan ong i: Se en kola en netiada prot me denar riepuki, ap ki ong irail ren tungole? <b>38</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Lopon en prot depa mi re omail? Kowei kalelapok! Re lao kalelepok, rap potoan: Me limau o mam riamen. <b>39</b> A masani ong irail, re en wia pwini ir pasang, kamodang ir di ong nan rä. <b>40</b> Re ap mondi ni pwin akai; wad epuki o limek ni pwin ko. 
<b>41</b> A ap kotikidar lopon en prot limau o mam riamen masandang nanlang laolaoki pwilitiki pasang o ari, kotiki ong sapwilim a tounpadak kan, pwe re en nek ong irail. O mam riamen oko pil nene ong irail karos. <b>42</b> Irail karos ari mangaer o medier. <b>43</b> Rap deuk pena kopou eisokriau maremor en kamadip o luan mam oko. <b>44</b> Irail me tungoler prot oko me ol limekid. <b>45</b> I ari kotin madang pain sapwilim a tounpadak kan, irail en ko ong nin sop o, tiong wong Petsaida palio, pwe a pan kotin kadar pasang pokon o. 
<b>46</b> A lao kotin kadar ir pasang, a ap kotidalang nan nana eu, pwen laolao. <b>47</b> A lao sautik penaer, sop o mi nan ailep en sed. A a kotin kalakalapuk nan sap. <b>48</b> I ari masani arail wiawia roro, pwe me ang matong irail. A nin soran a koti dong irail, weid pon sed, a pan dauli ir wei. <b>49</b> Ari, ni arail kilang a kotin weweid pon sed, re kiki ong, me ani amen. Re ap weriwer. <b>50</b> Pwe irail karos kilang i, ap masapwekada i ari madang masani ong irail, masani: Komail perenda! Ngai met! Der masak! 
<b>51</b> I ari keredang pon sop o, ap moledi. Re ap puriamui kila kaualap o ponepone kida. <b>52</b> Pwe re sota lamelame prot oko; pwe mongiong arail kakaluakalar. <b>53</b> Irail lao kotido, re ap inen dong wein Kenesaret, ap kai ong liman sap o. <b>54</b> Irail lao keredi sang nan sop o, re ap madang asada i. <b>55</b> Re ap tang pasang wasa karos tapiada wa dong i me somau pon ro ’rail wasa re rong, me a kot ia. 
<b>56</b> O wasa, me a kot ia, kisin kanim laud akan de sap, re kin pwilikidi nani al akan me somau kan, ap poekipoeki, pwe re en sair kailan sapwilim a likau ta. Ari, me doke ko karos ap kelailada. 
<b>Marcos 7</b><br><b>1</b> PARISÄR akan o saunkawewe kai ap pokon dong re a, me kodo sang Ierusalem. <b>2</b> Ni ar kilang sapwilim a tounpadak kai kang proteki pa samin, so omiom, <b>3</b> Pwe Parisär o Sus akan sota kin manga, ma re so omiom mas; pwe re wiawia padok en men kaua ko. <b>4</b> O ni ar kin puredo sang wasan net, re sota kin manga, ma re so omiom mas o tiak toto, me re aledi, re kin wiawia: Widen dal tikitik, dal laud, dal pras, o men momod. 
<b>5</b> Parisär o saunkawewe ko ap kalelapok re a: Da me sapwilim omui tounpadak kan sota wiaki padok en men kaua kan? A re mangata prot so omiom? <b>6</b> A kotin sapeng masani ong irail: Melel eta en Iesaias a kopadar, me komail malaun akan, iduen a intingidi: Aramas pukat kin kaka kin ia au arail a mongiong arail me doo sang ia. <b>7</b> Ari, me mal ar kaudok ia, pwe kusoned en aramas eta, me re kin kaukawewe. 
<b>8</b> Pwe komail kin kasela kusoned en Kot, ap id wiawia padok en aramas; iet widen dal laud o tikitik ape; o pil akai, me komail kin wiawia. <b>9</b> A ap kotin masani ong ir, melel me komail kasela kusoned en Kot, pwe komail en apwaliela pein omail padok. <b>10</b> Pwe Moses masani: Kakaki sam om o in om; o meamen me lalaue sam a de in a en kamela! <b>11</b> A komail kin inda, ma aramas amen indai ong sam de in a: Korpan (iet wewe: Mairong eu), me koe men aneki sang ia, komail inda, me sota dip a. 
<b>12</b> Komail ari sola mueid ong i, en wiai ong sam a de in a meakot. <b>13</b> Ap kawe kila masan en Kot omail padok, me komail pein wiada. O pil me toto me due met, me komail kin wiawia. <b>14</b> Ni a kotin molipe pena pokon o re a, a kotin masani ong irail: Komail karos rong ia o weweki! <b>15</b> Sota meakot men likin aramas, me pan ko ong lole, kasaminela i. A meakan, me koda sang lole, iei eta, me kin kasaminela aramas. <b>16</b> Meamen me salong a mia men rong, i en rong! 
<b>17</b> Ni a kotilong ong nan im eu sang ren aramas oko, sapwilim a tounpadak kan ap kalelapok re a duen karaseras o. <b>18</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Da, komail me pil so lolekong? Komail sota dedeki, me meakot men likin aramas kin ko ong lole, sota kak kasaminela? <b>19</b> Pwe a sota ko ong nan mongiong; pwe nan kaped eta, ap pidok wei sang, koko sang meakaros? <b>20</b> A ap kotin masani: Meakan me koda sang lol en aramas, iei me kin kasaminela aramas. 
<b>21</b> Pwe sang lol en aramas, sang nan mongiong, lamelam sued kin tapida sang ia: Kamal, nenek, kamela aramas. <b>22</b> Pirap, norok, sarsued, widing, sengo, poosued, lalaue, lemai, soukoti. <b>23</b> Mesued pukat karos tapida sang lole, ap kasaminela aramas. <b>24</b> I ari kotila sang wasa o, kotilang wein Tirus o Sidon, a lao kotilong ong nan im eu, ap sota men, aramas en asa i. Ari, a sota kak rirala. <b>25</b> Pwe li amen, me na seripein ngen saut ti poa, madang rongada i, ap kodo dairukedi ong ni aluwilu a. 
<b>26</b> Li en Krik amen, kisan sap en Siro-Penisia, potoan ong, poekipoeki re a, en kausa sang na seripein tewil o. <b>27</b> Iesus ap kotin masani ong i: Seri ko pan med mas. Pwe a sota mau, ale en seri ar tungol prot, ap kasedi ong ren kiti. <b>28</b> A ap sapeng potoan ong i: Melel Maing! Ari, kiti kin tungole maremor en seri ar tungol prot pan tepel. <b>29</b> A ap kotin masani ong i: Pweki om lokaia en, kowei! Tewil o ko sanger ren noum serepein o! <b>30</b> Ari, a kolang im a, a ap diarada, me tewil o ko sanger, o na seripein wonon nan deu a. 
<b>31</b> A lao pil kotin purela sang wein Tirus o Sidon ap koti dong ni le en Kaliläa, weidedo nan wein Dekapolis. <b>32</b> Re ap wa dong i salongepon amen, me lotong; irail ari poeki re a, en kotin pwil poa lim a. <b>33</b> I ari kotin ukala sang i ni pokon o, ap kilong ong sondin lim a nan salong a o likidi sair lo a. <b>34</b> A ap masandang nanlang, asmamarada, masani ong i: Epata! (iet wewe: Wasok pasang). <b>35</b> Salong a ap madang wasok pasang o sal en lo a lapwada. A ap lokaia mau. 
<b>36</b> I ari kalik irail edi re depa indai ong meamen. A kainapwi irail; ari, re ap kalaudela ar kaloki sili. <b>37</b> Re puriamui kila kaualap, inda: Me mau, me a wia dar kan! Pwe a kakarongada me salongepon, ap kalokaiada, me lotong. 
<b>Marcos 8</b><br><b>1</b> NI ran oko pil pokon kalaimunia mia, ap sota me re tungole; a ap kotin molipedo sapwilim a tounpadak kan, masani ong irail: <b>2</b> I pokela pokon et, pwe re ieiang ia ran silu, ap sota ar tungol. <b>3</b> O ma I kadar ir wong ni im ar dupok, re pan lipongmas nani al, pwe akai ir kodo sang wasa doo. <b>4</b> A sapwilim a tounpadak kan ap sapeng potoan ong i: Ia wasa, me amen pan kak ki sang ia prot, men kamedela pokon et nan sap tan et? 
<b>5</b> A ap kotin kainoma re irail: Prot depa mi re omail? Re ap sapeng potoan ong: Me isu. <b>6</b> I ari kotin masani ong pokon o, en mondi ong nan pwel, ap kotikida lopon en prot isu, laolaoki, pilitiki pasang o ari, ap kotiki ong sapwilim a tounpadak kan, pwe ren nek. Irail ari nek ong pokon o. <b>7</b> Kisin mam kai pil mia, i ari laolaoki o masani, ren pil neki ong irail. <b>8</b> Irail ari manga o medier. Irail ap deuk pena kopou en maremor isu luan kamadip, <b>9</b> Me mangaer me koren iong pakid. A ap kotin kadar ir pasang. 
<b>10</b> Io sapwilim a tounpadak kan ap madang kotidang pon sop pot, kotelang nan wein Dalmanuta. <b>11</b> Parisär akai ap kodo, tapiada akamai ong i, men kilang re a kilel sang nanlang, pwe re kasongesong i. <b>12</b> A ap kotin kaangangala nan ngen i, masani: Menda di wet ngongki kilel eu? Melel I indai ong komail: Sota kilel eu pan ko ong di wet. <b>13</b> I ari kotila sang irail, pil pur ong nin sop o, kotilang palio. <b>14</b> Irail ap monokela prot oko, men kapinwar arail, lopon prot ta ieu mimi re rail nin sop o. 
<b>15</b> I ari kapung ong irail masani: Kalaka en Parisär ko ar kalap o en Erodes a kalap. <b>16</b> Irail ap lamelame nan pung arail: Sota atail prot oko. <b>17</b> Ni en Iesus a kotin mangiada, a masani ong irail: Da me komail toutouki a sota kan omail prot? Komail saikenta dedekila o weweki? Mongiong omail kapikapitakaita? <b>18</b> Mas omail mia, ap sota kilang; salong omail mia, ap sota rong. O komail sota tamataman, 
<b>19</b> Ni ai pilingong me limekid lopon en prot limau o, kopou en maremor depa, me komail deukada? Re ap potoan ong i: Me eisokriau. <b>20</b> O isu nan pung en me pakid, kopou en maremor depa, me komail deukada? Re ap potoan: Me isu. <b>21</b> A ap kotin kainoma re ’rail: A da me komail sota dedeki? <b>22</b> A ap koti dong Petsaida. Re ap wa dong i maskun amen, poekipoeki en sair i. <b>23</b> A ap kotin ale pa en me maskun o kaluala sang ni kisin kanim o; A ap likidi ong ni mas a o pwil poa lim a ko ap kainoma re a, ma a kilangada wasa de so? 
<b>24</b> I ari sarada potoan: I kilang aramas akan likamada tuka kai, me alialu sili. <b>25</b> A pil pwil pon mas a lim a ko, ap kasaradakeda. I ari kelailada ap kilangada mau. <b>26</b> A ap kotin kadaralang nin im a masani: Koe depa pur wong ni kisin kanim o, o pil depa indai ong meamen nin kanim o! <b>27</b> Iesus i sapwilim a tounpadak kan ap kotieila nan kanim en Säsarea Pilipi ko. Ari, ni a kotikot kowei, ap kotin kainoma ren sapwilim a tounpadak kan masani ong irail: Is me aramas inda me ngai? 
<b>28</b> Irail sapeng i potoan ong: Ioanes saunpaptais; a akai inda, me Elias; a akai, me amen sang ren saukop akan. <b>29</b> A ap kotin kainoma re irail: A komail, is me komail kin inda me ngai? Petrus ap sapeng potoan ong i: Komui Kristus! <b>30</b> I ari kotin kalik irail edi, re depa indai ong meamen i. <b>31</b> A ap kotin tapiada kawewe kin irail, me Nain aramas en kalokolok toto o sokonekonla pan saumas akan, o samero lapalap akan, o saunkawewe kan, o pan kamela, ap pan maureda murin ran silu. 
<b>32</b> Masan wet a kotin masani sansal. Petrus ap ukada i tapiada kapung ong i. <b>33</b> Iesus ap kotin saupeido aupwil sili pon sapwilim a tounpadak kan, kapung ong Petrus masani: Kola muri, Satan! Pwe om lamelam kaidin en Kot, a en aramas. <b>34</b> A kotin molipedo pokon o sapwilim a tounpadak kan, a masani ong irail: Meamen pan idauen ia, en tounmeteki pein i, ap wada a lopu, idauen ia. <b>35</b> Pwe meamen kanakanai maur i, pan pupe sang. A meamen kasela maur i pweki ngai o rongamau, pan dorela i. 
<b>36</b> Pwe da me katepan aramas a aneki sappa u, ap kasela maur? <b>37</b> O da me aramas pan wiliankida maur i? <b>38</b> Pwe meamen namenok kin ia o ai padak kan nan pung en di en kamal o sued wed, i me Nain aramas pil pan ailaki, ni a pan kodo ni lingan en Sam a iang tounlang saraui kan. 
<b>Marcos 9</b><br><b>1</b> A AP kotin masani ong irail: Melel I indai ong komail: Akai me iang mi met, sota pan mela, lao kilang wein Kot a kokido manaman. <b>2</b> Murin pong wonu pong Iesus ap kotin ukada Petrus o Iakopus o Ioanes kalua ir dalang pon nana ileile eu, ir kalapuk. I ari kotin wukila mo ’rail. <b>3</b> Sapwilim a likau ko lingalingala, puetepueteta sno. Sota saunlopolop nan sappa kak kapuetepuetetala dueta. <b>4</b> Elias i Moses ap pwara dong ir, ari, ira o Iesus ap mamasan pena. 
<b>5</b> Petrus ap sapeng potoan ong Iesus: Rapi, me mau ong kitail en kotikot met. Kitail en kauada kisin im silikis: Tanpas ar, o en Moses o en Elias. <b>6</b> Pwe a sasa, da me a pan inda, pwe re masapwekada. <b>7</b> Pali en dapok pali ap kadupwal irail edi; o ngil eu peidido sang nan dapok o: I met nai. Ol kompok! Komail rong i! <b>8</b> Ari, re saliokeda, solar amen me re kilang, Iesus ta, me kotikot re ’rail. 
<b>9</b> Ari, ni arail kotikot kotido sang nin nana o, a kotin kalik irail edi, re depa indai ong meamen, me re kilang ko, lao Nain aramas mauredar sang ren me melar akan. <b>10</b> Re ap edie pena masan pot et nan pung ar, ap peipeidokki pena, da wewe en maureda sang ren me melar akan? <b>11</b> Irail ap peidok re a potoan ong: Da me saunkawewe kan indaki, Elias pan pwarado mas? <b>12</b> I ari kotin sapeng, masani ong irail: Melel Elias kodo mas, kamauiada meakan. O iaduen intingidi duen Nain aramas? Me pan kolokolok toto o sokonekon. 
<b>13</b> A I indai ong komail, Elias pwarer, o re wiai ong i, me re mauki duen a intingidi due. <b>14</b> A lao koti dong tounpadak kan, a masani pokon kalaimunia eu kapil ir pena o saunkawewe kan peipeidok re irail. <b>15</b> Ni ar kilangadar i, pokon o ap puriamuiki la tangado ranamau ong i. <b>16</b> A ap kotin kainoma re irail: Peidok da kai, me komail idedok re ’rail? <b>17</b> Amen toun pokon o sapeng i potoan ong: Saunpadak, i potoan dong komui nai putak o, me ngen lotong mi poa. 
<b>18</b> Ari, wasa karos me a kin tiada poa, a kin kapururada; a ap kin pudopud o katekatete ngi o tongetongela. I potoan ong sapwilim omui tounpadak kan, pwe re en kausa sang i, ari, re sota kak. <b>19</b> A ap kotin sapeng irail masani: Di wet meid soposon. Kilang wasa, re nekidedi ia. <b>7</b> Komail kowei, kaireki a tounpadak kan o Petrus, me a pan tiong mo omail koti wong Kaliläa. I wasa, komail ap pan kilang i, duen a kotin masani ong komail. <b>8</b> Re ap madangiei sang o tang sang nin sousou o, pwe re masapwekada rerer, re pil sota indai ong meakot meamen, pwe re masapwekada. 
<b>9</b> Ari, murin a iasada sangkonai nin tapin wik a ap kotin pwar ong mas Maria men Magdala, me a kotin kause sang tewil isimen. <b>10</b> A ap kola indai ong irail me iang i ko mas, ni ar mamaiei o sangesang. <b>11</b> Ni arail ronger, me a iasadar, o me a kotin pwar onger i, re sota kamelele. <b>12</b> Muri a kotin pwar ong riamen irail ni eu mom, ni ara kokolang nan sap. <b>13</b> Re ap kola indai ong me tei kan. A re sota pil kamelele ira. 
<b>14</b> Mur a pil kotin pwar ong ir ekamen oko ni ar momod tungotungol, ap kotin kapung ong irail ar soposon o kapitakai, pweki ar sota kamelele ir me kilang i murin a iasadar. <b>15</b> A ap kotin masani ong irail: Komail ko ong sappa u, padaki ong aramas karos rongamau! <b>16</b> Meamen me poson o paptaisola, pan dorela. A me so poson pan kariala. <b>17</b> A kilel pukat pan idauen ir, me poson: Ni ad ai re pan kausaki sang tewil akan, re pan lokaia toror. 
<b>18</b> Re pan kawela serpent; ma re nimala men kamela kot, a sota pan kawe ir ala; irail pan pwil pa ar pon me somau, re ap pan kelailada. <b>19</b> Ari, Kaun o lao kotin masani ong irail mepukat, a ap peukadala nanlang o kotido ong pali maun en Kot. <b>20</b> Irail ap kola, padapadak sili wasa karos. A Kaun o kotin iang dodok, kameleleki masan manaman me iang.
