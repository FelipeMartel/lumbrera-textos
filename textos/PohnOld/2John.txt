<b>2 Juan</b><br><b>1</b> IET ngai me laud o ranamau wong li lipilipil o na seri kan, me i kin pokepoke melel; a kaidin ngai ta, pwe pil irail karos, me asa melel, <b>2</b> Pweki melel o, me mi re atail, o a pan mi re atail kokolata. <b>3</b> Mak o kalangan, o popol pan mi re atail sang ren Kot Sam, o sang ren Iesus Kristus, me Sapwilim en Sam ni melel o limpok. <b>4</b> I ap peren kida kaualap, pwe i diaradar, me akai noum seri kan alialu wei ta ni melel, duen atail aleer kusoned sang ren Sam. 
<b>5</b> Ari, i poeki re omui, Maing, kaidin kusoned kap pot, me i inting wong komui, a me kitail aleer sang ni tapi o kitail en pololol pena. <b>6</b> A iet limpok, pwe sen alu weita duen a kusoned akan. A met kusoned o, duen komail ronger sang ni tapi, sen alialuta ia. <b>7</b> Men kotaue me toto pwara dong sappa, me sota kin kadede, me Iesus Kristus wia uduk ala, i men kotaue o kristus likam o. <b>8</b> Komail kalaka pein komail, pwe omail pai, me komail dodok ong er, de salongala a komail en ale pwaipwai pokon. 
<b>9</b> Meamen me dauli mepukat, o me sota tengedi ong padak en Kristus, a sota paieki Kot; a me tengedi ong padak o, i me paiekier karos, Sam o sapwilim a Ol. <b>10</b> Ma amen ko dong komail, me sota wado padak et, der kasamo long ong nan im omail, o der ranamaui i. <b>11</b> Pwe me kin ranamaui i, a pan pwaisaneki a wiawia sued. <b>12</b> Kasoi me toto mi re i, me i men inting wong komail, a i sota pan wiaki ki kisin likau o ink, pwe i kaporoporeki, me i pan pwara wong komail, kitail ap pan kasoi pena, pwe atail peren en unsokela. 
<b>13</b> Ari, nain ri om li lipilipil seriko me ranamau wong komui. Amen.
