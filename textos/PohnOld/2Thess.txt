2 Thessalonians|3|0,4,9
<b>2 Thessalonians 1</b><br><b>1</b> PAULUS o Silwanus, o Timoteus ong momodisou en Tesalonik me ren Kot Sam atail, o atail Kaun Iesus Kristus. <b>2</b> Mak o popol sang ren Kot Sam atail o atail Kaun Iesus Kristus en mi re omail! <b>3</b> Mak eta sen poden danke Kot pweki komail, ri ai kan, pwe omail poson kin laudela kaualap, o omail limpok pena pwaida kaualap. <b>4</b> I me kit suai kin komail ong momodisou en Kot akan pweki omail kanongama o poson ni omail kalokolok o kamekam, me lel ong komail. 
<b>5</b> Iei me kasansal pan kadeik pung en Kot, komail ap pan kon ong wein Kot, me komail kamekamki. <b>6</b> Pwe me pung ta ong Kot en depukki ong irail me moromorouo ong komail. <b>7</b> A komail, me kalokolok, pan iang kit meleileila, ni kasale pan Kaun Iesus sang nanlang iang sapwilim a tounlang manaman akan, <b>8</b> Sansaldo ni kisiniai umpumpul, kapukapung ong irail me poponsasa Kot, o ong irail, me so peiki ong rongamau en atail Kaun Iesus Kristus. 
<b>9</b> O me pan lokidokilang kamekam soutuk sang mon silang en Kaun o sang mon a manaman lingan. <b>10</b> Ni a pan kotido o lingan kila sapwilim a saraui kan, ni ran o, o usun kin ir karos, me posonlar i, pwe komail kamelele atail kadede. <b>11</b> I me se kin pil poden kapakapa kin komail, pwe Kot en kotin kakonekon komail ong paeker o a kotin kaerela omail kapunglol o kapwaiada wiawia en poson ki manaman. 
<b>12</b> Pwe mar en atail Kaun Iesus Kristus en kalinganada re omail, o komail en konekonla re a duen mak en atail Kot o atail Kaun Iesus Kristus. 
<b>2 Thessalonians 2</b><br><b>1</b> SE men poeki re omail ri at akan iran atail Kaun Iesus Kristus a pan kotin pwarado, o atail pan peukedalang i, <b>2</b> Komail der wukiwuk sili o der wapon kida ngen eu, de kasoi pot, de kisin likau, me dene kit kadar wong komail, me dene ran en Kaun o mier. <b>3</b> Sota amen en kotau komail la ni meakot. Pwe ran o sota pan pwarado, lao wiawia en mas more wei sang, o aramas en dip sansal la, nain me sued. 
<b>4</b> Me pan palian meakaros o kasapwilada pein i, en ileila sang karos, me udan Kot de men kaudok kan, A pan mondi nan tanpas en Kot o mome ong pein i Kot. <b>5</b> Komail sota tamanda ni ai mimi re omail, i katiti ong komail mepukat? <b>6</b> Ari met komail asaer, me meakot mia, me kin kapwandela a pan pwarado. <b>7</b> A rir en sapung kan ap mur pwaida met. Ari so, a mimieta me kin kapwandela, lao a pan poioki wei sang; 
<b>8</b> I ansau me likin kapung o pan sansaleda, me Kaun Iesus pan kotin kame kila ang en silang i, o kawe kila lingan en a pan pwarado. <b>9</b> I me pan pwara kido mana en Satan, iangaki manaman o kilel akai o kapuriam ni likam kai. <b>10</b> O iangaki widing en sapung karos ong irail, me pan lokidokila, pwe re kang limpok ong melel, pwe ren dorela. <b>11</b> I me Kot kotin mueid ong irail ni manaman sued eu men kaping ir ada, pwe ren kamelele likam. <b>12</b> Pwe karos pan pakadeikada me so kamelele masan melel, a me mauki me sapung. 
<b>13</b> A mak eta, sen danke Kot ansau karos pweki komail, ri ai kompok kan, o pwe sang nin tapi o Kot me kotin pila kidar komail ong maur potopot ni saraui la pan Ngen o ni poson melel o. <b>14</b> Pwe ong mepukat a kotin molipei komail er pan at rongamau, pwe komail en pwais aneki lingan en atail Kaun Iesus Kristus. <b>15</b> Ari ri at akan, tengedi ong o apwali padak, me komail padakkier, ma sang ni kasoi de at kisin likau. 
<b>16</b> A pein atail Kaun Iesus Kristus o Kot o Sam atail, me pok ong kitail o kupura dong kitail kamait soutuk o kapor mau pan mak, <b>17</b> I en kamaitala mongiong omail, o katingi komail ong ni wiawia mau karos o padak. 
<b>2 Thessalonians 3</b><br><b>1</b> ARI ri at akan, komail kapakapa kin kit, pwe masan en Kaun o en pwaida o linganla duen a wiauier re omail. <b>2</b> O pwe kit en sapedokala sang aramas solelapok kan; pwe poson kaidin pwais en aramas karos. <b>3</b> A Kaun o me melel, me pan katenge komail o nikid sang ni me sued. <b>4</b> Se kaporoporeki Kaun o re omail, me komail kin dadaurata o pan wiada, me se kakaliki ong komail er. <b>5</b> A Kaun o en kotin wiawia mongiong omail, pwe komail en direki limpok en Kot o kanongama en Kristus. 
<b>6</b> Ri ai kan, se kakaliki ong komail ni mar en atail Kaun Iesus Kristus, me komail en muei sang saulang karos, me kin dadaurata padak, me komail ronger sang kit. <b>7</b> Pwe komail asa duen idedauen kit, pwe kit sota wiadar sapung nan pung omail. <b>8</b> O se sota ale sang amen kan at manga, ap sota pwaipwai, pwe se dodok apwal ni pong o ni ran, pwe sen der katoutouki amen komail. <b>9</b> A kaidin pwe a sota muei ong kit, a pwe sen wia kin kit kilel en me komail en alale sang. 
<b>10</b> Pwe ni at mimi re omail, iet me se kakaliki ong komail, meamen me kang wia dodok, a pil sota pan manga. <b>11</b> Pwe se ronger, me akai mi re omail, me kin weweid ni mesued ni ar solar kin dodok, a re apwali kisin tiak mal. <b>12</b> Ari, irail me due met, se indai ong o panaui irail, pan Kaun Iesus Kristus, me irail en dodok, o nenenla o tungole pein kan ar manga. <b>13</b> A komail, ri ai kan, der pangki omail wiawia mau. 
<b>14</b> A ma amen kang oke at padak nan kisin likau et, i me komail en kileledi, o komail der apwali i, pwe i en namenokela. <b>15</b> A komail der wiaki i imwintiti amen, a sauasa i dueta ri omail amen. <b>16</b> A Kaun en popol en pein kotiti ong komail popol ansau karos o ni song karos! Kaun o en kotikot re omail karos! <b>17</b> Ngai Paulus ranamau ni mangin pa i, me kisin likau karos kilelekidi, iduen ai inting. <b>18</b> Mak en atail Kaun Iesus Kristus en mi re omail karos!
