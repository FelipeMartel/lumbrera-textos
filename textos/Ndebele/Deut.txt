<b>Deuteronomy 1</b><br><b>1</b> La ngamazwi uMozisi awakhuluma kuIsrayeli wonke nganeno kweJordani enkangala, egcekeni eliqondene leSufi, phakathi kweParani leTofeli leLabani a leHazerothi leDizahabi b. <b>2</b> Kulensuku ezilitshumi lanye kusukela eHorebe ngendlela yentaba yeSeyiri kusiya eKadeshi-Bhaneya c. <b>3</b> Kwasekusithi ngomnyaka wamatshumi amane, ngenyanga yetshumi lanye, ngolokuqala lwenyanga d, uMozisi wakhuluma kubantwana bakoIsrayeli njengakho konke iNkosi eyamlaya ngakho kubo, 
<b>4</b> esemtshayile uSihoni inkosi yamaAmori e owayehlala eHeshiboni f, loOgi inkosi yeBashani g owayehlala eAshitarothi eEdreyi. <b>5</b> Nganeno kweJordani, elizweni lakoMowabi, uMozisi waqala ukuchasisa lumlayo esithi: <b>6</b> INkosi uNkulunkulu wethu yakhuluma kithi eHorebe isithi: Selihlale isikhathi eseneleyo kulintaba h. 
<b>7</b> Phendukani lisuke, liye entabeni yamaAmori, lendaweni ezakhelene lawo, egcekeni, entabeni, lesihotsheni, leningizimu, lekhunjini lolwandle, ilizwe lamaKhanani, leLebhanoni, kuze kube semfuleni omkhulu, umfula iYufrathi. <b>8</b> Bonani, ngibeke ilizwe phambi kwenu. Ngenani, lidle ilifa lelizwe iNkosi eyalifungela oyihlo, oAbrahama, uIsaka, loJakobe, ukubanika lona lenzalo yabo emva kwabo i. <b>9</b> Njalo ngakhuluma kini ngalesosikhathi ngisithi: Kangilakulithwala ngingedwa j. 
<b>10</b> INkosi uNkulunkulu wenu ilandisile k, khangelani-ke, lamuhla linjengenkanyezi zamazulu ngobunengi. <b>11</b> INkosi, uNkulunkulu waboyihlo, kayandise kini, njengoba linjalo, ngokuphindwe kankulungwane, ilibusise, njengokukhuluma kwayo kini l. <b>12</b> Ngingedwa ngingathwala njani m ubunzima benu lomthwalo wenu lenkani yenu n? <b>13</b> Zithatheleni amadoda ahlakaniphileyo, azwisisayo, laziwayo ezizweni zenu, ngiwabeke-ke abe zinhloko zenu o. <b>14</b> Laselingiphendula lisithi: Ilizwi olikhulumileyo lilungile ukulenza. 
<b>15</b> Ngasengithatha inhloko zezizwe zenu, amadoda ahlakaniphileyo, aziwayo, ngawabeka aba zinhloko phezu kwenu, induna zezinkulungwane, lenduna zamakhulu, lenduna zamatshumi amahlanu, lenduna zamatshumi p, leziphathamandla zezizwe zenu q. <b>16</b> Ngasengilaya abahluleli benu ngalesosikhathi ngisithi: Zwanini okuphakathi kwabafowenu, lahlulele ngokulunga phakathi komuntu lomfowabo lowezizweni okuye r. 
<b>17</b> Lingakhangeli ubuso ekwahluleleni, zwanini omncinyane njengomkhulu; lingesabi ubuso bomuntu s, ngoba ukwahlulela kungokukaNkulunkulu. Lendaba elukhuni kini t lizayiletha kimi, besengiyizwa u. <b>18</b> Langalesosikhathi ngalilaya zonke izinto elifanele ukuzenza. <b>19</b> Sasesisuka eHorebe, sahamba kuyo yonke leyonkangala enkulu leyesabekayo elayibonayo ngendlela yentaba yamaAmori, njengokusilaya kweNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu v, saze safika eKadeshi-Bhaneya w. 
<b>20</b> Ngasengisithi kini: Selifikile entabeni yamaAmori iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu ezasinika yona. <b>21</b> Bona, iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ilinikile ilizwe phambi kwakho; yenyuka udle ilifa lalo, njengokutsho kweNkosi uNkulunkulu waboyihlo kuwe; ungesabi, ungadideki x. <b>22</b> Laselisondela kimi lonke lathi: Asithume amadoda phambi kwethu ukuze asihlolele ilizwe, abuyise ilizwi kithi, indlela esizakwenyuka ngayo, lemizi esizangena kuyo. <b>23</b> Lendaba yayinhle emehlweni ami; ngakho ngathatha kini amadoda alitshumi lambili, indodanye ngesizwe y. 
<b>24</b> Wona aphenduka, enyukela entabeni z aze afika esihotsheni seEshikoli, asihlola. <b>25</b> Asethatha okwezithelo zelizwe esandleni sabo, azehlisela kithi; abuyisa ilizwi kithi athi: Ilizwe lihle iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu esinika lona. <b>26</b> Kanti kalifunanga ukwenyuka, kodwa lavukela umlomo weNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu aa, <b>27</b> langunguna emathenteni enu lathi ab: Ngoba iNkosi yayisizonda, yasikhupha elizweni leGibhithe ukusinikela esandleni samaAmori ukusibhubhisa ac. 
<b>28</b> Sizakwenyukela ngaphi? Abafowethu benze inhliziyo yethu iphele amandla ad besithi: Abantu bakhulu bade kulathi; imizi mikhulu, ibiyelwe ngemithangala efika emazulwini ae; futhi-ke sibonile khona amadodana amaAnaki af. <b>29</b> Ngasengisithi kini: Lingatshaywa luvalo, libesabe. <b>30</b> INkosi uNkulunkulu wenu ehamba phambi kwenu, yona izalilwela njengakho konke eyalenzela khona eGibhithe phambi kwamehlo enu ag, 
<b>31</b> lenkangala, lapho obone khona ukuthi iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho yakuthwala njengendoda ithwala indodana yayo, endleleni yonke elahamba ngayo laze lafika kulindawo ah. <b>32</b> Kodwa ngalelilizwi kalikholwanga iNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu ai <b>33</b> eyahamba phambi kwenu endleleni aj, ukulidingela indawo ukuze limise inkamba, ngomlilo ebusuku, ukulitshengisa indlela elizahamba ngayo, langeyezi emini ak. <b>34</b> Kwathi iNkosi isizwile ilizwi lamazwi enu, yathukuthela yafunga yathi al: 
<b>35</b> Isibili kakho kulamadoda alesisizukulwana esibi ozabona ilizwe elihle engafunga ukulinika oyihlo am, <b>36</b> ngaphandle kukaKalebi indodana kaJefune; yena uzalibona. Njalo ngizanika kuye ilizwe anyathela kulo, lakubantwana bakhe, ngoba ulandele iNkosi ngokupheleleyo an. <b>37</b> Lami iNkosi yangithukuthelela ngenxa yenu, isithi: Lawe kawuyikungena khona ao. <b>38</b> UJoshuwa indodana kaNuni ap oma phambi kwakho, yena uzangena khona aq; mqinise, ngoba yena uzakwenza uIsrayeli adle ilifa lalo ar. 
<b>39</b> Labantwanyana benu elalisithi bazakuba yimpango, labantwana benu as, abangaziyo lamuhla okuhle lokubi, bona bazangena khona; lakubo ngizalinika, bona-ke bazakudla ilifa lalo at. <b>40</b> Kodwa lina ziphendukeleni, lisuke liye enkangala, indlela yoLwandle oluBomvu au. <b>41</b> Laseliphendula lisithi kimi: Sonile eNkosini; thina sizakwenyuka siyekulwa, njengakho konke iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu eyasilaya khona av. Selihlomile, ngulowo lalowo isikhali sakhe sempi, lacabanga ukuthi kulula ukwenyukela entabeni. 
<b>42</b> INkosi yasisithi kimi: Tshono kubo: Lingenyuki, lingalwi, ngoba kangikho phakathi kwenu, hlezi litshaywe phambi kwezitha zenu aw. <b>43</b> Ngasengikhuluma kini, kodwa kalilalelanga, lavukela umlomo weNkosi ax, laqholoza lenyukela entabeni. <b>44</b> Kwathi amaAmori ayehlala entabeni leyo aphuma ukumelana lani, alixotsha ay njengokwenziwa zinyosi, alihlakaza eSeyiri, kwaze kwaba seHorma az. <b>45</b> Laselibuya, lakhala inyembezi phambi kweNkosi, kodwa iNkosi kayilizwanga ilizwi lenu, kayibekanga indlebe kini. 
<b>46</b> Laselihlala eKadeshi insuku ezinengi, njengensuku elahlala ngazo ba. 
<b>Deuteronomy 2</b><br><b>1</b> Sasesiphenduka, sasuka saya enkangala, indlela yoLwandle oluBomvu, njengokutsho kweNkosi kimi; njalo sayibhoda intaba yeSeyiri insuku ezinengi a. <b>2</b> INkosi yasikhuluma kimi isithi: <b>3</b> Selibhode intaba le okweneleyo; phendukelani lina enyakatho b. <b>4</b> Balaye-ke abantu usithi: Lidabula umngcele wabafowenu, abantwana bakaEsawu c abahlala eSeyiri d. Njalo bazalesaba, ngakho lizinanzelele kakhulu, 
<b>5</b> lingalwi labo; ngoba kangiyikulinika okwelizwe labo, lelingangokunyathela kwangaphansi konyawo e, ngoba ngimnikile uEsawu intaba yeSeyiri ibe yilifa f. <b>6</b> Lizathenga ukudla kubo ngemali ukuze lidle, lithenge kubo lamanzi ngemali ukuze linathe. <b>7</b> Ngoba iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ikubusisile emsebenzini wonke wesandla sakho; iyazi ukuhamba kwakho kulinkangala enkulu. Le iminyaka engamatshumi amane iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ibilawe, kawuswelanga lutho g. 
<b>8</b> Uba sisedlula sisuka kubafowethu, abantwana bakaEsawu abahlala eSeyiri h, ngendlela yegceke sisuke eElathi i njalo sisuka eEziyoni-Geberi j, saphenduka, sadlula ngendlela yenkangala yakoMowabi. <b>9</b> INkosi yasisithi kimi: Ungahlukuluzi uMowabi, ungalwi labo empini; ngoba kangiyikukunika okwelizwe labo kube yilifa k, ngoba ngibanikile abantwana bakaLothi iAri l ibe yilifa m. <b>10</b> Kwakuhlala khona endulo amaEmi, abantu abakhulu, labanengi, labade n, njengamaAnaki o; 
<b>11</b> babebalwa njengeziqhwaga labo njengamaAnaki, kodwa amaMowabi ababiza ngokuthi ngamaEmi p. <b>12</b> LamaHori ayehlala eSeyiri endulo q, kodwa abantwana bakaEsawu bawakhupha emfuyweni yabo, bawachitha phambi kwabo, bahlala endaweni yawo, njengokwenza kukaIsrayeli elizweni lelifa lakhe, iNkosi eyabanika lona r. <b>13</b> Khathesi, sukumani lina lichaphe isifula iZeredi. Ngakho sachapha isifula iZeredi s. 
<b>14</b> Lensuku esahamba ngazo sisuka eKadeshi-Bhaneya t saze sachapha isifula iZeredi zaziyiminyaka engamatshumi amathathu lesificaminwembili, kwaze kwaphela isizukulwana sonke samadoda empi sisuka phakathi kwenkamba, njengokufunga kweNkosi kuwo u. <b>15</b> Ngoba isibili isandla seNkosi sasimelene lawo, ukuwachitha esuka phakathi kwenkamba, aze aphela. <b>16</b> Kwasekusithi lapho wonke amadoda empi esephelile esifa esuka phakathi kwabantu, <b>17</b> INkosi yasikhuluma kimi isithi: <b>18</b> Lamuhla uzakwedlula iAri, umngcele wakoMowabi, 
<b>19</b> lapho ususondele maqondana labantwana bakoAmoni, ungabahlukuluzi, ungalwi labo, ngoba kangiyikukunika okwelizwe labantwana bakoAmoni kube yilifa v, ngoba ngilinike abantwana bakaLothi libe yilifa w. <b>20</b> Lalelo labalwa njengelizwe leziqhwaga. Iziqhwaga zahlala kulo endulo, kodwa amaAmoni bazibiza ngokuthi ngamaZamuzumu x; <b>21</b> abantu abakhulu, labanengi, labade, njengamaAnaki; kodwa iNkosi yabachitha phambi kwabo, babakhupha emfuyweni yabo, bahlala endaweni yabo y, 
<b>22</b> njengalokho eyakwenzela abantwana bakaEsawu, ababehlala eSeyiri, lapho yawachitha amaHori esuka phambi kwabo, bawakhupha emfuyweni yabo, bahlala endaweni yawo kuze kube lamuhla z. <b>23</b> LamaAvi, ayehlala eHazerimi aa kuze kube seGaza ab; amaKafitori, aphuma eKafitori, awachitha, ahlala endaweni yawo ac. <b>24</b> Sukumani, lisuke ad lichaphe isifula iArinoni. Bona, nginikele esandleni sakho uSihoni, inkosi yeHeshiboni, umAmori, lelizwe lakhe ae; qala ukudla ilifa lalo, ulwe laye empini af. 
<b>25</b> Lamuhla ngizaqalisa ukufaka uvalo ngawe lokwesabeka kwakho phezu kobuso bezizwe, ngaphansi kwamazulu wonke, ezizakuzwa umbiko ngawe, zithuthumele, ziqhaqhazele ngenxa yakho ag. <b>26</b> Ngasengithuma izithunywa zisuka enkangala yeKedemothi kuSihoni inkosi yeHeshiboni, zilamazwi okuthula, zisithi ah: <b>27</b> Ake ai ngidabule elizweni lakho aj; ngizahamba ngomgwaqo kuphela, ngingaphambukeli ngakwesokunene loba ngakwesokhohlo. 
<b>28</b> Uzangithengisela ukudla ngemali ukuze ngidle, ungiphe amanzi ngemali ukuze nginathe; kuphela ngizadabula ngenyawo zami, <b>29</b> njengalokhu abantwana bakaEsawu abahlala eSeyiri lamaMowabi ahlala eAri benza kimi ak, ngize ngichaphe iJordani, ngingene elizweni iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu esinika lona. <b>30</b> Kodwa uSihoni inkosi yeHeshiboni kasivumelanga ukuthi sidlule kuye al; ngoba iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho yenza umoya wakhe waba lukhuni, yenza lenhliziyo yakhe yaba lenkani, ukuze imnikele esandleni sakho, njengalamuhla am. 
<b>31</b> INkosi yathi kimi: Bona, sengiqalisile ukunikela phambi kwakho uSihoni lelizwe lakhe; qalisa ukudla ilifa, ukuze udle ilifa lelizwe lakhe an. <b>32</b> Kwasekuphuma uSihoni ukumelana lathi, yena labantu bakhe bonke, ukulwa eJahazi ao. <b>33</b> INkosi uNkulunkulu wethu yasimnikela phambi kwethu ap; sasesimtshaya lamadodana akhe labantu bakhe bonke aq. <b>34</b> Ngalesosikhathi sasesithumba yonke imizi yakhe, satshabalalisa wonke umuzi, abesilisa labesifazana labantwanyana; kasitshiyanga insali ar; 
<b>35</b> sazithumbela izifuyo kuphela, lempango yemizi esayithumbayo. <b>36</b> Kusukela eAroweri esekhunjini lwesifula iArinoni, lomuzi osesifuleni, kuze kube seGileyadi kwakungekho muzi owawuphakeme kakhulu kithi as. INkosi uNkulunkulu wethu yayinikela yonke phambi kwethu at. <b>37</b> Kuphela kawusondelanga elizweni labantwana bakoAmoni, endaweni yonke yesifula iJaboki, lemizini esezintabeni au, lakukuphi iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu esenqabela khona av. 
<b>Deuteronomy 3</b><br><b>1</b> Sasesiphenduka sisenyuka ngendlela yeBashani. UOgi inkosi yeBashani wasephuma ukumelana lathi, yena labantu bakhe bonke a, ukulwa eEdreyi b. <b>2</b> Kodwa iNkosi yathi kimi: Ungamesabi, ngoba ngizamnikela labantu bakhe bonke lelizwe lakhe esandleni sakho; njalo uzakwenza kuye njengokwenza kwakho kuSihoni inkosi yamaAmori owayehlala eHeshiboni c. <b>3</b> Ngakho iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu yanikela esandleni sethu loOgi inkosi yeBashani labo bonke abantu bakhe; sasesimtshaya, kakwaze kwasala lansali kuye d. 
<b>4</b> Sasesithumba yonke imizi yakhe ngalesosikhathi; kwakungelamuzi esingawuthathanga kibo; imizi engamatshumi ayisithupha, isabelo sonke seArigobi, umbuso kaOgi eBashani e. <b>5</b> Yonke limizi yayivikelwe ngemithangala ephakemeyo, amasango lemigoqo, ngaphandle kwemizana eminengi kakhulu engelamithangala f. <b>6</b> Sasesiyitshabalalisa g, njengokwenza kwethu kuSihoni inkosi yeHeshiboni h, satshabalalisa yonke imizi, abesilisa labesifazana labantwanyana i. <b>7</b> Kodwa zonke izifuyo lempango yemizi, saziphangela. 
<b>8</b> Ngalesosikhathi sasesithatha esandleni samakhosi amabili amaAmori ilizwe, elalinganeno kweJordani, kusukela esifuleni iArinoni kuze kube sentabeni yeHermoni. <b>9</b> (AmaSidoni ayibiza iHermoni j ngokuthi yiSiriyoni k, lamaAmori ayibiza ngokuthi yiSeniri l.) <b>10</b> Yonke imizi yemagcekeni, leGileyadi yonke, leBashani yonke m, kuze kube seSaleka leEdreyi n, imizi yombuso kaOgi eBashani. 
<b>11</b> Ngoba nguOgi kuphela, inkosi yeBashani, owasalayo kunsali yeziqhwaga o; khangela, umbheda wakhe wawungumbheda wensimbi; kawukho yini eRaba yabantwana bakoAmoni p? Ubude bawo babuzingalo eziyisificamunwemunye, lobubanzi bawo babuzingalo ezine, ngokwengalo yomuntu q. <b>12</b> Lalelilizwe sadla ilifa lalo ngalesosikhathi; kusukela eAroweri, elisesifuleni seArinoni, lengxenye yentaba yeGileyadi r lemizi yayo ngayipha abakoRubeni labakoGadi s. 
<b>13</b> Lokuseleyo kweGileyadi leBashani yonke, umbuso kaOgi, ngakunika kungxenye yesizwe sakoManase t; isabelo sonke seArigobi, layo yonke iBashani u, eyayithiwa yilizwe leziqhwaga v. <b>14</b> UJayiri indodana kaManase wathatha isabelo sonke seArigobi w, kuze kube semngceleni wamaGeshuri lowamaMahakathi; wayibiza imizi ngebizo lakhe x wathi yiBashani-Havothi-Jayiri, kuze kube yilolusuku y. <b>15</b> Ngasengimnika uMakiri iGileyadi z. 
<b>16</b> LakwabakoRubeni lakwabakoGadi nganika kusukela eGileyadi ngitsho kuze kufike esifuleni seArinoni, phakathi laphakathi kwesifula, lomngcele, njalo kuze kube sesifuleni iJaboki aa, umngcele wabantwana bakoAmoni ab. <b>17</b> Lamagceke, leJordani, lomngcele, kusukela eKinerethi ac njalo kuze kufike elwandle lwemagcekeni ad, ulwandle lwetshwayi ae, ngaphansi kweAshidodi-Pisiga ngasempumalanga. 
<b>18</b> Ngasengililaya ngalesosikhathi ngisithi: INkosi uNkulunkulu wenu ilinikile lelilizwe libe yilifa lenu. Lizachapha lihlomile phambi kwabafowenu, abantwana bakoIsrayeli, wonke amadodana angamaqhawe af. <b>19</b> Kuphela omkenu labantwanyana benu lezifuyo zenu - ngiyazi ukuthi lilezifuyo ezinengi - kuzahlala emizini yenu engilinike yona ag, 
<b>20</b> iNkosi ize ibanike ukuphumula abafowenu njengani, baze bathi labo badle ilifa lelizwe iNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu ebaphe lona, ngaphetsheya kweJordani ah; beselibuyela, ngulowo lalowo elifeni lakhe, engilinike lona. <b>21</b> Njalo ngamlaya uJoshuwa ngalesosikhathi ngisithi: Amehlo akho abonile konke iNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu ekwenze kulawomakhosi amabili ai; izakwenza njalo iNkosi kuyo yonke imibuso ozakwedlulela kuyo. <b>22</b> Lingabesabi, ngoba iNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu, yona izalilwela aj. 
<b>23</b> Ngasengiyincenga iNkosi ngalesosikhathi ngisithi: <b>24</b> Nkosi Jehova, wena usuqalile ukutshengisa inceku yakho ubukhulu bakho lesandla sakho esilamandla ak; ngoba nguwuphi unkulunkulu emazulwini lemhlabeni ongenza njengemisebenzi yakho lanjengamandla akho al? <b>25</b> Ngicela ngichaphe, ngibone ilizwe elihle elingaphetsheya kweJordani, leyontaba enhle am, leLebhanoni an. <b>26</b> Kodwa iNkosi yangithukuthelela ngenxa yenu, kayingizwanga ao; iNkosi yasisithi kimi: Kwenele kuwe; ungabe usaphinda ukukhuluma kimi ngaloludaba ap. 
<b>27</b> Yenyukela engqongeni yePisiga aq, uphakamisele amehlo akho ngentshonalanga langenyakatho langeningizimu langempumalanga, ukhangele ngamehlo akho; ngoba kawuyikuchapha le iJordani. <b>28</b> Kodwa laya uJoshuwa ar, umkhuthaze as, umqinise at; ngoba yena uzachapha phambi kwalababantu, laye abenze badle ilifa lelizwe ozalibona au. <b>29</b> Sasesihlala esigodini maqondana leBeti-Peyori av. 
<b>Deuteronomy 4</b><br><b>1</b> Khathesi-ke, Israyeli, zwana izimiso lezahlulelo engilifundisa zona a, ukuzenza, ukuze liphile, lingene lidle ilifa lelizwe iNkosi uNkulunkulu waboyihlo elinika lona. <b>2</b> Kaliyikwengezelela elizwini engililaya lona, lingaphunguli lutho kulo, ukuze ligcine imilayo yeNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu engililaya yona b. <b>3</b> Amehlo enu abonile iNkosi eyakwenzayo ngenxa kaBhali-Peyori c; ngoba wonke umuntu owalandela uBhali-Peyori, iNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu yambhubhisa phakathi kwenu; 
<b>4</b> kodwa lina elanamathela eNkosini uNkulunkulu wenu liyaphila lonke lamuhla. <b>5</b> Bonani, ngilifundisile izimiso lezahlulelo, njengokungilaya kweNkosi uNkulunkulu wami, ukuthi lenze njalo phakathi kwelizwe eliya kulo ukudla ilifa lalo. <b>6</b> Ngakho zigcineni, lizenze; ngoba lokhu kuyinhlakanipho yenu lokuqedisisa kwenu emehlweni ezizwe d ezizakuzwa zonke lezizimiso zithi: Isibili lesisizwe esikhulu singabantu abahlakaniphileyo labaqedisisayo e.

<b>15</b> LeNkosi izasusa kuwe wonke umkhuhlane; kayiyikukwehlisela lasinye sezifo ezimbi zeGibhithe ozaziyo, kodwa izazehlisela phezu kwabo bonke abakuzondayo z. <b>16</b> Njalo uzabaqothula bonke abantu ezabanikela kuwe iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho aa; ilihlo lakho lingabazweli, ungakhonzi onkulunkulu babo ab, ngoba lokhu kuzakuba ngumjibila kuwe ac. <b>17</b> Nxa uzakuthi enhliziyweni yakho: Izizwe lezi zinengi kulami; ngingazixotsha njani elifeni lazo ad? 
<b>18</b> Ungazesabi ae, khumbula kuhle lokho iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho eyakwenza kuFaro lakuGibhithe lonke af, <b>19</b> izilingo ezinkulu ilihlo lakho elazibonayo, lezibonakaliso, lezimangaliso, lesandla esilamandla, lengalo eyeluliweyo, iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho eyakukhupha ngakho. INkosi uNkulunkulu wakho izakwenza njalo kuzo zonke izizwe ozesabayo ag. <b>20</b> Futhi-ke iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho izathumela olonyovu phakathi kwazo, zize zibhujiswe eziseleyo lezicatshele ubuso bakho ah.

<b>24</b> Izanikela ezandleni zakho amakhosi azo am, njalo uzacitsha ibizo lawo lisuke ngaphansi kwamazulu an; kakulamuntu ozakuma phambi kwakho uze uzibhubhise ao. <b>25</b> Izithombe ezibaziweyo zabonkulunkulu bazo lizazitshisa ngomlilo ap. Ungafisi isiliva legolide elikuzo, ungazithatheli lona aq, hlezi ubanjwe emjibileni ngalo ar, ngoba liyisinengiso eNkosini uNkulunkulu wakho as. 
<b>26</b> Njalo ungasingenisi isinengiso endlini yakho, hlezi ube yinto eqalekisiweyo njengaso; usenyanye lokusenyanya, unengwe lokunengwa yiso, ngoba siyinto eqalekisiweyo at. 
<b>Deuteronomy 8</b><br><b>1</b> Yonke imithetho engikulaya yona lamuhla lizananzelela ukuyenza, ukuze liphile, lande, lingene lidle ilifa lelizwe, iNkosi eyalifungela oyihlo a. <b>2</b> Njalo uzakhumbula indlela yonke iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ekukhokhele ngayo liminyaka engamatshumi amane enkangala, ukuze ikuthobise b, ikuhlole c, ukwazi okusenhliziyweni yakho, uba uzagcina imithetho yayo, loba hatshi d. 
<b>3</b> Yasikuthobisa, yakulambisa e, yakudlisa imana owawungayazi, laboyihlo babengayazi f, ukuze ikwazise ukuthi umuntu kaphili ngesinkwa sodwa, kodwa ngakho konke okuphuma emlonyeni weNkosi umuntu uyaphila g. <b>4</b> Izembatho zakho kaziguganga phezu kwakho, lonyawo lwakho kaluvuvukanga, le iminyaka engamatshumi amane h. <b>5</b> Ngakho yazi enhliziyweni yakho ukuthi njengendoda ilaya indodana yayo, iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho iyakulaya i. <b>6</b> Ngakho uzagcina imithetho yeNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho, ukuhamba endleleni zayo, lokuyesaba j. 
<b>7</b> Ngoba iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ikungenisa elizweni elihle, ilizwe lezifula zamanzi, lemithombo lokujula okugobhoza ezihotsheni lezintabeni k; <b>8</b> ilizwe lengqoloyi, lebhali*, lezivini, lemikhiwa, lezithelo zepomegranati*, ilizwe lezihlahla zemihlwathi zamafutha, loluju l, <b>9</b> ilizwe ozakudla kulo isinkwa kungekho ukusilela, ongayikuswela lutho kulo; ilizwe elilamatshe ayinsimbi, lelilezintaba elingagebha kuzo ithusi m. <b>10</b> Usudlile wasutha, uzabusisa iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ngelizwe elihle ekunike lona. 
<b>11</b> Ziqaphele hlezi ukhohlwe iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ngokungagcini imilayo yayo lezahlulelo zayo lezimiso zayo, engikulaya zona lamuhla. <b>12</b> Hlezi lapho usudlile wasutha, usuwakhe izindlu ezinhle wahlala kuzo n, <b>13</b> sekwandile inkomo zakho lezimvu zakho, lesiliva legolide kwakho sekwandile, lakho konke olakho sekwandile, <b>14</b> inhliziyo yakho ibisiziphakamisa o, ubusukhohlwa iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho eyakukhupha elizweni leGibhithe, endlini yobugqili p, 
<b>15</b> eyakukhokhela enkangala enkulu leyesabekayo q, okwakulenyoka ezilokutshisa r lezinkume s lomhlabathi owomileyo, lapho okwakungelamanzi khona t, eyakuvezela amanzi edwaleni lelitshe elilukhuni u, <b>16</b> eyakudlisa imana enkangala, oyihlo ababengayazi, ukuze ikuthobise, lokuze ikuhlole v, ikwenzele okuhle ekucineni kwakho w, <b>17</b> ubususithi enhliziyweni yakho: Amandla ami lokuqina kwesandla sami kungizuzele linotho. 
<b>18</b> Kodwa uzakhumbula iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho, ngoba yiyo ekunika amandla okuzuza inotho x, ukuze iqinise isivumelwano sayo eyasifungela oyihlo njengalamuhla y. <b>19</b> Kuzakuthi-ke, nxa ukhohlwa lokukhohlwa iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho, ulandele abanye onkulunkulu, ubakhonze, ubakhothamele z, ngiyalifakazela lamuhla ukuthi lizabhubha lokubhubha aa. <b>20</b> Njengezizwe iNkosi ezichithileyo phambi kwenu, ngokunjalo lizabhubha, ngoba lingalilalelanga ilizwi leNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu ab. 
<b>Deuteronomy 9</b><br><b>1</b> Zwana, Israyeli; uzachapha iJordani lamuhla, ukungenela ukudla ilifa lezizwe a ezinkulu lezilamandla kulawe, imizi emikhulu b ebiyelwe ngemithangala efika emazulwini c. <b>2</b> Abantu abakhulu labade, abantwana bamaAnaki wena obaziyo, wena osuzwile ngabo kuthiwa: Ngubani ongema phambi kwabantwana bakaAnaki d? 
<b>3</b> Ngakho yazi lamuhla ukuthi yiNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho echapha phambi kwakho e, engumlilo oqothulayo f; yona izabachitha, njalo yona izabehlisela phansi phambi kwakho g; njalo lizabaxotsha basuke elifeni labo, libabhubhise masinyane, njengokutsho kweNkosi kuwe h. <b>4</b> Ungakhulumi enhliziyweni yakho, lapho iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho isibaxotshile phambi kwakho, usithi: Ngenxa yokulunga kwami iNkosi ingingenise ukudla ilifa lelizwe leli i. Kodwa ngenxa yobubi balezizizwe iNkosi j izixotsha zisuke elifeni lazo phambi kwakho k. 
<b>5</b> Kakungenxa yokulunga kwakho kumbe ngenxa yobuqotho benhliziyo yakho ukuthi ungene ukudla ilifa lelizwe lazo l; kodwa ngenxa yobubi balezizizwe iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho izixotsha zisuke elifeni lazo phambi kwakho m, lokuthi iqinise ilizwi iNkosi eyalifungela oyihlo, uAbrahama, uIsaka loJakobe n. <b>6</b> Yazi-ke ukuthi kakungenxa yokulunga kwakho ukuthi iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho ikunika lelilizwe elihle ukuthi udle ilifa lalo; ngoba uyisizwe esilentamo elukhuni o. Abantwana bakoIsrayeli bavukela iNkosi enkangala 
<b>7</b> Khumbula, ungakhohlwa, ukuthi wayithukuthelisa iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho enkangala; kusukela osukwini owaphuma ngalo elizweni leGibhithe laze lafika kulindawo, beliyivukela iNkosi p. <b>8</b> LeHorebe layithukuthelisa iNkosi, yaze yalithukuthelela iNkosi ukulichitha q. <b>9</b> Sengenyukele entabeni ukwemukela izibhebhe zamatshe, izibhebhe zesivumelwano iNkosi eyasenza lani r, ngasengihlala entabeni insuku ezingamatshumi amane lobusuku obungamatshumi amane, angidlanga sinkwa, anginathanga manzi s. 
<b>10</b> INkosi yasinginika lezozibhebhe ezimbili zamatshe zilotshwe ngomunwe kaNkulunkulu, laphezu kwazo kulotshiwe ngokwamazwi wonke iNkosi eyayiwakhulume lani entabeni, iphakathi komlilo t ngosuku lokuhlangana u. <b>11</b> Kwasekusithi ekupheleni kwensuku ezingamatshumi amane lobusuku obungamatshumi amane, iNkosi yangipha izibhebhe ezimbili zamatshe, izibhebhe zesivumelwano. 
<b>12</b> INkosi yasisithi kimi: Sukuma wehle ngokuphangisa usuke lapha, ngoba abantu bakho owabakhupha eGibhithe bonakalisile v, baphambukile ngokuphangisa endleleni engabalaya yona, bazenzela isithombe esibunjwe ngokuncibilikisa w. <b>13</b> INkosi yasikhuluma kimi isithi: Ngibabonile lababantu x, khangela-ke, bangabantu abantamo zilukhuni y. <b>14</b> Ngiyekela ukuze ngibabhubhise z, ngesule ibizo labo lisuke ngaphansi kwamazulu aa; njalo ngizakwenza ube yisizwe esilamandla lesikhulu kulabo ab. 
<b>15</b> Ngasengiphenduka ngisehla entabeni ac, lentaba yayivutha umlilo; lezibhebhe zombili zesivumelwano zazisezandleni zami zombili ad. <b>16</b> Ngasengibona, khangelani-ke, lalonile eNkosini uNkulunkulu wenu, lalizenzele ithole elibunjwe ngokuncibilikisa, laliphambukile ngokuphangisa endleleni iNkosi eyayililayile ae. <b>17</b> Ngasengibamba izibhebhe zombili, ngazilahla ezandleni zami zombili, ngazibulala phambi kwamehlo enu. 
<b>18</b> Ngasengiziwisela phansi phambi kweNkosi af njengakuqala, insuku ezingamatshumi amane lobusuku obungamatshumi amane ag, ngingadli sinkwa, nginganathi manzi, ngenxa yaso sonke isono senu elisonileyo, lisenza okubi emehlweni eNkosi, liyithukuthelisa ah. <b>19</b> Ngoba ngangisesaba ukuthukuthela lolaka iNkosi eyayilithukuthelela ngakho ukulibhubhisa; kodwa iNkosi yangizwa langalesosikhathi ai. <b>20</b> INkosi yasimthukuthelela kakhulu uAroni ukuze imbhubhise; kodwa ngamkhulekela loAroni ngalesosikhathi aj. 
<b>21</b> Ngasengithatha isono senu, ithole elalilenzile, ngalitshisa ngomlilo, ngalichoboza, ngalicholisisa laze laba lincinyane njengothuli; ngaphosa uthuli lwalo esifuleni esasisehla entabeni. <b>22</b> LeTabera ak, leMasa al, leKiribothi-Hathava am laliyithukuthelisa iNkosi. <b>23</b> Lalapho iNkosi yalithuma lisuka eKadeshi-Bhaneya an isithi: Yenyukani, lidle ilifa lelizwe engilinike lona, laselihlamukela umlayo weNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu, kaliyikholwanga, kalilalelanga ilizwi layo ao. 
<b>24</b> Lalivele liyivukele iNkosi kusukela osukwini engalazi ngalo ap. <b>25</b> Ngasengiziwisela phansi phambi kweNkosi insuku ezingamatshumi amane lobusuku obungamatshumi amane, engaziwisela phansi ngazo aq, ngoba iNkosi yayithe izalichitha. <b>26</b> Ngasengikhuleka eNkosini ngathi: Nkosi Jehova, ungabachithi abantu bakho lelifa lakho, obahlenge ngobukhulu bakho, owabakhupha eGibhithe ngesandla esilamandla ar. <b>27</b> Khumbula inceku zakho, uAbrahama, uIsaka, loJakobe; ungakhangeli inkani yalababantu lobubi babo lesono sabo, 
<b>28</b> hlezi ilizwe owasikhupha kilo lithi: Ngenxa yokuthi iNkosi yayingelamandla okubangenisa elizweni eyabathembisa lona, langenxa yokuthi ibazonda, yabakhupha ukuze ibabulalele enkangala as. <b>29</b> Kube kanti bangabantu bakho lelifa lakho, owabakhupha ngamandla akho amakhulu langengalo yakho eyeluliweyo at. 
<b>Deuteronomy 10</b><br><b>1</b> Ngalesosikhathi iNkosi yathi kimi: Zibazele izibhebhe ezimbili zamatshe njengezakuqala, wenyukele kimi entabeni a, uzenzele umtshokotsho wezigodo b. <b>2</b> Ngizabhala-ke phezu kwezibhebhe amazwi ayesezibhebheni zakuqala owazibulalayo, uzibeke emtshokotshweni c. <b>3</b> Ngasengisenza umtshokotsho ngesihlahla sesinga d, ngabaza izibhebhe ezimbili zamatshe njengezakuqala, ngenyukela entabeni, lezibhebhe ezimbili zisesandleni sami e. 
<b>4</b> Yasibhala ezibhebheni njengokubhala kwakuqala, imilayo elitshumi f iNkosi eyayikhuluma kini entabeni iphakathi komlilo g ngosuku lokuhlangana h; njalo iNkosi yanginika zona. <b>5</b> Ngasengiphenduka ngisehla entabeni i, ngazifaka izibhebhe emtshokotshweni engangiwenzile j. Njalo zikhona lapho, njengokungilaya kweNkosi k. <b>6</b> Abantwana bakoIsrayeli basebesuka eBeyerothi-Bene-Jakani l, baya eMosera m; uAroni wafela lapho, wangcwatshwa khona n; indodana yakhe uEleyazare wasebenza njengompristi esikhundleni sakhe.

<b>Deuteronomy 11</b><br><b>1</b> Ngakho wothanda iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho a, ugcine umlayo wayo lezimiso zayo lezahlulelo zayo lemithetho yayo insuku zonke b. <b>2</b> Yazini lamuhla, ngoba kangikhulumi ebantwaneni benu abangakwaziyo labangakubonanga ukulaya kweNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu c, ubukhulu bayo d, isandla sayo esilamandla, lengalo yayo eyeluliweyo e, <b>3</b> lezibonakaliso zayo lezenzo zayo eyazenza phakathi kweGibhithe kuFaro inkosi yeGibhithe, lelizweni lakhe lonke f, 
<b>4</b> lalokho eyakwenza ebuthweni leGibhithe, emabhizeni ayo lenqoleni zayo, lokuthi yenza amanzi oLwandle oluBomvu abakhukhule lapho bexotshana lani, lokuthi iNkosi yababhubhisa, kuze kube lamuhla g, <b>5</b> lalokho eyakwenza kini enkangala laze lafika kulindawo, <b>6</b> lalokho eyakwenza kuDathani lakuAbiramu amadodana kaEliyabi indodana kaRubeni, ukuthi umhlaba wakhamisa umlomo wawo wabaginya bona labezindlu zabo, lamathente abo h, lakho konke okwakungokwabo, phakathi kukaIsrayeli wonke i.

Izibusiso zelizwe elithenjisiweyo 
<b>10</b> Ngoba ilizwe ongena kilo ukudla ilifa lalo kalinjengelizwe leGibhithe eliphuma kilo, lapho owahlanyela khona inhlanyelo yakho, wayithelela ngonyawo lwakho, njengesivande semibhida n; <b>11</b> kodwa ilizwe elichaphela kilo ukudla ilifa lalo liyilizwe lamaqaqa lezigodi, linatha amanzi ngezulu lamazulu o; <b>12</b> ilizwe iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho elikhathalelayo; amehlo eNkosi uNkulunkulu wakho aphezu kwalo njalo, kusukela ekuqaleni komnyaka kuze kube sekupheleni komnyaka p. 
<b>13</b> Kuzakuthi-ke, uba lilalela lokulalela imithetho yami engililaya yona lamuhla, ukuthanda iNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu, lokuyikhonza ngenhliziyo yenu yonke langomphefumulo wenu wonke q, <b>14</b> ngizanika izulu lelizwe lenu ngesikhathi salo r, izulu elokuqala lezulu lokucina, ukuze ubuthe amabele akho lewayini lakho elitsha lamafutha akho s. <b>15</b> Ngizanika izifuyo zakho utshani edlelweni lakho t, njalo uzakudla, usuthe u. <b>16</b> Ziqapheleni, hlezi inhliziyo yenu ikhohliseke, liphambuke v, likhonze abanye onkulunkulu, libakhothamele w, 
<b>17</b> ukuze ulaka lweNkosi lulivuthele x, njalo ivale amazulu, ukuze kungabi khona izulu y, lelizwe lingatheli isivuno salo z, libhubhe masinyane lisuke elizweni elihle iNkosi elinika lona aa. <b>18</b> Ngakho lizabeka lamazwi ami enhliziyweni yenu lemphefumulweni wenu ab, liwabophele esandleni senu abe luphawu, ukuze abe ngamafilakteriyu* phakathi laphakathi kwamehlo enu ac. <b>19</b> Lizawafundisa abantwana benu, ukhulume ngawo lapho uhlezi endlini yakho, lalapho uhamba endleleni, lalapho ulala, lalapho uvuka ad.
