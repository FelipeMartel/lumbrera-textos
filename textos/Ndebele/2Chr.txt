<b>2 Crónicas 1</b><br><b>1</b> USolomoni indodana kaDavida waseqina embusweni wakhe a, leNkosi uNkulunkulu wakhe yayilaye b, yamenza waba mkhulu kakhulu c. <b>2</b> USolomoni wasekhuluma kuIsrayeli wonke, kubaphathi bezinkulungwane labamakhulu, lakubahluleli, lakuzo zonke induna kuIsrayeli wonke, inhloko zaboyise d. <b>3</b> USolomoni lebandla lonke laye basebesiya endaweni ephakemeyo eyayiseGibeyoni e; ngoba kwakukhona lapho ithente lenhlangano kaNkulunkulu uMozisi inceku yeNkosi alenzayo enkangala f. 
<b>4</b> Kodwa uDavida wayewenyusile umtshokotsho kaNkulunkulu g uvela eKiriyathi-Jeyarimi h, wawusa lapho uDavida ayewulungisele khona, ngoba wayewumisele ithente eJerusalema. <b>5</b> Lelathi lethusi i, uBhezaleli indodana kaUri indodana kaHuri ayelenzile, walibeka phambi kwethabhanekele leNkosi j; uSolomoni lebandla badinga-ke kulo k. <b>6</b> USolomoni wasesenyukela lapho elathini lethusi phambi kweNkosi elalisethenteni lenhlangano, wanikela phezu kwalo iminikelo yokutshiswa eyinkulungwane l. 
<b>7</b> Ngalobobusuku uNkulunkulu wabonakala kuSolomoni, wathi kuye: Cela engingakupha khona m. <b>8</b> USolomoni wasesithi kuNkulunkulu: Wena wenzele uDavida ubaba umusa omkhulu, wangenza ngaba yinkosi esikhundleni sakhe. <b>9</b> Khathesi, Nkosi Nkulunkulu, kaliqiniswe ilizwi lakho kuDavida ubaba n, ngoba wena ungenze ngaba yinkosi phezu kwabantu abanengi ngangothuli lomhlabathi. <b>10</b> Khathesi ngipha inhlakanipho lolwazi ukuze ngiphume ngingene phambi kwalababantu; ngoba ngubani ongehlulela lababantu bakho abakhulu kangaka? 
<b>11</b> UNkulunkulu wasesithi kuSolomoni: Ngenxa yokuthi lokhu bekusenhliziyweni yakho, njalo ungacelanga inotho, ingcebo, lodumo, lempilo yezitha zakho, futhi ungacelanga insuku ezinengi, kodwa uzicelele inhlakanipho lolwazi ukuze wahlulele abantu bami engikwenze waba yinkosi phezu kwabo o: <b>12</b> Inhlakanipho lolwazi ukuphiwe; ngizakunika futhi inotho lengcebo lodumo p, angazange abe lakho okunjalo amakhosi ayengaphambi kwakho, langemva kwakho kakuyikuba khona okunjalo q. 
<b>13</b> Wasebuya uSolomoni evela endaweni ephakemeyo eseGibeyoni waya eJerusalema, evela phambi kwethente lenhlangano; wasebusa phezu kukaIsrayeli r. <b>14</b> USolomoni wasebuthanisa izinqola labagadi bamabhiza s; wayelezinqola ezingamakhulu alitshumi lane, labagadi bamabhiza abazinkulungwane ezilitshumi lambili t; wasekubeka emizini yezinqola, njalo kanye lenkosi eJerusalema u. <b>15</b> Inkosi yasisenza isiliva legolide eJerusalema kwaba njengamatshe, njalo wenza imisedari yaba njengemikhiwa yesikhamore esesihotsheni, ngobunengi. 
<b>16</b> Wasengenisa ngokuthenga amabhiza avela eGibhithe, ayengekaSolomoni, lelembu elicolekileyo; labathengi benkosi babelithatha ilembu elicolekileyo ngentengo. <b>17</b> Basebesenyusa bekhupha inqola eGibhithe ngamashekeli* esiliva angamakhulu ayisithupha, lebhiza ngamashekeli alikhulu lamatshumi amahlanu. Ngokunjalo bakukhuphela wonke amakhosi amaHethi lamakhosi eSiriya ngesandla sabo. 
<b>2 Crónicas 2</b><br><b>1</b> USolomoni wasezimisela ukwakhela ibizo leNkosi indlu, lendlu yombuso wakhe a. <b>2</b> USolomoni wasebala amadoda azinkulungwane ezingamatshumi ayisikhombisa b ukuthwala imithwalo, lamadoda ayizinkulungwane ezingamatshumi ayisificaminwembili ukubaza entabeni c, lababonisi abayizinkulungwane ezintathu lamakhulu ayisithupha phezu kwawo d. <b>3</b> USolomoni wasethumela kuHuramu inkosi yeTire esithi: Njengokwenza kwakho kuDavida ubaba umthumela imisedari ukumakhela indlu yokuhlala kuyo, yenza njalo lakimi. 
<b>4</b> Khangela, mina ngakhela ibizo leNkosi uNkulunkulu wami indlu, ukuyehlukanisela yona, ukutshisa phambi kwayo impepha elephunga elimnandi, lezinkwa zokubukiswa njalonjalo, leminikelo yokutshiswa ekuseni lantambama, ngamasabatha, lekuthwaseni kwezinyanga, langemikhosi emisiweyo yeNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu. Lesi yisimiso saphakade kuIsrayeli. <b>5</b> Lendlu engiyakhayo inkulu, ngoba uNkulunkulu wethu mkhulu kulabonkulunkulu bonke. 
<b>6</b> Kodwa ngubani ongaba lamandla okumakhela indlu, ngoba amazulu, yebo, amazulu amazulu, angemanele? Mina-ke ngingubani ukuthi ngimakhele indlu, ngaphandle kokutshisa impepha phambi kwakhe? <b>7</b> Ngakho-ke thuma kimi umuntu oyingcitshi ekusebenzeni ngegolide langesiliva langethusi langensimbi langokuyibubende langokubomvu langokuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, lokwaziyo ukubaza okubazwe ngokugujwa, labazingcitshi abalami koJuda leJerusalema, uDavida ubaba abamisayo. 
<b>8</b> Ngithumela njalo izigodo zemisedari, amafiri, lama-aligumi* ezivela eLebhanoni; ngoba mina ngiyazi ukuthi inceku zakho ziyakwazi ukugamula izihlahla zeLebhanoni; khangela-ke, inceku zami zizakuba lenceku zakho; <b>9</b> ngitsho ukungilungisela izigodo ezinengi, ngoba indlu engiyakhayo izakuba nkulu imangalise. 
<b>10</b> Khangela-ke, inceku zakho, abagamuli abagamula izihlahla, ngizazinika amakhori* engqoloyi echoliweyo azinkulungwane ezingamatshumi amabili, lamakhori ebhali* azinkulungwane ezingamatshumi amabili, lamabhathi* ewayini azinkulungwane ezingamatshumi amabili, lamabhathi amafutha azinkulungwane ezingamatshumi amabili. <b>11</b> UHuramu inkosi yeTire wasephendula ngokubhala akuthumela kuSolomoni: Ngoba uJehova uthanda abantu bakhe, ukubeke waba yinkosi phezu kwabo. 
<b>12</b> UHuramu wabuya wathi: Kayibusiswe iNkosi uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli eyenza amazulu lomhlaba, enike uDavida inkosi indodana ehlakaniphileyo, eyazi inhlakanipho lokuqedisisa, ezayakhela iNkosi indlu, lendlu yombuso wayo. <b>13</b> Khathesi sengithuma indoda eyingcitshi eyazi ukuqedisisa, uHuramu Abhi, 
<b>14</b> indodana yowesifazana emadodakazini akoDani, oyise wayeyindoda yeTire; uyakwazi ukusebenza ngegolide, langesiliva, ngethusi, ngensimbi, ngamatshe, langezigodo, ngokuyibubende, ngokuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka, langamalembu acolekileyo kakhulu, langokubomvu, lokubaza lakuphi okubaziweyo ngokugubha, lokuqamba lakuphi okuqanjiweyo angakunikwa, kanye lezingcitshi zakho lezingcitshi zenkosi yami uDavida uyihlo. <b>15</b> Ngakho-ke ingqoloyi, lebhali*, amafutha, lewayini, inkosi yami ekhulume ngakho, kayikuthumele ezincekwini zayo. 
<b>16</b> Thina-ke sizagamula izihlahla eLebhanoni, njengokuswela kwakho konke, sizilethe kuwe ngokundendayo phezu kolwandle eJopha ▼▼ iJafo: eyiJopha., njalo wena uzazenyusela eJerusalema. <b>17</b> USolomoni wasebala wonke amadoda ezizweni ayeselizweni lakoIsrayeli f emva kokubalwa uDavida uyise ayewabale ngakho g; asetholakala ezinkulungwane ezilikhulu lamatshumi amahlanu lantathu lamakhulu ayisithupha. 
<b>18</b> Wasesenza kuwo abathwali bemithwalo abayizinkulungwane ezingamatshumi ayisikhombisa, lababazi entabeni abayizinkulungwane ezingamatshumi ayisificaminwembili, lababonisi abayizinkulungwane ezintathu lamakhulu ayisithupha bokwenza abantu basebenze h. 
<b>2 Crónicas 3</b><br><b>1</b> USolomoni waseqala ukwakha indlu yeNkosi eJerusalema a entabeni yeMoriya b lapho eyabonakala khona kuDavida uyise, endaweni uDavida ayilungisayo ebaleni lokubhulela likaOrinani umJebusi c. <b>2</b> Waseqalisa ukwakha ngenyanga yesibili ngolwesibili ngomnyaka wesine wokubusa kwakhe. <b>3</b> Lalezi yizisekelo zikaSolomoni zokwakha indlu kaNkulunkulu. Ubude ngezingalo ngesilinganiso esidala babuzingalo ezingamatshumi ayisithupha, lobubanzi buzingalo ezingamatshumi amabili. 
<b>4</b> Lekhulusi elaliphambili, ubude njengobubanzi bendlu babuzingalo ezingamatshumi amabili, lokuphakama kwakuzingalo ezilikhulu lamatshumi amabili. Wayihuqa ngaphakathi ngegolide elicwengekileyo. <b>5</b> Lendlu enkulu wayembesa ngezigodo zefiri*, wayihuqa ngegolide elihle, wamisa phezu kwayo amalala lamaketane. <b>6</b> Wayembesela ubuhle indlu ngamatshe aligugu; njalo igolide laliligolide leParivayimi. <b>7</b> Waseyihuqa indlu, imijabo, imigubazi, lemiduli yayo, leminyango yayo ngegolide, wabaza ngokugubha amakherubhi* emidulwini. 
<b>8</b> Wasesenza indlu yengcwele yezingcwele; ubude bayo njengobubanzi bendlu babuzingalo ezingamatshumi amabili, lobubanzi bayo buzingalo ezingamatshumi amabili; wayihuqa ngegolide elihle kwaze kwaba ngamathalenta* angamakhulu ayisithupha. <b>9</b> Lesisindo sezipikili saze saba ngamashekeli* egolide angamatshumi amahlanu. Wawahuqa amakamelo aphezulu ngegolide. <b>10</b> Wasesenza endlini yengcwele yezingcwele d amakherubhi* amabili ngomsebenzi obaziweyo e, wawahuqa ngegolide. 
<b>11</b> Lempiko zamakherubhi, ubude bazo babuzingalo ezingamatshumi amabili. Uphiko lwelilodwa lwaluzingalo ezinhlanu lufinyelela emdulini wendlu, lolunye uphiko lwaluzingalo ezinhlanu lufinyelela ophikweni lwelinye ikherubhi. <b>12</b> Ngokunjalo uphiko lwelinye ikherubhi lwaluzingalo ezinhlanu lufinyelela emdulini wendlu; lolunye uphiko lwaluzingalo ezinhlanu lunamathele ephikweni lwelinye ikherubhi. <b>13</b> Impiko zalamakherubhi* zeluleka ingalo ezingamatshumi amabili; njalo ema ngenyawo zawo, lobuso bawo bukhangele ngasendlini. 
<b>14</b> Wasesenza iveyili ngokuluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka lokuyibubende lokubomvu f langelembu elicolekileyo kakhulu g, wabeka kulo amakherubhi*. <b>15</b> Wasesenza phambi kwendlu insika ezimbili h, ubude babuzingalo ezingamatshumi amathathu lanhlanu, lesiduku phezu kwengqonga yayo buzingalo ezinhlanu i. <b>16</b> Wasesenza amaketane njengendaweni yelizwi, wawafaka esihlokweni sezinsika j; wenza lamapomegranati* alikhulu k, wawafaka emaketaneni. 
<b>17</b> Wasemisa izinsika ngaphambi kwethempeli, enye ngakwesokunene, lenye ngakwesokhohlo; wasebiza ibizo lengakwesokunene uJakini, lebizo lengakwesokhohlo uBhowazi. 
<b>2 Crónicas 4</b><br><b>1</b> Wasesenza ilathi lethusi, ubude balo buzingalo ezingamatshumi amabili, lobubanzi balo buzingalo ezingamatshumi amabili, lokuphakama kwalo kuzingalo ezilitshumi a. <b>2</b> Wasesenza ulwandle olubunjwe ngokuncibilikisa, luzingalo ezilitshumi kusukela okhunjini lwalo kusiya okhunjini lwalo, lwalugombolozele inhlangothi zonke, lokuphakama kwalo kuzingalo ezinhlanu, lomzila wezingalo ezingamatshumi amathathu ulugombolozele inhlangothi zonke b. 
<b>3</b> Lokufanana kwenkabi kwakungaphansi kwalo, kubhodabhoda inhlangothi zonke zalo, ezilitshumi engalweni, lugombolozela ulwandle inhlangothi zonke; imizila emibili yenkabi yabunjwa ngokuncibilikisa ekubunjweni kwalo c. <b>4</b> Lwalumi phezu kwenkabi ezilitshumi lambili; ezintathu zikhangele enyakatho, lezintathu zikhangele entshonalanga, lezintathu zikhangele eningizimu, lezintathu zikhangele empumalanga; lolwandle lwaluphezu kwazo phezulu, lazo zonke ingemuva zazo zazingaphakathi. 
<b>5</b> Uhlonzi lwalo lwaluyibubanzi besandla, lomphetho walo wawunjengomsebenzi womphetho wenkezo, njengeluba lomduze, lwalumumethe amabhathi*, lumumethe azinkulungwane ezintathu d. <b>6</b> Wasesenza inditshi zokugezela ezilitshumi, wabeka ezinhlanu ngakwesokunene, lezinhlanu ngakwesokhohlo, ukugezisela kizo; babegezisela kizo okomnikelo wokutshiswa; kodwa ulwandle lwalungolwabapristi ukugezela kulo. <b>7</b> Wasesenza izinti zezibane zegolide ezilitshumi njengokumiswa kwazo, wazibeka ethempelini, ezinhlanu ngakwesokunene, lezinhlanu ngakwesokhohlo. 
<b>8</b> Wenza lamatafula alitshumi, wawabeka ethempelini, amahlanu ngakwesokunene, lamahlanu ngakwesokhohlo. Wenza lemiganu elikhulu yegolide. <b>9</b> Wenza leguma labapristi leguma elikhulu, lezivalo zeguma, wazihuqa izivalo zalo ngethusi. <b>10</b> Wasebeka ulwandle ehlangothini lokunene ngasempumalanga maqondana leningizimu. <b>11</b> UHuramu wasesenza izimbiza lamafotsholo lemiganu yokufafaza e. UHuramu waqeda ukwenza umsebenzi ayewenzela inkosi uSolomoni endlini kaNkulunkulu. 
<b>12</b> Insika ezimbili, lemiganu, leziduku phezu kwengqonga yezinsika ezimbili, lamambule amabili okwembesa imiganu emibili yeziduku ezaziphezu kwengqonga yezinsika f, <b>13</b> lamapomegranati* angamakhulu amane emambuleni womabili, imizila emibili yamapomegranati kulelo lalelombule g, okwembesa imiganu emibili yeziduku ezaziphezu ▼▼ phezu: Heb phambi. kwengqonga yezinsika i. <b>14</b> Wenza lezisekelo, wenza lezinditshi zokugezela phezu kwezisekelo j; <b>15</b> ulwandle olulodwa, lezinkabi ezilitshumi lambili ngaphansi kwalo. 
<b>16</b> Lezimbiza, lamafotsholo, lamafologwe k, lazo zonke izitsha zakho uHuramu Abhi wazenzela inkosi uSolomoni esenzela indlu yeNkosi ngethusi elikhazimulayo l. <b>17</b> Inkosi yazibumbela ngokuncibilikisa emagcekeni eJordani emhlabathini olibumba phakathi kweSukothi leZeredatha m. <b>18</b> USolomoni wasezenza zonke lezizitsha ngobunengi obukhulu, ngoba isisindo sethusi sasingehlolwe n. <b>19</b> USolomoni wazenza-ke zonke izitsha ezazingezendlu kaNkulunkulu, lelathi legolide, lamatafula okwakukhona kuwo izinkwa zokubukiswa o, 
<b>20</b> lezinti zezibane lezibane zazo ngegolide elicwengekileyo p, ukuthi zibhebhe ngokwesimiso q phambi kwendawo yelizwi r; <b>21</b> lamaluba lezibane lezindlawu, ngegolide, kwakuligolide elipheleleyo; <b>22</b> lezindlawu, lemiganu yokufafaza, lezinkezo, lezitsha zokuthwala umlilo, ngegolide elicwengekileyo s; njalo iminyango yendlu, izivalo zayo ezingaphakathi, ezengcwele yezingcwele, lezivalo zendlu yethempeli zazingezegolide t. 
<b>2 Crónicas 5</b><br><b>1</b> Usuphelile umsebenzi wonke uSolomoni ayewenzela indlu yeNkosi, wangenisa izinto ezingcwelisiweyo zikaDavida uyise, lesiliva legolide lazo zonke izitsha wazibeka esiphaleni senotho sendlu kaNkulunkulu. Ukungeniswa komtshokotsho wesivumelwano ethempelini <b>2</b> Ngalesosikhathi uSolomoni wabuthanisa eJerusalema abadala bakoIsrayeli, lazo zonke inhloko zezizwe, izinduna zaboyise babantwana bakoIsrayeli, ukuthi benyuse umtshokotsho wesivumelwano seNkosi usuke emzini kaDavida oyiZiyoni. 
<b>3</b> Wonke amadoda akoIsrayeli asebuthana enkosini emkhosini owenyanga yesikhombisa a. <b>4</b> Labo bonke abadala bakoIsrayeli beza, amaLevi asewuthwala umtshokotsho b, <b>5</b> awuletha umtshokotsho lethente lenhlangano lazo zonke izitsha ezingcwele ezazisethenteni; abapristi lamaLevi basebekwenyusa c. <b>6</b> Inkosi uSolomoni lenhlangano yonke yakoIsrayeli eyayibuthene phambi komtshokotsho basebenikela imihlatshelo yezimvu lezinkabi ezingelakubhalwa lezingelakubalwa ngobunengi. 
<b>7</b> Abapristi basebewungenisa umtshokotsho wesivumelwano seNkosi endaweni yawo, endaweni yelizwi yendlu, kungcwele yezingcwele, kwaze kwaba ngaphansi kwempiko zamakherubhi*. <b>8</b> Ngoba amakherubhi* elulela impiko zawo phezu kwendawo yomtshokotsho; amakherubhi awembesa umtshokotsho lemijabo yawo ngaphezulu. <b>9</b> Basebeselula imijabo, izihloko zemijabo zaze zabonakala emtshokotshweni phambi kwendawo yelizwi d, kodwa zingabonakali ngaphandle; ukhona lapho-ke kuze kube lamuhla. 
<b>10</b> Kwakungelalutho emtshokotshweni, ngaphandle kwezibhebhe ezimbili uMozisi azifaka khona eHorebe lapho iNkosi eyenza khona isivumelwano labantwana bakoIsrayeli ekuphumeni kwabo eGibhithe. <b>11</b> Kwasekusithi abapristi bephuma endaweni engcwele, ngoba bonke abapristi abatholakalayo babezihlambulule bengagcinanga izigaba, 
<b>12</b> lamaLevi angabahlabeleli babo bonke - akoAsafi, akoHemani, akoJeduthuni, lakumadodana abo lakubafowabo - begqoke amalembu acolekileyo amhlophe, belezinsimbi ezincencethayo lezigubhu zezintambo lamachacho, bema ngempumalanga kwelathi; njalo kanye labo kulabapristi abalikhulu lamatshumi amabili bekhalisa izimpondo. 
<b>13</b> Kwasekusithi bekhalisa izimpondo behlabelela kanyekanye ukuzwakalisa umsindo owodwa, ukudumisa lokubonga iNkosi, lalapho bephakamisa ilizwi ngezimpondo langezinsimbi ezincencethayo lezinto zokuhlabelela, lalapho bedumisa iNkosi ukuthi ilungile lokuthi umusa wayo umi kuze kube phakade, indlu yagcwaliswa liyezi, indlu yeNkosi, <b>14</b> abapristi kabaze baba lakho ukuma ukukhonza ngenxa yeyezi, ngoba inkazimulo yeNkosi yagcwalisa indlu kaNkulunkulu. 
<b>2 Crónicas 6</b><br><b>1</b> Ngalesosikhathi uSolomoni wathi: UJehova wathi uzahlala emnyameni onzima. <b>2</b> Kodwa mina ngikwakhele indlu yokuhlala, lendawo yokuhlala kwakho kuze kube nini lanini. <b>3</b> Inkosi yasiphendula ubuso bayo, yabusisa ibandla lonke lakoIsrayeli; ibandla lonke-ke lakoIsrayeli lalimi. <b>4</b> Yasisithi: Kayibusiswe iNkosi, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, eyakhuluma ngomlomo wayo kuDavida ubaba yakugcwalisa ngezandla zayo, isithi: 
<b>5</b> Kusukela ngosuku engakhupha ngalo abantu bami elizweni leGibhithe kangikhethanga muzi ezizweni zonke zakoIsrayeli ukwakha indlu, ukuthi ibizo lami libe lapho, kangikhethanga muntu ukuba ngumkhokheli phezu kwabantu bami uIsrayeli a. <b>6</b> Kodwa ngakhetha iJerusalema ukuthi ibizo lami libe lapho, ngakhetha uDavida ukuthi abe phezu kwabantu bami uIsrayeli b. <b>7</b> Njalo kwakusenhliziyweni kaDavida ubaba ukwakhela ibizo leNkosi, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, indlu. 
<b>8</b> Kodwa iNkosi yathi kuDavida ubaba: Njengoba kwakusenhliziyweni yakho ukwakhela ibizo lami indlu, wenze kuhle ngokuthi kwakusenhliziyweni yakho. <b>9</b> Lanxa kunjalo kawuyikuyakha indlu wena, kodwa indodana yakho ezaphuma ekhalweni lwakho, yiyo ezakwakhela ibizo lami indlu. <b>10</b> INkosi isiqinisile ilizwi layo eyalikhulumayo, ngoba ngivelile esikhundleni sikaDavida ubaba, ngihlezi esihlalweni sobukhosi sikaIsrayeli, njengokukhuluma kweNkosi; sengilakhele ibizo leNkosi, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, indlu. 
<b>11</b> Ngiwubeke khona umtshokotsho lapho okulesivumelwano seNkosi khona eyasenza labantwana bakoIsrayeli. <b>12</b> Wasesima phambi kwelathi leNkosi maqondana lebandla lonke lakoIsrayeli, welula izandla zakhe. <b>13</b> Ngoba uSolomoni wayenze isitubhu sethusi c, ubude baso buzingalo ezinhlanu, lobubanzi baso buzingalo ezinhlanu, lokuphakama kwaso kuzingalo ezintathu, wasimisa phakathi laphakathi kweguma d. Wema phezu kwaso, waguqa ngamadolo akhe phambi kwebandla lonke lakoIsrayeli, welulela izandla zakhe emazulwini e, 
<b>14</b> wathi: Nkosi, Nkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, kakho uNkulunkulu onjengawe emazulwini lemhlabeni, ogcina isivumelwano lomusa ezincekwini zakho ezihamba phambi kwakho ngayo yonke inhliziyo yazo, <b>15</b> ogcinele inceku yakho uDavida ubaba lokho owakutsho kuyo; ngoba wakhuluma ngomlomo wakho usukugcwalisile ngesandla sakho njengalamuhla.

<b>37</b> uba benakana ngakho enhliziyweni yabo elizweni abathunjelwe kilo, baphenduke bancenge kuwe elizweni lokuthunjwa kwabo bathi: Sonile, senze okubi, senza ngenkohlakalo; <b>38</b> uba bephendukela kuwe ngayo yonke inhliziyo yabo langawo wonke umphefumulo wabo elizweni lokuthunjwa kwabo, lapho ababathumbele khona, bakhuleke ngasendleleni yelizwe labo owalinika oyise, lomuzi owukhethileyo, langasendlini engiyakhele ibizo lakho, 
<b>39</b> zwana-ke usemazulwini, usendaweni yakho yokuhlala, umkhuleko wabo lokuncenga kwabo, wenze ilungelo labo, uthethelele abantu bakho abakonileyo. <b>40</b> Ngakho, Nkulunkulu wami, ake amehlo akho avuleke j lendlebe zakho zilalele umkhuleko walindawo k. <b>41</b> Khathesi-ke, sukuma, Nkosi Nkulunkulu l, uze endaweni yakho yokuphumula m, wena lomtshokotsho wamandla akho. Abapristi bakho, Nkosi Nkulunkulu, kabembathe usindiso, labangcwele bakho bathokoze ngokuhle n. 
<b>42</b> Nkosi Nkulunkulu, ungafulathelisi ubuso bogcotshiweyo wakho o. Khumbula p izisa zikaDavida inceku yakho q. 
<b>2 Crónicas 7</b><br><b>1</b> Kwathi uSolomoni eseqedile ukukhuleka a, umlilo wehla uvela emazulwini, wadla umnikelo wokutshiswa lemihlatshelo b, lenkazimulo yeNkosi yagcwalisa indlu c. <b>2</b> Njalo abapristi babengelakho ukungena endlini yeNkosi, ngoba inkazimulo yeNkosi yayigcwalise indlu yeNkosi d. <b>3</b> Lapho bonke abantwana bakoIsrayeli bebona ukwehla komlilo lenkazimulo yeNkosi phezu kwendlu, bakhothama ngobuso babo emhlabathini endaweni egandelweyo, bakhonza bedumisa iNkosi, ukuthi ilungile, ukuthi umusa wayo umi phakade e. 
<b>4</b> Inkosi labo bonke abantu banikela-ke imihlatshelo phambi kweNkosi. <b>5</b> Inkosi uSolomoni yasinikela imihlatshelo yezinkabi ezizinkulungwane ezingamatshumi amabili, lezimvu ezizinkulungwane ezilikhulu lamatshumi amabili. Ngokunjalo inkosi labo bonke abantu bayehlukanisa indlu kaNkulunkulu. 
<b>6</b> Abapristi basebesima ezikhundleni zabo; lamaLevi elezinto zokuhlabelela zikaJehova inkosi uDavida ayezenzele ukudumisa uJehova, ngoba umusa wakhe umi phakade, lapho uDavida edumisa ngenkonzo yabo; labapristi bakhalisa impondo phambi kwabo, loIsrayeli wonke wema. <b>7</b> USolomoni wasengcwelisa iphakathi leguma elaliphambi kwendlu yeNkosi, ngoba wenza lapho iminikelo yokutshiswa lamahwahwa eminikelo yokuthula, ngoba ilathi lethusi uSolomoni ayelenzile lalingenele umnikelo wokutshiswa, lomnikelo wokudla, lamahwahwa. 
<b>8</b> Langalesosikhathi uSolomoni wenza umkhosi okwensuku eziyisikhombisa, loIsrayeli wonke kanye laye, ibandla elikhulu kakhulu, kusukela ekungeneni kweHamathi kusiya esifuleni seGibhithe. <b>9</b> Langosuku lwesificaminwembili benza umhlangano onzulu, ngoba benza ukwehlukaniswa kwelathi okwensuku eziyisikhombisa, lomkhosi okwensuku eziyisikhombisa. 
<b>10</b> Ngosuku lwamatshumi amabili lantathu lwenyanga yesikhombisa waseyekela abantu bahamba baya emathenteni abo, bethokoza bejabula enhliziyweni ngokuhle iNkosi eyayikwenzele uDavida loSolomoni loIsrayeli abantu bayo. INkosi izibonakalisa kuSolomoni <b>11</b> Ngokunjalo uSolomoni waqeda indlu kaJehova lendlu yenkosi; lakho konke okwakuvela enhliziyweni kaSolomoni ukukwenza endlini yeNkosi lendlini yakhe wakuphumelelisa f. 
<b>12</b> INkosi yasibonakala kuSolomoni ebusuku yathi kuye: Ngizwile umkhuleko wakho, sengizikhethele lindawo ukuba yindlu yomhlatshelo g. <b>13</b> Uba ngivala amazulu ukuze kungabi lazulu h, kumbe uba ngilaya intethe ukuqeda ilizwe, kumbe uba ngithumela umatshayabhuqe wesifo phakathi kwabantu bami i, <b>14</b> uba abantu bami ibizo lami elibizwa phezu kwabo bezithoba, bakhuleke badinge ubuso bami, baphenduke ezindleleni zabo ezimbi j, mina-ke ngizakuzwa ngisemazulwini, ngithethelele isono sabo, ngelaphe ilizwe labo k. 
<b>15</b> Khathesi amehlo ami azavuleka, lendlebe zami zilalele umkhuleko walindawo l. <b>16</b> Ngoba sengiyikhethile ngayingcwelisa lindlu ukuze ibizo lami libe khona kuze kube nininini; lamehlo ami lenhliziyo yami kuzakuba lapho zonke izinsuku m. <b>17</b> Wena-ke, uba uhamba phambi kwami njengokuhamba kukaDavida uyihlo, wenze njengakho konke engikulaye khona, ugcine izimiso zami lezahlulelo zami, 
<b>18</b> ngizaqinisa-ke isihlalo sobukhosi sombuso wakho, njengokwenza kwami isivumelwano loDavida uyihlo ngisithi: Kakuyikusweleka ▼▼ kakuyikusweleka: Heb kakuyikuqunywa. muntu kuwe obusa koIsrayeli. <b>19</b> Kodwa uba libuyela emuva lina, litshiya izimiso zami lemilayo yami engikubeke phambi kwenu, lihambe likhonze abanye onkulunkulu libakhothamele o, <b>20</b> ngizabasiphuna-ke elizweni engibanike lona, lalindlu engiyingcwelisele ibizo lami ngizayilahla isuke phambi kwami, ngiyenze ibe yisiga lento yokuhozwa phakathi kwezizwe zonke p. 
<b>21</b> Lalindlu ephakemeyo izakuba yisimangaliso kulowo lalowo odlula kuyo, aze athi: INkosi ikwenzeleni okunje kulelilizwe lakulindlu? <b>22</b> Bazakuthi-ke: Kungoba beyidelile iNkosi uNkulunkulu waboyise eyabakhupha elizweni leGibhithe, babambelela kwabanye onkulunkulu, babakhonza babasebenzela; ngenxa yalokho ibehlisele bonke lobububi. 
<b>2 Crónicas 8</b><br><b>1</b> Kwasekusithi ekupheleni kweminyaka engamatshumi amabili uSolomoni akha ngayo indlu yeNkosi lendlu yakhe a, <b>2</b> imizi uHuramu ayeyinike uSolomoni, uSolomoni wayakha, wahlalisa abantwana bakoIsrayeli khona b. <b>3</b> USolomoni wasesiya eHamathi c-Zoba, wayinqoba d. <b>4</b> Wakha iTadimori enkangala layo yonke imizi eyiziphala ayakha eHamathi. <b>5</b> Wakha leBhethi-Horoni engaphezulu leBhethi-Horoni engaphansi, imizi ebiyelweyo elemithangala, amasango, lemigoqo, 
<b>6</b> leBahalathi, lemizi yonke eyiziphala uSolomoni ayelayo, layo yonke imizi yezinqola, lemizi yabagadi bamabhiza, laso sonke isifiso sikaSolomoni ayefisa ngaso ukwakha eJerusalema leLebhanoni lelizweni lonke lombuso wakhe. <b>7</b> Abantu bonke ababesele kumaHethi lamaAmori lamaPerizi lamaHivi lamaJebusi, ababengeyisibo bakoIsrayeli, <b>8</b> ebantwaneni babo ababesele emva kwabo elizweni abantwana bakoIsrayeli abangabaqedanga uSolomoni wabenyusa baba yizibhalwa kuze kube lamuhla.

<b>12</b> Ngalesosikhathi uSolomoni wanikela eNkosini iminikelo yokutshiswa phezu kwelathi leNkosi ayelakhile phambi kwekhulusi f. <b>13</b> Ngitsho njengokumiselwe g usuku ngosuku h, enikela njengokomlayo kaMozisi ngamasabatha, langokuthwasa kwezinyanga, langemikhosi emisiweyo i, kathathu ngomnyaka, ngomkhosi wesinkwa esingelamvubelo, langomkhosi wamaviki, langomkhosi wamadumba j.

<b>2 Crónicas 9</b><br><b>1</b> Indlovukazi yeShebha isizwile udumo lukaSolomoni yeza eJerusalema ukuhlola uSolomoni ngemibuzo enzima, ilexuku elikhulu kakhulu, lamakamela ethwele amakha legolide ngobunengi lamatshe aligugu; isifikile kuSolomoni yakhuluma laye konke okwakusenhliziyweni yayo. <b>2</b> USolomoni wayichasisela zonke indaba zayo, njalo kwakungekho lutho olwamcatshelayo uSolomoni angaluchasisanga kuyo. <b>3</b> Indlovukazi yeShebha isibonile inhlakanipho kaSolomoni, lendlu ayeyakhile, 
<b>4</b> lokudla kwetafula lakhe, lokuhlala kwenceku zakhe, lokuma kwezikhonzi zakhe, lezigqoko zazo, labaphathinkezo bakhe, lezigqoko zabo, lokwenyuka kwakhe enyuka ngakho endlini yeNkosi, kakusabanga lamoya kuyo. <b>5</b> Yasisithi enkosini: Wawuliqiniso umbiko engawuzwa elizweni lami ngendaba zakho langenhlakanipho yakho. <b>6</b> Kodwa kangiwakholwanga amazwi abo ngaze ngeza lamehlo ami akubona. Khangela-ke, bengingatshelwanga ingxenye yobukhulu benhlakanipho yakho; uyedlula umbiko engawuzwayo.

<b>2 Crónicas 10</b><br><b>1</b> URehobhowamu wasesiya eShekema ngoba uIsrayeli wonke wayeze eShekema ukumbeka abe yinkosi a. <b>2</b> Kwasekusithi uJerobhowamu indodana kaNebati esizwa, owayeseGibhithe lapho ayebalekele khona ubuso bukaSolomoni inkosi, uJerobhowamu wabuya evela eGibhithe b. <b>3</b> Basebethumela bambiza; uJerobhowamu loIsrayeli wonke basebesiza, bakhuluma kuRehobhowamu besithi: 
<b>4</b> Uyihlo wenza ijogwe lethu laba lukhuni; ngakho-ke yenza ube lulanyana umsebenzi kayihlo olukhuni, lejogwe lakhe elinzima abesithwalise lona, njalo sizakukhonza. <b>5</b> Wasesithi kibo: Buyani kimi emva kwensuku ezintathu. Abantu basebehamba. <b>6</b> Inkosi uRehobhowamu yasicebisana labadala ababesima phambi kukaSolomoni uyise esaphila, isithi: Licebisa ukubuyisela mpendulo bani kulababantu? <b>7</b> Basebekhuluma kuye besithi: Uba uzalunga kulababantu, ubathokozise, ukhulume kibo amazwi amahle, bazakuba zinceku zakho insuku zonke c. 
<b>8</b> Kodwa wadela iseluleko sabadala abamcebisa ngaso, wacebisana lamajaha ayeyintanga yakhe, ayesima phambi kwakhe. <b>9</b> Wasesithi kuwo: Licebisa ukuthi sibuyisele mpendulo bani kulababantu, abakhulume kimi besithi: Yenza libe lulanyana ijogwe uyihlo alibeka phezu kwethu? <b>10</b> Amajaha ayeyintanga yakhe asekhuluma laye esithi: Uzakutsho njalo ebantwini abakhuluma kuwe besithi: Uyihlo wenza ijogwe lethu laba nzima, kodwa wena lenze libe lulanyana kithi; uzakutsho njalo kubo: Ucikilicane wami uqatha kulokhalo lukababa. 
<b>11</b> Ngakho-ke ubaba walethwesa ijogwe elinzima, kodwa mina ngizakwengezelela ejogweni lenu. Ubaba walijezisa ngeziswepu, kodwa mina ngabofezela d. <b>12</b> Ngakho uJerobhowamu labo bonke abantu beza kuRehobhowamu ngosuku lwesithathu njengokutsho kwenkosi isithi: Libuye kimi futhi ngosuku lwesithathu. <b>13</b> Inkosi yasibaphendula kalukhuni; inkosi uRehobhowamu yasidela iseluleko sabadala, 
<b>14</b> yakhuluma kubo njengeseluleko samajaha isithi: Ubaba wenza ijogwe lenu laba nzima, kodwa mina ngizakwengezelela kulo; ubaba walijezisa ngeziswepu, kodwa mina ngabofezela e. <b>15</b> Ngokunjalo inkosi kayibalalelanga abantu; ngoba inguquko yayivela kuNkulunkulu ukuze iNkosi iqinise ilizwi layo eyayilikhulume ngesandla sikaAhiya umShilo kuJerobhowamu indodana kaNebati f. 
<b>16</b> Lapho uIsrayeli wonke ebona ukuthi inkosi kayibalalelanga, abantu bayiphendula inkosi besithi: Silesabelo bani kuDavida? Njalo kasilalifa endodaneni kaJese. Ngulowo emathenteni akho, Israyeli; khathesi, zibonele indlu yakho, Davida. Ngakho uIsrayeli wonke waya emathenteni akhe. <b>17</b> Kodwa abantwana bakoIsrayeli ababehlala emizini yakoJuda, uRehobhowamu wabusa phezu kwabo. 
<b>18</b> Inkosi uRehobhowamu yasithuma uAdoramu owayengumphathi wezibhalwa g; abantwana bakoIsrayeli basebemkhanda ngamatshe, wasesifa. Inkosi uRehobhowamu yaphangisa ukukhwela enqoleni ukubalekela eJerusalema. <b>19</b> Ngalokho uIsrayeli wayihlamuka indlu kaDavida kuze kube lamuhla. 
<b>2 Crónicas 11</b><br><b>1</b> URehobhowamu esefikile eJerusalema wabuthanisa indlu yakoJuda leyakoBhenjamini, abakhethiweyo abazinkulungwane ezilikhulu lamatshumi ayisificaminwembili, abalwa impi, ukuze balwe loIsrayeli ukubuyisela umbuso kuRehobhowamu. <b>2</b> Kodwa ilizwi leNkosi lafika kuShemaya umuntu kaNkulunkulu lisithi: <b>3</b> Khuluma kuRehobhowamu indodana kaSolomoni, inkosi yakoJuda, lakuIsrayeli wonke koJuda lakoBhenjamini a, uthi: 
<b>4</b> Itsho njalo iNkosi: Kaliyikwenyuka njalo lingalwi labafowenu. Buyelani ngulowo endlini yakhe, ngoba linto yenziwe yimi. Basebelilalela ilizwi leNkosi, baphenduka ekuyenimelana loJerobhowamu b. Ukuphumelela kukaRehobhowamu <b>5</b> URehobhowamu wasehlala eJerusalema, wakha imizi eyizinqaba koJuda c. <b>6</b> Wasesakha iBhethelehema d leEtamu e leThekhowa f <b>7</b> leBeti-Zuri g leSoko h leAdulamu i <b>8</b> leGathi j leMaresha k leZifi l <b>9</b> leAdorayimi leLakishi m leAzeka n 
<b>10</b> leZora o leAjaloni p leHebroni q, eyayikoJuda lakoBhenjamini, imizi ebiyelweyo. <b>11</b> Waseqinisa izinqaba, wabeka abalawulimabutho kizo, leziphala zokudla lamafutha lewayini. <b>12</b> Lakuwo wonke umuzi lomuzi wabeka izihlangu ezinkulu lemikhonto, wayiqinisa kakhulukazi. Njalo uJuda loBhenjamini babengabakhe. <b>13</b> Labapristi lamaLevi ababekoIsrayeli wonke bazimisa kuye bevela kuyo yonke imingcele yabo. 
<b>14</b> Ngoba amaLevi atshiya amadlelo awo lemfuyo yabo, aya koJuda leJerusalema r, ngoba uJerobhowamu lamadodana akhe bawala ekusebenzeleni iNkosi njengabapristi s; <b>15</b> wasezibekela abapristi bendawo eziphakemeyo t, labamadimoni u, labamathole ayewenzile v. <b>16</b> Lemva kwawo kweza eJerusalema labo ababenikele inhliziyo yabo ukuyidinga iNkosi, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, bevela kuzo zonke izizwe zakoIsrayeli, ukuyahlabela iNkosi, uNkulunkulu waboyise w. 
<b>17</b> Ngokunjalo bawuqinisa umbuso wakoJuda, benza uRehobhowamu indodana kaSolomoni waba lamandla iminyaka emithathu; ngoba bahamba endleleni kaDavida loSolomoni iminyaka emithathu x. <b>18</b> URehobhowamu wasezithathela uMahalathi indodakazi kaJerimothi indodana kaDavida waba ngumkakhe, loAbihayili indodakazi kaEliyabi indodana kaJese y, <b>19</b> owamzalela amadodana, oJewushi loShemariya loZahamu. <b>20</b> Langemva kwakhe wathatha uMahaka z indodakazi kaAbisalomu aa, owamzalela oAbhiya ab loAthayi loZiza loShelomithi. 
<b>21</b> URehobhowamu wasemthanda uMahaka indodakazi kaAbisalomu okwedlula bonke omkakhe labafazi bakhe abancinyane - ngoba wathatha abafazi abalitshumi lesificaminwembili, labafazi abancinyane abangamatshumi ayisithupha, wazala amadodana angamatshumi amabili lesificaminwembili lamadodakazi angamatshumi ayisithupha. <b>22</b> URehobhowamu ac wasebeka uAbhiya indodana kaMahaka waba yinhloko ukubusa phakathi kwabafowabo, ngoba kwakuyikumenza inkosi ad. 
<b>23</b> Wenza ngokuhlakanipha, wasabalalisa kuwo wonke amadodana akhe emazweni wonke akoJuda lakoBhenjamini, kuwo wonke umuzi obiyelweyo, wawanika ukudla ngobunengi ae, wadinga inengi labafazi af. 
<b>2 Crónicas 12</b><br><b>1</b> Kwasekusithi uRehobhowamu eseqinise umbuso, eseziqinisile a, watshiya umlayo weNkosi, loIsrayeli wonke laye b. <b>2</b> Kwasekusithi ngomnyaka wesihlanu wenkosi uRehobhowamu, uShishaki inkosi yeGibhithe wenyuka wamelana leJerusalema, ngoba babephambukile eNkosini c, <b>3</b> elezinqola ezingamakhulu alitshumi lambili labagadi bamabhiza abazinkulungwane ezingamatshumi ayisithupha; labantu babengelakubalwa abeza laye bephuma eGibhithe, amaLibiya d, amaSukhi, lamaEthiyophiya e. 
<b>4</b> Wasethumba imizi ebiyelweyo eyayingeyakoJuda weza eJerusalema f. <b>5</b> UShemaya umprofethi wasefika kuRehobhowamu g lezikhulu zakoJuda ezazibuthene eJerusalema ngenxa kaShishaki, wathi kubo: Itsho njalo iNkosi: Lina lingilahlile, ngakho lami ngililahlele esandleni sikaShishaki h. <b>6</b> Iziphathamandla zakoIsrayeli kanye lenkosi basebezithoba i bathi: INkosi ilungile j. 
<b>7</b> INkosi isibona ukuthi bazithobile k, ilizwi leNkosi lafika kuShemaya lisithi: Bazithobile; kangiyikubabhubhisa, kodwa ngizabanika isilinganiso sokukhululwa, lolaka lwami kaluyikuthululelwa eJerusalema ngesandla sikaShishaki l. <b>8</b> Kodwa bazakuba zinceku zakhe ukuze bazi inkonzo yami lenkonzo yemibuso yamazwe m. <b>9</b> Ngakho uShishaki inkosi yeGibhithe wenyukela eJerusalema, wathatha okuligugu kwendlu kaJehova lokuligugu kwendlu yenkosi n, wathatha konke; wathatha lezihlangu zegolide uSolomoni ayezenzile o. 
<b>10</b> Inkosi uRehobhowamu yasisenza izihlangu zethusi endaweni yazo, yazinikela ngaphansi kwesandla senduna zabalindi ababegcina umnyango wendlu yenkosi. <b>11</b> Kwasekusithi lapho inkosi ingena endlini kaJehova bafika abalindi bezithwele, bazibuyisela ekamelweni labalindi. <b>12</b> Isizithobile, ulaka lweNkosi lwaphenduka lwasuka kuyo ukuze ingayibhubhisi ngokuphelelelyo; futhi lakoJuda kwakulezinto ezinhle. 
<b>13</b> Inkosi uRehobhowamu yasiziqinisa eJerusalema, yabusa p. Ngoba uRehobhowamu wayeleminyaka engamatshumi amane lanye lapho esiba yinkosi q, wabusa iminyaka elitshumi lesikhombisa eJerusalema, umuzi iNkosi eyayiwukhethile phakathi kwazo zonke izizwe zakoIsrayeli, ukubeka ibizo layo khona. Lebizo likanina lalinguNahama umAmonikazi r. <b>14</b> Wasesenza okubi ngoba engalungisanga inhliziyo yakhe ukudinga iNkosi. 
<b>15</b> Lendaba zikaRehobhowamu s, ezokuqala lezokucina t, kazibhalwanga yini ezindabeni zikaShemaya umprofethi u lezikaIdo v umboni w mayelana lemibhalo yosendo x, lezimpi zikaRehobhowamu loJerobhowamu zonke izinsuku? <b>16</b> URehobhowamu waselala laboyise, wangcwatshelwa emzini kaDavida. UAbhiya indodana yakhe wasesiba yinkosi esikhundleni sakhe. 
<b>2 Crónicas 13</b><br><b>1</b> Ngomnyaka wetshumi lesificaminwembili wenkosi uJerobhowamu uAbhiya wasesiba yinkosi phezu kukaJuda a. <b>2</b> Wabusa iminyaka emithathu eJerusalema. Lebizo likanina lalinguMikhaya indodakazi kaUriyeli b weGibeya c. Kwasekusiba khona impi phakathi kukaAbhiya loJerobhowamu d. <b>3</b> UAbhiya wasehlela impi elebutho lamaqhawe empi, amadoda akhethiweyo azinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amane. UJerobhowamu wasehlela impi ukumelana laye elamadoda akhethiweyo azinkulungwane ezingamakhulu ayisificaminwembili, amaqhawe alamandla e. 
<b>4</b> UAbhiya wasesima entabeni iZemarayimi f esentabeni yakoEfrayimi g wathi: Ngizwa Jerobhowamu, loIsrayeli wonke! <b>5</b> Kakusikwenu yini ukwazi ukuthi iNkosi uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli yanika uDavida umbuso phezu kukaIsrayeli phakade h, kuye lakumadodana akhe ngesivumelwano setshwayi i? <b>6</b> Kodwa uJerobhowamu indodana kaNebati, inceku kaSolomoni indodana kaDavida, wasukuma wavukela inkosi yakhe j. 
<b>7</b> Kwasekubuthana kuye abantu abayize k, abantwana bakaBheliyali l, baziqinisa ukumelana loRehobhowamu indodana kaSolomoni, uRehobhowamu esesengumfana njalo ebuthakathaka ngenhliziyo engemelane labo m. <b>8</b> Khathesi-ke lina lithi lizamelana lombuso weNkosi esandleni samadodana kaDavida n; futhi lilixuku elikhulu, kodwa lilamathole egolide o uJerobhowamu alenzela wona abe ngonkulunkulu. 
<b>9</b> Kalibaxotshanga yini abapristi beNkosi, amadodana kaAroni, lamaLevi, lazenzela abapristi njengezizwe zelizwe p? Loba ngubani ozegcwalisa isandla sakhe ngejongosi, ithole lenkomo, lezinqama eziyisikhombisa q uba ngumpristi wabangeyisibo onkulunkulu r. <b>10</b> Kodwa thina, iNkosi inguNkulunkulu wethu, kasiyidelanga; labapristi abayikhonzayo iNkosi bangamadodana kaAroni, lamaLevi asemsebenzini s. 
<b>11</b> Njalo bayahlabela iNkosi ikuseni ngekuseni lentambama ngentambama iminikelo yokutshiswa t lempepha elephunga elimnandi u; lokulungisa izinkwa etafuleni elihlambulukileyo v , w, lokulumathisa uluthi lwesibane lwegolide lezibane zalo x kusihlwa ngakusihlwa y. Ngoba thina sigcina umlindo weNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu z, kodwa lina liyidelile. 
<b>12</b> Ngakho khangelani, uNkulunkulu ulathi eyinhloko, labapristi bakhe belempondo zokukhalisa ukuhlaba umkhosi bemelene lani aa. Bantwana bakoIsrayeli, lingalwi leNkosi, uNkulunkulu waboyihlo, ngoba kaliyikuphumelela ab. <b>13</b> Kodwa uJerobhowamu wayebhodise abacathameli ukuthi beze bengasemva kwabo. Ngakho babephambi kukaJuda, labacathameli bengemva kwabo ac. <b>14</b> Lapho uJuda ephenduka, khangela-ke, impi yayimelene labo ngaphambili langemuva; basebekhala eNkosini, labapristi bakhalisa izimpondo ad, 
<b>15</b> labantu bakoJuda bamemeza; kwasekusithi ekumemezeni kwabantu bakoJuda uNkulunkulu watshaya oJerobhowamu loIsrayeli wonke phambi kukaAbhiya loJuda ae. <b>16</b> Abantwana bakoIsrayeli basebebaleka phambi kobuso bukaJuda, uNkulunkulu wasebanikela esandleni sabo af. <b>17</b> UAbhiya labantu bakhe basebebatshaya ngokutshaya okukhulu; kwasekusiwa ababuleweyo koIsrayeli, amadoda akhethiweyo azinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amahlanu. 
<b>18</b> Ngokunjalo abantwana bakoIsrayeli bathotshiswa ngalesosikhathi, labantwana bakoJuda baba lamandla ngoba beyama eNkosini, uNkulunkulu waboyise ag. <b>19</b> UAbhiya wasexotshana loJerobhowamu, wathumba imizi kuye, iBhetheli lemizana yayo ah, leJeshana lemizana yayo, leEfroni lemizana yayo ai. <b>20</b> UJerobhowamu akabe esaba lamandla ensukwini zikaAbhiya aj. INkosi yasimtshaya ak, wafa al. 
<b>21</b> Kodwa uAbhiya waziqinisa, wazithathela abafazi abalitshumi lane, wazala amadodana angamatshumi amabili lambili, lamadodakazi alitshumi lesithupha am. <b>22</b> Ezinye-ke zezindaba zikaAbhiya, lezindlela zakhe, lamazwi akhe, kubhaliwe ezindabeni zomprofethi uIdo an. 
<b>2 Crónicas 14</b><br><b>1</b> UAbhiya waselala laboyise, bamngcwabela emzini kaDavida. UAsa indodana yakhe wasesiba yinkosi esikhundleni sakhe. Ensukwini zakhe ilizwe laba lokuthula iminyaka elitshumi a. <b>2</b> UAsa wasesenza okuhle lokulungileyo emehlweni eNkosi uNkulunkulu wakhe. <b>3</b> Ngoba wasusa amalathi awemzini lendawo eziphakemeyo b, wadiliza insika eziyizithombe, wagamula izixuku c. <b>4</b> Wasesithi kuJuda kabayidinge iNkosi, uNkulunkulu waboyise, lokuthi bagcine umthetho lomlayo. 
<b>5</b> Wasesusa kuyo yonke imizi yakoJuda indawo eziphakemeyo lamalathi okutshisela impepha. Lombuso waba lokuthula phambi kwakhe d. <b>6</b> Wasesakha imizi ebiyelweyo koJuda, ngoba ilizwe lalilokuthula e, njalo kungelampi laye ngaleyominyaka, ngoba iNkosi yamphumuza f. <b>7</b> Wasesithi kuJuda: Asiyakheni limizi, siyizingelezele ngemiduli, lemiphotshongo, amasango, lemigoqo, ilizwe lisesephambi kwethu g; ngoba siyidingile iNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu, siyidingile, yasinika ukuthula inhlangothi zonke. Basebesakha, baphumelela h. 
<b>8</b> Njalo uAsa wayelebutho labazinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amathathu abavela koJuda, bethwele ihawu lomkhonto, labazinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amabili lamatshumi ayisificaminwembili abavela koBhenjamini i bethwele isihlangu esikhulu benyathela idandili; bonke laba babengamaqhawe alamandla j. <b>9</b> UZera umEthiyophiya k wasephuma ukumelana labo elebutho eliyinkulungwane yezinkulungwane lezinqola ezingamakhulu amathathu, wasefika eMaresha l. <b>10</b> UAsa wasephuma ukumelana laye, bahlela impi esihotsheni seZefatha m eMaresha n. 
<b>11</b> UAsa wasekhala eNkosini uNkulunkulu wakhe o wathi: Nkosi, kakusilutho kuwe ukunceda, loba kwabanengi loba kwabangelamandla; sisize, Nkosi Nkulunkulu wethu p, ngoba seyeme kuwe q; njalo ngebizo lakho sizemelana lalelixuku; Nkosi, wena unguNkulunkulu wethu r; umuntu kangakwehluli s. <b>12</b> Ngakho iNkosi yawatshaya amaEthiyophiya ▼▼ amaKushi: Heb amaKhushi. phambi kukaAsa laphambi kukaJuda; amaEthiyophiya asebaleka u. 
<b>13</b> UAsa labantu ababelaye baxotshana lawo kwaze kwaba seGerari v; kwasekusiwa kumaEthiyophiya okokuthi kakwaze kwaba lokuvuseleleka kiwo, ngoba acotshodiswa phambi kweNkosi laphambi kwebutho layo. Basebemuka lempango enengi kakhulu w. <b>14</b> Basebetshaya yonke imizi ezingelezele iGerari x, ngoba uvalo lweNkosi lwaluphezu kwayo. Basebephanga yonke imizi, ngoba kwakulempango enengi kiyo y. <b>15</b> Basebetshaya lamathente ezifuyo, bathumba izimvu ezinengi lamakamela, babuyela eJerusalema. 
<b>2 Crónicas 15</b><br><b>1</b> UMoya kaNkulunkulu a wasesehlela phezu kukaAzariya indodana kaOdedi b. <b>2</b> Wasephuma ukuhlangabezana loAsa, wathi kuye: Ngilalela, Asa loJuda wonke loBhenjamini; iNkosi ilani liselayo. Uba-ke liyidinga, izafunyanwa yini c; kodwa uba liyilahla, izalilahla d. <b>3</b> Okwensuku ezinengi uIsrayeli wayengelaye uNkulunkulu weqiniso e, njalo engelaye umpristi ofundisayo, njalo engelawo umlayo f. <b>4</b> Kodwa ekubandezelweni kwakhe waphendukela eNkosini, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, basebemdinga, wasefunyanwa yibo g. 
<b>5</b> Langalezozikhathi kwakungelakuthula kophumayo lakongenayo, ngoba inhlupheko ezinengi zaziphezu kwabo bonke abakhi bamazwe h. <b>6</b> Basebephahlazwa, isizwe ngesizwe, lomuzi ngomuzi, ngoba uNkulunkulu wabahlupha ngayo yonke imbandezelo. <b>7</b> Ngakho lina qinani i, zingadangali izandla zenu j, ngoba kulomvuzo ngomsebenzi wenu k. 
<b>8</b> Lapho uAsa esizwa lamazwi lesiprofetho sikaOdedi umprofethi, waziqinisa l, wasusa izinengiso elizweni lonke lakoJuda loBhenjamini m lemizini ayeyithumbile entabeni yakoEfrayimi n, wavuselela ilathi leNkosi elalingaphambi kwekhulusi leNkosi o. <b>9</b> Wasebuthanisa wonke uJuda loBhenjamini labemzini labo ababevela koEfrayimi loManase lakoSimeyoni; ngoba bawelela kuye bevela koIsrayeli ngobunengi lapho bebona ukuthi iNkosi uNkulunkulu wakhe yayilaye p. 
<b>10</b> Basebebuthana eJerusalema ngenyanga yesithathu ngomnyaka wetshumi lanhlanu wokubusa kukaAsa q. <b>11</b> Basebehlabela iNkosi ngalolosuku okwempango ababeyilethile, inkabi ezingamakhulu ayisikhombisa lezimvu ezizinkulungwane eziyisikhombisa r. <b>12</b> Basebengena esivumelwaneni sokudinga iNkosi, uNkulunkulu waboyise, ngenhliziyo yabo yonke langomphefumulo wabo wonke s. <b>13</b> Njalo wonke ongadingi iNkosi uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli uzabulawa, kusukela komncinyane kusiya komkhulu, kusukela kowesilisa kusiya kowesifazana t. 
<b>14</b> Basebefunga eNkosini ngelizwi elikhulu langokumemeza langezimpondo langempondo zezinqama u. <b>15</b> AbakoJuda bonke bathokoza-ke ngesifungo, ngoba babefungile ngenhliziyo yabo yonke, beyidingile ngaso sonke isifiso sabo v, yasifunyanwa yibo w; ngakho iNkosi yabaphumuza inhlangothi zonke x. <b>16</b> Njalo mayelana loMahaka unina kaAsa inkosi, yamsusa ekubeni yindlovukazi, ngoba wayenzele isixuku into enengekayo y. UAsa wasegamula into yakhe enengekayo, wayichola, wayitshisela esifuleni iKidroni z. 
<b>17</b> Kanti indawo eziphakemeyo kazisuswanga koIsrayeli; lanxa kunjalo inhliziyo kaAsa yayiphelele insuku zakhe zonke aa. <b>18</b> Wasengenisa endlini kaNkulunkulu izinto ezingcwele zikayise, lezinto zakhe ezingcwele, isiliva legolide lezitsha. <b>19</b> Kakusabanga khona impi kwaze kwaba ngumnyaka wamatshumi amathathu lanhlanu wokubusa kukaAsa. 
<b>2 Crónicas 16</b><br><b>1</b> Ngomnyaka wamatshumi amathathu lesithupha wokubusa kukaAsa a uBahasha inkosi yakoIsrayeli wenyuka emelana loJuda b, wakha iRama ukuze angavumeli ophumayo longenayo kuAsa inkosi yakoJuda. <b>2</b> UAsa wasekhupha isiliva legolide esiphaleni senotho sendlu kaJehova lendlu yenkosi, wathumela kuBenihadadi inkosi yeSiriya owayehlala eDamaseko, esithi: 
<b>3</b> Kulesivumelwano phakathi kwami lawe laphakathi kukababa loyihlo; khangela, ngiyakuthumela isiliva legolide; hamba, yephula isivumelwano sakho loBahasha inkosi yakoIsrayeli ukuze enyuke asuke kimi. <b>4</b> UBenihadadi waseyilalela inkosi uAsa, wathuma induna zamabutho ayelawo ukuyamelana lemizi yakoIsrayeli, zasezitshaya iIjoni leDani leAbeli-Mayimi c, lemizi yonke eyiziphala yakoNafithali d. <b>5</b> Kwasekusithi uBahasha esizwa wayekela ukwakha iRama, wawumisa umsebenzi wakhe. 
<b>6</b> UAsa inkosi wasethatha uJuda wonke, bathutha amatshe eRama lezigodo zayo uBahasha ayesakha ngakho; wasesakha ngakho iGeba leMizipa. <b>7</b> Ngalesosikhathi uHanani umboni wasefika kuAsa inkosi yakoJuda, wathi kuye: Ngoba weyeme enkosini yeSiriya, ungeyamanga eNkosini uNkulunkulu wakho, ngalokho ibutho lenkosi yeSiriya liphunyukile esandleni sakho. <b>8</b> AmaEthiyophiya lamaLibiya ayengeyisilo ibutho elikhulu, elezinqola labagadi bamabhiza abanengi kakhulu yini? <b>9</b> Ngoba iNkosi, amehlo ayo agijima aye le lale emhlabeni wonke ukuthi izitshengise ilamandla ngalabo abanhliziyo yabo iphelele kuyo. Wenze ngobuthutha kulokhu, ngoba kusukela khathesi kuzakuba lezimpi kuwe. <b>10</b> UAsa wasemcunukela umboni, wamfaka entolongweni, ngoba wamthukuthelela ngalokhu. UAsa wasecindezela abanye ebantwini ngalesosikhathi. <b>11</b> Khangela-ke, izindaba zikaAsa e, ezokuqala lezokucina, khangela, zibhaliwe egwalweni lwamakhosi akoJuda lakoIsrayeli f. 
<b>12</b> UAsa waba lomkhuhlane enyaweni zakhe, ngomnyaka wamatshumi amathathu lesificamunwemunye wokubusa kwakhe, umkhuhlane wakhe waze wakhula kakhulu; kanti lekuguleni kwakhe kayidinganga iNkosi, kodwa izinyanga g. <b>13</b> UAsa waselala laboyise wafa ngomnyaka wamatshumi amane lanye wokubusa kwakhe h. 
<b>14</b> Basebemngcwaba emangcwabeni akhe ayezigebhele wona emzini kaDavida i; bambeka embhedeni ayewugcwalise amaphunga amnandi j lezinhlobonhlobo zamakha k, ayenziwe ngokuhlakanipha kwezingcitshi zamakha l; basebembasela umlilo omkhulu, omkhulukazi m. 
<b>2 Crónicas 17</b><br><b>1</b> UJehoshafathi indodana yakhe wasesiba yinkosi esikhundleni sakhe; waziqinisa ukumelana loIsrayeli a. <b>2</b> Wasebeka amabutho kuyo yonke imizi ebiyelweyo yakoJuda b; wabeka lamabutho enqaba elizweni lakoJuda c, lemizini yakoEfrayimi uyise uAsa ayeyithumbile d. <b>3</b> INkosi yasisiba loJehoshafathi, ngoba wahamba ngendlela zakuqala zikaDavida uyise, kabadinganga oBhali; <b>4</b> kodwa wadinga kuNkulunkulu kayise, wahamba ngemilayo yakhe e, njalo kungenjengokwenza kukaIsrayeli f. 
<b>5</b> Ngakho iNkosi yawuqinisa umbuso esandleni sakhe, loJuda wonke waletha izipho kuJehoshafathi g; wasesiba lenotho lodumo ngobunengi h. <b>6</b> Inhliziyo yakhe yasiphakama ezindleleni zeNkosi; futhi-ke wasusa indawo eziphakemeyo i lezixuku koJuda j. <b>7</b> Langomnyaka wesithathu wokubusa kwakhe wathuma kuziphathamandla zakhe, kuBeni-Hayili lakuObhadiya lakuZekhariya lakuNethaneli lakuMikhaya, ukufundisa emizini yakoJuda. 
<b>8</b> Kanye lazo-ke amaLevi, oShemaya loNethaniya loZebhadiya loAsaheli loShemiramothi loJehonathani loAdonija loTobija loTobi-Adonija, amaLevi k; kanye labo-ke oElishama loJehoramu, abapristi l. <b>9</b> Basebefundisa koJuda, belogwalo lomlayo weNkosi; babhoda kuyo yonke imizi yakoJuda, bafundisa abantu. <b>10</b> Kwasekusiba lokuyesaba iNkosi phezu kwayo yonke imibuso yamazwe ayehanqe uJuda, okokuthi kayilwanga loJehoshafathi m. 
<b>11</b> Labanye bamaFilisti n bamlethela uJehoshafathi o izipho p, lesiliva lokuthela; amaArabhiya lawo amlethela imihlambi, inqama eziyizinkulungwane eziyisikhombisa lamakhulu ayisikhombisa, lezimpongo eziyizinkulungwane eziyisikhombisa lamakhulu ayisikhombisa q. <b>12</b> UJehoshafathi wayelokhu ekhula njalo r, waze waba sengqongeni. Wasesakha koJuda izinqaba lemizi yeziphala s. <b>13</b> Wayelomsebenzi omnengi emizini yakoJuda, lamadoda empi, amaqhawe alamandla, eJerusalema. 
<b>14</b> Lala ngamanani awo ngezindlu zaboyise: KoJuda, induna zezinkulungwane, uAdina engumphathi, njalo kanye laye amaqhawe alamandla azinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amathathu; <b>15</b> leceleni kwakhe uJohanani umphathi, njalo kanye laye izinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amabili lamatshumi ayisificaminwembili; <b>16</b> leceleni kwakhe uAmasiya indodana kaZikiri, owazinikela ngesihle eNkosini, njalo kanye laye amaqhawe alamandla azinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amabili t. 
<b>17</b> LakoBhenjamini: UEliyada iqhawe elilamandla, njalo kanye laye izinkulungwane ezingamakhulu amabili, ezihlome ngedandili langesihlangu u; <b>18</b> leceleni kwakhe uJehozabadi, njalo kanye laye izinkulungwane ezilikhulu lamatshumi ayisificaminwembili, abahlomele impi v. <b>19</b> Laba babesebenzela inkosi ngaphandle kwalabo inkosi eyayibabeke emizini ebiyelweyo kuye wonke uJuda w. 
<b>2 Crónicas 18</b><br><b>1</b> Njalo uJehoshafathi wayelenotho lodumo ngobunengi a; wahlangana ngobuhlobo loAhabi b. <b>2</b> Ekupheleni kweminyaka wasesehlela kuAhabi c eSamariya d. UAhabi wamhlabela izimvu lenkabi ngobunengi, labantu ababelaye, wamncenga ukuthi enyukele e eRamothi-Gileyadi f. <b>3</b> UAhabi inkosi yakoIsrayeli wasesithi kuJehoshafathi inkosi yakoJuda: Uzahamba lami eRamothi-Gileyadi yini? Wasesithi kuye: Njengami unjalo wena, labantu bami banjengabantu bakho. Sizakuba lawe empini. 
<b>4</b> UJehoshafathi wasesithi enkosini yakoIsrayeli: Akubuze lamuhla ilizwi leNkosi. <b>5</b> Inkosi yakoIsrayeli yasibuthanisa abaprofethi, amadoda angamakhulu amane, yathi kubo: Sihambe yini eRamothi-Gileyadi empini kumbe ngiyekele? Basebesithi: Yenyuka, ngoba uNkulunkulu uzayinikela esandleni senkosi. <b>6</b> Kodwa uJehoshafathi wathi: Kasekho yini lapha umprofethi weNkosi ukuthi sibuze kuye? 
<b>7</b> Inkosi yakoIsrayeli yasisithi kuJehoshafathi: Kusekhona indoda eyodwa esingabuza ngayo eNkosini, kodwa mina ngiyamzonda ngoba kaprofethi lutho oluhle ngami, kodwa okubi zonke izinsuku zakhe; lo nguMikhaya indodana kaImla. UJehoshafathi wasesithi: Inkosi ingatsho njalo. <b>8</b> Inkosi yakoIsrayeli yasibiza umthenwa othile yathi: Landa masinyane uMikhaya indodana kaImla. 
<b>9</b> Njalo inkosi yakoIsrayeli loJehoshafathi inkosi yakoJuda babehlezi ngulowo lalowo esihlalweni sakhe sobukhosi, begqoke izembatho, behlezi ebaleni engenelweni lesango leSamariya, bonke abaprofethi beprofetha-ke phambi kwabo. <b>10</b> UZedekhiya indodana kaKhenahana wasezenzela impondo zensimbi, wathi: Itsho njalo iNkosi: Ngalezi uzahlaba amaSiriya uze uwaqede. <b>11</b> Labo bonke abaprofethi baprofetha njalo, besithi: Yenyukela eRamothi-Gileyadi uphumelele, ngoba iNkosi izayinikela esandleni senkosi. 
<b>12</b> Isithunywa esasiyebiza uMikhaya sakhuluma kuye sisithi: Khangela, amazwi abaprofethi ngamlomo munye mahle enkosini; ngakho ilizwi lakho ake lifanane lelomunye wabo, ukhulume okuhle. <b>13</b> Kodwa uMikhaya wathi: Kuphila kukaJehova, ngeqiniso lokho uNkulunkulu wami akutshoyo, lokho ngizakukhuluma. <b>14</b> Esefikile enkosini, inkosi yathi kuye: Mikhaya, siye eRamothi-Gileyadi empini kumbe ngiyekele yini? Wasesithi: Yenyukani, liphumelele, ngoba bazanikelwa esandleni senu. 
<b>15</b> Inkosi yasisithi kuye: Ngizakufungisa kuze kube yizikhathi ezingaki ukuze ungakhulumi kimi ngaphandle kweqiniso ebizweni leNkosi? <b>16</b> Wasesithi: Ngabona uIsrayeli wonke echithekile phezu kwezintaba njengezimvu ezingelamelusi; iNkosi yasisithi: Laba kabalamakhosi, kababuyele ngulowo endlini yakhe ngokuthula. <b>17</b> Inkosi yakoIsrayeli yasisithi kuJehoshafathi: Kangikutshelanga yini: Kayikuprofetha okuhle ngami kodwa okubi kuphela? 
<b>18</b> Wasesithi: Ngakho zwanini ilizwi leNkosi. Ngabona iNkosi ihlezi esihlalweni sayo sobukhosi, lexuku lonke lamazulu limi ngakwesokunene sayo langakwesokhohlo sayo. <b>19</b> INkosi yasisithi: Ngubani ozahuga uAhabi inkosi yakoIsrayeli ukuze enyuke awe eRamothi-Gileyadi g? Omunye wasekhuluma esitsho ngokunjalo, lomunye esitsho ngokunjalo. <b>20</b> Kwasekuphuma umoya, wema phambi kweNkosi wathi: Mina ngizamhuga h. INkosi yasisithi kuwo: Ngani? 
<b>21</b> Wasusithi: Ngizaphuma ngibe ngumoya wamanga emlonyeni wabo bonke abaprofethi bakhe. Yasisithi: Uzamhuga, njalo uzakuba lamandla; phuma wenze njalo i. <b>22</b> Ngakho-ke, khangela, iNkosi ifake umoya wamanga emlonyeni walababaprofethi bakho, njalo iNkosi ikhulume okubi ngawe. <b>23</b> UZedekhiya indodana kaKhenahana wasesondela, watshaya uMikhaya esihlathini, wathi: UMoya weNkosi wedlula ngandlela bani esuka kimi ukuthi akhulume lawe? 
<b>24</b> UMikhaya wasesithi: Khangela, uzabona ngalolosuku mhla uzangena ekamelweni phakathi kwekamelo ukuthi ucatshe. <b>25</b> Inkosi yakoIsrayeli yasisithi: Thathani uMikhaya, limbuyisele kuAmoni umbusi womuzi lakuJowashi indodana yenkosi, <b>26</b> lithi: Itsho njalo inkosi: Fakani lo entolongweni, limdlise isinkwa senhlupho lamanzi enhlupho ngize ngibuye ngokuthula. <b>27</b> UMikhaya wasesithi: Uba ubuya lokubuya ngokuthula, iNkosi izabe ingakhulumanga ngami. Wathi: Lalelani, bantu lonke. 
<b>28</b> Ngakho inkosi yakoIsrayeli loJehoshafathi inkosi yakoJuda benyukela eRamothi-Gileyadi. <b>29</b> Inkosi yakoIsrayeli yasisithi kuJehoshafathi: Ngizazifihla isimo, ngingene empini, kodwa wena gqoka izembatho zakho. Inkosi yakoIsrayeli yasizifihla isimo, basebengena empini. <b>30</b> Inkosi yeSiriya yayilayile-ke izinduna zenqola eyayilazo isithi: Lingalwi lomncane loba omkhulu, kodwa lenkosi yakoIsrayeli kuphela. 
<b>31</b> Kwasekusithi induna zezinqola zibona uJehoshafathi zona zathi: Yena uyinkosi yakoIsrayeli. Ngakho zamhanqela ukulwa j. Kodwa uJehoshafathi wahlaba umkhosi; iNkosi yasimsiza k , l. UNkulunkulu wazihuga zasuka kuye m. <b>32</b> Ngoba kwathi lapho induna zezinqola zibona ukuthi kwakungayisiyo inkosi yakoIsrayeli, zaphenduka ekuyilandeleni. 
<b>33</b> Umuntu othile wasedonsa idandili engananzelele, wahlaba inkosi yakoIsrayeli phakathi kwenhlangano lebhatshi lensimbi; yasisithi kumtshayeli wenqola: Phendula isandla sakho, ungikhuphe ebuthweni, ngoba ngilimele. <b>34</b> Impi yasikhula mhlalokho; inkosi yayisekelwe enqoleni iqondene lamaSiriya kwaze kwaba ntambama; yafa ngesikhathi sokutshona kwelanga. 
<b>2 Crónicas 19</b><br><b>1</b> UJehoshafathi inkosi yakoJuda wasebuyela endlini yakhe ngokuthula eJerusalema a. <b>2</b> UJehu indodana kaHanani b umboni wasephuma ukumhlangabeza c, wathi enkosini uJehoshafathi: Unganceda yini omubi, uthande labo abazonda iNkosi d? Ngenxa yalokhu-ke kulolaka phezu kwakho oluvela ebusweni beNkosi e. <b>3</b> Loba kunjalo izinto ezinhle zitholwe kuwe f, ngoba ususile izixuku elizweni g, walungisa inhliziyo yakho ukumdinga uNkulunkulu h. 
<b>4</b> UJehoshafathi wasehlala eJerusalema; wabuya waphuma wahamba phakathi kwabantu, kusukela eBherishebha i kusiya entabeni yakoEfrayimi, wababuyisela eNkosini, uNkulunkulu waboyise j. UJehoshafathi umisa abahluleli <b>5</b> Wasemisa abahluleli elizweni k kuyo yonke imizi ebiyelweyo yakoJuda, kumuzi ngomuzi l. <b>6</b> Wasesithi kubahluleli: Bonani elikwenzayo, ngoba kalehluleleli umuntu kodwa iNkosi, elani endabeni yokwahlulela m. 
<b>7</b> Ngakho-ke uvalo ngeNkosi kalube phezu kwenu, linanzelele likwenze, ngoba eNkosini uNkulunkulu wethu kakulabubi n lokukhangela ubuso lokwemukela izivalamlomo o. <b>8</b> Futhi leJerusalema uJehoshafathi wasemisa abanye kumaLevi labapristi lakunhloko zaboyise zakoIsrayeli p kusahlulelo seNkosi lakumibango, sebebuyele eJerusalema q. <b>9</b> Wasebalaya esithi: Lizakwenza njalo ekuyesabeni iNkosi r, ngobuqotho langenhliziyo epheleleyo s. 
<b>10</b> Lawuphi umbango oza kini ovela kubafowenu abahlala emizini yabo, phakathi kwegazi legazi, phakathi komthetho lomlayo, izimiso lezahlulelo t, lizabaxwayisa ukuze bangabi lecala eNkosini u, lolaka lwehlele phezu kwenu laphezu kwabafowenu v. Lizakwenza njalo, futhi lingabi lacala w. <b>11</b> Khangelani-ke, uAmariya umpristi oyinhloko uphezu kwenu x kulo lonke udaba lukaJehova y, loZebhadiya indodana kaIshmayeli, umbusi wendlu yakoJuda, kulo lonke udaba lwenkosi; labaphathi amaLevi baphambi kwenu. Qinani likwenze; iNkosi-ke izakuba lolungileyo z. 
<b>2 Crónicas 20</b><br><b>1</b> Kwasekusithi emva kwalokho abantwana bakoMowabi a labantwana bakoAmoni b belabanye ngaphandle kwamaAmoni beza bamelana loJehoshafathi empini c. <b>2</b> Kwasekusiza abanye babikela uJehoshafathi besithi: Kuyeza ixuku elikhulu ukumelana lawe elivela phetsheya kolwandle, eSiriya; khangela-ke, liseHazazoni-Tamari d, eyiEngedi e. <b>3</b> UJehoshafathi wasesesaba, wamisa ubuso bakhe ukudinga iNkosi f, wamemezela ukuzila ukudla kuye wonke uJuda g. 
<b>4</b> UJuda wasezibuthanisa ukudinga eNkosini; lasemizini yonke yakoJuda baphuma beza ukudinga iNkosi. <b>5</b> UJehoshafathi wasesima ebandleni lakoJuda leJerusalema endlini yeNkosi phambi kweguma elitsha. <b>6</b> Wasesithi: Nkosi, Nkulunkulu wabobaba, wena kawusuye yini uNkulunkulu emazulwini h? Yebo, kawubusi yini kuyo yonke imibuso yezizwe? Lesandleni sakho kukhona amandla lengalo, ukuze kungabi khona ongamelana lawe i. 
<b>7</b> KawusiNkulunkulu wethu yini j? Uxotshile abahlali balelilizwe elifeni phambi kwabantu bakho uIsrayeli, walinika inzalo kaAbrahama k umngane wakho kuze kube phakade l. <b>8</b> Basebehlala kilo, bakwakhela indawo engcwele kulo eyebizo lakho, besithi: <b>9</b> Uba kusehlela phezu kwethu ububi, inkemba, isahlulelo, loba umatshayabhuqe wesifo, loba indlala m, sizakuma phambi kwalindlu laphambi kwakho n (ngoba ibizo lakho likulindlu o), sikhale kuwe ekubandezelekeni kwethu, uzakuzwa-ke usindise. 
<b>10</b> Ngakho-ke, khangela, abantwana bakoAmoni labakoMowabi p labentabeni iSeyiri q ongavumanga ukuthi uIsrayeli adlule phakathi kwabo lapho besuka elizweni leGibhithe r, kodwa babafulathela kabababhubhisanga s; <b>11</b> khangela-ke, basivuza beza ukuzasixotsha elifeni lakho owenze ukuthi silidle t. <b>12</b> Nkulunkulu wethu, kawuyikubahlulela yini u? Ngoba amandla kawakho kithi okumelana lalelixuku elikhulu elize ukumelana lathi; njalo thina kasikwazi esingakwenza, kodwa amehlo ethu akuwe v. 
<b>13</b> UJuda wonke wasesima phambi kweNkosi, lensane zabo, omkabo labantwana babo. <b>14</b> Khona uJahaziyeli indodana kaZekhariya indodana kaBhenaya indodana kaJeyiyeli indodana kaMathaniya, umLevi emadodaneni kaAsafi w, uMoya weNkosi wafika phezu kwakhe phakathi kwebandla x. <b>15</b> Wasesithi: Lalelani lonke bakoJuda labahlali beJerusalema, lawe nkosi Jehoshafathi; itsho njalo iNkosi kini: Lina lingesabi lingatshaywa luvalo ngenxa yalelixuku elikhulu y, ngoba impi kayisiyo yenu, kodwa ngekaNkulunkulu z. 
<b>16</b> Kusasa yehlani limelane labo; khangela, bayenyuka ngomqanso weZizi; lizabathola ekucineni kwesihotsha phambi kwenkangala yeJeruweli. <b>17</b> Kakusikwenu ukulwa kule impi; zimiseni lime, libone usindiso lweNkosi lani aa, Juda leJerusalema. Lingesabi lingatshaywa luvalo ab; kusasa phumani limelene labo, ngoba iNkosi izakuba lani ac. <b>18</b> UJehoshafathi wasekhothamisa ikhanda emhlabathini, laye wonke uJuda labahlali beJerusalema bawa phambi kweNkosi, beyikhonza iNkosi ad. 
<b>19</b> AmaLevi kubantwana bakoKohathi ae lakubantwana bakoKora asesukuma ukudumisa iNkosi af, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, ngelizwi elikhulu eliphezulu. <b>20</b> Basebevuka ekuseni kakhulu, baphuma baya enkangala yeThekhowa ag; lekuphumeni kwabo uJehoshafathi wema wathi: Ngilalelani Juda labahlali beJerusalema. Kholwani eNkosini uNkulunkulu wenu, lizaqiniswa; likholwe kubaprofethi bayo, lizaphumelela ah. 
<b>21</b> Esecebisene labantu wamisela iNkosi abahlabeleli, abazadumisa ubuhle bobungcwele ai, ekuphumeni phambi kwabahlomileyo, besithi: Dumisani iNkosi, ngoba umusa wayo umi kuze kube nininini aj. <b>22</b> Kwathi lapho beqalisa ukuhlabelela lokudumisa, iNkosi yabeka abacathameli ak ukumelana labantwana bakoAmoni al, uMowabi, lentaba iSeyiri, ababezemelana loJuda; basebetshaywa. 
<b>23</b> Ngoba abantwana bakoAmoni labakoMowabi basukuma bamelana labahlali bentaba yeSeyiri ukutshabalalisa lokuchitha am; njalo sebebaqedile abahlali beSeyiri, bancedisa ngulowo umakhelwane wakhe encithakalweni an. <b>24</b> UJuda esefikile enqabeni yokulinda enkangala bakhangela ngasexukwini, khangela-ke, kwakulezidumbu eziwele emhlabathini; njalo kungekho ophephileyo. 
<b>25</b> UJehoshafathi labantu bakhe sebefikile ukuphanga impango bathola phakathi kwabo ngobunengi, impahla lazo, lezidumbu, lezinto eziligugu, baziphangela baze behluleka ukuzithwala. Njalo baba lensuku ezintathu bephanga impango ngoba yayinengi ao. <b>26</b> Njalo ngosuku lwesine babuthana esihotsheni seBeraka, ngoba lapho bayibusisa iNkosi; ngenxa yalokho babiza ibizo laleyondawo ngokuthi yisihotsha seBeraka kuze kube lamuhla. 
<b>27</b> Basebebuyela, wonke umuntu wakoJuda leJerusalema, loJehoshafathi enhlokweni yabo, ukuthi babuyele eJerusalema belentokozo, ngoba iNkosi yabathokozisa phezu kwezitha zabo ap. <b>28</b> Basebengena eJerusalema belezigubhu zezintambo lamachacho lezimpondo, baya endlini yeNkosi aq. <b>29</b> Uvalo ngoNkulunkulu lwaselusiba phezu kwayo yonke imibuso yamazwe lapho besizwa ukuthi iNkosi yalwa lezitha zikaIsrayeli ar.

<b>15</b> Wasesusa onkulunkulu bezizweni n lesithombe endlini yeNkosi, lawo wonke amalathi ayewakhile entabeni yendlu yeNkosi, laseJerusalema, wakuphosela ngaphandle komuzi o. <b>16</b> Wasesakha ilathi leNkosi, wahlabela phezu kwalo iminikelo yokuthula leminikelo yokubonga. Wathi kuJuda kakhonze iNkosi, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli p. <b>17</b> Loba kunjalo abantu babelokhu behlaba endaweni eziphakemeyo, kuphela eNkosini uNkulunkulu wabo q. 
<b>18</b> Ezinye-ke zezindaba zikaManase, lomkhuleko wakhe kuNkulunkulu wakhe r, lamazwi ababoni abawakhuluma kuye ngebizo leNkosi uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli s, khangela, kusezindabeni zamakhosi akoIsrayeli t. <b>19</b> Umkhuleko wakhe-ke u, lokuncenga kwakhe kuNkulunkulu, lesono sakhe sonke, lesiphambeko sakhe v, lezindawo akha kuzo indawo eziphakemeyo, njalo wamisa izixuku lezithombe ezibaziweyo w, engakazithobi x, khangela, kubhaliwe emazwini ababoni y. 
<b>20</b> UManase waselala laboyise; basebemngcwaba endlini yakhe. UAmoni indodana yakhe wasesiba yinkosi esikhundleni sakhe z. Ukubusa kukaAmoni <b>21</b> UAmoni wayeleminyaka engamatshumi amabili lambili esiba yinkosi; wasebusa iminyaka emibili eJerusalema aa. <b>22</b> Wasesenza okubi emehlweni eNkosi njengokwenza kukaManase uyise; ngoba uAmoni wahlabela zonke izithombe ezibaziweyo uManase uyise ayezenzile, wazikhonza ab. <b>23</b> Kodwa kazithobanga phambi kweNkosi njengokuzithoba kukaManase uyise ac, kodwa uAmoni lo wandisa icala. 
<b>24</b> Inceku zakhe zasezimenzela ugobe, zambulala endlini yakhe. <b>25</b> Kodwa abantu belizwe batshaya bonke labo ababenzele inkosi uAmoni ugobe; abantu belizwe basebebeka uJosiya indodana yakhe ukuba yinkosi esikhundleni sakhe. 
<b>2 Crónicas 34</b><br><b>1</b> UJosiya wayeleminyaka eyisificaminwembili esiba yinkosi; wasebusa iminyaka engamatshumi amathathu lanye eJerusalema. <b>2</b> Wasesenza okuqondileyo emehlweni eNkosi, wahamba ngezindlela zikaDavida uyise, kaphambukelanga ngakwesokunene loba kwesokhohlo a. 
<b>3</b> Ngoba ngomnyaka wesificaminwembili wokubusa kwakhe esesengumfana waqala ukumdinga uNkulunkulu kaDavida uyise b; langomnyaka wetshumi lambili waqala ukuhlambulula uJuda leJerusalema kuzindawo eziphakemeyo, lezixuku, lezithombe ezibaziweyo c, lezithombe ezibunjwe ngokuncibilikisa d. 
<b>4</b> Basebediliza amalathi aboBhali phambi kwakhe e, lamalathi okutshisela impepha ayengaphezu kwawo wawagamulela phansi, lezixuku lezithombe ezibaziweyo lezithombe ezibunjwe ngokuncibilikisa waziphahlaza, wazichola, wazichithela phezu kwamangcwaba alabo ababenikele imihlatshelo kizo f. <b>5</b> Wasetshisela amathambo abapristi phezu kwamalathi abo, wahlambulula uJuda leJerusalema g. <b>6</b> Lemizini yakoManase loEfrayimi loSimeyoni kuze kufike koNafithali lemanxiweni ayo inhlangothi zonke h 
<b>7</b> wadiliza lamalathi, lezixuku, wachola izithombe ezibaziweyo zaze zaba yimpuphu i, wagamulela phansi wonke amalathi okutshisela impepha elizweni lonke lakoIsrayeli, wasebuyela eJerusalema j. UJosiya lencwadi yomlayo <b>8</b> Kwathi ngomnyaka wetshumi lesificaminwembili wokubusa kwakhe, esehlambulule ilizwe lendlu k, wathuma uShafani indodana kaAzaliya l loMahaseya m umbusi womuzi n loJowa indodana kaJowahazi umabhalane ukuthi baqinise indlu yeNkosi uNkulunkulu wakhe o. 
<b>9</b> Basebefika kuHilikhiya umpristi omkhulu p, bapha imali eyayilethwe endlini kaNkulunkulu, amaLevi ayelondoloza umbundu ayeyiqoqe esandleni sakoManase loEfrayimi lakuyo yonke insali kaIsrayeli lakuye wonke uJuda loBhenjamini; basebebuyela eJerusalema q. <b>10</b> Basebeyinikela esandleni sabenzi bomsebenzi ababekhethelwe indlu yeNkosi abayipha abenzi bomsebenzi ababesebenza endlini yeNkosi ukuthi balungise baqinise indlu. 
<b>11</b> Bayinika ababazi labakhi ukuze bathenge amatshe abaziweyo lezigodo zentungo lezokufulela izindlu amakhosi akoJuda ayezidilizile r. <b>12</b> Amadoda asesebenza ngobuqotho emsebenzini; lababonisi phezu kwawo babengoJahathi loObhadiya amaLevi, ebantwaneni bakoMerari s, loZekhariya loMeshulamu t, ebantwaneni bamaKohathi u, ukuqondisa; lamaLevi, wonke aqedisisa ngezinto zokuhlabelela v. 
<b>13</b> Njalo ayephezu kwabathwali bemithwalo w njalo babengababonisi phezu kwakhe wonke owenza umsebenzi wenkonzo ngenkonzo x; lakumaLevi kwakulababhali y labaphathi labagcini bamasango z. <b>14</b> Kwasekusithi bekhupha imali eyayilethwe endlini yeNkosi, uHilikhiya umpristi wafica ugwalo lomlayo weNkosiowawunikwe ngesandla sikaMozisi. <b>15</b> UHilikhiya wasephendula wathi kuShafani umabhalane: Ngifice ugwalo lomlayo endlini yeNkosi. UHilikhiya wasenika uShafani ugwalo. 
<b>16</b> UShafani waseletha ugwalo enkosini, futhi wabuyisela ilizwi enkosini esithi: Konke okwakunikelwe esandleni senceku zakho ziyakwenza. <b>17</b> Seziyiqoqile imali eyatholakala endlini yeNkosi, zayinikela esandleni sabaphathi lesandleni sabenzi bomsebenzi. <b>18</b> UShafani umabhalane wasetshela inkosi esithi: UHilikhiya umpristi unginike ugwalo. UShafani wasefunda kulo phambi kwenkosi. <b>19</b> Kwasekusithi inkosi isizwile amazwi omlayo yadabula izigqoko zayo. 
<b>20</b> Inkosi yasilaya oHilikhiya loAhikhamu indodana kaShafani loAbidoni indodana kaMika loShafani umabhalane loAsaya inceku yenkosi, isithi: <b>21</b> Hambani liyebuza eNkosini ngenxa yami langenxa yensali ekoIsrayeli lakoJuda mayelana lamazwi ogwalo olutholakeleyo; ngoba lukhulu ulaka lweNkosi oluthululelwe phezu kwethu, ngenxa yokuthi obaba kabaligcinanga ilizwi leNkosi ukwenza njengakho konke okubhaliweyo kulolugwalo aa. 
<b>22</b> Ngakho uHilikhiya lalabo ababengabenkosi baya kuHulida umprofethikazi, umkaShaluma indodana kaTokhathi indodana kaHasira, umgcini wezigqoko. (Yena-ke wayehlala eJerusalema esiGabeni seSibili.) Basebekhuluma kuye kanje. <b>23</b> Wasesithi kibo: Itsho njalo iNkosi uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli: Tshelani umuntu olithume kimi: <b>24</b> Itsho njalo iNkosi: Khangela, ngizakwehlisela ububi kulindawo laphezu kwabahlali bayo, zonke iziqalekiso ezibhaliweyo egwalweni abalubalileyo phambi kwenkosi yakoJuda. 
<b>25</b> Ngenxa yokuthi bengitshiyile, batshisela abanye onkulunkulu impepha, ukuze bangithukuthelise ngayo yonke imisebenzi yezandla zabo; ngakho ulaka lwami luzathululelwa phezu kwalindawo, kaluyikucitshwa ab. <b>26</b> Kodwa enkosini yakoJuda elithumileyo ukubuza eNkosini, lizakutsho njalo kuyo: Itsho njalo iNkosi uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli: Mayelana lamazwi owazwileyo, 
<b>27</b> ngoba inhliziyo yakho ibuthakathaka, wazithoba phambi kukaNkulunkulu usuzwe amazwi akhe amelene lalindawo lamelene labahlali bayo, wazithoba phambi kwami, wadabula izigqoko zakho, wakhala inyembezi phambi kwami, ngitsho lami ngizwile, itsho iNkosi. <b>28</b> Khangela, ngizakubuthela kuboyihlo, njalo uzabuthelwa emangcwabeni akho ngokuthula, lamehlo akho kawayikubona konke okubi engizakwehlisela kulindawo laphezu kwabahlali bayo. Basebebuyisela ilizwi enkosini. <b>29</b> Inkosi yasithuma yaqoqa bonke abadala bakoJuda labeJerusalema. 
<b>30</b> Inkosi yasisenyukela endlini kaJehova, kanye labantu bonke bakoJuda labahlali beJerusalema labapristi lamaLevi, labo bonke abantu kusukela komkhulu kusiya komncinyane; yasibala endlebeni zabo wonke amazwi ogwalo lwesivumelwano olwalutholakele endlini kaJehova ac. <b>31</b> Inkosi yasisima endaweni yayo, yenza isivumelwano phambi kukaJehova ukulandela uJehova, lokugcina imilayo yakhe lezifakazelo zakhe lezimiso zakhe ngayo yonke inhliziyo yayo langawo wonke umphefumulo wayo, ukwenza amazwi esivumelwano abhalwe kulolugwalo ad. 
<b>32</b> Yasibamisa bonke abatholakala eJerusalema loBhenjamini. Labahlali beJerusalema benza njengokwesivumelwano sikaNkulunkulu, uNkulunkulu waboyise ae. <b>33</b> UJosiya wasesusa zonke izinengiso kuwo wonke amazwe ayengawabantwana bakoIsrayeli, wenza wonke owaficwa koIsrayeli ukuthi akhonze, akhonze iNkosi uNkulunkulu wabo. Insuku zakhe zonke kabaphambukanga ekuyilandeleni iNkosi, uNkulunkulu waboyise. 
<b>2 Crónicas 35</b><br><b>1</b> UJosiya wasesenzela iNkosi iphasika eJerusalema a; basebehlaba iphasika ngolwetshumi lane lwenyanga yokuqala b. <b>2</b> Wasemisa abapristi kumfanelo zabo, wabaqinisela inkonzo yendlu yeNkosi c. <b>3</b> Wasesithi kumaLevi ayefundisa uIsrayeli wonke ayengcwele eNkosini d: Fakani umtshokotsho ongcwele e endlini uSolomoni indodana kaDavida inkosi yakoIsrayeli ayakhayo f; kakusengumthwalo kini emahlombe. Khathesi khonzani iNkosi uNkulunkulu wenu labantu bayo uIsrayeli g. 
<b>4</b> Njalo lizilungise ngokwendlu yaboyihlo ngezigaba zenu h, njengombhalo kaDavida inkosi yakoIsrayeli i lanjengombhalo kaSolomoni indodana yakhe j. <b>5</b> Lime endaweni engcwele k ngokwezigaba zendlu yaboyise babafowenu, abantwana besizwe, lesigaba sendlu kayise wamaLevi l. <b>6</b> Lihlabe iphasika, lizingcwelise, lilungisele abafowenu, ukwenza njengokwelizwi leNkosi ngesandla sikaMozisi m. 
<b>7</b> UJosiya wasenika abantwana babantu umhlambi wamawundlu lamazinyane embuzi, konke kungokwamaphasika n, kwabo bonke abatholakalayo, ngenani eliyizinkulungwane ezingamatshumi amathathu, lenkunzi ezizinkulungwane ezintathu; lokhu kwakuvela empahleni yenkosi o. <b>8</b> Iziphathamandla zakhe zasezinika abantu, abapristi lamaLevi ngokukhululeka. OHilikhiya p loZekhariya loJehiyeli q, izinduna zendlu yeNkosi, banikelela amaphasika r kubapristi amazinyane azinkulungwane ezimbili lamakhulu ayisithupha, lenkunzi ezingamakhulu amathathu s. 
<b>9</b> OKonaniya t loShemaya loNethaneli, abafowabo u, loHashabhiya loJeyiyeli loJozabadi, izinduna zamaLevi, basebenikelela amaphasika v kumaLevi amazinyane azinkulungwane ezinhlanu, lenkunzi ezingamakhulu amahlanu w. <b>10</b> Ngokunjalo inkonzo yalungiswa; abapristi basebesima endaweni yabo x, lamaLevi ngezigaba zawo, njengokomlayo wenkosi y. <b>11</b> Basebehlaba iphasika z, abapristi bafafaza kuvela ezandleni zabo aa, lamaLevi ahlinza ab. 
<b>12</b> Basebesusa iminikelo yokutshiswa ukuyinika njengokwezigaba zendlu yaboyise kubantwana babantu ukuze banikele eNkosini, njengokubhaliweyo egwalweni lukaMozisi. Ngokunjalo langezinkunzi. <b>13</b> Basebesosa iphasika ngomlilo njengokwesimiso ac; kodwa izinto ezingcwele bazipheka ad ngamabhodo ae langezimbiza af langezindengezi ag, bakusa masinyane kubo bonke abantwana babantu. 
<b>14</b> Lemva kwalokho basebezilungisela bona labapristi, ngoba abapristi amadodana kaAroni babesekunikeleni iminikelo yokutshiswa lamahwahwa kwaze kwaba sebusuku ah; ngakho amaLevi azilungisela wona labapristi amadodana kaAroni. <b>15</b> Labahlabeleli amadodana kaAsafi babesendaweni yabo njengokomlayo kaDavida ai, loAsafi, loHemani, loJeduthuni umboni wenkosi aj; labagcini bamasango babesesangweni lonke ak; kwakungesikwabo ukusuka enkonzweni yabo, ngoba abafowabo amaLevi abalungisela al. 
<b>16</b> Yasilungiswa yonke inkonzo yeNkosi ngalolosuku ukwenza iphasika lokunikela iminikelo yokutshiswa phezu kwelathi leNkosi, njengokomlayo wenkosi uJosiya. <b>17</b> Abantwana bakoIsrayeli ababekhona basebesenza iphasika ngalesosikhathi, lomkhosi wesinkwa esingelamvubelo, insuku eziyisikhombisa am. 
<b>18</b> Njalo kalenziwanga iphasika elinjengaleli an koIsrayeli kusukela ensukwini zikaSamuweli umprofethi ao; lawo wonke amakhosi akoIsrayeli kawenzanga iphasika elinje uJosiya alenzayo, labapristi lamaLevi, loJuda loIsrayeli wonke ababekhona, labahlali beJerusalema. <b>19</b> Ngomnyaka wetshumi lesificaminwembili wokubusa kukaJosiya iphasika leli lenziwa ap. Ukufa kukaJosiya <b>20</b> Emva kwakho konke lokho, uJosiya eseyilungisile indlu, uNeko inkosi yeGibhithe aq wenyuka ukuyakulwa leKarikemeshi eYufrathi ar; loJosiya waphuma ukuyamelana laye. 
<b>21</b> Kodwa wathuma izithunywa kuye esithi: Ngilani lawe, nkosi yakoJuda? Kangenyukelanga ukumelana lawe wena lamuhla, kodwa ngimelene lendlu elwa lami as. Ngoba uNkulunkulu uthe ngiphangise; yekela wena ukuphikisana loNkulunkulu, olami, ukuze angakubhubhisi at. <b>22</b> Kodwa uJosiya kaphendulanga ubuso bakhe kuye au, kodwa wazifihla isimo ukuze alwe laye av; njalo kawalalelanga amazwi kaNeko avela emlonyeni kaNkulunkulu aw, kodwa weza ukulwa esihotsheni ax seMegido ay. 
<b>23</b> Abatshoki bemitshoko basebemciba uJosiya; inkosi yasisithi encekwini zayo: Ngisusani ngoba ngizwa ubuhlungu obukhulu. <b>24</b> Inceku zakhe zasezimkhupha enqoleni, zamgadisa enqoleni yesibili ayelayo, zamletha eJerusalema. Wasesifa, wangcwatshelwa emangcwabeni aboyise az; wonke uJuda leJerusalema basebemlilela uJosiya ba. <b>25</b> LoJeremiya wenza isililo ngoJosiya bb. Labo bonke abahlabeleli labahlabelelikazi bakhuluma ngoJosiya ezililweni zabo kuze kube lamuhla bc. Bazenza zaba yisimiso koIsrayeli; khangela-ke, zibhaliwe ezililweni bd. 
<b>26</b> Ezinye-ke zezindaba zikaJosiya, lezisa zakhe njengokubhaliweyo emlayweni weNkosi, <b>27</b> izindaba zakhe-ke, ezokuqala lezokucina, khangela, zibhaliwe egwalweni lwamakhosi akoIsrayeli lawakoJuda be. 
<b>2 Crónicas 36</b><br><b>1</b> Abantu belizwe basebethatha uJehowahazi indodana kaJosiya, bambeka waba yinkosi esikhundleni sikayise eJerusalema a. <b>2</b> UJehowahazi wayeleminyaka engamatshumi amabili lantathu esiba yinkosi; wasebusa inyanga ezintathu eJerusalema. <b>3</b> Inkosi yeGibhithe yasimsusa eseJerusalema, yahlawulisa ilizwe amathalenta* esiliva alikhulu lethalenta legolide. 
<b>4</b> Inkosi yeGibhithe yasibeka uEliyakhimi umfowabo abe yinkosi phezu kukaJuda leJerusalema, yaguqula ibizo lakhe yathi nguJehoyakhimi. UNeko wasethatha uJehowahazi umfowabo wamletha eGibhithe. Ukubusa kukaJehoyakhimi <b>5</b> UJehoyakhimi wayeleminyaka engamatshumi amabili lanhlanu esiba yinkosi; wasebusa iminyaka elitshumi lanye eJerusalema. Wenza okubi emehlweni eNkosi uNkulunkulu wakhe b. <b>6</b> Kwenyukela kuye uNebhukadinezari inkosi yeBhabhiloni c, wambopha ngamaketane ethusi, ukumusa eBhabhiloni d. 
<b>7</b> UNebhukadinezari waseletha okwezitsha zendlu yeNkosi eBhabhiloni, wakufaka ethempelini lakhe eBhabhiloni e. <b>8</b> Ezinye-ke zezindaba zikaJehoyakhimi, lamanyala akhe awenzayo, lokwatholakala kuye, khangela, kubhaliwe egwalweni lwamakhosi akoIsrayeli loJuda f. UJehoyakhini indodana yakhe wasesiba yinkosi esikhundleni sakhe. UJehoyakhini uthunjelwa eBhabhiloni <b>9</b> UJehoyakhini g wayeleminyaka eyisificaminwembili esiba yinkosi; wasebusa inyanga ezintathu lensuku ezilitshumi eJerusalema. Wenza okubi emehlweni eNkosi h. 
<b>10</b> Lekuthwaseni komnyaka inkosi uNebhukadinezari i yathuma yamletha eBhabhiloni, kanye lezitsha eziloyisekayo zendlu yeNkosi j; yasibeka uZedekhiya umfowabo abe yinkosi phezu kukaJuda leJerusalema k. Ukubusa kukaZedekhiya <b>11</b> UZedekhiya wayeleminyaka engamatshumi amabili lanye esiba yinkosi; wasebusa iminyaka elitshumi lanye eJerusalema l. <b>12</b> Wasesenza okubi emehlweni eNkosi uNkulunkulu wakhe; kazithobanga phambi kukaJeremiya umprofethi, ekhuluma okomlomo weNkosi m. 
<b>13</b> Laye wasevukela inkosi uNebhukadinezari n eyayimfungise ngoNkulunkulu o, wenza yaba lukhuni intamo yakhe p, waqinisa inhliziyo yakhe, okokuthi kaphendukelanga eNkosini, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli q. <b>14</b> Futhi zonke induna zabapristi labantu banda ekuphambukeni ngeziphambeko njengawo wonke amanyala ezizwe, bayingcolisa indlu yeNkosi eyayiyingcwelisile eJerusalema r. <b>15</b> INkosi, uNkulunkulu waboyise, yasithuma kubo ngesandla sezithunywa zayo s, ivuka ngovivi ithuma, ngoba yayilesihawu ebantwini bayo lendaweni yayo yokuhlala. 
<b>16</b> Kodwa babezihleka usulu izithunywa zikaNkulunkulu t, bedelela amazwi akhe u, beklolodela abaprofethi bakhe v, lwaze lwavuka ulaka lweNkosi lwamelana labantu bayo kakwaze kwaba leselapho w. <b>17</b> Ngakho yabenyusela inkosi yamaKhaladiya x eyabulala amajaha abo ngenkemba endlini yendlu yabo engcwele, ingahawukeli ijaha lentombi, omdala lexhegu y; yabanikela bonke esandleni sayo z. 
<b>18</b> Lazo zonke izitsha zendlu kaNkulunkulu, ezinkulu lezincinyane, lezinto eziligugu zendlu kaJehova, lezinto eziligugu zenkosi lezeziphathamandla zayo, konke lokhu yakuletha eBhabhiloni aa. <b>19</b> Basebetshisa indlu kaNkulunkulu, badiliza umduli weJerusalema ab, batshisa zonke izigodlo zayo ngomlilo ac, batshabalalisa zonke izitsha zayo eziloyisekayo ad. <b>20</b> Yasithumbela eBhabhiloni abasele enkembeni ae; basebesiba zinceku zayo lezabantwana bayo af kwaze kwaba yikubusa kombuso wePerisiya ag; 
<b>21</b> ukugcwalisa ilizwi leNkosi ngomlomo kaJeremiya ah, ilizwe lize likholise amasabatha alo ai; lagcina isabatha zonke izinsuku zencithakalo, kwaze kwagcwaliseka iminyaka engamatshumi ayisikhombisa aj. Isimemezelo sikaKoresi <b>22</b> Kwathi ngomnyaka wokuqala kaKoresi inkosi yePerisiya, ekugcwalisekeni kwelizwi leNkosi ngomlomo kaJeremiya, iNkosi yavusa umoya kaKoresi inkosi yePerisiya ukuthi edlulise isimemezelo embusweni wakhe wonke, njalo langombhalo, esithi: 
<b>23</b> Utsho njalo uKoresi inkosi yePerisiya: INkosi, uNkulunkulu wamazulu, inginikile yonke imibuso yomhlaba; layo ingilayile ukuyakhela indlu eJerusalema ekoJuda. Ngubani ophakathi kwenu kubo bonke abantu bayo? INkosi uNkulunkulu wakhe kayibe laye, enyuke ak.
