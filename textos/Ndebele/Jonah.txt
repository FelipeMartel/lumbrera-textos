<b>Jonah 1</b><br><b>1</b> Ilizwi leNkosi laselifika kuJona indodana kaAmithayi, lisithi a: <b>2</b> Sukuma, uye eNineve b , c, umuzi omkhulu d, umemeze umelene lawo; ngoba ububi babo e benyukele phambi kwami f. <b>3</b> Kodwa uJona wasukuma ukuze abalekele g eTarshishi esuka ebusweni beNkosi h, wehlela eJopha, wathola umkhumbi oya eTarshishi i, wanika imali yawo, wehlela kuwo, ukuze ahambe labo eTarshishi j, asuke ebusweni beNkosi k. 
<b>4</b> Kodwa iNkosi yaphosa umoya omkhulu elwandle l; kwasekusiba lesiphepho esikhulu elwandle, okungangokuthi umkhumbi wacatshangelwa ukwephuka m. <b>5</b> Abatshayeli bomkhumbi basebesesaba n, bakhala, ngulowo lalowo kunkulunkulu wakhe o, baphosela olwandle izimpahla ezazisemkhunjini p, ukuwenza ube lula kuzo q. Kodwa uJona wayehlele emaceleni omkhumbi, walala, waba lobuthongo obukhulu r. 
<b>6</b> Ngakho induna yomkhumbi yasondela kuye s, yathi kuye: Ulani wena t olobuthongo obukhulu u? Vuka, ubize kuNkulunkulu wakho v; mhlawumbe lowoNkulunkulu w angasikhumbula, ukuze singabhubhi x. <b>7</b> Basebesithi, ngulowo kumngane wakhe: Wozani senze inkatho yokuphosa y, ukuze sazi ukuthi kungenxa kabani lobububi buphezu kwethu. Basebesenza inkatho yokuphosa; inkatho yasiwela phezu kukaJona. 
<b>8</b> Basebesithi kuye: Akusitshele ukuthi kungenxa kabani lobububi buphezu kwethu z. Umsebenzi wakho uyini? Njalo uvela ngaphi? Yiliphi ilizwe lakini? Njalo ungowasiphi isizwe? <b>9</b> Wasesithi kubo: NgingumHebheru, ngiyayesaba iNkosi aa, uNkulunkulu wamazulu, owenza ulwandle lomhlabathi owomileyo ab. <b>10</b> Amadoda asesesaba ngokwesaba okukhulu, athi kuye: Ukwenzeleni lokhu? Ngoba amadoda akwazi ukuthi ubalekile ebusweni beNkosi, ngoba wayesewatshelile ac. 
<b>11</b> Asesithi kuye: Senzeni kuwe ukuze ulwandle luthule kithi? Ngoba ulwandle lwaqhubeka lwaba lesiphepho esikhulu. <b>12</b> Wasesithi kiwo: Ngiphakamisani, lingiphosele olwandle; ngalokho ulwandle luzalithulela; ngoba ngiyazi ukuthi lesisiphepho esikhulu siphezu kwenu ngenxa yami. <b>13</b> Kodwa amadoda agwedla ngamandla ukuze awubuyisele umkhumbi emhlabathini owomileyo, kodwa ehluleka, ngoba ulwandle lwaqhubeka lwaba lesiphepho lumelene lawo. 
<b>14</b> Ngakho akhala eNkosini, athi: Siyakuncenga, Nkosi ad, kasingabhubhi ngenxa yempilo yalumuntu, ungabeki phezu kwethu igazi elingelacala ae; ngoba wena, Nkosi, wenzile njengokukuthokozisayo af. <b>15</b> Asephakamisa uJona, amphosela elwandle; ulwandle lwaselusima ekuvubekeni kwalo ag. <b>16</b> Amadoda aseyesaba iNkosi ngokwesaba okukhulu ah, anikela umhlatshelo eNkosini ai , aj, afunga izifungo ak. 
<b>17</b> Njalo iNkosi yayimise inhlanzi enkulu ukuze imginye uJona al; njalo uJona waba semathunjini enhlanzi insuku ezintathu lobusuku obuthathu am. 
<b>Jonah 2</b><br><b>1</b> UJona wasekhuleka eNkosini uNkulunkulu wakhe esemathunjini enhlanzi, <b>2</b> wathi: Ekuhluphekeni kwami ngakhala eNkosini a, yangiphendula b; ngisesiswini sengcwaba ngamemeza, wena walizwa ilizwi lami c. <b>3</b> Ngoba wangiphosela enzikini, enhliziyweni yezinlwandle, lempophoma zangihanqa d; wonke amagagasi akho lamavinqo akho kwedlula phezu kwami e. <b>4</b> Ngasengisithi mina: Ngixotshiwe phambi kobuso bakho f; kanti ngizabuya ngikhangele g ngasethempelini lakho elingcwele h , i. 
<b>5</b> Amanzi j angihanqile k kuze kube semphefumulweni l; inziki ingizingelezele; imihlanga yathandela ikhanda lami m. <b>6</b> Ngehlela ezisekelweni zezintaba n; umhlaba, imigoqo yawo o yangihanqa laphakade p; kanti wenyuse impilo yami q emgodini, Nkosi Nkulunkulu wami r , s. <b>7</b> Lapho umphefumulo wami uphela amandla phakathi kwami ngakhumbula iNkosi t; lomkhuleko wami wafika kuwe u, ethempelini lakho elingcwele v , w. <b>8</b> Labo abagcina amanga x ayize batshiya y umusa wabo z. 
<b>9</b> Kodwa mina ngizakwenza umhlatshelo kuwe ngelizwi lokubonga aa, ngikhokhe lokho engikufungileyo ab , ac; usindiso ngolweNkosi ad. <b>10</b> INkosi yasikhuluma kuyo inhlanzi, yasimhlanza uJona emhlabathini owomileyo. 
<b>Jonah 3</b><br><b>1</b> Ilizwi leNkosi laselifika kuJona ngokwesibili, lisithi: <b>2</b> Sukuma, uye eNineve a, lowana umuzi omkhulu, utshumayele kuwo intshumayelo engikutshela yona. <b>3</b> UJona wasesukuma, waya eNineve, njengokwelizwi leNkosi. Phela iNineve b yayingumuzi omkhulu kakhulu, uhambo lwezinsuku ezintathu. <b>4</b> UJona waseqala ukungena emzini, uhambo losuku olulodwa. Wamemeza, wathi: Kusesezinsuku ezingamatshumi amane, khona iNineve izagenqulwa. 
<b>5</b> Abantu beNineve basebekholwa kuNkulunkulu c, bamemezela ukuzila ukudla d, bagqoka amasaka e, kusukela komkhulu wabo kwaze kwaba komncinyane wabo f. <b>6</b> Ngoba ilizwi lafinyelela enkosini yeNineve; yasisukuma esihlalweni sayo sobukhosi g, yakhulula isembatho sayo kuyo h, yazimbomboza ngesaka i , j, yahlala emlotheni k , l. <b>7</b> Yasimemezela m yathi kuyo iNineve ngomthetho wenkosi labakhulu bayo, isithi n: Kakungabi lomuntu, kumbe inyamazana, inkomo kumbe imvu, okuzanambitha loba yini; kakungadli, kakunganathi amanzi o. 
<b>8</b> Kodwa kakuthi umuntu lenyamazana kwembeswe amasaka p, bakhale kuNkulunkulu ngamandla, yebo, baphenduke, ngulowo lalowo endleleni yakhe embi q, lodlakeleni olusezandleni zabo r. <b>9</b> Ngubani owaziyo, uNkulunkulu angaphenduka s , t azisole u, aphenduke asuke kukuvutha kolaka lwakhe v, ukuze singabhubhi. <b>10</b> UNkulunkulu wasebona imisebenzi yabo, ukuthi baphendukile endleleni yabo embi w , x; uNkulunkulu wazisola ngobubi ayethe uzabenza kubo, akaze abenza y , z. 
<b>Jonah 4</b><br><b>1</b> Kodwa kwaba kubi kakhulukazi kuJona a, wathukuthela yena b. <b>2</b> Wasekhuleka eNkosini, wathi: Ngiyakuncenga, Nkosi, leli lalingesilo ilizwi lami ngiseselizweni lakithi yini? Ngenxa yalokhu ngandulela ngokubalekela eTarshishi c; ngoba ngangisazi ukuthi wena unguNkulunkulu olomusa d lolesihawu e , f, ophuza ukuthukuthela g, omkhulu ngothando, njalo ozisola ngokubi h. <b>3</b> Ngakho-ke, Nkosi, susa, ngiyakuncenga, impilo yami kimi i, ngoba kungcono kimi ukufa kulokuphila j. 
<b>4</b> INkosi yasisithi: Wenza kuhle yini ngokuthukuthela kwakho? <b>5</b> UJona wasephuma emzini, wahlala ngasempumalanga komuzi, wazenzela idumba khona, wahlala ngaphansi kwalo emthunzini, aze abone okuzakwenzakala kuwo umuzi k. <b>6</b> INkosi uNkulunkulu l yasimisa umhlafutho, yawukhulisa ngaphezu kukaJona, ukuze ube ngumthunzi phezu kwekhanda lakhe, ukumkhulula m kubuhlungu bakhe n. UJona wasethokoza ngenxa yomhlafutho ngentokozo enkulu. <b>7</b> Kodwa uNkulunkulu wamisa impehlane ekuveleni kokusa kusisa, yatshaya umhlafutho, waze wabuna o. 
<b>8</b> Kwasekusithi ekuphumeni kwelanga, uNkulunkulu wamisa umoya wempumalanga p otshisayo q; njalo ilanga latshaya phezu kwekhanda likaJona r, waze waphela amandla; wafisa emphefumulweni wakhe ukuthi afe s, wathi: Kungcono kimi ukufa kulokuphila t. <b>9</b> UNkulunkulu wasesithi kuJona: Wenza kuhle yini ngokuthukuthela kwakho ngenxa yomhlafutho? Wasesithi: Ngenza kuhle ngokuthukuthela kwami kuze kube sekufeni u. 
<b>10</b> INkosi yasisithi: Wena ube lozwelo phezu komhlafutho ongawutshikatshikelanga, ongawukhulisanga; owaba khona ngobusuku bunye, wabhubha ngobusuku bunye. <b>11</b> Mina-ke ngabe kangihawukeli yini iNineve v, umuzi omkhulu, okuthi kuwo kulabantu abedlula izinkulungwane ezilikhulu lamatshumi amabili abangaziyo ukwehlukanisa phakathi kwesandla sabo sokunene lesandla sabo sokhohlo w, kanye lenkomo ezinengi x?
