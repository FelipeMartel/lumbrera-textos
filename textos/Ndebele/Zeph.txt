<b>7</b> Thula ebukhoneni beNkosi uJehova y; ngoba usuku lweNkosi luseduze; ngoba iNkosi ilungisile umhlatshelo z, yangcwelisa abanxusiweyo bayo aa. <b>8</b> Kuzakuthi-ke ngosuku lomhlatshelo weNkosi ngijezise iziphathamandla ab, lamadodana enkosi ac, labo bonke abagqoke ad izigqoko zabezizwe ae. <b>9</b> Langalolosuku ngizajezisa bonke abeqa phezu kombundu af, abagcwalisa indlu yamakhosi abo ngodlakela langenkohliso. 
<b>10</b> Langalolosuku, itsho iNkosi, kuzakuba lelizwi lokukhala livela esangweni lenhlanzi ag, lokuqhinqa isililo ah kuvela kwelesibili ai, lokufohloza okukhulu kuvela emaqaqeni. <b>11</b> Qhinqani isililo, lina bahlali aj beMaketeshi ak, ngoba bonke abantu abathengisayo baqunyelwe phansi al; bonke abathwala isiliva baqunyiwe. <b>12</b> Kuzakuthi-ke ngalesosikhathi ngihlole iJerusalema am , an ngezibane ao, ngijezise amadoda ajiyileyo ezinzeceni zawo ap, athi enhliziyweni yawo: INkosi kayiyikwenza okuhle, futhi kayiyikwenza okubi aq. 
<b>13</b> Ngakho inotho yabo izakuba yimpango, lezindlu zabo zibe lunxiwa ar. Futhi bazakwakha izindlu, kodwa bangahlali kuzo as; njalo bazahlanyela izivini at, kodwa banganathi iwayini lazo au. <b>14</b> Usuku olukhulu lweNkosi av luseduze, luseduze, luyaphangisa kakhulu aw; ilizwi losuku lweNkosi; iqhawe ax lizakhala ay khona kabuhlungu az. <b>15</b> Lolosuku lusuku lolaka ba, usuku lokuhlupheka bb losizi bc, usuku lwencithakalo bd lokuchitheka be, usuku lomnyama lobumnyama bf, usuku lwamayezi lobumnyama obunzima bg , bh, 
<b>16</b> usuku lophondo bi lolomkhosi bj omelene lemizi ebiyelwe ngemithangala, lolumelene lezingonsi eziphakemeyo bk. <b>17</b> Njalo ngizabacindezela abantu bl, ukuze bahambe njengeziphofu bm, ngoba bonile eNkosini; legazi labo lizathululwa njengothuli bn, lenyama bo yabo njengobulongwe bp. <b>18</b> Lesiliva sabo legolide labo kakuyikuba lakho ukubakhulula bq ngosuku lolaka lweNkosi br; kodwa ilizwe lonke lizaqedwa bs ngumlilo bt wobukhwele bayo bu; ngoba izaqeda, ngitsho ngesiphangiphangi, ngabo bonke abahlali belizwe bv. 
<b>Zephaniah 2</b><br><b>1</b> Zibuthaniseni, lizibuthanise, lina sizwe esingaloyisekiyo a, <b>2</b> isimiso singakazali b, lalolosuku lwedlule njengamakhoba, intukuthelo yolaka lweNkosi ingakalehleli c, usuku lolaka lweNkosi lungakalehleli. <b>3</b> Dingani iNkosi d, lina lonke abamnene bomhlaba e, elenze isahlulelo sayo; dingani ukulunga, lidinge ubumnene; mhlawumbe f lingafihlwa ngosuku lolaka lweNkosi g. 
<b>4</b> Ngoba iGaza h , i , j izadelwa k, leAshikeloni ibe lunxiwa l , m , n; bazaxotsha iAshidodi o , p emini enkulu q, leEkhironi r , s isitshunwe. <b>5</b> Maye kwabakhileyo belizwe t lolwandle u, isizwe samaKerethi v! <b>7</b> Njalo ilizwe lizakuba ab ngelensali yendlu kaJuda ac; bazakudla kulo ad; kusihlwa bazalala ae ezindlini af zeAshikeloni ag; ngoba iNkosi uNkulunkulu wabo izabahambela ah, iphendule ukuthunjwa kwabo ai. <b>8</b> Ngiyizwile aj inhlamba kaMowabi ak, lokuthuka al kwamadodana kaAmoni am, abahlambaze ngakho abantu bami, bezikhulisa an bemelene lomngcele wabo ao. 
<b>9</b> Ngakho kuphila kwami ap, itsho iNkosi yamabandla, uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, isibili uMowabi uzakuba aq njengeSodoma ar, labantwana bakaAmoni as njengeGomora at, indawo yezimbabazane au, lemigodi yetshwayi av, lencithakalo kuze kube phakade aw. Insali yabantu bami izabaphanga ax, labaseleyo babantu bami badle ilifa labo ay. <b>10</b> Lokhu bazakuba lakho endaweni yokuziphakamisa kwabo az, ngoba bathuka, bazikhulisa bemelene labantu beNkosi yamabandla ba. 
<b>11</b> INkosi izakwesabeka kubo, ngoba izacakisa bonke onkulunkulu bomhlaba bb; njalo bazakhothama kuyo bc, ngulowo lalowo esendaweni yakhe bd, zonke izihlenge zezizwe be. <b>12</b> Lani maEthiyophiya bf, lizakuba ngababuleweyo bg ngenkemba yami bh. <b>13</b> Njalo izakwelula isandla sayo imelene lenyakatho, ichithe iAsiriya bi, ibeke iNineve bj ibe lunxiwa, yome njengenkangala bk. 
<b>14</b> Njalo imihlambi izalala phansi phakathi kwayo bl , bm, zonke izinyamazana zezizwe bn; lalo iwunkwe bo kanye lenhloni kulale ezidukwini zayo bp; ilizwi lizahlabela ewindini, incithakalo ibe sembundwini bq; ngoba ezakwembula br umsebenzi womsedari bs. <b>15</b> Lo ngumuzi ojabulayo bt, obuhlezi ngokuvikeleka bu, owawusithi enhliziyweni yawo: Yimi, njalo kakho omunye ngaphandle kwami bv , bw. Yeka ukuba kwawo yincithakalo, indawo yokulala yezinyamazana bx! Wonke owedlula kiwo by uyancifa bz , ca, anyikinye isandla sakhe cb. 
<b>Zephaniah 3</b><br><b>1</b> Maye kowenyanyekayo longcolileyo, umuzi ocindezelayo a! <b>2</b> Kawulalelanga ilizwi b; kawemukelanga ukulaywa c; kawuthembelanga eNkosini; kawusondelanga kuNkulunkulu wawo d. <b>3</b> Iziphathamandla zawo phakathi kwawo e , f ziyizilwane ezibhongayo g; abahluleli bawo h bazimpisi i zokuhlwa j; kazikhukhuzi ithambo ekuseni. <b>4</b> Abaprofethi bawo k , l bangabangakhathaliyo, abantu abakhohlisayo m; abapristi bawo n sebengcolise indawo engcwele o, benze udlakela kumthetho p. 
<b>5</b> INkosi elungileyo q iphakathi kwawo r, kayiyikwenza ububi s; ukusa ngokusa ibeka isahlulelo sayo ekukhanyeni, kayiphuthi t; kodwa ongalunganga kazi inhloni u. <b>6</b> Ngiziqumile izizwe v, izingonsi zazo zingamanxiwa w, ngenzile izitalada zazo incithakalo, ukuze kungadluli muntu x; imizi yazo idiliziwe, kakusekho muntu, kakulamhlali y. <b>7</b> Ngathi: Isibili uzangesaba z, wemukele ukulaywa aa; ukuze indawo yakhe yokuhlala ingaqunywa, konke engimjezisa ngakho ab; sibili bavuka ngovivi ac, bazonakalise zonke izenzo zabo ad. 
<b>8</b> Ngakho ngilindelani, itsho iNkosi ae, kuze kube lusuku engizavukela impango af ngalo.
