<b>39</b> ilathi lethusi, lesihlengo sethusi elalilaso, imijabo yalo, lazo zonke izitsha zalo, inditshi yokugezela lonyawo lwayo, <b>40</b> amakhetheni eguma q, insika zalo, lezisekelo zalo, lesilenge sesango leguma r, intambo zalo s, lezikhonkwane zalo, lempahla yonke yomsebenzi wethabhanekele, wethente lenhlangano, <b>41</b> izembatho zomsebenzi zokusebenza endaweni engcwele, izembatho ezingcwele zikaAroni umpristi, lezembatho zamadodana akhe ukuze basebenze njengabapristi.

<b>Exodus 40</b><br><b>1</b> INkosi yasikhuluma kuMozisi isithi: <b>2</b> Ngosuku lwenyanga yokuqala a, ngolokuqala lwenyanga, uzamisa ithabhanekele, ithente lenhlangano b, <b>3</b> ubusufaka lapho umtshokotsho wobufakazi, umbomboze umtshokotsho ngeveyili c. <b>4</b> Ungenise itafula d, uhlele okuhlelwayo phezu kwalo; ungenise uluthi lwesibane, ulumathise izibane zalo e. <b>5</b> Ubeke ilathi legolide lempepha phambi komtshokotsho wobufakazi f, ulengise isilenge somnyango wethabhanekele. 
<b>6</b> Ubeke ilathi lomnikelo wokutshiswa phambi komnyango wethabhanekele wethente lenhlangano g, <b>7</b> ubeke inditshi yokugezela phakathi kwethente lenhlangano lelathi h, uthele amanzi lapho. <b>8</b> Umise iguma inhlangothi zonke, ulengise isilenge sesango leguma. <b>9</b> Uthathe amafutha okugcoba, ugcobe ithabhanekele lakho konke okukulo i, ulingcwelise lempahla yalo yonke, njalo lizakuba ngcwele. <b>10</b> Njalo ugcobe ilathi lomnikelo wokutshiswa j lezitsha zalo zonke, ulingcwelise ilathi, njalo ilathi lizakuba yingcwelengcwele. 
<b>11</b> Njalo ugcobe inditshi yokugezela lonyawo lwayo k, uyingcwelise. <b>12</b> Usondeze uAroni lamadodana akhe emnyango wethente lenhlangano l, ubagezise ngamanzi. <b>13</b> Ugqokise uAroni izembatho ezingcwele, umgcobe, umngcwelise, ukuze angisebenzele njengompristi m. <b>14</b> Usondeze amadodana akhe, uwagqokise izigqoko, <b>15</b> uwagcobe njengalokhu ugcobe uyise, ukuze angisebenzele njengabapristi; kuzakuthi-ke ukugcotshwa kwabo kuzakuba kubo yibupristi obuphakade kuzizukulwana zabo n. 
<b>16</b> UMozisi wenza njengakho konke iNkosi eyayimlaye khona; wenza njalo. <b>17</b> Kwasekusithi ngenyanga yokuqala ngomnyaka wesibili ngolokuqala lwenyanga ithabhanekele lamiswa o. <b>18</b> UMozisi wasemisa ithabhanekele; wabeka phansi izisekelo zalo, wamisa amapulanka alo, wafaka imithando yalo, wamisa insika zalo. <b>19</b> Wendlala ithente phezu kwethabhanekele, wabeka isifulelo sethente phezu kwalo phezulu, njengokulaya kweNkosi kuMozisi. 
<b>20</b> Wathatha wafaka ubufakazi emtshokotshweni, wafaka imijabo emtshokotshweni, wafaka isihlalo somusa ngaphezu komtshokotsho phezulu p. <b>21</b> Wangenisa umtshokotsho ethabhanekeleni, wamisa iveyili lesembeso wambomboza umtshokotsho wobufakazi q, njengokulaya kweNkosi kuMozisi. <b>22</b> Wasefaka itafula ethenteni lenhlangano r, eceleni kwethabhanekele ngenyakatho ngaphandle kweveyili, <b>23</b> wahlela isinkwa ngohlelo phezu kwalo phambi kweNkosi s, njengokulaya kweNkosi kuMozisi. 
<b>24</b> Wafaka uluthi lwesibane ethenteni lenhlangano t, maqondana letafula, eceleni kwethabhanekele ngeningizimu. <b>25</b> Walumathisa izibane phambi kweNkosi u, njengokulaya kweNkosi kuMozisi. <b>26</b> Wafaka ilathi legolide ethenteni lenhlangano phambi kweveyili v, <b>27</b> watshisa kulo impepha elephunga elimnandi w, njengokulaya kweNkosi kuMozisi. <b>28</b> Walengisa isilenge somnyango wethabhanekele x. 
<b>29</b> Wabeka ilathi lomnikelo wokutshiswa emnyango wethabhanekele lethente lenhlangano y, wanikela kulo umnikelo wokutshiswa lomnikelo wokudla z, njengokulaya kweNkosi kuMozisi. <b>30</b> Wabeka inditshi yokugezela phakathi kwethente lenhlangano lelathi aa, wathela lapho amanzi okugeza. <b>31</b> UMozisi loAroni lamadodana akhe bagezela kuyo izandla zabo lenyawo zabo; <b>32</b> ekungeneni kwabo ethenteni lenhlangano lekusondeleni kwabo elathini bageza, njengokulaya kweNkosi kuMozisi ab. 
<b>33</b> Wasemisa iguma lazingelezela ithabhanekele lelathi ac, walengisa isilenge sesango leguma. UMozisi wasewuqeda umsebenzi.
