<b>Esdras 3</b><br><b>1</b> Isifikile inyanga yesikhombisa, labantwana bakoIsrayeli sebesemizini, abantu babuthana njengamuntu munye eJerusalema a. <b>2</b> Kwasekusukuma uJeshuwa indodana kaJozadaki labafowabo abapristi, loZerubhabheli indodana kaSalatiyeli labafowabo, bakha ilathi likaNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli ukuze banikele iminikelo yokutshiswa phezu kwalo, njengokubhaliweyo emlayweni kaMozisi b umuntu kaNkulunkulu c. 
<b>3</b> Basebemisa ilathi ezisekelweni zalo, ngoba ukwesaba kwakuphezu kwabo ngenxa yabantu belizwe, basebenikela eNkosini iminikelo yokutshiswa phezu kwalo, iminikelo yokutshiswa ekuseni lantambama d. <b>4</b> Basebesenza umkhosi wamadumba e njengokubhaliweyo f, banikela iminikelo yokutshiswa insuku ngensuku ngenani, njengomkhuba, udaba losuku ngosuku lwalo g. 
<b>5</b> Langemva kwalokho umnikelo wokutshiswa njalonjalo h, lowekuthwaseni kwezinyanga i, lowemikhosi yonke emisiweyo yeNkosi eyayingcwelisiwe, lowakhe wonke owanikela ngesihle umnikelo wesihle eNkosini j. <b>6</b> Ngosuku lokuqala lwenyanga yesikhombisa baqalisa ukunikela iminikelo yokutshiswa eNkosini, kodwa isisekelo sethempeli leNkosi sasingakabekwa k. 
<b>7</b> Basebenika imali kubabazi bamatshe lakubabazi bezigodo, lokudla lokunathwayo lamafutha kumaSidoni lakumaTire l ukuze balethe izigodo zemisedari zivela eLebhanoni bezisa elwandle eJopha m, ngokwemvumo kaKoresi inkosi yePerisiya kubo n. Ukwakhiwa kwethempeli kuyaqaliswa 
<b>8</b> Langomnyaka wesibili wokufika kwabo endlini kaNkulunkulu eJerusalema, ngenyanga yesibili, baqalisa oZerubhabheli indodana kaSalatiyeli o loJeshuwa indodana kaJozadaki p, lensali yabafowabo, abapristi lamaLevi, labo bonke ababebuyile eJerusalema bevela ekuthunjweni; basebemisa amaLevi kusukela koleminyaka engamatshumi amabili kusiya phezulu q ukongamela umsebenzi wendlu yeNkosi r. 
<b>9</b> Kwasekusukuma oJeshuwa, amadodana akhe, labafowabo s, uKadimiyeli lamadodana akhe t, abantwana bakoJuda, njengomuntu munye ukongamela abenzi bomsebenzi endlini kaNkulunkulu u, amadodana kaHenadadi v, amadodana abo labafowabo amaLevi. <b>10</b> Abakhi sebebekile isisekelo sethempeli leNkosi, bamisa abapristi begqokile w belezimpondo x, lamaLevi, amadodana kaAsafi, elensimbi ezincencethayo, ukudumisa iNkosi y, njengokwezandla zikaDavida inkosi yakoIsrayeli z. 
<b>11</b> Baphendulana ngokuhlabela bedumisa bebonga iNkosi aa, ngoba ilungile, ngoba umusa wayo umi kuze kube nininini kuIsrayeli ab. Bonke abantu basebememeza ngokumemeza okukhulu ekuyidumiseni iNkosi, ngoba isisekelo sendlu yeNkosi sasesibekiwe. <b>12</b> Kodwa abanengi babapristi lamaLevi lenhloko zaboyise, abadala ababeyibonile indlu yokuqala, isisekelo salindlu sesibekiwe emehlweni abo, bakhala inyembezi ngelizwi elikhulu; kodwa abanengi baphakamisa ilizwi ngokumemeza ngentokozo ac. 
<b>13</b> Ngokunjalo abantu kabehlukanisanga umsindo wokumemeza kwentokozo lomsindo wokukhala kwabantu, ngoba abantu baklabalala ngokuklabalala okukhulu, umsindo waze wezwakala khatshana. 
<b>Esdras 4</b><br><b>1</b> Kwathi izitha zakoJuda loBhenjamini sezizwile ukuthi abantwana bokuthunjwa babesakhela iNkosi uNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli ithempeli a, <b>2</b> basebesondela kuZerubhabheli lenhlokweni zaboyise bathi kibo: Kasakhe lani, ngoba siyamdinga uNkulunkulu wenu njengani, samhlabela kusukela ensukwini zikaEsarihadoni inkosi yeAsiriya owasenyusela lapha b.

Mina Dariyusi ngimise umthetho, kakwenziwe masinyane l. <b>13</b> Khonokho uTathenayi umbusi nganeno komfula, uShethari-Bozenayi, labangane babo benza njalo masinyane njengalokho uDariyusi inkosi ayekuthumele.

<b>Esdras 7</b><br><b>1</b> Njalo emva kwalezizinto, ekubuseni kukaAthakisekisi inkosi yePerisiya a, uEzra indodana kaSeraya, indodana kaAzariya, indodana kaHilikhiya, <b>2</b> indodana kaShaluma, indodana kaZadoki, indodana kaAhitubi, <b>3</b> indodana kaAmariya, indodana kaAzariya, indodana kaMerayothi, <b>4</b> indodana kaZerahiya, indodana kaUzi, indodana kaBuki, <b>5</b> indodana kaAbishuwa, indodana kaPhinehasi, indodana kaEleyazare, indodana kaAroni umpristi oyinhloko b.

<b>3</b> Kwathi ngisizwa loludaba ngadabula isembatho sami lebhatshi lami, ngangcothula okwenwele zekhanda lami lendevu zami i, ngahlala phansi ngathithibala j. <b>4</b> Kwasekubuthana kimi wonke othuthumela ngamazwi kaNkulunkulu kaIsrayeli ngenxa yesiphambeko sabathunjwa k; ngasengihlala ngithithibele l kwaze kwaba ngumnikelo wakusihlwa m. <b>5</b> Kwathi ngomnikelo wakusihlwa ngasukuma ekuthotshisweni kwami, lekudabuleni kwami isembatho sami lebhatshi lami, ngaguqa ngamadolo ami n, ngelulela izandla zami eNkosini uNkulunkulu wami o, 
<b>6</b> ngathi: Nkulunkulu wami, ngilenhloni, ngiyangekile ukuphakamisela ubuso bami kuwe, Nkulunkulu wami, ngoba ububi bethu bandile ngaphezu kwekhanda p, lecala lethu selikhule kwaze kwaba semazulwini q. <b>7</b> Kusukela ensukwini zabobaba besiphakathi kwecala elikhulu kuze kube lamuhla r, langenxa yobubi bethu thina, amakhosi ethu, abapristi bethu, sinikelwe esandleni samakhosi amazwe, enkembeni, ekuthunjweni s, lekuphangweni, lehlazweni lobuso, njengalamuhla t. 
<b>8</b> Khathesi-ke ngokwesikhatshana umusa wenziwe yiNkosi uNkulunkulu wethu, ukusitshiyela ukuphepha, lokusinika isikhonkwane endaweni yayo engcwele u, ukuze uNkulunkulu wethu akhanyisele amehlo ethu, njalo asinike imvuselelo encinyane ebugqilini bethu v. <b>9</b> Ngoba siyizigqili, kanti ebugqilini bethu uNkulunkulu wethu kasitshiyanga w, kodwa uselulele umusa phambi kwamakhosi ePerisiya, ukusinika imvuselelo, ukuphakamisa indlu kaNkulunkulu wethu x, lokumisa amanxiwa ayo, lokusinika umduli koJuda laseJerusalema y. 
<b>10</b> Khathesi-ke, Nkulunkulu wethu, sizakuthini emva kwalokhu? Ngoba siyitshiyile imilayo yakho, <b>11</b> owasilaya yona ngesandla senceku zakho abaprofethi, usithi: Ilizwe elingena kulo ukudla ilifa lalo liyilizwe elingcolileyo ngokungcola kwezizwe zamazwe, ngokunengekayo kwazo eziligcwalise ngakho kusukela ephethelweni kusiya ephethelweni, ngokungcola kwazo z. 
<b>12</b> Ngakho-ke linganiki amadodana azo amadodakazi enu, futhi lingathatheli amadodana enu amadodakazi azo aa, lingadingi ukuthula kwazo lokuhle kwazo kuze kube phakade ukuze liqine lidle okuhle kwelizwe ab, lilitshiyele abantwana benu libe yilifa kuze kube phakade ac. <b>13</b> Lemva kwakho konke okusehleleyo ngenxa yezenzo zethu ezimbi langenxa yecala lethu elikhulu ad, ngoba wena Nkulunkulu wethu uzibambile ekusijeziseni kancane kulezono zethu ae, wasinika ukuphepha okunje af; 
<b>14</b> sizayephula futhi yini imilayo yakho, sithathane lesizwe salezizinengiso ag? Kawuyikusithukuthelela yini uze usiqede ah, kuze kungabi lensali kumbe ukuphepha ai? <b>15</b> Nkosi, Nkulunkulu kaIsrayeli, ulungile aj; ngoba sitshiyelwe ukuphunyuka njengalamuhla ak. Khangela, siphambi kwakho ecaleni lethu al, ngoba kakho ongema phambi kwakho ngenxa yalokhu am. 
<b>Esdras 10</b><br><b>1</b> Kwathi uEzra esakhuleka esavuma a, ekhala inyembezi eziwisela phansi b phambi kwendlu kaNkulunkulu c, kwabuthana kuye ibandla elikhulu kakhulu lamadoda labesifazana labantwana, livela kuIsrayeli, ngoba abantu bakhala inyembezi ngokukhala okukhulu. <b>2</b> UShekaniya indodana kaJehiyeli d owamadodana kaElamu wasephendula wathi kuEzra: Thina siphambukile kuNkulunkulu wethu e, sathatha abafazi bezizweni abavela ezizweni zelizwe; kodwa khathesi kukhona ithemba koIsrayeli mayelana lalokhu. 
<b>3</b> Ngakho-ke asenze isivumelwano loNkulunkulu wethu ukuze sikhuphe bonke abafazi lokuzelwe yibo ngokweseluleko senkosi yami lesabathuthumela emlayweni kaNkulunkulu wethu f; kakwenziwe-ke ngokomlayo g. <b>4</b> Sukuma, ngoba udaba luphezu kwakho, lathi silawe. Qina, wenze h. <b>5</b> Wasesukuma uEzra, wafungisa induna zabapristi, amaLevi, loIsrayeli wonke, ukuthi benze ngokwalelilizwi. Basebefunga i. 
<b>6</b> UEzra wasesukuma esuka phambi kwendlu kaNkulunkulu j, waya endlini k kaJehohanani l indodana kaEliyashibi m; wathi efika lapho kadlanga sinkwa kanathanga manzi, ngoba elilela ububi babathunjiweyo n. <b>7</b> Basebesenza isimemezelo sedlule koJuda leJerusalema kubo bonke abantwana bokuthunjwa ukuthi babuthane eJerusalema o, <b>8</b> lokuthi loba ngubani ongafikanga ngensuku ezintathu, ngokweseluleko seziphathamandla labadala, impahla yakhe yonke izadliwa, yena ehlukaniswe lebandla labathunjiweyo. 
<b>9</b> Wonke amadoda akoJuda loBhenjamini asebuthana eJerusalema ngensuku ezintathu. Kwakuyinyanga yesificamunwemunye ngolwamatshumi amabili lwenyanga. Bonke abantu basebehlala egumeni lendlu kaNkulunkulu beqhaqhazela ngenxa yaloludaba langenxa yezulu elikhulu p. <b>10</b> UEzra umpristi wasukuma wathi kibo: Lina liphambukile lathatha abafazi bezizweni, ukwandisa isiphambeko sikaIsrayeli. <b>11</b> Ngakho-ke yenzani uvumo eNkosini uNkulunkulu waboyihlo, lenze intando yayo q, lizehlukanise lezizwe zelizwe, lakubafazi bezizweni r. 
<b>12</b> Ibandla lonke laseliphendula lathi ngelizwi elikhulu: Njengamazwi akho, ngokunjalo kuphezu kwethu ukuthi sikwenze. <b>13</b> Kodwa abantu banengi, njalo kuyisikhathi sezulu elinengi, njalo singeme phandle. Futhi kakusimsebenzi wosuku olulodwa njalo hatshi owezimbili, ngoba sibanengi esiphambukileyo kuloludaba. 
<b>14</b> Ake zime iziphathamandla zethu zebandla lonke, labo bonke abasemizini yethu abathethe abafazi bezizweni beze ngezikhathi ezimisiweyo, njalo kanye labo abadala bomuzi ngomuzi labahluleli bawo s, size sidedise ukuvutha kolaka lukaNkulunkulu wethu mayelana laloludaba t. <b>15</b> NgoJonathani indodana kaAsaheli loJahazeya indodana kaTikiva kuphela abamelana lalokho; loMeshulamu u loShabethayi umLevi babancedisa v. 
<b>16</b> Abantwana bokuthunjwa besebesenza njalo. UEzra umpristi, amadoda, inhloko zaboyise ngendlu yaboyise, labo bonke ngamabizo, basebesehlukaniswa; basebehlala ngosuku lokuqala lwenyanga yetshumi ukuhlola loludaba w. <b>17</b> Basebeqeda lawo wonke amadoda ayethethe abafazi bezizweni kwaze kwaba lusuku lokuqala lwenyanga yokuqala x. <b>18</b> Kwasekutholakala phakathi kwamadodana abapristi ayethethe abafazi bezizweni: Emadodaneni kaJeshuwa indodana kaJozadaki labafowabo: OMahaseya loEliyezeri loJaribi loGedaliya y. 
<b>19</b> Basebenika isandla sabo ukuthi bazakhupha abafazi babo z; ngoba belecala banikela inqama yomhlambi ngecala labo aa. <b>20</b> Lemadodaneni kaImeri: OHanani loZebhadiya ab. <b>21</b> Lemadodaneni kaHarimi ac: OMahaseya ad loEliya loShemaya ae loJehiyeli loUziya. <b>22</b> Lemadodaneni kaPashuri af: OEliyowenayi ag, uMahaseya, uIshmayeli, uNethaneli, uJozabadi, loElasa ah. <b>23</b> LakumaLevi: OJozabadi ai, loShimeyi, loKelaya onguKelita aj, uPethahiya, uJuda, loEliyezeri ak. 
<b>24</b> Lakubahlabeleli: UEliyashibi. <b>29</b> Lemadodaneni kaBani as: OMeshulamu, uMaluki at, loAdaya, uJashubi, loSheyali, loRamothi. <b>30</b> Lemadodaneni kaPahathi-Mowabi au: OAdina, loKelali, uBhenaya, uMahaseya av, uMathaniya, uBhezaleli, loBinuwi, loManase. <b>31</b> Lamadodana kaHarimi aw: OEliyezeri, uIshiya, uMalikiya ax, uShemaya, uShimeyoni, <b>32</b> uBhenjamini ay, uMaluki az, uShemariya. <b>33</b> Emadodaneni kaHashuma: OMathenayi, uMathatha, uZabadi, uElifeleti, uJeremayi, uManase, uShimeyi ba. <b>34</b> Emadodaneni kaBani: OMahadayi, uAmramu, loUweli, 
<b>35</b> uBhenaya, uBedeya, uKeluhi, <b>36</b> uVaniya, uMeremothi, uEliyashibi, <b>37</b> uMathaniya, uMathenayi, loJahasa, <b>38</b> loBani, loBinuwi, uShimeyi bb, <b>39</b> loShelemiya, loNathani, loAdaya bc, <b>40</b> uMakinadebayi, uShashayi, uSharayi, <b>41</b> uAzareli, uShelemiya, uShemariya, <b>42</b> uShaluma, uAmariya, uJosefa. <b>43</b> Lemadodaneni kaNebo bd: OJeyiyeli, uMathithiya, uZabadi, uZebina, uJadayi, loJoweli, uBhenaya be.
