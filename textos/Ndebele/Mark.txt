<b>43</b> Wasemlayisisa ao, wahle wamyekela wahamba, <b>44</b> esithi kuye: Khangela ungatsheli muntu ulutho; kodwa hamba, uziveze kumpristi, unikele ngokuhlanjululwa kwakho lokho uMozisi akulayayo, kube yibufakazi kubo ap. <b>45</b> Kodwa yena esephumile waqala ukumemezela kakhulu aq walwandisa udaba ar, kwaze kwathi wayengaselakho ukungena emzini obala, kodwa wayengaphandle ezindaweni ezizinkangala as; beza kuye bevela enhlangothini zonke at. 
<b>Marcos 2</b><br><b>1</b> Njalo wabuya wangena eKapenawume a emva kwezinsuku b; kwasekuzwakala ukuthi usendlini c. <b>2</b> Njalo kwahle kwabuthana abanengi, akwazabe kusaba lendawo yabo d, ngitsho langasemnyango; wasekhuluma ilizwi kubo. <b>3</b> Basebesiza kuye, beletha owome umhlubulo, ethwelwe ngabane e. <b>4</b> Babengelakusondela kuye ngenxa yexuku, bavula f uphahla lapho ayekhona, bathi sebelubhobozile behlisa ithala alele kulo owome umhlubulo g. 
<b>5</b> Njalo uJesu ebona ukholo lwabo wathi kowome umhlubulo: Ndodana, izono zakho uzithethelelwe h. <b>6</b> Kodwa abanye bababhali ababehlezi lapho, banakana enhliziyweni zabo i, bathi: <b>7</b> Ukhulumelani inhlamba enje lo? Ngubani ongathethelela izono ngaphandle koyedwa, uNkulunkulu j? <b>8</b> Njalo khonokho uJesu ekwazi emoyeni wakhe ukuthi babenakana njalo phakathi kwabo, wathi kubo: Linakanelani izinto lezi enhliziyweni zenu k? 
<b>9</b> Yikuphi okulula, ukuthi kowome umhlubulo: Izono zakho uzithethelelwe l, kumbe ukuthi: Sukuma, uthathe icansi lakho, uhambe? <b>10</b> Kodwa ukuze lazi ukuthi iNdodana yomuntu ilamandla okuthethelela izono emhlabeni (wathi kowome umhlubulo): <b>11</b> Ngithi kuwe: Sukuma uthathe icansi lakho, uye endlini yakho. <b>12</b> Njalo wahle wasukuma, wathatha icansi, waphuma phambi kwabo bonke; baze bamangala bonke m, bamdumisa uNkulunkulu, besithi: Kasizange sikubone okunje. Ukubizwa kukaLevi 
<b>13</b> Wasebuya esiya elwandle, ixuku lonke laselisiza kuye, wasebafundisa n. <b>14</b> Wathi esedlula wabona uLevi okaAlfewu ehlezi endlini yokuthelisela, wasesithi kuye: Ngilandela. Wasesukuma wamlandela o. <b>15</b> Kwasekusithi ehlezi endlini yakhe, labathelisi abanengi lezoni bahlala ekudleni loJesu labafundi bakhe p; ngoba babebanengi, njalo bemlandela. <b>16</b> Kwathi ababhali labaFarisi, bembona esidla labathelisi lezoni, bathi kubafundi bakhe: Kungani esidla enatha labathelisi lezoni? 
<b>17</b> Kwathi uJesu esezwile wathi kubo: Abaphilileyo kabadingi melaphi, kodwa labo abagulayo. Kangizanga ukubiza abalungileyo, kodwa izoni zize ekuphendukeni. Ukuzila ukudla <b>18</b> Njalo abafundi bakaJohane lababaFarisi babezila ukudla; basebesiza bathi kuye: Kungani abafundi bakaJohane lababaFarisi bezila ukudla, kodwa abafundi bakho bengazili ukudla q? <b>19</b> UJesu wasesithi kubo: Amadodana endlu yomthimba angazila yini ukudla, umyeni eselawo? Angezile, ingqe ngesikhathi umyeni eselawo; 
<b>20</b> kodwa zizafika insuku lapho umyeni azasuswa kuwo, njalo khona azazila ukudla ngalezonsuku r. <b>21</b> Futhi kakho othungela isichibi selembu elingakafinyeli esembathweni esidala; uba-ke kungenjalo okubekelweyo kudabuke kiso, okutsha kwesidala, kuqhele ukudabuka. <b>22</b> Futhi kakho othela iwayini elitsha emigodleni emidala; uba-ke kungenjalo iwayini elitsha lidabula imigodla, lichitheke iwayini lemigodla yonakale; kodwa iwayini elitsha limele lifakwe emigodleni emitsha. UJesu uchasisa umlayo wesabatha 
<b>23</b> Kwasekusithi edabula emasimini amabele ngesabatha s, abafundi bakhe basebeqala ukukha izikhwebu behamba t. <b>24</b> AbaFarisi basebesithi kuye: Khangela, kungani besenza lokho okungavunyelwayo ngesabatha? <b>25</b> Yena wasesithi kubo: Kalizange libale yini uDavida akwenzayo, mhla eswele, elambile, yena lababelaye? <b>26</b> Ukuthi wayangena njani endlini kaNkulunkulu esikhathini uAbhiyatha engumpristi omkhulu u, wadla izinkwa zokubukiswa ezingavunyelwa ukudliwa ngaphandle kwabapristi, wabanika lababelaye? 
<b>27</b> Wasesithi kubo: Isabatha lenziwa ngenxa yomuntu, njalo umuntu kenziwanga ngenxa yesabatha v; <b>28</b> ngakho iNdodana yomuntu iyiNkosi leyesabatha w. 
<b>Marcos 3</b><br><b>1</b> Wasebuya engena a esinagogeni, njalo kwakukhona lapho umuntu olesandla esitshwabheneyo b. <b>2</b> Basebemqaphela ukuthi uzamsilisa ngesabatha c, ukuze bambeke icala d. <b>3</b> Wasesithi kumuntu olesandla esitshwabheneyo: Sukuma uye phakathi. <b>4</b> Wathi kubo: Kuvunyelwe yini ngamasabatha ukwenza okulungileyo, kumbe ukwenza okubi? Ukusindisa impilo, kumbe ukubulala? Kodwa bathula. 
<b>5</b> Wasebathalaza ngokuthukuthela, edabukile ngobulukhuni benhliziyo yabo e, wathi kumuntu: Yelula isandla sakho. Waselula, isandla sakhe sasesisiliswa saphila njengesinye. <b>6</b> Basebephuma abaFarisi bahle bacebisana labakaHerodi bemelane laye, ukuthi bangambulala njani f. UJesu usilisa abanye abantu <b>7</b> Kodwa uJesu wasuka wahamba labafundi bakhe ngaselwandle g; ixuku elikhulu laselimlandela livela eGalili h, leJudiya;

<b>12</b> Wasebalayisisa kakhulu ukuthi bangamvezi q. Ukukhethwa kwabaphostoli <b>13</b> Wasesenyukela entabeni, wabizela kuye labo ayebafuna yena r; basebesiza kuye. <b>14</b> Wasemisa abalitshumi lambili, ukuze babe laye, lokuze abathume ukuyatshumayela, <b>15</b> futhi babe lamandla okwelapha imikhuhlane, lawokukhupha amadimoni. <b>16</b> LoSimoni wamutha ibizo lokuthi nguPetro; <b>17</b> loJakobe okaZebediya, loJohane umfowabo kaJakobe s; wabatha amabizo wathi ngoBowanegesi, okuyikuthi: Amadodana omdumo; 
<b>18</b> loAndreya, loFiliphu, loBartolomewu, loMathewu, loTomasi, loJakobe okaAlfewu t, loTadewu, loSimoni umKhanani, <b>19</b> loJudasi uIskariyothi, laye owamnikelayo u. Basebengena endlini v. <b>23</b> Wasebabizela kuye, wathi kubo ngemifanekiso: USathane angakhupha njani uSathane? <b>24</b> Futhi uba umbuso wehlukene umelene lawo ngokwawo, lowombuso ungeme. <b>25</b> Lendlu uba yehlukene imelana layo ngokwayo, leyondlu ingeme. <b>26</b> LoSathane uba ezivukela yena, ahlukane, angeme, kodwa uyaphela. <b>27</b> Kakho ongangena endlini yesiqhwaga, aphange impahla yaso, uba engasibophi isiqhwaga kuqala, abesephanga indlu yaso ab. 
<b>28</b> Ngiqinisile ngithi kini: Zonke izono zizathethelelwa kubantwana babantu, lezinhlamba loba ziphi abahlambaza ngazo; <b>29</b> kodwa loba ngubani ohlambaza emelene loMoya oyiNgcwele, kalakuthethelelwa lanini ac, kodwa ulecala lokulahlwa okulaphakade; <b>30</b> ngoba bathi: Ulomoya ongcolileyo ad. Izihlobo zikaJesu eziyizo <b>31</b> Kwasekufika ae abafowabo lonina, bema ngaphandle bathumela kuye, bembiza af. <b>32</b> Njalo ixuku lalihlezi limphahlile; basebesithi kuye: Khangela, unyoko labafowenu baphandle bayakudinga. 
<b>33</b> Wasebaphendula wathi: Ngubani umama kumbe abafowethu? <b>34</b> Wasethalaza labo ababehlezi bemphahlile, wathi: Khangelani umama labafowethu. <b>35</b> Ngoba loba ngubani owenza intando kaNkulunkulu, nguye ongumfowethu lodadewethu lomama. 
<b>Marcos 4</b><br><b>1</b> Wasebuya waqala ukufundisa ngaselwandle; kwasekubuthana kuye ixuku elikhulu a, waze wangena emkhunjini wahlala olwandle; njalo ixuku lonke lalisemhlabathini ngaselwandle b. <b>2</b> Wasebafundisa izinto ezinengi ngemifanekiso, lekufundiseni kwakhe wathi kubo c: <b>3</b> Lalelani; khangelani, kwaphuma umhlanyeli ukuyahlanyela. <b>4</b> Kwasekusithi ekuhlanyeleni, enye yawela eceleni kwendlela, kwasekufika inyoni zezulu zayidla zayiqeda. 
<b>5</b> Njalo enye yawela emhlabathini olamatshe, lapho eyayingelamhlabathi omnengi khona; futhi yahle yamila, ngoba yayingelakutshona komhlabathi; <b>6</b> kwathi ekuphumeni kwelanga yahangulwa yabuna ngoba yayingelampande. <b>7</b> Enye yasiwela emeveni, ameva asekhula, ayiminyanisa, futhi ayithelanga isithelo. <b>8</b> Njalo enye yawela emhlabathini omuhle; yathela isithelo esakhulayo sanda, yazala enye amatshumi amathathu, lenye amatshumi ayisithupha, lenye ikhulu. <b>9</b> Wasesithi kubo: Olendlebe zokuzwa, akezwe. Umsebenzi wemifanekiso 
<b>10</b> Kwathi eseyedwa, labo ababemphahlile kanye labalitshumi lambili bambuza ngomfanekiso. <b>11</b> Wasesithi kubo: Kuphiwe kini ukwazi imfihlo yombuso kaNkulunkulu; kodwa labo abangaphandle d benzelwa izinto zonke ngemifanekiso; <b>12</b> ukuze bebona babone, bangabonisisi; njalo besizwa bezwe, bangaqedisisi; hlezi loba nini baphenduke, bathethelelwe izono zabo e. Ukuchasiswa komfanekiso womhlanyeli <b>13</b> Wasesithi kubo: Kaliwazi lumfanekiso yini? Pho lizayazi njani imifanekiso yonke? <b>14</b> Umhlanyeli uhlanyela ilizwi.

<b>19</b> kodwa impisekelo yalumhlaba, lenkohliso yenotho, lenkanuko yezinye izinto kungene kuminyanise ilizwi, futhi ibe ingasatheli. <b>20</b> Lalaba yilabo abahlanyelwe emhlabathini omuhle, abathi belizwile ilizwi, balemukele, bathele, enye amatshumi amathathu, lenye amatshumi ayisithupha, lenye ikhulu f. Umfanekiso wesibane lowenhlanyelo <b>21</b> Wasesithi kubo: Kanti isibane silethwa ukuze sibekwe ngaphansi kwesitsha kumbe ngaphansi kombheda? Angithi ukuze sibekwe esiqobaneni sesibane g? 
<b>22</b> Ngoba kakukho okufihliweyo okungayikubonakaliswa; akukho okwenzeka kube yimfihlo, kodwa ukuze kube sobala h. <b>23</b> Nxa umuntu elendlebe zokuzwa akezwe i. <b>24</b> Wasesithi kubo: Qaphelisani elikuzwayo j. Ngesilinganiso elilinganisa ngaso lizalinganiselwa ngaso, njalo lina elizwayo lizaphiwa ngokwengezelelweyo k. <b>25</b> Ngoba loba ngubani olakho, uzaphiwa; kodwa loba ngubani ongelakho, uzakwemukwa lalokho alakho l. <b>26</b> Wasesithi: Unjalo umbuso kaNkulunkulu, njengokuba nxa umuntu ephosa inhlanyelo emhlabathini, 
<b>27</b> njalo alale abesevuka ebusuku lemini, lenhlanyelo imile njalo ikhule, yena kakwazi ukuthi njani. <b>28</b> Ngoba umhlabathi uyathela ngokwawo, kuqala usungulo, kulandele isikhwebu, kulandele amabele apheleleyo esikhwetshini m. <b>29</b> Kodwa nxa sesivuthiwe isithelo, uhle athumeze isikela n, ngoba ukuvuna sekufikile o. <b>30</b> Wathi futhi p: Sizawufananisa lani umbuso kaNkulunkulu?
