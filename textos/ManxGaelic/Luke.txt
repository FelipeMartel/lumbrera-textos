<b>Lucas 1</b><br><b>1</b> Son wheesh as dy vel ymmodee er ghoaill ayns laue dy chur coontey cairagh jeh ny reddyn ta dy firrinagh er nyn gredjal nyn mast'ain. <b>2</b> Eer myr hoilshee ad dooinyn ad, va er-dyn toshiaght nyn veanishyn sooilley as nyn shirveishee jeh'n ghoo. <b>3</b> Ta mish myrgeddin er vakin eh mie, myr ta slane toiggal aym jeh dy chooilley nhee er-dyn eer toshiaght, dy scrieu hoods ayns order, Theophilus smoo ooasle, <b>4</b> Dy vod tushtey share y ve ayd jeh firrinys ny reddyn shen, t'ou er ny ve ynsit ayndoo. 
<b>5</b> Rish lhing Heron ree Yudea, va saggyrt dy row enmyssit Zacharias, jeh coorse Abia; as va'n ven echey jeh inneenyn Aaron, as yn ennym eck Elizabeth. <b>6</b> As v'ad ny-neesht cairagh kiongoyrt rish Jee, gimmeeaght ayns ooilley annaghyn as oardaghyn y Chiarn gyn loght. <b>7</b> As cha row sluight oc, er-yn-oyr dy row Elizabeth gennish, as v'ad ny-neesht er roie dy mie ayns eash. <b>8</b> As haink eh gy-kione, choud as v'eh cooilleeney oik y taggyrt kiongoyrt rish Jee, myr va e choorse er jeet magh, 
<b>9</b> Cordail rish cliaghtey oik y taggyrt, dy haghyr eh dy ve yn currym echey dy lostey oural-millish, tra v'eh goll stiagh ayns chiamble y Chiarn. <b>10</b> As va'n slane chaglym jeh'n pobble cheu-mooie ec padjer rish traa yn oural-millish. <b>11</b> As haink ainle y Chiarn, as hass eh er cheu yesh altar yn oural-millish. <b>12</b> As tra honnick Zacharias eh, v'eh seaghnit, as huitt aggle er. 
<b>13</b> Agh dooyrt yn ainle rish, Ny gow aggle Zacharias: son ta dty phadjer er ny chlashtyn, as nee yn ven ayd Elizabeth mac y ymmyrkey dhyt, as nee oo genmys eh Ean. <b>14</b> As bee boggey as gerjagh ayd, as nee ymmodee boggey y ghoaill tra hig eh er y theihll. <b>15</b> Son bee eh ooasle ayns shilley yn Chiarn, as cha n'iu eh feeyn ny jough lajer, as bee eh er ny lhieeney lesh y Spyrryd Noo, veih eer brein e vayrey. <b>16</b> As ymmodee jeh cloan Israel nee eh y hyndaa gys y Chiarn y Jee oc. 
<b>17</b> As hed eh roishyn ayns spyrryd as pooar Elias, dy hyndaa creeaghyn ny ayragh yn gys y chloan, as ny mee-viallee gys creenaght yn vooinjer chairal, dy yannoo aarloo pobble kiarit son y Chiarn. <b>18</b> As dooyrt Zacharias rish yn ainle, Cre liorish vees fys aym's er shoh son ta mee shenn, as ta'n ven er roie dy mie ayns eash. <b>19</b> As dreggyr yn aile as dooyrt e rish, Mish Gabriel ta shirveish fenish Yee: as ta mee-er my choyrt dy loayrt rhyt, as dy hoilshaghey dhyt ny naightyn mie shoh. 
<b>20</b> As cur-my-ner bee oo balloo, as cha jig dty ghlare lhiat, derrey yn laa vees ny reddyn shoh cooilleenit, er-y-fa nagh vel oo er chredjal my ghoan's vees er nyn gooilleeney ayns nyn imbagh cooie. <b>21</b> As va'n pobble fieau son Zacharias, as yindys orroo dy row eh cumrail choud ayns y chiamble. <b>22</b> As tra haink eh magh, cha voddagh eh loayrt roo; as hoig ad dy row eh er vakin ashlish ayns y chiamble: son v'eh jannoo signyn daue, as hannee eh balloo. 
<b>23</b> As shen myr ve, tra v'eh er chooilleeney laghyn e hirveish, dy jimmee eh gys e hie hene. <b>24</b> As lurg ny laghyn shen ren e ven Elizabeth giennaghtyn, as dollee ee ee-hene queig meeghyn, gra, <b>25</b> Shoh myr ta'n Chiarn er n'yannoo er my hon tra yeeagh eh orrym, dy ghoaill ersooyl my oltooan mastey deiney. <b>26</b> As ayns y cheyoo vee, va'n ainle Gabriel er ny choyrt veih Jee, gys ard-valley dy Ghalilee, va enmyssit Nazareth, <b>27</b> Gys moidyn nasht rish dooinney enmyssit Joseph, jeh thie Ghavid; as va ennym y voidyn Moirrey. 
<b>28</b> As haink yn ainle stiagh as dooyrt eh r'ee, Dy vannee dhyt's ta ayns ard-foayr ta'n Chiarn mayrt; bannit t'ou uss mastey mraane. <b>29</b> As tra honnick ee eh, v'ee seaghnit ec y raa echey, as smooinee ee r'ee hene cre'n monney dy vannaghey va shoh. <b>30</b> As dooyrt yn ainle r'ee, Ny bee aggle ort, O Voirrey; son t'ou er gheddyn foayr veih Jee. <b>31</b> As cur-my-ner, nee oo giennaghtyn, as mac y ymmyrkey, as enmyssee oo yn ennym echey Yeesey. 
<b>32</b> Bee eshyn cronnal, as enmyssit Mac yn Er-syrjey; as ver y Chiarn Jee dasyn stoyl-reeoil e ayr David. <b>33</b> As nee eh reill harrish thie Yacob gys earishyn foddey beayn, as er e reeriaght cha bee jerrey erbee. <b>34</b> Eisht dooyrt Moirrey rish yn ainle, Kys oddys shoh y ve, fakin nagh nione dou dooinney? <b>35</b> As dreggyr yn ainle as dooyrt eh r'ee, Hig yn Spyrryd Noo neose ort, as bee pooar yn Er-syrjey er ny skeayley harryd: shen-y-fa bee yn sluight chasherick shen ver oo son y theihll, enmyssit Mac Yee. 
<b>36</b> As cur-my-ner, dty ven-vooinjerey Elizabeth, t'eeish myrgeddin er ghiennaght yn mac ayns ny shenn laghyn eck: as shoh yn cheyoo vee jeeish va raait dy ve gennish. <b>37</b> Son da Jee cha bee nhee erbee neu-phossible. <b>38</b> As dooyrt Moirrey, Cur-my-ner sharvaant y Chiarn, dy jig eh lhiam cordail rish dty ghoo. As jimmee yn ainle voee. <b>39</b> As mysh ny laghyn shen hrog Moirrey urree lesh siyr dy gholl gys cheer ny liargagh, gys ard-valley dy Yudah, <b>40</b> As hie ee stiagh ayns thie Zacharias as vannee ee da Elizabeth. 
<b>41</b> As haink eh gy-kione, tra cheayll Elizabeth bannaghey Moirrey, dy lheim yn oikan ayns e brein, as va Elizabeth lhieent lesh y Spyrryd Noo. <b>42</b> As loayr ee lesh ard-choraa as dooyrt ee, Bannit t'ou uss mastey mraane, as bannit ta sluight dty vreïn. <b>43</b> As kys ta'n (ooashley) aym, dy vel moir my Hiarn er jeet dy my yeeaghyn? <b>44</b> Son cur-my-ner, cha leah as ren coraa dty vannaghey bingys ayns my chleayshyn, lheim yn oikan ayns my vrein son boggey. 
<b>45</b> As bannit t'eeish ta er chredjal: son bee slane cooilleeney ny reddyn shen v'er ny hoilshaghey jee liorish y Chiarn. <b>46</b> As dooyrt Moirrey, Ta m'annym cur ard-voylley da'n Chiarn. <b>47</b> As ta my spyrryd er ghoaill boggey ayns Jee my Haualtagh. <b>48</b> Son t'eh er chur tastey da stayd injil e harvaant: son cur-my-ner, maghey shoh nee dy chooilley heeloghe mish y enmys bannit. <b>49</b> Son s'mooar ta'n Ooilley-niartal er n'yannoo er my hon, as s'casherick ta e ennym. 
<b>50</b> As ta e vyghin orroosyn ta goaill aggle roish, veih sheeloghe gys sheeloghe. <b>51</b> T'eh er hoilshaghey niart lesh e roih t'eh er skeayley ny moyrnee ayns mooaralys nyn greeaghyn. <b>52</b> T'eh er lhieggal sheese ny treanee veih nyn stuill-reeoil, as er hoiaghey seose ny imlee. <b>53</b> T'eh er lhieeney ny accryssee lesh reddyn mie, as ny berchee t'eh er chur follym ersooyl. <b>54</b> T'eh er chooney lesh e harvaant Israel ayns cooinaghtyn jeh e vyghin. 
<b>55</b> (Myr loayr eh rish nyn shenn-ayryn, rish Abraham as e luight) er son dy bragh. <b>56</b> As duirree Moirrey mâree mysh three meeghyn, as haink ee reesht gys e thie hene. <b>57</b> Nish va slane traa Elizabeth er jeet dy ve livreit, as hug ee mac son y theihll. <b>58</b> As cheayll ny naboonyn as y sleih mooinjerey eck, kys va'n Chiarn er hoilshaghey ard-vyghin jee; as ghow ad boggey mâree. <b>59</b> As haink eh gy-kione er yn hoghtoo laa dy daink ad dy ghiarey-chymmylt yn lhiannoo; as denmys ad eh Zacharias, lurg ennym e ayrey. 
<b>60</b> As dreggyr e voir, as dooyrt ee, Cha nee; agh bee eh enmyssit Ean. <b>61</b> As dooyrt ad r'ee, Cha vel veg jeh dty chynney jeh'n ennym shen. <b>62</b> As ren ad signyn gys e ayr, cre'n ennym bailIishyn y ve er. <b>63</b> As hir eh boayrd-screeuee, as scrieu eh, gra, Ta'n ennym echey Ean. As ghow ad ooilley yindys. <b>64</b> As er-y-chooyl va e veeal er ny osley, as e hengey er ny eaysley, as loayr eh, as hug eh moylley da Jee. 
<b>65</b> As huitt aggle (arrymagh) er ooilley nyn naboonyn: as hie goo jeh ooilley ny reddyn shoh magh trooid ooilley cheer sleitagh Yudea. <b>66</b> As adsyn ooilley cheayll shoh, hasht ad ayns nyn greeaghyn ad, gra, Cren monney dy ghooinney vees eh shoh? as va laue yn Chiarn mârish. <b>67</b> As va'n ayr echey Zacharias lhieent lesh y Spyrryd Noo, as ren eh phadeyrys, gra, <b>68</b> Bannit dy row yn Chiarn Jee Israel, son t'eh er yeeaghyn er, as er livrey e phobble, 
<b>69</b> As t'eh er hroggal seose saualtys niartal er nyn son, ayns thie e harvaant David; <b>70</b> Myr loayr eh liorish beeal e adeyryn crauee, t'er ny ve er-dy hoshiaght y theihll; <b>71</b> Dy hauail shin veih nyn noidyn, as veih'n lane ocsyn ooilley ta dwoaie oc orrin. <b>72</b> Dy chooilleeney yn vyghin va giallit da ny ayraghyn ain, as dy chooinaghtyn er e chonaant casherick: <b>73</b> Eer yn breearrey ren eh gys yn ayr ain Abraham, 
<b>74</b> Dy giallagh eh dooinyn, dy beagh shin er nyn livrey veih laueyn nyn noidyn, as shirveish eh fegooish aggle. <b>75</b> Ayns craueeaght as cairys kiongoyrt rish, ooilley laghyn nyn mea. <b>76</b> As bee uss lhiannoo enmyssit phadeyr yn Er-syrjey; son hed oo roish eddin y Chiarn, dy yannoo aarloo e raaidyn; <b>77</b> Dy chur tushtey dy haualtys da e phobble, liorish leih nyn beccaghyn, <b>78</b> Trooid myghin meiygh yn Jee ain; liorish ta irree-ny-greiney veih yn yrjid er hoilshean orrin 
<b>79</b> Dy chur soilshey dauesyn ta nyn soie ayns dorraghys as ayns scadoo yn vaaish, dy leeideil ny cassyn ain ayns raad y chee. <b>80</b> As daase y lhiannoo, as v'eh er ny niartaghey 'sy spyrryd, as v'eh ayns ny faasaghyn, derrey'n laa v'eh er ny hoilshaghey da Israel. 
<b>Lucas 2</b><br><b>1</b> As haink eh gy-kione ayns ny laghyn shen, dy jagh sarey magh veih Cesar Augustus, dy beagh coontey er ny ghoaill jeh'n slane rheam. <b>2</b> (As va'n recortys shoh jeant magh roish my va Cyremus ny chiannoort er Syria.) <b>3</b> As hie ad ooilley dy chur stiagh nyn enmyn, dagh unnane gys e ard-valley hene. <b>4</b> As hie Joseph myrgeddin seose veih Galilee, veih ard-valley Nazareth, gys Judea, gys ard-valley Ghavid, ta enmyssit Bethlehem (son dy row eh jeh thie as kynney Ghavid.) 
<b>5</b> Dy chur stiagh e ennym hene as ennym Moirrey yn ven va nasht rish, va trome torragh. <b>6</b> As shen myr ve, choud as v'ad ayns shen, dy row yn traa er jeet jeeish dy ve livreit. <b>7</b> As hug ee son y theihll yn chied mac rug jee, as hoill ee eh ayns aanrityn soïllee, as hug ee eh ny lhie ayns manjoor, er-y-fa nagh row room er nyn son ayns y thie-oast. <b>8</b> Nish v'ayns y cheer cheddin bochilley mooie er y vagher freayll arrey er nyn shioltaneyn 'syn oie. 
<b>9</b> As cur-my-ner haink ainle y Chiarn neose orroo, as ren gloyr y Chiarn soilshean mygeayrt-y-moo; as v'ad fo aggle feer atchimagh. <b>10</b> As dooyrt yn ainle roo, Ny bee aggle erriu: son cur-my-ner, ta mish coyrt lhiam hiu naightyn mie dy voggey mooar vees gys dy chooilley phobble. <b>11</b> Son diuish t'er ny ruggey jiu ayns ard valley Ghavid, Saualtagh, ta shen Creest yn Chiarn. <b>12</b> As bee shoh son cowrey diu; Yiow shiu yn oikan ayns aanrityn soillee, as ny lhie ayns manjoor. 
<b>13</b> As ayns y tullogh va marish yn ainle ymmodee jeh'n cheshaght flaunyssagh cur moylley da Jee, as gra, <b>14</b> Gloyr dy row gys Jee ayns yn yrjid, as er y thalloo shee, aigney-mie gys deiney. <b>15</b> As haink eh gy-kione, cha leah as va ny ainleyn er n'gholl ersooyl voue gys niau, dy dooyrt ny bochillyn yn derrey yeh rish y jeh elley, Lhig dooin goll nish derrey Bethlehem, dy akin yn red shoh t'er jeet gy-kione, ta'n Chiarn er hoilshaghey dooin. <b>16</b> As haink ad lesh siyr, as hooar ad Moirrey as Joseph, as yn oikan ny lhie ayns manjoor. 
<b>17</b> As tra honnick ad eh, hoilshee ad magh yn raa va er ny insh daue mychione y lhiannoo shoh. <b>18</b> As ghow dy chooilley unnane cheayll eh, yindys jeh ny reddyn va inshit daue liorish ny bochillyn. <b>19</b> Agh hasht Moirrey ooilley ny raaghyn shoh (ayns e haigney) as smooinee ee orroo dy dowin ayns e cree. <b>20</b> As haink ny bochillyn roue, cur gloyr as moylley da Jee son dy chooilley nhee v'ad er chlashtyn as er vakin, myr ve inshit daue. 
<b>21</b> As tra va hoght laa ec kione son giarey-chymmylt y lhiannoo, v'eh enmyssit YEESEY, son shen yn ennym hug yn ainle er, roish my row eh er ny ghiennaghtyn 'sy vrein. <b>22</b> As tra va laghyn nyn ghlenney, cordail rish leigh Voses, er jeet, hug ad lhieu eh gys Jerusalem, dy hebbal eh gys y Chiarn, <b>23</b> (Myr te scruit ayns leih yn Chiarn, Bee dy chooilley chied-ruggit yrrynagh er ny chasherickey gys y Chiarn.) <b>24</b> As dy hebbal yn oural t'er ny harey ayns leigh yn Chiarn, Piyr dy chalmaneyn turtle, ny daa chalmane aegey. 
<b>25</b> As cur-my-ner va dooinney ayns Jerusalem va enmyssit Simeon; as va'n dooinney cheddin cairagh as crauee, farkiaght son gerjagh Israel: as va'n Spyrryd Noo ersyn. <b>26</b> As ve er ny hoilshaghey da liorish y Spyrryd Noo, nagh noghe eh baase, roish da v'er vakin Creest yn Chiarn. <b>27</b> As haink eh liorish y Spyrryd stiagh ayns y chiamble: as tra va'n ayr as y voir er chur lhieu yn lhiannoo Yeesey stiagh, dy yannoo er e hon, lurg cliaghtey yn leigh; <b>28</b> Eisht ghow eh seose eh ayns e roihaghyn, as hug eh moylley da Jee, as dooyrt eh, 
<b>29</b> Hiarn nish t'ou cur kied da dty harvaant paartail ayns she, cordail rish dty ghoo. <b>30</b> Son ta my hooillyn er vakin dty haualtys; <b>31</b> T'ou er chiarail dy ve er ny hoilshaghey fenish dy chooilley phobble; <b>32</b> Soilshey dy hoilshean er ny Ashoonee as gloyr dty phobble Israel. <b>33</b> As ghow Joseph as e voir yindys jeh ny reddyn va loayrit my-e-chione, 
<b>34</b> As vannee Simeon ad, as dooyrt eh rish Moirrey e voir, Cur-my-ner ta'n lhiannoo shoh soit dy chur sheese as dy hoiaghey seose ymmodee ayns Israel; as son cowrey dy oltooan. <b>35</b> (As dy jarroo hed cliwe trooid dty annym hene neesht) dy vod smooinaghtyn ymmodee creeaghyn v'er ny chronnaghey. <b>36</b> As va myrgeddin phadeyrben enmyssit Anna, inneen Phanuel, jeh kynney Aser: v'ee dy eash vooar, as v'ee er veaghey marish e sheshey shiaght bleeaney neayr as v'ee ny moidyn. 
<b>37</b> As v'ee ny ben-treoghe mysh kiare feed as kiare bleeaney dy eash, as v'ee dy kinjagh taaghey yn chiamble, shirveish Jee lesh trostey as padjeryn oie as laa. <b>38</b> As haink ish stiagh ec y traa cheddin, as hug ee booise myrgeddin da'n Chiarn, as loayr ee jeh roosyn ooilley va jeeaghyn son livrey-ys ayns Jerusalem. <b>39</b> As tra v'ad er chooilleeney dy chooilley nhee cordail rish leigh yn Chiarn, haink ad reesht gys Galilee, gys yn ard-valley oc hene Nazareth. 
<b>40</b> As daase y lhiannoo, as v'eh er ny niartaghey 'sy spyrryd, as lhieent lesh creenaght, as va grayse Yee mârish. <b>41</b> Nish va e ayr as e voir goll seose gys Jerusalem dy chooilley vlein ec feailley ny caisht. <b>42</b> As tra v'eh daa vlein jeig dy eash, hie ad seose gys Jerusalem lurg cliaghtey ny feailley. <b>43</b> As tra va ny laghyn harrish, as ad goll thie reesht, duirree yn lhiannoo Yeesey nyn yei ayns Jerusalem, gyn-yss da Joseph as e voir. 
<b>44</b> Agh smooinaghtgn dy row eh ayns y cheshaght, hie ad jurnah laa; as v'ad briaght er e hon mastey nyn gheiney mooinjerey as nyn ainjys. <b>45</b> As tra nagh dooar ad eh, hie ad reesht gys Jerusalem shirrey er e hon. <b>46</b> As haink eh gy-kione, lurg three laa dy dooar ad eh ayns y chiamble, ny hoie mastey ny fir-ynsee, chammah geaishtagh roo, as cur feyshtyn orroo. <b>47</b> As ghow ooilley ny va geaishtagh rish yindys jeh'n toiggal as jeh ny ansooryn echey. 
<b>48</b> As tra honnick ad eh, v'ad fo mooarane atchim; as dooyrt e voir rish, Vac, cre'n-fa t'ou er n'yannoo myr shoh rooin? cur-my-ner, ta dty ayr as mish er ve briaght dy trimshagh er dty hon. <b>49</b> As dooyrt eh roo, Kys dy row shiu briaght er my hon's? nagh row fys eu dy beign dou ve mysh obbyr my Ayrey ? <b>50</b> As cha row toiggyl oc jeh'n raa loayr eh roo. <b>51</b> As hie eh sheese mâroo, as haink eh gys Nazareth, as v'eh biallagh daue: as hasht e voir ooilley ny raaghyn shoh ayns e cree. 
<b>52</b> As ren Yeesey bishaghey ayns creenaght as aase, as ayns foayr rish Jee as dooinney. 
<b>Lucas 3</b><br><b>1</b> NISH ayns y wheiggoo vlein jeig jeh reill, Tiberius Cesar, tra va Pontius Pilate ny chiannoort er Judea, as Herod ny hetrarch er Galilee, as e vraar Philip reill ayns Iturea as cheer Trachonitis, as Lysanias reill ayns Abilene. <b>2</b> Tra va Annas as Caiaphas nyn ard-saggyrtyn, haink goo Yee gys Ean mac Zacharias ayns yn aasagh. <b>3</b> As haink eh gys ooilley yn cheer my geayrt Jordan, preacheil bashtey arrys son leih peccaghyn. 
<b>4</b> Myr te scruit ayns lioar goan Esaias yn phadeyr, gra, Coraa fer geamagh ayns yn aasagh, Kiartee-jee raad y Chiarn, jean-jee ny cassanyn echey jeeragh. <b>5</b> Bee dy chooilley choan er ny lhieeney as dy chooilley slieau as cronk er nyn injillaghey, as ny ynnydyn cammey er nyn yannoo jeeragh, as ny raaidyn garroo er nyn yannoo rea. <b>6</b> As hee dy chooilley eill saualtys Yee. 
<b>7</b> Eisht dooyrt eh rish y pobble v'er jeet magh huggey dy ve er nyn mashtey, O shiuish heeloghe dy ard-nieughyn, quoi t'er chur raaue diu dy hea veih yn jymmoose ta ry-heet? <b>8</b> Cur-jee magh nish er-y-fa shen messyn cooie dy arrys, as ny jean-jee toshiaght dy ghra riu hene, Ta Abraham ain son nyn ayr: son ta mee gra riu, dy vel pooar ec Jee eer jeh ny claghyn shoh cloan y hroggal seose da Abraham. 
<b>9</b> As nish hannah ta'n teigh er ny choyrt gys fraue ny biljyn: shen-y-fa ta dy chooilley villey nagh vel gymmyrkey mess mie, dy ve giarit sheese as tilgit ayns yn aile. <b>10</b> As denee yn pobble jeh, gra, Cre ta orrinyn dy yannoo? <b>11</b> Dreggyr eh as dooyrt eh roo, Eshyn ta daa chooat echey, lhig da y choyrt dasyn nagh vel cooat eddyr echey as eshyn ta beaghey echey, lhig da jannoo er yn aght cheddin. <b>12</b> Eisht haink myrgeddin ny publicanee dy ve er nyn mashtey, as dooyrt ad rish, Vainshter, cre ta orrinyn dy yannoo?

<b>Lucas 24</b><br><b>1</b> Nish er y chied laa jeh'n chiaghtin, eer sy vadran, haink ad gys yn oaie, cur lhieu ny spisyn v'ad er gheddyn aarloo, as mraane elley maroo. <b>2</b> As hooar ad y chlagh rowlit ersooyl jeh'n oaie. <b>3</b> As hie ad stiagh, as cha dooar ad corp y Chiarn Yeesey. <b>4</b> As haink eh gy-kione, myr v'ad ayns imnea mooar son shoh, cur-my-ner hass daa ghooinney lioroo ayns coamrey sollys. 
<b>5</b> As myr v'ad ayns aggle, as croymmey sheese lesh nyn eddin gys y thalloo, dooyrt ad roo, Cre'n-fa ta shiu shirrey yn vio mastey ny merriu? <b>6</b> Cha vel eh ayns shoh, agh t'eh er n'irree: cooinee-jee cre dooyrt eh-hene riu choud's v'eh foast ayns Galilee, <b>7</b> Gra, Shegin da'n Mac dooinney v'er ny lirrey gys laueyn deiney peccoil, as ve er ny chrossey, as girree reesht er y trsss laa. <b>8</b> As chooinee ad er e ghoan, <b>9</b> As haink ad back veih yn oaie, as dinsh ad ooilley shoh da'n un ostyl yeig, as da ooilley yn vooinjer elley.
