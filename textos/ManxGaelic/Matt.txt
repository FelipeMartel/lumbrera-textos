<b>Matthew 1</b><br><b>1</b> Lioar sheeloghe Yeesey Creest, mac Ghavid, mac Abraham <b>2</b> Hooar Abraham Isaac, as hooar Isaac Jacob, as hooar Jacob Judas as e vraaraghyn. <b>3</b> As hooar Judas Phares as Zarah rish Thamar, as hooar Phales Esrom, as hooar Esrom Aram. <b>4</b> As hooar Aram Aminadab, as hooar Aminadab Naason, as hooar Naason Salmon <b>5</b> As hooar Salmon Booz rish Rachab, as hooar Booz Obed rish Ruth, as hooar Obed Jesse. <b>6</b> As hooar Jesse David yn ree, as hooar David yn ree Solomon reeish v'er ny ve ben Urias. 
<b>7</b> As hooar Solomon Roboam, as hooar Roboam Abia, as hooar Abia Asa: <b>8</b> As hooar Asa Josaphat, as hooar Josaphat Joram, as hooar Joram Ozias. <b>9</b> As hooar Ozias Joatham, as hooar Joatham Achaz, as hooar Achaz Ezekias. <b>10</b> As hooar Ezekias Manasses, as hooar Manasses Amon, as hooar Amon Josias. <b>11</b> As hooar Josias Jechonias as e vraaraghyn, mysh traa yn chappeeys Babylonagh. <b>12</b> As rish earish yn chappeeys Babylon agh hooar Jechonias Salathiel, as hooar Salathiel Zorobabel. 
<b>13</b> As hooar Zorobabel Abiud, as hooar Abiud Eliakim, as hooar Eliakim Azor. <b>14</b> As hooar Azor Sadoc, as hooar Sadoc Achim, as hooar Achim Eliud. <b>15</b> As hooar Eliud Eliazar, as hooar Eliazar Matthan, as hooar Matthan Jacob. <b>16</b> As hooar Jacob Joseph sheshey Voirrey, j'eeish ruggyr Yeesey, ta enmyssit Creest. 
<b>17</b> Myr shen ta ooilley ny sheelogheyn veih Abraham gys David, kiare sheelogheyn jeig: as veih David derrey'n chappeeys ayns Babylon, kiare sheelogheyn jeig: as veih'n chappeeys ayns Babylon derrey Creest kiare sheelogheyn jeig. <b>18</b> Nish shoh myr haink (Yeesey) Creest er y theihll: Erreish da e voir Moirrey ve nasht rish Joseph, roish my daink ad dy vaghey cooidjagh, v'ee ry-akin dy ve corrym liorish yn Spyrryd Noo. 
<b>19</b> As myr va Joseph e sheshey ny ghooinney cairal, as neu-wooiagh dy choyrt ee gys nearey foshlit, v'eh smooinaghtyn dy scarrey <b>20</b> Agh choud as va ny reddyn shoh er e aigney, cur-my-ner haink ainle y Chiarn huggey ayns ashlish, gra, Yoseph, vac Ghavid, ny bee aggle ort dy ghoaill hood Moirrey dty ven: son shen t'er ny ghientyn aynjee, te jeh'n Spyrryd Noo. <b>21</b> As ver ee mac son y theihll, as nee oo genmys eh Yeesey, son sauee eh e phobble veih nyn beccaghyn. 
<b>22</b> (Shoh myr va er ny chooilleeney ooilley ny loayr y Chiarn liorish y phadeyr, gra <b>23</b> Cur-my-ner nee moidyn gientyn, as ver ee mac son y theihll, as nee ad genmys eh Emmanuel, ta shen dy ghra, Jee marin.) <b>24</b> Tra va Joseph eisht er n'irree veih cadley, ren eh myr va ainle y Chiarn er chur roish, as ghow eh thie e ven: <b>25</b> As cha row veg echey dy yannoo r'ee, derrey hug ee e ynrycan mac son y theihll, as denmys eh yn ennym echey Yeesey. 
<b>Matthew 2</b><br><b>1</b> Nish tra rug Yeesey ayns Bethlehem dy Yudea, rish lhing ree Herod, cur-my-ner haink deiney-creeney veih'n shiar gys Jerusalem, <b>2</b> Gra, Cre vel ree ny Hewnyn t'er jeet er y theihll? son honnick shin y rollage echey ayns y shiar, as ta shin er jeet dy chur ooashley da. <b>3</b> Tra cheayll ree Herod shoh, v'eh dy mooar seaghnit, as ooilley Jerusalem marish. <b>4</b> As tra v'eh er symney cooidjagh ooilley ny ard-saggyrtyn, as scrudeyryn y phobble, denee eh jeu cre'n raad va Creest dy heet er y theihll. 
<b>5</b> As dooyrt adsyn rish, Ayns Bethlehem dy Yudea: son shoh myr te scruit liorish y phadeyr: <b>6</b> As uss Vethlehem ayns cheer Yudea, cha nee oo sloo ta mastey princeyn Yuda: son assyd's hig Kiannoort nee reill my phobble Israel. <b>7</b> Eisht hug Herod fys follit er ny deiney creeney, as deysht eh ad dy imneagh mychione y traa haink yn rollage rish. 
<b>8</b> As hug eh ad gys Bethlehem, gra, Immee-jee, as shir-jee magh dy jeidjagh son y lhiannoo, as tra vees shiu er gheddyn magh eh, tar-jee lesh fys hym's, dy voddym's neesht cheet as ooashley 'choyrt da. <b>9</b> Erreish daue v'er chlashtyn y ree, jimmee ad rhymboo, as cur-my-ner hie yn rollage honnick ad ayns y shiar er e toshiaght roue, derrey haink ee, as hass ee erskyn yn ynnyd raad va'n lhiannoo. <b>10</b> Tra honnick ad y rollage, va boggey erskyn-towse orroo. 
<b>11</b> As goll stiagh 'sy thie, hooar ad y lhiannoo marish Moirrey e voir, as gliooney sheese, hug ad ooashley da as fosley ny tashtaghyn oc, heb ad giootyn da; airh, as frankincense, as myrrh. <b>12</b> As myr va Jee er chur raaue daue ayns ashlish, gyn ad dy hyndaa gys Herod, ghow ad raad elley gys nyn jeer hene. 
<b>13</b> As tra v'ad er n'immeeaght, cur-my-ner haink ainle y Chiarn gys Joseph ayns ashlish, gra, Trog ort, gow yn lhiannoo as e voir, cosne royd gys Egypt, as fuirree ayns shen derrey ver-yms fys hood: son shirree Herod y lhiannoo dy stroie eh. <b>14</b> Eisht dirree eh, as ghow eh yn lhiannoo, as e voir ayns yn oie, as hie eh roish er-chea gys Egypt: <b>15</b> As shen-y raad v'eh derrey baase Herod: as shoh myr va cooilleenit shen loayr y Chiarn liorish y phadeyr, gra, Magh ass Egypt deie mee er my vac. 
<b>16</b> Eisht Herod toiggal dy row eh mollit liorish ny deiney-creeney, v'eh erskyn-towse eulyssagh, as hug eh magh sarey, as varr eh ooilley ny lhiennoo-mec v'ayns Bethlehem, as ayns ooilley ny ardjyn shen, veih daa vlein dy eash as fo, corrym rish y traa v'eh dy imneagh er vriaght jeh ny deiney creeney. <b>17</b> Shen y traa va cooilleenit ny va loayrit liorish Jeremy yn phadeyr, gra, 
<b>18</b> Ayns Rama va eam treih er ny chlashtyn, yllaghey as keayney, as dobberan hrimshagh, Rachel keayney son e cloan, as gobbal dy ve er ny gherjaghey, er-yn-oyr nagh row ad er-mayrn. <b>19</b> Agh lurg baase Herod, cur-my-ner haink ainle y Chiarn ayns ashlish gys Joseph ayns Egypt, <b>20</b> Gra, Irree, as gow yn lhiannoo as e voir, as immee gys cheer Israel: son t'adsyn marroo va shleeuit er bioys y lhiannoo. <b>21</b> As dirree eh, as ghow eh yn lhiannoo as e voir, as haink eh gys cheer Israel. 
<b>22</b> Agh clashtyn dy row Archelaus reill ayns Judea, ayns ynnyd e ayr Herod, va aggle er dy gholl gys shen: agh myr va Jee er chur roish ayns ashlish, hyndaa eh gys ardjyn Ghalilee. <b>23</b> As jimmee eh dy chummal ayns ard-valley enmyssit Nazareth: as shoh myr va cooilleenit shen loayr ny phadeyryn, Bee eh enmyssit ny Nazarene. 
<b>Matthew 3</b><br><b>1</b> Ayns ny laghyn shen haink Ean Bashtey, preacheil ayns faasagh Yudea, <b>2</b> As gra, Gow-jee arrys: son ta reeriaght Yee er gerrey. <b>3</b> Son shoh eshyn va loayrit jeh liorish y phadeyr Esaias, gra, Coraa fer geamagh ayns yn aasagh, Kiartee-jee raad y Chiarn, jean-jee ny cassanyn echey jeeragh. <b>4</b> As v'ec Ean cheddin coamrey dy fynney chamel, as cryss liare mysh e veeghyn; as va e veaghey locustyn as mill feie. <b>5</b> Eisht hie magh huggey Jerusalem as ooilley Judea, as Ooilley yn cheer mygeayrt Jordan. 
<b>6</b> As goaill-rish nyn beccaghyn v'ad er nyn mashtey liorish ayns Jordan. <b>7</b> Agh tra honnick eh ymmodee jeh ny Phariseeyn as ny Sadduceeyn cheet gys e vashtey, dooyrt eh roo, O shiuish heeloghe dy ard-nieughyn, quoi t'er chur raaue diu dy hea veih yn jymmoose ta ry-heet? <b>8</b> Cur-jee magh nish er-y-fa shen messyn cooie dy arrys. <b>9</b> As ny smooinee-jee dy ghra, riu hene, Ta Abraham ain son nyn ayr: son ta mee gra riu, dy vel Jee pooaral eer jeh ny claghyn shoh dy hroggal seose cloan da Abraham 
<b>10</b> As nish hene ta'n teigh er ny choyrt gys fraue ny biljyn: shen-y-fa ta dy chooilley villey nagh vel gymmyrkey magh mess mie, dy ve giarit sheese as tilgit ayns yn aile. <b>11</b> Ta mish dy jarroo bashtey shiu lesh ushtey gys arrys: agh t'eshyn ta cheet my yei ny s'pooaral ny mish, ny braagyn echey cha vel mee feeu dy ymmyrkey: bashtee eshyn shiu lesh y Spyrryd Noo as lesh aile. 
<b>12</b> Ta'n cleayst echey ayns e laue, as nee eh dy bollagh e laare-vooie y ghlenney, as gowee eh e churnaght stiagh ayns e hie-tashtee: agh loshtee eh yn choau lesh aile nagh bee er ny vooghey. <b>13</b> Eisht haink Yeesey veih Galilee gys Jordan, dy ve bashtit liorish Ean. <b>14</b> Agh va Ean noi shen, gra, Ta feme aym's dy ve er my vashtey liort's, as vel uss cheet hym's? <b>15</b> Dreggyr Yeesey, as dooyrt eh rish, Cur raad dou nish: son shoh myr te cooie dooin dy chooilley chairys y chooilleeney. Eisht lhig eh da. 
<b>16</b> As lurg da Yeesey ve bashtit, hie eh seose jeeragh ass yn ushtey: as cur-my-ner va ny niaughyn er nyn vosley er-e-skyn, as honnick eh Spyrryd Yee cheet neose myr calmane, as soie er. <b>17</b> As cur-my-ner, coraa veih niau, gra, Shoh my vac ennoil, jeh ta mee dy mooar soiaghey. 
<b>Matthew 4</b><br><b>1</b> Eisht va Yeesey er ny leeideil liorish Spyrryd Yee gys yn aasagh, dy v'er ny violaghey liorish y drogh-spyrryd. <b>2</b> As tra v'eh er drostey da-eed laa as da-eed oie v'eh ny lurg shen accryssagh. <b>3</b> As haink y mioleyder huggey, gra, My she mac Yee oo, cur sarey da ny claghyn shoh dy ve jeant nyn arran. <b>4</b> Agh dreggyr eshyn, gra, Te, scruit, Cha nee liorish arran ny lomarcan nee dooinney beaghey, agh liorish dy chooilley ockle ta cheet magh ass beeal Yee. 
<b>5</b> Eisht ghow y drogh-spyrryd lesh seose eh gys yn ard-valley casherick, as hoie eh eh er beinn toor syrjey yn chiamble, <b>6</b> As dooyrt eh rish, My she Mac Yee oo tilg oo hene sheese: son te scruit, Ver eh currym da e ainleyn my-dty-chione, as ayns nyn laueyn nee ad oo y ymmyrkey, nagh bwoaill oo ec traa erbee dty chass noi chlagh. <b>7</b> Dooyrt Yeesey rish, Te scruit myrgeddin, Cha jean oo yn Chiarn dty Yee y violaghey. 
<b>8</b> Reesht hug y drogh-spyrryd lesh eh seose er slieau feer ard, as yeeagh eh da ooilley reeriaghtyn y theihll as y ghloyr oc: <b>9</b> As dooyrt eh rish, Ooilley shoh ver-ym's dhyt's, my huittys oo sheese, as ooashley chur dou. <b>10</b> Eisht dooyrt Yeesey rish, Ass my enish oo, Noid-ny-hanmey: son te scruit, Ver oo ooashley da'n Chiarn dty Yee, as eshyn ynrycan nee oo shirveish. <b>11</b> Eisht daag y drogh-spyrryd eh, as cur-my-ner haink ainleyn as ren ad shirveish er. 
<b>12</b> Nish tra cheayll Yeesey dy row Ean ny phryssoonagh, jimmee eh dy lhiattee gys Galilee. <b>13</b> As faagail Nazareth, haink eh dy chummal ayns Capernaum, ta rish oirr ny marrey, rish ardjyn Zabulon as Nephthalim: <b>14</b> As shoh myr va cooilleenit shen ny loayr Esaias yn phadeyr, gra <b>15</b> Thalloo Zabulon, as thalloo Nephthalim, er slyst ny marrey, cheu elley dy Yordan, Galilee ny Ashoonee: 
<b>16</b> Ta'n pobble va nyn soie ayns dorraghys, er vakin soilshey mooar: as orroosyn va nyn soie ayns cheer as scadoo'n vaaish, ta soilshey er n'irree seose. <b>17</b> Veih yn traa shen ren Yeesey toshiaght dy phreacheil, as dy ghra, Gow-jee arrys, son ta reeriaght niau er-gerrey. <b>18</b> As myr va Yeesey shooyl liorish keayn Ghalilee, honnick eh daa vraar, Simon va enmyssit Peddyr, as Andreays e vraar, cur magh lieen 'sy cheayn: (son v'ad nyn eeasteyryn) <b>19</b> As dooyrt eh roo, Eiyr-jee orrym's, as neem's shiu nyn eeasteyryn dy gheiney. 
<b>20</b> As chelleeragh daag ad nyn lieenteenyn, as deiyr ad er. <b>21</b> As goll er e hoshiaght veih shen, honnick eh daa vraar elley, Jamys mac Zebedee as Ean e vraar, ayns lhong marish Zebedee nyn ayr, karraghey nyn lieen: as deie eh orroo. <b>22</b> As chelleeragh daag ad y lhong as nyn ayr, as deiyr ad er. <b>23</b> As hie Yeesey mygeayrt ooilley Galilee, gynsaghey ayns nyn gialteenyn, as preacheil sushtal y reeriaght, as lheihys dy chooilley hingys, as dy chooilley ghoghan mastey'n pobble. 
<b>24</b> As hie goo jeh magh trooid ooilley Syria: as hug ad lhieu huggey ooilley ny aslayntee, fo doghanyn as pianyn, adsyn va seaghnit lesh drogh-spyrrydyn, sleih ass nyn geeayl, as an-lheiltee, as ren eh ad y lheihys. <b>25</b> As deiyr er chaglym mooar dy leih veih Galilee, as Decapolis, as Jerusalem, as Judea, as veih cheu elley dy Yordan. 
<b>Matthew 5</b><br><b>1</b> As fakin lheid ny earrooyn dy leih, hie eh seose er slieau: as tra v'eh er hoie sheese, haink e ynseydee huggey <b>2</b> As hrog eh seose e choraa, as dynsee eh ad, gra, <b>3</b> Bannit t'adsyn ta imlee ayns spyrryd: son lhieusyn reeriaght niau. <b>4</b> Bannit t'adsyn ta dobberan: son bee ad er nyn gherjaghey. <b>5</b> Bannit t'adsyn ta meen surransagh: son bee yn thalloo oc ayns eiraght. <b>6</b> Bannit t'adsyn ta accryssagh as paagh lurg yn ynrickys: son bee ad er nyn yannoo magh. 
<b>7</b> Bannit ta'n vooinjer vyghinagh: son bee myghin er ny hoilshaghey daue. <b>8</b> Bannit t'adsyn ta glen ayns cree: son ver ad Jee my-ner. <b>9</b> Bannit t'adsyn ta tayrn sleih gys shee: son bee ad enmyssit nyn gloan dy Yee. <b>10</b> Bannit t'adsyn ta surranse tranlaase er graih cairys: son lhieusyn reeriaght niau. <b>11</b> Bannit ta shiuish tra vees shiu oltooanit, as tranlaasit, as dy chooilley ghrogh-ghoo currit ass nyn lieh dy aggairagh er y choontey aym's. 
<b>12</b> Gow-jee boggey, as bee-jee erskyn towse gennal: son s'mooar ta nyn leagh ayns niau: son shoh myr ren ad tranlaase er ny phadeyryn va rhymbiu. <b>13</b> Shiuish sollan y thallooin: agh my ta'n sollan er choayl e vlass, kys hig e vree ayn reesht? cha vel ymmyd erbee sodjey ny chour, agh dy ve tilgit magh, as stampit fo cassyn deiney. <b>14</b> Shiuish soilshey yn theihll Cha vod ard-valley ta soit er cronk ve follit. <b>15</b> Chamoo ta sleih foaddey cainle, as dy choyrt ee fo saagh; agh ayns cainleyr, as t'ee cur soilshey da'n slane lught-thie. 
<b>16</b> Lhig da'n soilshey euish myr shen soilshean fenish deiney, dy vod ad fakin ny obbraghyn mie eu, as gloyr y choyrt da nyn ayr t'ayns niau. <b>17</b> Ny smooinee-jee dy daink mish dy chur naardey yn leigh as ny phadeyryn: cha nee dy chur naardey haink mee, agh dy chooilleeney. <b>18</b> Son dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, Derrey nee yn aer as yn ooir lheie ersooyl, cha bee un ockle, ny'n floag sloo er ny ghoaill veih'n leigh, derrey vees ooilley jeant mie. 
<b>19</b> Quoi-erbee er-y-fa shen vrishys unnane jeh ny annaghyn sloo shoh, as ynsys myr shen, bee eh enmyssit yn fer sloo ayns reeriaght niau: agh quoi erbee nee nyn yannoo as nyn ynsaghey, bee eh enmyssit mooar ayns reeriaght niau. <b>20</b> Son ta mee gra riu, Mannagh bee yn ynrickys euish erskyn ynrickys ny Scrudeyryn as ny Phariseeyn, cha vod shiu er aght erbee goll stiagh ayns reeriaght niau. <b>21</b> Ta shiu er chlashtyn dy row eh er ny ghra rish nyn shenn-ayryn, Cha jean oc dunverys: as quoi-erbee nee dunverys, hig eh fo kerraghey yn vriwnys. 
<b>22</b> Agh ta mish gra riuish, Quoi-erbee ta fargagh rish e vraar gyn oyr, dy jig eshyn neesht fo kerraghey yn vriwnys: as quoi erbee jir rish e vraar, Raca: dy jig eh fo briwnys yn ard-whuaiyl: agh quoi-erbee jir, Uss ommydan, dy bee eh ayns gaue aile niurin, <b>23</b> Shen-y-fa tra hebbys oo dty oural ec yn altar, my chooinys oo ayns shen dy vel nhee erbee ec dty vraar dt'oi <b>24</b> Faag ayns shen dty oural roish yn altar, as immee royd, jean coardail rish dty vraar hoshiaght, as eisht tar, as cheb dty oural. 
<b>25</b> Coard dy tappee rishyn ta streeu rhyt, choud as vees shiu goll cooidjagh er y raad, er-aggle dy der eshyn oo fo laue yn vriw, as dy der yn briw oo fo currym y tidoor, as dy bee oo tilgit ayns pryssoon. <b>26</b> Dy firrinagh ta mee gra rhyt, Nagh jig oo magh ass shen, derrey t'ou er n'eeck yn farling s'jerree. <b>27</b> Ta shiu er chlashtyn dy row eh er ny ghra rish nyn shenn-ayryn, Cha brish oo poosey. <b>28</b> Agh ta mish gra riu, Quoi-erbee ta jeeaghyn er ben, dy chur saynt j'ee, dy vel eh er vrishey poosey r'ee hannah ayns e chree. 
<b>29</b> As my ta dty hooill yesh dy dty hayrn gys peccah, pluck assyd ee, as tilg void ee: son te ny share dhyt unnane jeh dty hooillyn y choayl, na dty slane corp dy ve tilgit ayns niurin. <b>30</b> As my ta dty laue yesh dy dty hayrn gys peccah, giare jeed, as tilg void ee: son te ny share dhyt unnane jeh dty oltyn y choayl, na dty slane corp dy ve tilgit ayns niurin. <b>31</b> Ve er ny ghra, Quoi-erbee ver e ven voish, lhig da screeuyn-scarree y chur jee. 
<b>32</b> Agh ta mish gra riu, Quoi-erbee scarrys rish e ven, agh son oyr maarderys, dy vel eh cur urree poosey y vrishey: as quoi-erbee phoosys ish ta scart, t'eh ny vaarderagh. <b>33</b> Myrgeddin ta shiu er chlashtyn dy row eh er ny ghra rish nyn shenn-ayryn, Cha jean oo loo-oaiagh y ghoaill, agh cooilleenee oo gys y Chiarn dty vreearaghyn. <b>34</b> Agh ta mish gra riu, Ny loo-jee er aght erbee; edyr liorish niau, son she stoyl-reeoil Yee eh: 
<b>35</b> Ny liorish y thalloo, son shen stoyl e choshey: ny liorish Jerusalem, son shen ard-valley yn Ard-ree. <b>36</b> Chamoo looys oo liorish dty chione, er-yn-oyr nagh vod oo un renaig y yannoo bane ny doo. <b>37</b> Agh lhig da'n ghlare eu ve, She, she, Cha nee, cha nee: son cre-erbee ta harrish shoh, te cheet veih yn olk. <b>38</b> Ta shiu er chlashtyn dy row eh er ny ghra, Sooill son sooill, as feeackyl son feeackyl. 
<b>39</b> Agh ta mish gra riu, Ny shir-jee aggair y chooilleeney: agh quoi-erbee woaillys oo er y lieckan yesh, chyndaa huggey yn lieckan elley. <b>40</b> As my ta fer erbee dy dty eignagh ec y leigh, as goaill ersooyl dty chooat, lhig dty chloagey lesh myrgeddin. <b>41</b> As quoi-erbee chionnys ort dy gholl un veeley marish, gow marish jees. <b>42</b> Cur dasyn ta jannoo aghin hood, as veihsyn ta shirrey geeasaght void, ny chyndaa ersooyl. <b>43</b> Ta shiu er chlashtyn dy row eh er ny ghra, Ver oo graih da dty naboo, as feoh da dty noid. 
<b>44</b> Agh ta mish gra riu, Bee-jee dooie da nyn noidyn, bannee-jee adsyn ta gwee mollaght diu, jean-jee mie dauesyn ta cur dwoaie diu, as gow-jee padjer er nyn son ocsyn ta dy goanlyssagh jannoo tranlaase erriu: <b>45</b> Dy vod shiu ve cloan nyn Ayr t'ayns niau, son t'eshyn cur er e ghrian girree er mie as sie, as cur fliaghey neose er y vooinjer chairagh as neu-chairagh. <b>46</b> Son my ta shiu graihagh orroosyn ta graihagh erriuish, cre'n leagh ta nyn gour? nagh vel eer ny publicanee jannoo yn lheid cheddin?

<b>Matthew 16</b><br><b>1</b> As haink ny Phariseeyn as ny Sadduceeyn dy phrowal eh, as hir ad er, eh dy yeeaghyn cowrey ennagh daue veih niau. <b>2</b> Dreggyr eshyn, as dooyrt eh roo, Tra ta'n fastyr er, jir shiuish, Bee emshyr aalin ayn: son dy vel jirgid 'syn aer. <b>3</b> As ayns y voghrey, Bee emshyr vroghe ayn jiu; son ta'n aer jiarg as dullyr. <b>4</b> She drogh-heeloghe as neu-ghooghyssagh ta shirrey cowrey, as cha bee cowrey er ny chur daue, agh cowrey yn adeyr Jonas. As daag eh ad, as jimmee eh roish. <b>5</b> Nish tra va e ostyllyn er n'aagail y cheu elley, v'ad er yarrood arran y chur <b>6</b> Eisht dooyrt Yeesey roo, Gow-jee twoaie, as bee-jee er nyn arrey noi soorid ny Phariseeyn as ny Sadduceeyn. <b>7</b> As loayr ad re-cheilley, gra, Ta shoh er yn oyr nagh dug shin lhien arran. 
<b>8</b> Tra hoig Yeesey shoh, dooyrt eh roo, Gheiney faase-chredjuagh, cre'n-fa ta shiu resooney riu hene son nagh dug shiu lhieu arran? <b>9</b> Nagh vel shiu foast toiggal, as nagh vel cooinaghtyn eu er ny queig bwilleenyn ren magh ny queig thousaneyn, as cre whilleen baskad d'ooilliagh v'eu? <b>10</b> Ny er ny shiaght bwilleenyn ren magh ny kiare thousaneyn, as cre-woad baskad hrog shiu? <b>11</b> Kys te nagh vel shiu goaill eu hene nagh nee er arran va mee cheet, agh shiu dy ghoaill twoaie jeh soorid ny Phariseeyn as ny Sadduceeyn? 
<b>12</b> Eisht hoig ad nagh dug eh raaue daue noi soorid arran, agh noi ynsagh ny Phariseeyn as ny Sadduceeyn. <b>13</b> Tra va Yeesey er jeet gys ardjyn Cesarea Philippi, denee eh jeh e ostyllyn, gra, Cre ta deiney dy ghra my-mychione's, Mac y dooinney, quoi mee hene? <b>14</b> Dooyrt adsyn, Ta paart gra dy nee Ean Bashtey oo, as paart Elias, as ta feallagh elley gra dy nee Jeremias oo, ny fer jeh ny phadeyryn. <b>15</b> Dooyrt eshyn roo, Agh quoi ta shiuish gra, dy vel mee? <b>16</b> Dreggyr Simon Peddyr, as dooyrt eh, Uss yn Creest, Mac y Jee bio. 
<b>17</b> Dansoor Yeesey, as dooyrt eh rish, Bannit t'ou uss Simon mac Jona: son cha nee feill as fuill t'er hoilshaghey shoh dhyt's agh my Ayr t'ayns niau. <b>18</b> As ta mee myrgeddin gra rhyt, Dy nee uss Peddyr, as dy nee er y chreg shoh trog-ym's my agglish: as cha vow giattyn niurin yn varriaght urree. <b>19</b> As ver-yms dhyt's ogheryn reeriaght niau: as cre-erbee chianglys oo er y thalloo, bee eh kianlt ayns niau: as cre-erbee eayslys oo er y thalloo, bee eh er ny eaysley ayns niau. 
<b>20</b> Eisht hug eh currym er e ostyllyn gyn ad dy insh da dooinney erbee, dy nee eshyn va Yeesey yn Creest. <b>21</b> Veih yn traa shen magh ren Yeesey toshiaght dy hoilshaghey da e ostyllyn, kys dy beign da goll seose gys Jerusalem, as goll fo mooarane surranse veih'n chanstyr, as ny ard-saggyrtyn, as ny scrudeyryn, as dy beagh eh er ny choyrt gy baase, agh dy n'irragh eh reesht er y trass laa. <b>22</b> Eisht ghow Peddyr eh er-lheh, as ghow eh toshiaght dy choyrlaghey eh, gra, Foddey dy row eh void, Hiarn, lheid shoh dy haghyrt dhyt. 
<b>23</b> Agh hyndaa eshyn, as dooyrt eh rish Peddyr, Ass my enish, y violeyder, t'ou cur ribbey ayns my raad: son cha vel ennaghtyn ayd jeh ny reddyn ta bentyn rish Jee, agh jeh ny reddyn ta bentyn rish deiney. <b>24</b> Eisht dooyrt Yeesey rish e ostyllyn My ta dooinney erbee aggindagh geiyrt orrym's, lhig da eh-hene y obbal, as e chrosh y hroggal, as geiyrt orrym. <b>25</b> Son quoi-erbee baillish e vioys y hauail, caillee eh eh: as quoi-erbee chaillys e vioys er my ghraih's, sauee eh eh. 
<b>26</b> Son cre'n vondeish t'ec dooinney, my chosnys eh yn seihll ooilley, as e annym hene y choayl? ny cre nee dooinney y choyrt ayns coonrey son e annym? <b>27</b> Son hig Mac y dooinney ayns gloyr e Ayrey, as e ainleyn casherick marish: as ec y traa shen cooilleenee eh da dy chooilley ghooinney cordail rish e obbraghyn. <b>28</b> Dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, Dy vel paart nyn shassoo ayns shoh, nagh jean blashtyn er y baase, derrey hee ad Mac y dooinney cheet ayns e reeriaght. 
<b>Matthew 17</b><br><b>1</b> As ec kione shey laa, ghow Yeesey Peddyr, as Jamys, as Ean e vraar, as hug eh lesh ad er-lheh seose er slieau ard, <b>2</b> As va'n cummey echey er ny chaghlaa kiongoyrt roo, as ren yn eddin echey soilshean myr y ghrian, as va'n coamrey echey gial myr y soilshey. <b>3</b> As cur-my-ner, honnick ad Moses as Elias taggloo rish. 
<b>4</b> Eisht dreggyr Peddyr as dooyrt eh rish Yeesey, Hiarn, s'maynrey dooin dy hannaghtyn ayns shoh: my she dty aigney's eh, lhig dooin ayns shoh troggal three cabbaneyn: cabbane er dty hon's, as cabbane son Moses, as cabbane son Elias. <b>5</b> Choud as v'eh foast loayrt, ren bodjal sollys skeayley harrystoo: as cur-my-ner haink coraa ass y vodjal, gra, Shoh my vac ennoil, ayn ta mee goaill slane taitnys, eaisht-jee rishyn. <b>6</b> As tra cheayll ny ostyllyn eh, huitt ad sheese er nyn eddin gys y thalloo, son s'mooar yn aggle v'orroo. 
<b>7</b> As haink Yeesey as venn eh roo, as dooyrt eh, Trog-jee erriu, as ny gow-jee aggle. <b>8</b> As tra hrog ad seose nyn sooillyn, cha vaik ad fer erbee, agh Yeesey ny-lomarcan. <b>9</b> As myr v'ad cheet neose jeh'n clieau hug Yeesey currym orroo, gra, Ny insh-jee yn ashlish da unnane erbee, derrey vees Mac yn dooinney er n'irree veih ny merriu. <b>10</b> As denee e ostyllyn jeh, gra, Cre'n-oyr jir ny scrudeyryn dy vel Elias dy heet hoshiaght? 
<b>11</b> Dansoor Yeesey as dooyrt eh roo, Hig Elias dy jarroo hoshiaght, as lhiassee eh dy chooilley nhee: <b>12</b> Agh ta mish gra riu, dy vel Elias er jeet hannah, as cha dug ad geill da, agh t'ad er ghellal rish lurg nyn aigney hene as er yn aght cheddin nee Mac y dooinney neesht surranse lioroo. <b>13</b> Eisht hoig e Ostyllyn dy nee er Ean Bashtey v'eh cheet. <b>14</b> As tra v'ad er jeet gys y cheshaght, haink huggey dooinney dy row, gliooney sheese as gra, 
<b>15</b> Hiarn, jean chymmey er my vac, son t'eh ass e cheeayl, as dy mooar seaghnit: son t'eh dy mennick tuittym ayns yn aile, as dy mennick ayns yn ushtey. <b>16</b> As hug mee lhiam eh gys dty ostyllyn, as cha yarg ad eh y lheihys. <b>17</b> Dansoor Yeesey as dooyrt eh, O heeloghe vee-chredjuagh as roonagh, caid ta mish dy ve meriu? caid ta mee dy hurranse diu? cur lhiat eh hym's ayns shoh. <b>18</b> As vaggyr Yeesey er y drogh-spyrryd, as hie eh magh ass: as va'n lhiannoo er ny lheihys veih'n oor shen. 
<b>19</b> Eisht haink e ostyllyn gys Yeesey er-lheh, as dooyrt ad, Cre liorish nagh voddagh shinyn eh y lheihys? <b>20</b> Dooyrt Yeesey roo, Kyndagh rish nyn gredjue moal: son dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, Dy beagh nyn gredjue wheesh grine dy rass mustard, jirragh shiu rish y slieau shoh, Scugh veih shoh noon gys shid, as nee eh scughey: as cha bee nhee erbee ro-ghoillee diu. <b>21</b> Ny-yeih cha vel y dooghys shoh dy ghrogh-spyrrydyn dy ve castit, agh liorish padjer as trostey. 
<b>22</b> As choud as v'ad ayns Galilee, dooyrt Yeesey roo, Bee Mac y dooinney er ny vrah gys laueyn deiney: <b>23</b> As ver ad eh gy-baase, as y trass laa nee eh girree reesht: as v'ad feer trimshagh. <b>24</b> As tra v'ad er jeet gys Capernaum, haink y vooinjer va troggal argid y cheesh gys Peddyr, gra, Nagh vel y mainshter euish geeck y cheesh? 
<b>25</b> Dooyrt eshyn, Dy row. As tra v'eh er jeet stiagh ayns y thie, va Yeesey ro-laue rish, gra, Cre t'ou smooinaghtyn, Simon, quoi jeh ta reeaghyn y thallooin troggal custom ny keesh? jeh nyn gloan hene, ny jeh joarreeyn? <b>26</b> Dooyrt Peddyr rish, Jeh joarreeyn. Dooyrt Yeesey rishyn, Eisht ta'n chloan seyr. 
<b>27</b> Ny-yeih, son nagh bee cumys er ny chur hooin, immee uss gys y cheayn, as lhig sheese dooan, as trog y chied eeast hig hood: as tra vees oo er vosley e veeal, yiow peesh dy argid ayn; shen gow, as eeck eh er my hon's as er dty hon hene. 
<b>Matthew 18</b><br><b>1</b> Ec y traa cheddin haink e ostyllyn gys Yeesey, gra, Quoi syrjey ta ayns reeriaght niau? <b>2</b> As deie Yeesey er lhiannoo beg, as hoie eh eh kiongoyrt roo <b>3</b> As dooyrt eh, Dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, Mannagh jean shiu chyndaa, as cheet dy ve myr cloan veggey, cha jed shiu stiagh ayns reeriaght niau. <b>4</b> Quoi-erbee er-y-fa shen, nee eh-hene y injillaghey myr y lhiannoo beg shoh, eh syrjey vees ayns reeriaght niau. 
<b>5</b> As quoi-erbee nee soiaghey jeh lheid y lhiannoo beg shoh ayns yn ennym aym's, t'eh jannoo soiaghey jeem's. <b>6</b> Agh quoi-erbee ver oyr da unnane jeh'n vooinjer veggey shoh ta credjal aynym's dy huittym (veih'n chredjue) veagh eh ny baare da dy beagh clagh-wyllin er ve croghit mysh e wannal, as eh dy ve baiht ayns diunid ny marrey. <b>7</b> Smerg da'n theihll kyndagh rish oyryn peccah: son shegin da nyn lheid 've ayn; agh smerg da'n dooinney, liorish ta'n loght cheet. 
<b>8</b> Shen-y-fa my ta dty laue ny dty chass hene dy dty hayrn gys yn olk, giare jeed, as tilg void ad; te ny share dhyt goll stiagh ayns bea croobagh ny baccagh, na daa laue ny daa chass y ve ayd, as oo dy ve tilgit ayns yn aile dy bragh farraghtyn. <b>9</b> As my ta dty hooill dy dty hayrn gys peccah, pluck assyd, as tilg void ee: te ny share dhyt goll stiagh ayns bea lesh un hooill, na daa hooill y ve ayd, as oo dy ve tilgit ayns aile niurin. 
<b>10</b> Cur-jee twoaie nagh soie shiu beg jeh unnane jeh'n vooinjer veggey shoh: son ta mee gra riu, dy vel ny ainleyn ocsyn ayns niau, kinjagh cur-my-ner eddin my Ayr flaunyssagh. <b>11</b> Son ta Mac y dooinney er jeet dy hauail shen ny va caillit. <b>12</b> Cre ta shiu smooinaghtyn? My ta keead keyrrey ec dooinney, as unnane jeu v'er n'gholl er-shaghryn, nagh vel eh faagail yn chiare-feed as yn nuy-jeig, as goll gys ny sleityn, as shirrey yn cheyrrey chailjey? 
<b>13</b> As my she shen dy vel eh dy gheddyn ee, dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, dy vel eh goaill ny smoo dy voggey jeh'n cheyrrey shen, ny jeh'n chiare-feed as yn nuy-jeig, nagh jagh er-shaghryn. <b>14</b> Er yn aght cheddin, cha nee aigney nyn Ayr t'ayns niau eh, unnane jeh'n vooinjer veggey shoh dy herraghtyn. <b>15</b> Myrgeddin my nee dty vraar aggair dhyt, immee as insh da e oill eddyr shiu hene nyn-lomarcan: my eaishtys eh rhyt, t'ou er chosney dty vraar. 
<b>16</b> Agh mannagh n'eaisht eh rhyt, eisht gow mayrt fer ny jees elley, ayns beeal ghaa ny three dy eanishyn dy vod dy chooilley ockle ve jeant mie. <b>17</b> As mannagh der eh geill dauesyn, insh eh da'n agglish: agh mannagh der eh geill da'n agglish, jeeagh er myr an-chreestee as myr publican. <b>18</b> Dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, Cre erbee nee shiuish y chiangley er y thalloo, bee eh kianlt ayns niau: as cre-erbee nee shiu y eaysley er y thalloo, bee eh er ny eaysley ayns niau. 
<b>19</b> Reesht ta mee gra riu, my nee jees jiuish coardail er y thalloo 'syn un aghin bee nyn aghin er ny chooilleeney liorish my Ayr t'ayns niau. <b>20</b> Son raad ta ghaa ny three er nyn jaglym cooidjagh ayns m'ennym's, shen y raad ta mish nyn mastey. <b>21</b> Eisht haink Peddyr huggey, as dooyrt eh, Hiarn, cre cha mennick as ta orrym's leih da my vraar, tra yioym aggair liorish? nee choud as shiaght keayrtyn? <b>22</b> Dooyrt Yeesey rish, Cha vel mish gra rhyt, Choud as shiaght keayrtyn: agh choud as three-feed as jeih keayrtyn shiaght. 
<b>23</b> Shen-y-fa ta reeriaght niau er ny hoylaghey gys ree dy row, baillish coontey rish e harvaantyn. <b>24</b> As tra v'eh er n'yannoo toshiaght dy choontey, haink fer huggey va lhiastyn da jeih thousane talent. <b>25</b> Agh son wheesh as nagh row echey dy eeck, doardee e hiarn eh dy ve creckit as e ven, as e chloan, as ooilley ny v'echey, as e eeaghyn dy ve eeckit. <b>26</b> Huitt y charvaant er-y-fa shen sheese, as ghuee eh er, gra, Cur daill dou, Hiarn, as nee'm ooilley y eeck dhyt. 
<b>27</b> Eisht va chiarn y charvaant shen er ny veiyghey lesh chymmey, as ren eh feaysley er, as leih eh da ny v'eh dy lhiastyn. <b>28</b> Agh hie yn charvaant cheddin magh, as hooar eh fer jeh e heshaghyn-sharvaant va lhiastyn keead ping da: as haare eh greme er, as ghow eh eh er y scoarnagh, gra, Eeck shen ny t'ou dy lhiastyn. <b>29</b> As huitt e heshey-harvaant sheese ec e chassyn, as ghuee eh er, gra, Cur daill dou as nee'm ooilley y eeck dhyt. 
<b>30</b> Agh cha lhiggagh eh lesh: agh hie eh, as hilg eh eh ayns pryssoon, derrey eeckagh eh ny v'eh dy lhiastyn. <b>31</b> Myr shen tra honnick e heshaghyn-sharvaant kys hie eh er, ghow ad dy feer olk rish, as haink ad, as dinsh ad da nyn jiarn ooilley ny va er daghyrt. <b>32</b> Eisht deie e hiarn er, as dooyrt eh rish, O ghrogh harvaant, leih mish dhyt's yn slane lhiastynys shen, er-yn-oyr dy ren oo lheid yn aghin hym: <b>33</b> Nagh lhisagh uss myrgeddin as chymmey er ve ayd er dty heshey-harvaant, eer myr va chymmey aym's ort's? 
<b>34</b> As va e hiarn jymmoosagh, as livrey eh seose eh gys laue y tidoor ny phryssoonagh, derrey eeckagh eh ooilley ny v'eh dy lhiastyn. <b>35</b> Er yn aght shoh myrgeddin nee my Ayr flaunyssagh riuish, mannagh leihee shiu veih nyn greeaghyn dy chooilley ghooinney da e vraar e loghtyn. 
<b>Matthew 19</b><br><b>1</b> As haink eh gy-kione, erreish da Yeesey v'er loayrt ny goan shoh, dy jimmee eh veih Galilee, as dy daink eh gys ardjyn Yudea, er cheu elley dy Yordan: <b>2</b> As deiyr earrooyn mooarey dy leih er, ren eh ad y lheihys ayns shen. <b>3</b> Haink ny Phariseeyn huggey myrgeddin dy phrowal eh, as dooyrt ad rish, Vel eh lowal da dooinney e ven y chur er sooyl son dy chooilley oyr? <b>4</b> Dreggyr eshyn, as dooyrt eh roo, Nagh vel shiu er lhaih, eshyn chroo ad ec y toshiaght, dy chroo eh ad fyrrynagh as bwoirrynagh? 
<b>5</b> As dooyrt eh roo, Er-yn-oyr shoh nee dooinney faagail e ayr as e voir, as lhiannee eh gys e ven: as bee ad ny-neesht un eill. <b>6</b> Shen-y-fa cha vel ad ny sodjey jees, agh yn un eill. Cre-erbee er-y-fa shen ta Jee er chur cooidjagh, ny lhig da dooinney erbee y scarrey veih-my-cheilley. <b>7</b> Dooyrt adsyn rish, Cre'n-oyr eisht doardee Moses screeuyn-scarree y choyrt, as y ven y chur ersooyl? <b>8</b> Dooyrt eshyn roo, kyndagh rish creoghys nyn greeaghyn, lhow Moses diu nyn mraane y chur ersooyl: agh ec y toshiaght cha row eh myr shen. 
<b>9</b> As ta mish gra riu, Quoi-erbee scarrys rish e ven, cheu-mooie jeh son maarderys, as phoosys ben elley, t'eh brishey-poosey: as quoi-erbee phoosys ish ta scarrit, t'eh brishey poosey: <b>10</b> Dooyrt e ostyllyn rish, My she shoh yn chooish eddyr dooinney as e ven, cha vel eh mie dy phoosey. <b>11</b> Agh dooyrt eshyn roo, Cha nee dy chooilley ghooinney oddys shen y ghoaill er, agh adsyn ny-lomarcan daue te er ny choyrt. 
<b>12</b> Son ta deiney gennish ayn, rug myr shen veih brein nyn mayrey: as ta deiney gennish ayn, v'er nyn yannoo gennish liorish deiney: as ta deiney gennish ayn, t'er n'yannoo ad-hene gennish er graih reeriaght niau. Eshyn oddys shoh y ghoaill er, lhig da 'ghoaill eh. <b>13</b> Eisht hug ad lhieu huggey cloan veggey, dy chur e laueyn orroo, as dy vannaghey ad: as hug e ostyllyn oghsan daue. <b>14</b> Agh dooyrt Yeesey, Sur-jee cloan aegey, as ny jean-jee ad y lhiettal dy heet hym's: son jeh nyn lheid ta reeriaght niau. 
<b>15</b> As hug eh e laueyn orroo, as jimmee eh veih shen. <b>16</b> As cur-my-ner, haink fer huggey, as dooyrt eh rish, Vainshter vie, cre'n obbyr ne lhisins y yannoo dy chosney yn vea veayn? <b>17</b> As dooyrt eshyn rish, Cre'n-fa t'ou genmys mish mie? cha vel ayn ny ta mie, agh unnane, ynrycan Jee: agh my sailts goll stiagh ayns bea, freill ny annaghyn. <b>18</b> Dooyrt eshyn rish, Cre ny annaghyn? Dooyrt Yeesey, Cha jean oo dunverys, Cha brish oo poosey, Cha jean oo geid, Cha n'ymmyrk oo feanish foalsey, 
<b>19</b> Cur arrym da dty ayr as da dty voir: as, Ver oo graih da dty naboo myr dhyt hene. <b>20</b> Dooyrt y dooinney aeg rish, Ooilley ny annaghyn shoh ta mee er vreayll veih my aegid: cre sodjey t'orrym dy vannoo? <b>21</b> Dooyrt Yeesey rish, My sailt ve dy firrinagh mie, immee, as creck, as cur ny t'ayd da ny boghtyn, as bee berchys ayd ayns niau; as tar as eiyr orrym's. <b>22</b> Agh tra cheayll yn dooinney aeg yn raa shen, hie eh ersooyl dy trimshagh: son va state mooar hallooin echey. 
<b>23</b> Eisht dooyrt Yeesey rish e ostyllyn, Dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, S'doillee hed dooinney berchagh stiagh ayns reeriaght niau. <b>24</b> As reesht ta mee gra riu, Te ny sassey da camel goll trooid croae snaidey, na da dooinney berchagh dy gholl stiagh ayns reeriaght Yee. <b>25</b> Tra cheayll e ostyllyn shoh, ghow ad yindys erskyn-towse, gra, Quoi eisht oddys ve er ny hauail? <b>26</b> Agh yeeagh Yeesey orroo, as dooyrt eh roo, Gys deiney ta shoh neu-phossible, agh da Jee ta dy chooilley nhee possible. 
<b>27</b> Eisht dreggyr Peddyr, as dooyrt eh rish, Cur-my-ner, ta shinyn er dreigeil ooilley, as er n'eiyrt ort's; cre yiow mainyn er-y-fa shen. <b>28</b> Dooyrt Yeesey roo, Dy firrinagh ta mee gra riu, 'Sy stayd noa, tra nee Mac y dooinney soie er stoyl-reeoil e ghloyr, dy jean shiuish myrgeddin t'er n'eiyrt orrym's soie er daa stoyl-reeoil yeig, briwnys daa heeloghe yeig Israel. 
<b>29</b> As dy chooilley unnane t'er hreigeil thieyn, ny braaraghyn, ny shayraghyn, ny ayr, ny moir, ny ben, ny cloan, ny thalloo, er graih m'ennym's, yiow ad keead filley, as eiraght ayns y vea veayn. <b>30</b> Agh bee ymmodee jeusyn ta hoshiaght, er-jerrey; as jeusyn ta er-jerrey hoshiaght. 
<b>Matthew 20</b><br><b>1</b> Son ta reeriaght niau casley rish dooinney va ny er-thie, hie magh dy moghey dy ailley labreeyn son e gharey-feeyney. <b>2</b> As tra v'eh er choardail rish ny labreeyn son ping 'sy laa, hug eh ad stiagh ayns e gharey-feeyney. <b>3</b> As hie eh magh mysh y trass oor, as honnick eh feallagh elley shassoo nyn daaue 'syn ynnyd-margee, <b>4</b> As dooyrt eh roo, Immee-jee shiuish myrgeddin gys y gharey-feeyney, as cre erbee ta cair, shen yiow shiu. As hie ad rhymboo.

<b>64</b> Cur sarey er-y-fa shen yn oaie dy ve jeant shickyr, derrey yn trass laa, er-aggle dy jig e ostyllyn ayns yn oie, as dy geid ad eh ersooyl, as dy jir ad rish yn pobble, T'eh er n'irree veih ny merriu: myr shen bee yn molteyrys s'jerree ny smessey na'n chied volteyrys. <b>65</b> Dooyrt Pilate roo, Ta watch eu immee-jee reue, jean-jee cha shickyr jeh as oddys shiu. <b>66</b> Myr shen hie ad, as ren ad yn oaie shickyr, sealal y chlagh, as soiaghey watch. 
<b>Matthew 28</b><br><b>1</b> Tra va'n doonaght harrish, as y vadran cheet rish er y chied laa jeh'n chiaghtin, haink Moirrey Malaine, as Moirrey elley dy voddagh ad yn oaie y akin. <b>2</b> As cur-my-ner va craa thallooin mooar ayn; son va ainle y Chiarn er jeet neose veih niau, as er jeet as er rowlal back yn chlagh jeh beeal yn oaie, as er hoie urree. <b>3</b> Va'n eddin echey goll-rish y tendreil, as e choamrey gial myr y sniaghtey. <b>4</b> As ayns aggle roish va ny arreyderyn er creau, as haink ad dy ve myr deiney marroo. 
<b>5</b> As dreggyr yn ainle, as dooyrt eh rish ny mraane, Ny gow-jee aggle: son ta fys aym's dy vel shiu shirrey Yeesey, v'er ny chrossey. <b>6</b> Cha vel eh ayns shoh, son t'eh er n'irree myr dooyrt eh-hene: tar-jee, jeeagh-jee er yn ynnyd raad va'n Chiarn oanluckit. <b>7</b> As immee-jee dy tappee, as insh-jee da e ostyllyn, dy vel eh er n'irree veih ny merriu; as cur-my-ner, t'eh goll reue gys Galilee, ayns shen hee shiu eh, cur-my-ner, ta mish er n'insh diu. 
<b>8</b> As jimmee ad dy tappee veih'n oaie lesh aggle as boggey mooar, as roie ad dy chur lhieu yn naight gys e ostyllyn. <b>9</b> As myr v'ad goll dy insh eh da e ostyllyn, cur-my-ner haink Yeesey nyn guail, gra, Dy vannee diu. As haink ad, as huitt ad sheese ec e chassyn, lesh nyn laueyn orroo, cur ooashley da. <b>10</b> Eisht dooyrt Yeesey roo, Ny gow-jee aggle: immee-jee, as abbyr-jee rish my vraaraghyn ad dy gholl gys Galilee, as ayns shen hee ad mee. 
<b>11</b> Nish myr v'ad goll, cur-my-ner, haink shiartanse jeh'n arrey gys yn ard-valley, as dinsh ad da ny ard-saggyrtyn ooilley ny va er daghyrt. <b>12</b> As tra v'adsyn as y chanstyr er nyn jaglym cooidjagh, as er chur nyn goyrle dy cheilley hug ad sym mooar dy argid da ny sidooryn, <b>13</b> Gra, Abbyr-jee shiuish, Haink e ostyllyn ayns yn oie, as gheid ad eh ersooyl choud as va shin nyn gadley. <b>14</b> As my chlinnys y kiannoort jeh, nee mayd eshyn y vreagey as shiuish y heyrey. 
<b>15</b> Myr shen ghow ad yn argid, as ren ad myr va currit roue: as ta'n raa shoh dy cadjin er ny insh magh mastey ny Hewnyn gys y laa t'ayn jiu. <b>16</b> Eisht hie yn un ostyl yeig roue gys Galilee, gys slieau raad va Yeesey er n'oardaghey daue. <b>17</b> As tra honnick ad eh, hug ad ooashley da: agh va paart jeu fo mee-ourys. <b>18</b> As haink Yeesey, as loayr eh roo, gra, Ta dy chooilley phooar er ny chur dou ayns niau as er y thalloo. 
<b>19</b> Immee-jee er-y-fa shen, as ynsee-jee dy chooilley ashoon, as bashtee-jee ad ayns ennym yn Ayr, as y Vac, as y spyrryd Noo: <b>20</b> Gynsaghey daue dy reayll dy chooilley nhee cre-erbee ta mish er chur ayns sarey diu: as cur-my-ner, ta mish meriu kinjagh, eer gys jerrey yn theihll.
