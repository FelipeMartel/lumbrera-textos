Colossians|4|0,7,13,19
<b>Colossians 1</b><br><b>1</b> Pauro muapositori waJesu Kristu nechido chaMwari, naTimotio hama, <b>2</b> kuvatsvene nehama dzakatendeka muna Kristu dziri paKorose: Nyasha kwamuri nerugare rwunobva kuna Mwari Baba vedu naIshe Jesu Kristu. Kuvonga kwaPauro pamusoro pavo <b>3</b> Tinovonga Mwari naBaba vaIshe wedu Jesu Kristu, tichikunyengetererai nguva dzese, <b>4</b> sezvo takanzwa zverutendo rwenyu muna Kristu Jesu, uye zverudo kuvatsvene vese, <b>5</b> nekuda kwetariro yakaisirwa imwi kumatenga, yamakagara manzwa pashoko rechokwadi cheevhangeri, 
<b>6</b> yasvika kwamuri sepanyika yesewo; ichibereka chibereko, sezvainoitawo kwamuri, kubvira pazuva ramakanzwa nekuziva nyasha dzaMwari muchokwadi; <b>7</b> sezvamakadzidzawo kuna Epafrasi, muranda anodikanwa pamwe nesu, anova mushumiri wakatendeka waKristu nekuda kwenyu, <b>8</b> wakatiudzawo zverudo rwenyu muMweya. Pauro anovanyengeterera <b>9</b> Nekuda kweizvi isuwo, kubvira pazuva ratakanzwa, hatimiri kukunyengetererai, nekukumbira kuti muzadzwe neruzivo rwechido chake pauchenjeri hwese nekunzwisisa kwemweya, 
<b>10</b> kuti mufambe zvinofanira Ishe pane zvese zvinofadza, muchibereka zvibereko pabasa rese rakanaka, muchikura paruzivo rwaMwari; <b>11</b> muchisimbiswa nesimba rese, zvichienderana nesimba rekubwinya kwake, pamoyo murefu wese nekutsungirira pamwe nemufaro; <b>12</b> muchivonga kuna Baba, vakatiita kuti tifanire kugoverana kwenhaka yevatsvene muchiedza, <b>13</b> vakatinunura pasimba rerima, vakatishandurira muushe hweMwanakomana werudo rwavo, <b>14</b> watine dzikunuro maari neropa rake, ndiko kukanganwirwa kwezvivi. 
<b>15</b> Anova mufananidzo waMwari asingaonekwi, dangwe rechisikwa chega-chega. <b>16</b> Nokuti maari zvinhu zvese zvakasikwa, zviri kumatenga nezviri panyika, zvinoonekwa nezvisingaonekwi, kana zviri zvigaro zveushe, kana umambo, kana ukuru, kana masimba; zvinhu zvese zvakasikwa naye, zvikasikirwa iye. <b>17</b> Ndiyewo anotangira zvinhu zvese, uye zvinhu zvese zvakabatanidzwa maari. <b>18</b> Uye iye musoro wemuviri, wekereke; anova kutanga, dangwe kubva kune vakafa; kuti pazvese iye ave wekutanga; 
<b>19</b> nokuti zvakafadza Baba, kuti kuzara kwese kugare maari, <b>20</b> uye ayananisa naye zvinhu zvese kwaari, aita rugare neropa remuchinjikwa wake kubudikidza naiye, kunyange zviri panyika kunyange zviri kumatenga. <b>21</b> Nemwi maimbova vaeni nevavengi mufungwa nemabasa akaipa, asi ikozvinowo wayanana nemwi <b>22</b> mumuviri wenyama yake kubudikidza nerufu, akukumikidzei muri vatsvene uye musina mhosva uye musingamhuriki pamberi pake; 
<b>23</b> zvino kana muchirambira parutendo makateyiwa uye makasimbiswa, uye hamubviswi patariro yeevhangeri yamakanzwa, yakaparidzirwa chisikwa chega-chega chiri pasi pedenga; iyo yandakaitwa mushumiri wayo ini Pauro. Kutambudzika kwaPauro pamusoro pavo <b>24</b> Zvino ikozvino ndinofara pamatambudziko angu nekuda kwenyu, nekuzadzisa zvakasara pamatambudziko aKristu munyama yangu nekuda kwemuviri wake, unova kereke; <b>25</b> ini yandakaitwa mushumiri wayo zvichienderana nekubatiswa kwaMwari chandakapiwa pamusoro penyu, kuzadzisa shoko raMwari, 
<b>26</b> chakavanzika chakange chakavanzwa kubvira kare uye kubva kumazera, asi ikozvino chinoratidzwa kuvatsvene vake, <b>27</b> kune ivo Mwari vaakada kuzivisa kuti fuma yekubwinya kwechakavanzika ichi pakati pevahedheni chii, chinova Kristu mamuri, tariro yekubwinya; <b>28</b> watinoparidza isu, tichiyambira munhu umwe neumwe, nekudzidzisa munhu umwe neumwe munjere dzese, kuti tikumikidze munhu umwe neumwe akakwana muna Kristu Jesu; <b>29</b> ndizvo zvandinoshingairirawo, ndichirwa zvinoenderana nekubata kwake kunobata mandiri nesimba. 
<b>Colossians 2</b><br><b>1</b> Nokuti ndinoda kuti muzive kuti kurwa kukuru zvakadini kwandinako pamusoro penyu, nevari paRaodhikia, nevese vasina kuona chiso changu panyama, <b>2</b> kuti moyo yavo inyaradzwe, vasonanidzwe murudo, nemufuma yese yekuvimbiswa kwakakwana kwekunzwisisa, paruzivo rwechakavanzika chaMwari naBaba, nechaKristu, <b>3</b> maari fuma yese yeuchenjeri neyeruzivo yakavigwa. <b>4</b> Ndinoreva izvi, kuti kusava nemunhu anokunyengerai nemashoko anonyengera. 
<b>5</b> Nokuti kunyange ndisipo panyama, zvakadaro ndinemwi pamweya, ndichifara nekuvona kurongeka kwenyu, nekusimba kwerutendo rwenyu muna Kristu. Anovanyevera kuti varege kutsauswa <b>6</b> Naizvozvo sezvamakagamuchira Kristu Jesu Ishe, fambai maari, <b>7</b> makadzika midzi nekuvakwa maari, nekusimbiswa murutendo, sezvamakadzidziswa, muchiwedzera makuri nekuvonga. <b>8</b> Chenjerai, zvimwe umwe munhu angakupambai neuchenjeri, nekunyengera kusina maturo, setsika dzevanhu, sedzidziso dzekuvamba dzenyika, uye kwete sekuna Kristu. 
<b>9</b> Nokuti kuzara kwese kweuMwari kunogara maari pamuviri. <b>10</b> Uye makazadziswa maari, iye musoro weutungamiriri hwese nesimba; <b>11</b> wamakadzingiswawo maari nedzingiso isina kuitwa nemaoko, pakubvisa muviri wezvivi zvenyama, pakudzingisa kwaKristu; <b>12</b> makavigwa pamwe naye pabhabhatidzo, pamakamutswawo pamwe naye nerutendo rwekuita kwaMwari, wakamumutsa kubva kuvakafa. <b>13</b> Nemwi makafa mukudarika nekusadzingiswa kwenyama yenyu, wakakuitai vapenyu pamwe naye, akukanganwirai kudarika kwese, 
<b>14</b> akadzima rugwaro rwezviga rwaipikisana nesu, rwaipesana nesu, akarubvisa pakati, aruroverera pamuchinjikwa; <b>15</b> akurura vatungamiriri nemasimba, akavabudisa pachena, akatungamira kuvakunda mauri. <b>16</b> Naizvozvo kusava nemunhu anokutongai pakudya kana pachinwiwa, kana panhau yemutambo kana yekugara kwemwedzi kana yemasabata; <b>17</b> zvinova mumvuri wezvinhu zvichauya, asi muviri ndewa Kristu. 
<b>18</b> Kusava nemunhu anokutorerai mubairo wenyu nemano, achifarira kuzvininipisa nekunamata vatumwa, achipindira pazvinhu zvaasina kuona, achizvikudza pasina nefungwa dzake dzenyama, <b>19</b> uye asingabatisisi Musoro, uyo maari muviri wese nezvisungo nemarunda unoriritirwa, nekurukirwa pamwe, uchikura nekukura kwaMwari. <b>20</b> Naizvozvo kana makafa pamwe naKristu kune dzidziso dzekuvamba dzenyika, munosungikanirei nemirairo semugere munyika, <b>21</b> (usabata, usaravira, usagunzva, 
<b>22</b> izvo zvese zvichaparara nekushandiswa) semirairo nedzidziso dzevanhu? <b>23</b> Zvine chimiro cheuchenjeri pakuzvidira kunamata, nekuzvininipisa, nekutambudza muviri; hazvitongobatsiri kukunda kugutsa nyama. 
<b>Colossians 3</b><br><b>1</b> Naizvozvo kana makamutswa pamwe naKristu, tsvakai zvekumusoro, uko kuna Kristu agere kuruoko rwerudyi rwaMwari. <b>2</b> Rangarirai zviri kumusoro, kwete zviri panyika. <b>3</b> Nokuti makafa, uye upenyu hwenyu hwakavigwa pamwe naKristu muna Mwari. <b>4</b> Kana Kristu upenyu hwedu achionekwa, ipapo imwiwo muchaonekwa pamwe naye mukubwinya. <b>5</b> Naizvozvo urayai mitezo yenyu iri panyika, upombwe, tsvina, kushumba kwakaipa, kuda zvakaipa, uye kuchiva, kunova kunamata zvifananidzo. 
<b>6</b> Nekuda kwezvinhu izvi, hasha dzaMwari dzinouya pavana vekusateerera; <b>7</b> imwiwo maimbofamba mazviri, pamairarama mazviri. <b>8</b> Asi ikozvino imwiwo bvisai zvese izvi, kutsamwa, hasha, uipi, kunyomba, nekutaura zvinonyadzisa kubva mumuromo wenyu; <b>9</b> regai kureverana nhema, zvamakabvisa munhu wekare nemabasa ake, <b>10</b> mukapfeka mutsva, anovandudzwa paruzivo semufananidzo wewakamusika; 
<b>11</b> apo pasina muGiriki uye muJudha, kudzingiswa uye kusadzingiswa, mutorwa, muSikitia, musungwa, wakasununguka; asi Kristu ndiye zvese, uye ari mune zvese. <b>12</b> Naizvozvo pfekai sevasanangurwa vaMwari, vatsvene nevanodikanwa, moyo yetsitsi, moyo munyoro, kuzvininipisa, unyoro, nemoyo murefu; <b>13</b> muchiitirana moyo murefu, nekukanganwirana kana umwe ane mhosva neumwe; Kristu sezvaakakukanganwiraiwo, saizvozvowo imwi; <b>14</b> zvino pamusoro pezvinhu zvese izvi pfekai rudo, ndicho chisungo chekupedzeredza. 
<b>15</b> Nerugare rwaMwari ngarwutonge mumoyo yenyu, ndirwo rwamakadanirwawo mumuviri mumwe; uye ivai vanovonga. <b>16</b> Shoko raKristu ngarigare mamuri neufumi muuchenjeri hwese; muchidzidzisana nekurairana mumapisarema, nenziyo, nenziyo dzemweya, muimbire Ishe nenyasha mumoyo wenyu; <b>17</b> uye zvese chero chipi chamunoita pashoko kana pachiito, itai zvese muzita raIshe Jesu, muchivonga Mwari naBaba kubudikidza naye. VaKristu mumba mavo <b>18</b> Vakadzi zviisei pasi pevarume venyu pachenyu, sezvo zvakafanira muna Ishe. 
<b>19</b> Varume, idai vakadzi venyu, uye regai kuvavavira. <b>20</b> Vana, teererai vabereki pazvinhu zvese, nokuti izvi zvinofadza Ishe. <b>21</b> Madzibaba, musatsamwisa vana venyu, kuti varege kuora moyo. <b>22</b> Varanda, teererai pazvinhu zvese vari vatenzi venyu panyama; kwete nekushandira maziso sevafadzi vevanhu, asi nemoyo wakarurama, muchitya Mwari; <b>23</b> uye zvese kunyange chipi chamunoita, itai nemoyo, sekuna Ishe, uye kwete kuvanhu; <b>24</b> muchiziva kuti kuna Ishe muchagamuchira mubairo wenhaka; nokuti munoshandira Ishe Kristu. 
<b>25</b> Asi anoita zvisakarurama achagamuchira zvisakarurama zvaakaita; uye hakuna kusarurwa kwavanhu. 
<b>Colossians 4</b><br><b>1</b> Madzishe, ipai varanda zvakarurama nezvakaringana, muchiziva kuti imwiwo muna Ishe kumatenga. Kutsungirira pakunamata <b>2</b> Tsungirirai pamunyengetero, murinde pakuri nekuvonga; <b>3</b> mutinyengetererewo panguva imweyo, kuti Mwari atizarurire mukova weshoko, kutaura chakavanzika chaKristu, chandakasungirwawo; <b>4</b> kuti ndichiratidze, sezvandinofanira kutaura. <b>5</b> Fambai muuchenjeri kune vari kunze, muchidzikunura nguva. 
<b>6</b> Shoko renyu ngarigare munyasha, rakarungwa nemunyu, kuti muzive kuti munofanira kupindura sei umwe neumwe. Anotuma Tikiko naOnesimo <b>7</b> Zvese zvakanangana neni Tikiko achakuzivisai, hama inodikanwa nemushumiri wakatendeka, nemushandi pamwe neni muna Ishe; <b>8</b> wandatumira kwamuri ichochi, kuti azive zviri maererano nemwi, nekunyaradza moyo yenyu; <b>9</b> pamwe naOnesimo, hama yakatendeka inodikanwa, anova wekwenyu. Vachakuzivisai zvese zviri pano. Kuwonekana 
<b>10</b> Arisitako musungwa pamwe neni anokukwazisai, naMariko muzukuru waBhanabhasi, wamakagamuchira mirairo maererano naye; kana achisvika kwamuri, mumugamuchire, <b>11</b> naJesu anonzi Justo, vevekudzingiswa; ivava chete vabati pamwe neni paushe hwaMwari, vaiva nyaradzo kwandiri. <b>12</b> Epafrasi wekwenyu, muranda waKristu, anokukwazisai, achirwa nguva dzese muminyengetero nekuda kwenyu, kuti mumire makazara uye makakwana muchido chese chaMwari. <b>13</b> Nokuti ndinomupupurira kuti ane shungu kwazvo nemwi, neveRaodhikia, neveHierapori. 
<b>14</b> Anokukwazisai Ruka chiremba anodikanwa, naDhemasi. <b>15</b> Kwazisai hama dziri Raodhikia, naNimifa, nekereke iri mumba make. <b>16</b> Zvino kana tsamba iyi yaverengwa pakati penyu, itai kuti nemukereke yeVaRaodhikia iverengwe; kuti nemwi muverengewo inobva Raodhikia. <b>17</b> Vudzai Akipo, muti: Chenjerera ushumiri hwawakagamuchira muna Ishe, kuti uhupedzise. <b>18</b> Kwaziso neruoko rwangu rwaPauro. Rangarirai zvisungo zvangu. Nyasha ngadzive nemwi. Ameni.
