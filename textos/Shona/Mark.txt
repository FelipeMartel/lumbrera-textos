<b>Marcos 1</b><br><b>1</b> Kutanga kweevhangeri yaJesu Kristu, Mwanakomana waMwari; <b>2</b> sezvazvakanyorwa muvaporofita zvichinzi: Tarira, ini ndinotuma mutumwa wangu pamberi pechiso chako, achagadzira nzira yako pamberi pako. <b>3</b> Inzwi reanodanidzira murenje, richiti: Gadzirai mugwagwa waIshe; ruramisai nzira dzake. <b>4</b> Johwani wakati achibhabhatidza murenje, achiparidza rubhabhatidzo rwekutendeukira kukanganwiro yezvivi; 
<b>5</b> zvino kwakabudira kwaari nyika yese yeJudhiya, naivo veJerusarema, ndokubhabhatidzwa vese naye murwizi rweJoridhani vachireurura zvivi zvavo. <b>6</b> Uye Johwani wakange akapfeka makushe ekamera, nebhanhire rechikumba rakapoteredza chiuno chake, waidya mhashu neuchi hwedondo. <b>7</b> Akaparidza, achiti: Kunouya ane simba kupfuura ini shure kwangu, wandisina kufanira kukotama kuti ndisunungure tambo yeshangu dzake. <b>8</b> Ini zvirokwazvo ndakubhabhatidzai nemvura; asi iye achakubhabhatidzai neMweya Mutsvene. Kubhabhatidzwa kwaJesu 
<b>9</b> Zvino zvakaitika nemazuva ayo kuti Jesu wakabva Nazareta yeGarirea, akabhabhatidzwa naJohwani muna Joridhani. <b>10</b> Pakarepo achibuda mumvura, wakaona matenga akazarurwa, neMweya senjiva achiburukira pamusoro pake. <b>11</b> Zvino kwakaitika inzwi richibva kumatenga richiti: Iwe uri Mwanakomana wangu anodikanwa, wandinofara maari kwazvo. Kuedzwa kwaJesu <b>12</b> Zvino pakarepo Mweya wakamutinhira kurenje. 
<b>13</b> Akavako murenje mazuva makumi mana achiidzwa naSatani, uye wakange ane mhuka dzesango, vatumwa vachimushandira. Jesu anodana vadzidzi vekutanga <b>14</b> Zvino shure kwekukumikidzwa kwaJohwani, Jesu wakasvika muGarirea, achiparidza evhangeri yeushe hwaMwari, <b>15</b> achiti: Nguva yazadziswa, uye ushe hwaMwari hwaswedera; tendeukai, mutende evhangeri. <b>16</b> Wakati achifamba-famba parutivi rwegungwa reGarirea, akaona Simoni naAndiriya munin'ina wake vachikandira mumbure mugungwa; nokuti vakange vari varedzi. 
<b>17</b> Jesu akati kwavari: Nditeverei, ndichakuitai muve varedzi vevanhu. <b>18</b> Pakarepo vakasiya mimbure yavo, ndokumutevera. <b>19</b> Wakati apfuura zvishoma ipapo, akaona Jakobho waZebhedhi naJohwani munin'ina wake, naivo vari muchikepe vachigadzira mimbure. <b>20</b> Pakarepo akavadana; ndokusiya baba vavo Zebhedhi muchikepe nevarikichi, vakamutevera. Jesu anoporesa munhu waiva nedhimoni paKapenaume <b>21</b> Zvino vakapinda muKapenaume; zvino pakarepo nesabata wakapinda musinagoge, akadzidzisa. 
<b>22</b> Vakashamisika nedzidziso yake; nokuti wakavadzidzisa seane simba, kwete sevanyori. <b>23</b> Zvino kwaiva musinagoge ravo nemunhu wakange ane mweya wetsvina, akadanidzira, <b>24</b> achiti: Regai, tinei nemwi, Jesu weNazareta? Mauya kuzotiparadza here? Ndinokuzivai kuti ndimwi ani: Mutsvene waMwari. <b>25</b> Jesu ndokumutsiura achiti: Nyarara, ubude maari! <b>26</b> Zvino mweya wetsvina wakati amugwinhisa, ndokudanidzira nenzwi guru, akabuda kwaari. 
<b>27</b> Vakashamisika vese, kusvikira vabvunzana, vachiti: Chii ichi? Idzidziso itsva yei iyi? Nokuti nesimba anoraira kunyange mweya yetsvina, uye inomuteerera. <b>28</b> Zvino pakarepo shoko rake rikabudira panyika yese yakapoteredza Garirea. Jesu anoporesa mai vemukadzi waPetro nevamwe <b>29</b> Zvino pakarepo vakati vachibuda musinagoge, vakapinda mumba maSimoni naAndiriya vana Jakobho naJohwani. <b>30</b> Asi mai vemukadzi waSimoni vakange varere vane fivhiri, pakarepo vakamuudza nezvavo; 
<b>31</b> akaswedera, akavabata ruoko, akavamutsa; fivhiri ikavarega pakarepo, vakavashandira. <b>32</b> Zvino ava madekwani, zuva rovira, vakauisa kwaari vese vakange vachirwara nevakange vane madhimoni. <b>33</b> Zvino guta rese rakange rakaungana pamukova. <b>34</b> Akaporesa vazhinji vakange vachirwara nezvirwere zvakasiyana-siyana, nekubudisa madhimoni mazhinji; uye haana kutendera madhimoni kutaura, nokuti aimuziva. <b>35</b> Zvino mangwanani kuchine rima guru, wakasimuka, akabuda, akaenda kunzvimbo murenje, akanonyengeterapo. 
<b>36</b> Simoni nevakange vanaye vakamutsvakisisa. <b>37</b> Zvino vakati vamuwana vakati kwaari: Vese vanokutsvakai. <b>38</b> Ndokubva ati kwavari: Ngatiende kumisha iri pedo, kuti ndinoparidzawo ikoko, nokuti ndizvo zvandakabudira. <b>39</b> Akabva aparidza mumasinagoge avo paGarirea rese, achibudisa madhimoni. Jesu anoporesa munhu ane maperembudzi <b>40</b> Zvino kwakauya kwaari ane maperembudzi, achimukumbirisa, ndokumufugamira achiti kwaari: Kana muchida munogona kundinatsa. 
<b>41</b> Jesu akanzwa tsitsi, akatandavadza ruoko, akamubata akati kwaari: Ndinoda, natswa; <b>42</b> achangotaura, maperembudzi akabva kwaari pakarepo, akanatswa. <b>43</b> Zvino akamuraira zvikuru, akamurega achienda pakarepo, <b>44</b> akati kwaari: Ona kuti urege kuudza munhu chinhu; asi enda, uzviratidze kumupristi, ubaire pamusoro pekunatswa kwako Mozisi zvaakaraira, chive chapupu kwavari. 
<b>45</b> Asi iye wakabuda akatanga kuparidza zvikuru nekuvavaza kunze shoko iro, zvekuti wakange asingachagoni kupinda muguta pachena, asi wakange ari kunze kunzvimbo dzerenje. Vakauya kwaari vachibva kumativi ese. 
<b>Marcos 2</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakapindazve muKapenaume pashure pemazuva; zvikanzwikwa kuti ari mumba. <b>2</b> Zvino pakarepo vazhinji vakaungana zvekuti pakange pasisina nzvimbo, kunyange pamukova; akataura shoko kwavari. <b>3</b> Vakauya kwaari, vakauisa wakange akafa mitezo, akatakurwa nevanhu vana. <b>4</b> Zvino vakati vasingagoni kusvika pedo naye nekuda kwechaunga, vakapfurunyura denga paakange ari, vakati varidzura vakaburusira hwanyanza hwaakange arere pahuri iye wakange akafa mitezo. 
<b>5</b> Jesu wakati aona rutendo rwavo akati kune wakange akafa mitezo: Mwana, zvivi zvako zvakanganwirwa kwauri. <b>6</b> Zvino vamwe vevanyori vakange vagerepo, vakafunga mumoyo yavo vachiti: <b>7</b> Uyu anotaurirei kunyomba sezvizvi? Ndiani anogona kukanganwira zvivi kunze kweumwe chete, Mwari? <b>8</b> Zvino pakarepo Jesu achiziva mumweya wake kuti vanofunga saizvozvo mukati mavo wakati kwavari: Munofungirei zvinhu izvi mumoyo yenyu? 
<b>9</b> Chii chakareruka zvikuru, kuti kune wakafa mitezo: Zvivi zvako zvakanganwirwa kwauri; kana kuti: Simuka, utore hwanyanza hwako, ufambe? <b>10</b> Asi kuti muzive kuti Mwanakomana wemunhu ane simba panyika rekukanganwira zvivi (akati kune wakange akafa mitezo): <b>11</b> Ndinoti kwauri: Simuka utore hwanyanza hwako, uende kumba kwako. <b>12</b> Zvino pakarepo wakasimuka, akatora hwanyanza, ndokubuda pamberi pavo vese; zvekuti vese vakashamisika, vakarumbidza Mwari, vachiti: Hatina kumbozviona zvakadai. Kudanwa kwaRevhi 
<b>13</b> Wakabudazve parutivi rwegungwa, chaunga chese chikauya kwaari, akavadzidzisa. <b>14</b> Wakati achipfuura, akaona Revhi waArifiyosi agere paimba yemutero, akati kwaari: Nditevere. Akasimuka akamutevera. <b>15</b> Zvino zvakaitika agere pakudya mumba make, kuti vateresi vazhinji nevatadzi vakagarawo pamwe naJesu nevadzidzi vake; nokuti vakange vari vazhinji vakamutevera. <b>16</b> Zvino vanyori neVaFarisi vakati vamuona achidya nevateresi nevatadzi, vakati kuvadzidzi vake: Nemhaka yei achidya nekunwa nevateresi nevatadzi? 
<b>17</b> Jesu wakati achizvinzwa akati kwavari: Vatano havadi chiremba, asi vanorwara. Handina kuuya kuzodana vakarurama, asi vatadzi mukutendeuka. Kutsanya <b>18</b> Uye vadzidzi vaJohwani neveVaFarisi vaitsanya; vakauya vakati kwaari: Nemhaka yeyi vadzidzi vaJohwani neveVaFarisi vachitsanya asi vadzidzi venyu havatsanyi? <b>19</b> Jesu akati kwavari: Vana veimba yemuchato vanogona kutsanya muwani achiri kwavari here? Kana vachingova nemuwani kwavari, havagoni kutsanya; 
<b>20</b> asi mazuva achasvika apo muwani achabviswa kwavari, ipapo mumazuva ayo vachatsanya. <b>21</b> Uye hakuna anosonera chigamba chemucheka usakaunyana panguvo sharu; kana zvikasadaro chinoiswa chinobvaruka kubva pachiri, chitsva kubva pane chakasakara, buri rikanyanya. <b>22</b> Uye hakuna anodira waini itsva muhombodo sharu; kana zvikasadaro waini itsva inoparusa hombodo, waini ikateuka nehombodo ikaparara; asi waini itsva inofanira kudirwa muhombodo itsva. Jesu Ishe wesabata 
<b>23</b> Zvino zvakaitika kuti achigura nemuminda yezviyo nesabata, vadzidzi vake ndokutanga kutanha hura vachifamba. <b>24</b> VaFarisi ndokuti kwaari: Tarira, vanoitirei nesabata izvo zvisiri pamutemo? <b>25</b> Iye akati kwavari: Hamuna kutomboverenga here Dhavhidhi zvaakaita, paakange ashaiwa, ava nenzara, iye nevakange vanaye? <b>26</b> Kuti wakapinda sei mumba maMwari nenguva yaAbhiatari mupristi mukuru, akadya zvingwa zvekuratidza zvakange zvisiri pamutemo kudyiwa, kunze nevapristi, akapawo kwavari vaiva naye? 
<b>27</b> Akati kwavari: Sabata rakaitirwa munhu, kwete munhu kuitirwa sabata. <b>28</b> Naizvozvo Mwanakomana wemunhu ndiIshe kunyange wesabata. 
<b>Marcos 3</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakapindazve musinagoge, zvino kwakange kune munhuko wakange ane ruoko rwakawonyana. <b>2</b> Zvino vakamutarisisa kuona kana angamuporesa nesabata, kuti vamupomere. <b>3</b> Zvino akati kumunhu waiva neruoko rwakawonyana: Simuka uende pakati. <b>4</b> Akati kwavari: Zviri pamutemo here kuita zvakanaka nemasabata, kana kuita zvakaipa? Kuponesa upenyu, kana kuuraya? Asi vakanyarara. 
<b>5</b> Zvino wakati aringa-ringa kwavari, nekutsamwa, ane shungu nekuda kwekuoma kwemoyo wavo, akati kumunhu: Tandavadza ruoko rwako, ndokutandavadza, ruoko rwake ndokuporeswa rwukagwinya serumwe. <b>6</b> VaFarisi vakabuda pakarepo vakarangana neVaHerodhe vachimupikisa kuti vangamuparadza sei. Jesu anoporesa vazhinji <b>7</b> Zvino Jesu akabva nevadzidzi vake vakaenda kugungwa; chaunga chikuru chikamutevera chichibva kuGarirea, nekuJudhiya, 
<b>8</b> nekuJerusarema, nekuIdhumeya, nemhiri kwaJoridhani, nevakapoteredza Tire neSidhoni, chaunga chikuru, vakati vanzwa makuriro ezvinhu zvaanoita vakauya kwaari. <b>9</b> Akataura kuvadzidzi vake kuti igwa diki rimugarire nekuda kwechaunga, kuti varege kumutsikirira. <b>10</b> Nokuti wakange aporesa vazhinji zvekuti vaiwira pamusoro pake kuti vamubate vese vakange vane zvirwere. <b>11</b> Nemweya yetsvina, payakamuona yakawira pasi pamberi pake, ikadanidzira ichiti: Imwi muri Mwanakomana waMwari. 
<b>12</b> Akairairisa zvikuru kuti irege kumubudisa pachena. Jesu anodana vadzidzi vake <b>13</b> Zvino wakakwira mugomo, akadanira kwaari vaakange achida iye; vakauya kwaari. <b>14</b> Akagadza gumi nevaviri, kuti vave naye, nokuti avatume kunoparidza, <b>15</b> uye vave nesimba rekuporesa zvirwere nekubudisa madhimoni. <b>16</b> NaSimoni akamutumidza zita rinonzi Petro, <b>17</b> naJakobho waZebhedhi, naJohwani munin'ina waJakobho; akavatumidza mazita anonzi Bowanegesi, ndokuti, vanakomana vekutinhira; 
<b>18</b> naAndiriya, naFiripi, naBhatoromiyo, naMatewu, naTomasi, naJakobho waArifiyosi, naTadhiyo, naSimoni muKenani, <b>19</b> naJudhasi Isikariyoti, iye wakamutengesawo. Ndokubva vapinda mumba. Kumhura Mweya Mutsvene <b>20</b> Chaunga chikaunganazve zvekuti vakange vasingagoni kunyange kudya chingwa. <b>21</b> Vekwake vakati vachizvinzwa vakabuda kunomubata; nokuti vakati: Anopenga. <b>22</b> Nevanyori vakaburuka vachibva kuJerusarema vakati: Ana Bheerizebhuri, uye kuti: Anobudisa madhimoni nemutungamiriri wemadhimoni. 
<b>23</b> Zvino akavadanira kwaari, akati kwavari nemifananidzo: Satani angagona kubudisa Satani sei? <b>24</b> Kana ushe hwakapesana huchizvipikisa, ushe uhwo hahungagoni kumira. <b>25</b> Kana imba yakapesana ichizvipikisa, imba iyo haigoni kumira. <b>26</b> Uye kana Satani achizvimukira, akapesana, haangagoni kumira, asi anoguma. <b>27</b> Hakuna angagona kupinda mumba mechikakarara, akapamba nhumbi dzacho, kunze kwekuti atanga kusunga chikakarara, ndokuzopamba imba yacho. 
<b>28</b> Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Zvivi zvese zvichakanganwirwa kuvanakomana vevanhu, nekunyomba kwese kwavanonyomba nako; <b>29</b> asi ani nani anonyomba achipikisa Mweya Mutsvene haana kanganwiro kusvikira narinhi asi ane mhosva yekutongwa kusingaperi. <b>30</b> Nokuti vakati: Ane mweya wetsvina. Ukama naJesu <b>31</b> Zvino kwakauya vanin'ina namai vake, vakamira kunze, vakatumira nhume kwaari vachimudana. <b>32</b> Chaunga chakange chigere chakamukomba; vakati kwaari: Tarirai, mai venyu nevanin'ina venyu vari panze vanokutsvakai. 
<b>33</b> Akavapindura achiti: Mai vangu ndiani kana vanin'ina vangu? <b>34</b> Zvino akaringa-ringa kupoteredza avo vakange vagere vakamukomba akati: Tarirai mai vangu nevanin'ina vangu. <b>35</b> Nokuti ani nani anoita kuda kwaMwari, ndiye munin'ina wangu nehanzvadzi yangu namai. 
<b>Marcos 4</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakatangazve kudzidzisa parutivi rwegungwa; kukaungana kwaari chaunga chikuru zvekuti wakapinda muchikepe akagara mugungwa; uye chaunga chese chakange chiri pagungwa panyika. <b>2</b> Akavadzidzisa zvinhu zvizhinji nemifananidzo, akati kwavari padzidziso yake: <b>3</b> Inzwai; tarirai, mukushi wakabuda kunokusha. <b>4</b> Zvino zvakaitika pakukusha, imwe yakawira parutivi rwenzira; shiri dzekudenga dzikauya dzikaidya dzikaipedza. 
<b>5</b> Asi imwe yakawira panzvimbo dzine mabwe, payakange isina ivhu zhinji; pakarepo ikamera, nokuti yakange isina udziku hwevhu; <b>6</b> asi zuva rakati rabuda, yakapiswa, uye nokuti yakange isina mudzi, yakasvava. <b>7</b> Uye imwe yakawira paminzwa, minzwa ikakura ikaivhunga, ikasabereka chibereko. <b>8</b> Uye imwe yakawira muvhu rakanaka; ikabereka chibereko chakakura ikawedzera, ikabereka, imwe makumi matatu, neimwe makumi matanhatu, neimwe zana. <b>9</b> Ndokuti kwavari: Ane nzeve dzekunzwa, ngaanzwe. 
<b>10</b> Zvino wakati ava ega, vakange vakamukomba nevanegumi nevaviri vakamubvunza zvemufananidzo. <b>11</b> Uye akati kwavari: Kwamuri kwakapiwa kuziva chakavanzika cheushe hwaMwari; asi kune avo vari kunze, zvese zvinoitwa nemifananidzo; <b>12</b> kuti vachiona vaone asi varege kuonesesa, vachinzwa vanzwe asi vasinganzwisisi, zvimwe chero nguva varege kutendeuka, vakanganwirwe zvivi. <b>13</b> Zvino akati kwavari: Hamuzivi mufananidzo uyu here? Zvino muchaziva sei mifananidzo yese? <b>14</b> Mukushi unokusha shoko. 
<b>15</b> Zvino ava ndivo veparutivi rwenzira, apo panokushwa shoko, uye kana vachirinzwa, pakarepo Satani anouya ndokubvisa shoko rakakushwa mumoyo yavo. <b>16</b> Uye saizvozvo ava ndivo vakakushwa panzvimbo dzine mabwe, ivo kana vanzwa shoko, pakarepo vanorigamuchira nemufaro, <b>17</b> asi havana mudzi mavari, asi ndevenguva; pashure kana matambudziko kana kushushwa zvichiuya nekuda kweshoko, pakarepo vanogumburwa. <b>18</b> Zvino ava ndivo vanokushwa muminzwa, vanonzwa shoko, 
<b>19</b> asi kufunganya kwenyika ino nekunyengera kwefuma neruchiva pamusoro pezvimwe zvinhu zvichipinda, zvichivhunga shoko, rova risingabereki. <b>20</b> Naava ndivo vakakushwa muvhu rakanaka, ndivo vanonzwa shoko, ndokugamuchira, vagobereka chibereko, umwe makumi matatu, uye umwe makumi matanhatu, uye umwe zana. Mufananidzo wemwenje <b>21</b> Zvino wakati kwavari: Mwenje unouiswa kuti uiswe pasi pedengu kana pasi pemubhedha here? Kwete kuti uiswe pachigadziko chemwenje here? 
<b>22</b> Nokuti hapana chakavigwa chisingazovhundunurwi, kana chakachengetwa chakavanda, asi kuti zvibude pachena. <b>23</b> Kana munhu ane nzeve dzekunzwa, ngaanzwe. <b>24</b> Zvino wakati kwavari: Chenjererai zvamunonzwa; nechiyero chamunoyera nacho chichayerwa kwamuri, nekwamuri imwi munonzwa, chichawedzerwa. <b>25</b> Nokuti ani nani anacho achapiwa; asi asina achatorerwa kunyange nechaanacho. Kukura kweushe hwekudenga <b>26</b> Zvino akati: Hwakadaro ushe hwaMwari, sezvinonzi munhu anokusha mbeu muvhu. 
<b>27</b> Zvino orara nekumuka usiku nemasikati, mbeu inomera ichikura iye asingazivi kuti sei. <b>28</b> Nokuti ivhu rinozviberekera chibereko pacharo, pakutanga chipande, pashure hura, pashure zviyo zvakakora pahura. <b>29</b> Asi kana chibereko chaibva, pakarepo anotuma jeko nokuti kukohwa kwasvika. Mufananidzo wetsanga yemasitadha* <b>30</b> Zvino wakati: Tichafananidza nei ushe hwaMwari? Kana tingahuenzanisa nemuenzaniso upi? <b>31</b> Setsanga yemasitadha*, iyo kana ichidzvarwa muvhu, idikisa kumbeu dzese dzepanyika; 
<b>32</b> asi kana yadzvarwa, inokura ichiva huru kumiriwo yese, ichiita matavi makuru zvekuti shiri dzekudenga dzinogona kuvakira pasi pemumvuri wawo. <b>33</b> Zvino nemifananidzo mizhinji yakadaro wakataura shoko kwavari, sepavaigona kunzwa napo. <b>34</b> Asi kunze kwemufananidzo haana kutaura kwavari; asi vava vega, wakadudzira vadzidzi vake zvese. Jesu anonyaradza dutu remhepo <b>35</b> Zvino nezuva iro ava madekwani, wakati kwavari: Ngatiyambukire mhiri. 
<b>36</b> Vakarega chaunga chichienda, vakaenda naye akangodaro muchikepe. Zvino kwaivawo nemamwe magwa naye. <b>37</b> Zvino kwakamuka dutu guru remhepo, mafungu akazvirovera muchikepe, zvekuti chakange zvino chozadzwa. <b>38</b> Asi iye wakange ari shure muchikepe arere pamutsago; vakamumutsa, vakati kwaari: Mudzidzisi, hamuna hanya kuti toparara here? <b>39</b> Akamuka akatsiura mhepo, akati kugungwa: Nyarara, gadzikana! Mhepo ikamira, kukava nekudzikama kukuru. <b>40</b> Ndokuti kwavari: Munotyirei zvakadaro? Hamuna rutendo sei? 
<b>41</b> Vakatya nekutya kukuru, vakati umwe kune umwe: Zvino ndiani uyu, kuti kunyange mhepo negungwa zvinomuteerera? 
<b>Marcos 5</b><br><b>1</b> Vakasvika mhiri kwegungwa, kunyika yeVaGadharini. <b>2</b> Zvino wakati abuda muchikepe, pakarepo munhu akamuchingamidza achibva kumarinda ane mweya wetsvina, <b>3</b> uyo wakange ane ugaro kumarinda, uye kwakange kusina wakange achigona kumusunga kunyange nemaketani, <b>4</b> nokuti kazhinji waisungwa nezvisungo zvemakumbo nemaketani, asi maketani aigurwa nepakati naye, nezvisungo zvemakumbo zvaigurwa kuita zvidimbu; uye kwakange kusina waigona kumupingudza.

Mwana haana kufa, asi arere. <b>40</b> Vakamuseka. Asi iye wakati avabudisa vese panze, akatora baba vemwana namai nevakange vanaye, akapinda makange murere mwana. <b>41</b> Akabata ruoko rwemwana, akati kwaari: "Tarita kumi"; ndokuti kana zvichishandurwa: Musikana mudiki, ndinoti kwauri, muka. 
<b>42</b> Pakarepo musikana wakasimuka, akafamba; nokuti waiva wemakore gumi nemaviri; vakashamisika nekushamisika kukuru. <b>43</b> Akavaraira zvikuru kuti kusava nemunhu anoziva izvi; akati apiwe kudya. 
<b>Marcos 6</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakabvapo, akasvika kunyika yekwake; vadzidzi vake vakamutevera. <b>2</b> Zvino rava sabata, akatanga kudzidzisa musinagoge; vazhinji vakamunzwa, vakashamisika vachiti: Izvi zvinobvepi kwaari? Injere dzipi dzakapiwa kwaari, kuti nemabasa esimba akadai anoitwa nemaoko ake? <b>3</b> Uyu haasi muvezi here, mwanakomana waMaria, nemukoma waJakobho, naJose, naJudha, naSimoni? Nehanzvadzi dzake hadzisi pano nesu here? Vakagumburwa naye. 
<b>4</b> Asi Jesu akati kwavari: Muporofita haangashaiwi kukudzwa, kunze kwemunyika yekwake, nepakati pehama, nemumba make. <b>5</b> Akasagona kubata chero basa resimba ipapo, kunze kwekuti wakaisa maoko pamusoro pevairwara vashoma, akaporesa. <b>6</b> Akashamisika nekusatenda kwavo. Uye akapota-pota nemisha achidzidzisa. Vanegumi nevaviri vanotumwa kunoparidza nekuporesa <b>7</b> Zvino wakadanira kwaari vanegumi nevaviri, akatanga kuvatuma vaviri-vaviri, akavapa simba pamusoro pemweya yetsvina. 
<b>8</b> Akavaraira kuti varege kutora chinhu cherwendo kunze kwemudonzvo chete; kwete chikwama, kwete chingwa, kwete mari mubhanhire. <b>9</b> Asi vapfeke manyatera, varege kupfeka nguvo refu mbiri. <b>10</b> Zvino akati kwavari: Pese pamunopinda mumba, mugaremo kusvikira muchibvapo. <b>11</b> Asi vese vasingakugamuchiriyi kana kukunzwai, kana muchibvapo, zuzai guruva riri pasi petsoka dzenyu, chive chapupu kwavari. Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Zvichava nani kuSodhoma kana Gomora nezuva rekutongwa kupfuura guta iro. 
<b>12</b> Vakati vabuda, vakaparidza kuti vatendeuke; <b>13</b> zvino vakabudisa madhimoni mazhinji, vakazodza nemafuta vazhinji vairwara, vachiporesa. Kuurawa kwaJohwani Mubhabhatidzi <b>14</b> Mambo Herodhe ndokuzvinzwa, nokuti zita rake rakava pachena, akati: Johwani anobhabhatidza akamutswa kubva kuvakafa, naizvozvo masimba anobata maari. <b>15</b> Vamwe vakati: NdiEria; nevamwe vakati: Muporofita, kana wakaita seumwe wevaporofita. <b>16</b> Asi Herodhe wakati anzwa, akati: Uyu ndiJohwani uya wandakagura ini musoro; iye wamuka kubva kuvakafa. 
<b>17</b> Nokuti Herodhe pachake wakange atuma vanhu akabata Johwani, akamusunga mutirongo nekuda kwaHerodhiasi, mukadzi waFiripi, munin'ina wake; nokuti Herodhe wakange amuwana. <b>18</b> Nokuti Johwani wakange ati kuna Herodhe: Hauna kutenderwa kuti uve nemukadzi wemunin'ina wako. <b>19</b> Herodhiasi akamuvenga nechemumoyo, akada kumuuraya, asi wakakonewa; <b>20</b> nokuti Herodhe aitya Johwani, achiziva kuti murume wakarurama, mutsvene, akamuchengetedza. Zvino paakamunzwa akaita zvinhu zvakawanda, akamunzwa nemufaro. 
<b>21</b> Asi zuva rakafanira rakati rasvika, Herodhe nezuva rekuberekwa kwake akaitira chirairo madzishe ake nevatungamiriri vezvuru nevatungamiriri veGarirea, <b>22</b> zvino mukunda waHerodhiasi uyo paakapinda akatamba, akafadza Herodhe nevakange vagere pakudya naye; mambo akati kumusikana: Kumbira kwandiri chipi zvacho chaunoda ndigokupa. <b>23</b> Zvino akamupikira achiti: Chero chipi chaunokumbira kwandiri ndichakupa, kusvikira pahafu yeushe hwangu. <b>24</b> Akabuda akati kuna mai vake: Ndichakumbirei? Vakati: Musoro waJohwani Mubhabhatidzi. 
<b>25</b> Pakarepo akakurumidza kupinda kuna mambo, akakumbira achiti: Ndinoda kuti mundipe mundiro ikozvino musoro waJohwani Mubhabhatidzi. <b>26</b> Mambo akashungurudzika zvikuru, asi nekuda kwemhiko, nekwevakange vagere naye pakudya, wakange asingadi kumurambira. <b>27</b> Pakarepo mambo akatuma muurai, akaraira kuti musoro wake uuyiswe. Akaenda, akamugura musoro mutirongo, <b>28</b> akauya nemusoro wake mundiro, akaupa kumusikana, musikana ndokuupa mai vake. 
<b>29</b> Vadzidzi vake vakati vachizvinzwa, vakauya vakatora chitunha chake, vakachiisa muguva. Vanezvuru zvishanu vanopiwa kudya naJesu <b>30</b> Zvino vaapositori vakaunganira kuna Jesu, vakamuudza zvese, nezvavakange vaita, nezvavakange vadzidzisa. <b>31</b> Akati kwavari: Uyai imwi pachenyu mega kunzvimbo yerenje, muzorore zvishoma. Nokuti vaiva vazhinji vaiuya nekuenda, vakasava nemukana kunyange wekudya. <b>32</b> Vakaenda nechikepe kunzvimbo yerenje vari vega. 
<b>33</b> Zvaunga zvikavaona vachienda, uye vazhinji vakamuziva, vakamhanyirako pamwe chete netsoka vachibva pamaguta ese, vakavatangira, vakasangana kwaari. <b>34</b> Zvino Jesu wakati achibuda, akaona chaunga chikuru, ndokuvanzwira tsitsi, nokuti vakange vakaita semakwai asina mufudzi; akatanga kuvadzidzisa zvinhu zvizhinji. <b>35</b> Zvino nguva yakati yaenda zvikuru, vadzidzi vake vakauya kwaari vakati: Iyi inzvimbo yerenje, uye nguva yatoenda; 
<b>36</b> varegei, kuti vaende kuruwa rwakapoteredza nemisha vanozvitengera zvingwa; nokuti havana chavangadya. <b>37</b> Akapindura akati kwavari: Vapei imwi kuti vadye. Vakati kwaari: Tingaenda here tinotenga zvingwa zvemadhenario* mazana maviri, tivape vadye? <b>38</b> Akati kwavari: Mune zvingwa zvingani? Endai munoona. Vakati vaziva vakati: Zvishanu, nehove mbiri. <b>39</b> Zvino akavaraira kuti vavagarise vese pasi nemapoka-mapoka pauswa unyoro. <b>40</b> Zvino vakagara pasi nemapoka-mapoka nemazana uye nemakumi mashanu. 
<b>41</b> Zvino wakati atora zvingwa zvishanu nehove mbiri, akatarira kumusoro kudenga, akavonga, akamedura zvingwa, akapa kuvadzidzi vake, kuti vazvigadzike pamberi pavo; nehove mbiri akavagovera vese. <b>42</b> Zvino vakadya vese, vakaguta. <b>43</b> Vakanunga matengu gumi nemaviri azere nezvimedu, nezvehove. <b>44</b> Zvino avo vakadya zvingwa vaiva varume vanenge zvuru zvishanu. Jesu anofamba pamusoro pemvura <b>45</b> Pakarepo akamanikidza vadzidzi vake kupinda muchikepe, vatungamire kuenda mhiri kuBhetisaidha, iye achirega chaunga chichienda. 
<b>46</b> Akati avaoneka, akabva akaenda mugomo kunonyengetera. <b>47</b> Zvino ava madekwani chikepe chakange chiri pakati pegungwa, iye ari ega panyika. <b>48</b> Zvino wakavaona vachirwadziwa nekukwasva, nokuti mhepo yakange ichipesana navo; akauya kwavari nenguva inenge seyechina yekurinda yeusiku, achifamba pamusoro pegungwa; ndokuda kuvapfuura. <b>49</b> Zvino vakati vachimuona achifamba pamusoro pegungwa, vakafunga kuti chipoko, vakaridza mhere.

Jesu anoporesa vanhu paGenesareti <b>53</b> Vakati vayambuka, vakasvika kunyika yeGenesareti, vakasungira chikepe pamahombekombe. <b>54</b> Vakati vabuda muchikepe, pakarepo vakamuziva,

<b>Marcos 7</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino kwakaunganira kwaari VaFarisi nevamwe vevanyori vaibva kuJerusarema; <b>2</b> vakati vaona vamwe vevadzidzi vake vachidya zvingwa nemaoko ane tsvina, ndiko kuti asina kushambwa, vakasvora. <b>3</b> Nokuti VaFarisi, neVaJudha vese, havadyi kunze kwekuti vashamba maoko kwazvo, vachibatirira tsika dzevakuru. <b>4</b> Kana vachibva pamusika, kunze kwekuti vashamba, havadyi; nezvimwe zvinhu zvizhinji zviripo zvavakange vagamuchira kuti vazvichengete, zvinoti: Kusuka mikombe, nehari, nemidziyo yendarira, nenhovo. 
<b>5</b> Ipapo VaFarisi nevanyori vakamubvunza, vachiti: Nemhaka yei vadzidzi venyu vasingafambi netsika yevakuru, asi vanodya chingwa nemaoko asina kushambwa? <b>6</b> Asi wakapindura akati kwavari: Isaya wakaporofita zvakanaka pamusoro penyu imwi vanyepedzeri, sezvazvakanyorwa, zvichinzi: Rudzi urwu rwunondikudza nemiromo, asi moyo wavo uri kure neni. <b>7</b> Asi vanondinamata pasina, vachidzidzisa dzidziso, dziri mirairo yevanhu. 
<b>8</b> Nokuti munosiya murairo waMwari, muchibatirira tsika yevanhu, sekusuka hari nemikombe, nezvimwe zvizhinji zvakadaro munozviita. <b>9</b> Zvino akati kwavari: Munoramba nemazvo murairo waMwari, kuti muchengete tsika yenyu. <b>10</b> Nokuti Mozisi wakati: Kudza baba vako namai vako; uye: Anotuka baba kana mai, anofanira kufa rufu. <b>11</b> Asi imwi munoti: Kana munhu achiti kuna baba kana mai: Chinhu chipi nechipi chamunobatsirwa nacho neni "iKorbani*", ndokuti chipo kuna Mwari; <b>12</b> hamuchamutenderi kuitira baba vake kana mai vake chinhu,

<b>18</b> Akati kwavari: Saizvozvo nemwi muri vasina kunzwisisa here? Hamunzwisisi here kuti chese chinobva kunze chichipinda mumunhu hachigoni kumusvibisa? <b>19</b> Nokuti hachipindi mumoyo make, asi mudumbu; chichibuda kunze, achinatsa kudya kwese. <b>20</b> Ndokubva ati: Icho chinobuda mumunhu, ndicho chinosvibisa munhu. <b>21</b> Nokuti kubva mukati mumoyo wevanhu inobuda mifungo yakaipa, upombwe, ufeve, umhondi, <b>22</b> umbavha, ruchiva, kuipa, kunyengera, meso-meso, ziso rakaipa, kunyomba, kuzvikudza, upenzi; 
<b>23</b> zvese zvinhu izvi zvakaipa zvinobva mukati, zvichisvibisa munhu. Jesu anoporesa mwanakomana wemukadzi weSirofenike <b>24</b> Zvino kubva ipapo wakasimuka akaenda kumiganhu yeTire neSidhoni; akapinda mumba, asingadi kuti pave neanozviziva, asi akakoniwa kuvanzika. <b>25</b> Nokuti mukadzi waiva nemukunda mudiki akange ane mweya wetsvina, akanzwa nezvake, akauya akawira patsoka dzake. <b>26</b> Uye mukadzi waiva muGiriki, muSirofenike pachizvarwa; akamukumbirisa kuti abudise dhimoni pamukunda wake. 
<b>27</b> Asi Jesu akati kwaari: Regai vana vatange kuguta, nokuti hazvina kunaka kutora chingwa chevana, ndokukandira kuimbwanana. <b>28</b> Akapindura akati kwaari: Hongu, Ishe; nokuti kunyange imbwanana dziri pasi petafura dzinodya zvezvimedu zvevana. <b>29</b> Akati kwaari: Nekuda kweshoko iri, enda; dhimoni rabuda pamukunda wako. <b>30</b> Akabva akaenda kumba kwake, akawana dhimoni rabuda, mukunda arere panhovo. <b>Marcos 8</b><br><b>1</b> Nemazuva iwayo zvakwakange kune chaunga chikuru kwazvo, vasina chavangadya, Jesu akadanira vadzidzi vake kwaari, akati kwavari: <b>2</b> Ndine tsitsi nechaunga; nokuti chadogara neni mazuva matatu vasina zvavangadya. <b>3</b> Kana ndikavarega vaende kumba kwavo nenzara, vachaziya panzira; nokuti vamwe vavo vanobva kure. <b>4</b> Vadzidzi vake vakamupindura, vakati: Zvingabvepi zvingwa munhu zvaangagutsa nazvo ava murenje muno? <b>5</b> Akavabvunza, akati: Mune zvingwa zvingani? Jesu anoramba kuratidza chiratidzo

Ndichakuitirai moyo murefu kusvikira rinhi? Muuyisei kwandiri. <b>20</b> Vakamuuisa kwaari; wakati achimuona, pakarepo mweya ukamubvambura; akawira pasi, akaumburuka achipupuma furo. <b>21</b> Ndokubva abvunza baba vake akati: Yava nguva yakadini kubvira izvozvi zvichiitika kwaari? Akati: Kubva paupwere.

<b>43</b> Pakarepo achataura, Judhasi wakasvika, umwe wevanegumi nevaviri, chaunga chikuru chinaye chine minondo netsvimbo, chichibva kuvapristi vakuru nevanyori nevakuru. <b>44</b> Zvino iye wakamutengesa wakange avapa chiratidzo, achiti: Uyo wandinongotsvoda ndiye; mumutore muende naye makamuchengetedza. <b>45</b> Zvino wakati asvika, pakarepo akaswedera kwaari akati: Rabhi*! Rabhi! Akamutsvoda. <b>46</b> Vakabva vaisa maoko avo kwaari, vakamubata. 
<b>47</b> Zvino umwe wevakange vamirepo wakavhomora munondo akatema muranda wemupristi mukuru, akagura nzeve yake. <b>48</b> Zvino Jesu akapindura akati kwavari: Mabuda semakanangana negororo neminondo netsvimbo kuzondibata here? <b>49</b> Ndakange ndinemwi mutembere zuva nezuva ndichidzidzisa, asi hamuna kundibata, asi izvi zvaitwa kuti magwaro azadziswe. <b>50</b> Vese ndokumusiya vakatiza. <b>51</b> Zvino rimwe jaya rakamutevera, rakamonera mucheka werineni rakashama. Majaya akaribata; 
<b>52</b> ndokubva rasiya mucheka werineni, rikavatiza rakashama. Jesu pamberi pemupristi mukuru nevakuru <b>53</b> Zvino vakaendesa Jesu kumupristi mukuru; vakaunganira kwaari vapristi vakuru vese nevakuru nevanyori. <b>54</b> Petro akamutevera ari kure kusvikira mukati mechivanze chemupristi mukuru; akange agere pamwe nevaranda, achidziya moto. <b>55</b> Zvino vapristi vakuru nedare rese remakurukota vakatsvaka uchapupu hwakanangana naJesu, kuti vamuuraye; asi havana kuhuwana. 
<b>56</b> Nokuti vazhinji vaimupupurira nhema, asi uchapupu hauna kupindirana. <b>57</b> Ndokubva kwasimuka vamwe vakamupupurira nhema vachiti: <b>58</b> Isu takamunzwa achiti: Ini ndichaputsa tembere iyi yakaitwa nemaoko, uye mumazuva matatu ndichavaka imwe isina kuitwa nemaoko. <b>59</b> Asi kunyange zvakadaro uchapupu hwavo hauna kupindirana. <b>60</b> Zvino mupristi mukuru wakasimuka pakati pavo, akabvunza Jesu, achiti: Haupinduri chinhu here? Vanopupurei ava chakanangana newe? 
<b>61</b> Asi wakanyarara, akasapindura chinhu. Mupristi mukuru akamubvunzazve, akati kwaari: Iwe uri Kristu, Mwanakomana weWakaropafadzwa here? <b>62</b> Jesu akati: Ndini iye. Uye muchaona Mwanakomana wemunhu agere kuruoko rwerudyi rwesimba, achiuya nemakore ekudenga. <b>63</b> Mupristi mukuru ndokubvarura nguvo dzake akati: Tichiri kudirei zvapupu? <b>64</b> Manzwa kunyomba; munofungei? Zvino vese vakamurasa kuti ave nemhosva yerufu. 
<b>65</b> Vamwe vakatanga kumupfira, nekufukidza chiso chake, nekumurova netsiva, nokuti kwaari: Porofita! Varanda vakamurova nembama dzamaoko. Kuramba kwaPetro <b>66</b> Zvino Petro achiri pachivanze pasi, kwakasvika umwe wevarandakadzi vemupristi mukuru; <b>67</b> achiona Petro achidziya moto, akamutarira akati: Iwewo wakange una Jesu weNazareta. <b>68</b> Asi wakaramba, achiti: Handizivi kana kunzwisisa zvaunoreva. Akabuda panze akaenda kumusuwo; jongwe rikarira. 
<b>69</b> Zvino murandakadzi akamuonazve, akatanga kuti kuna vakange vamirepo: Uyu ndewavo. <b>70</b> Asi wakarambazve. Zvino shure kwechinguvana, vakange vamirepo vakatizve kuna Petro: Zvirokwazvo, uri wavo, nokuti newe uri muGarirea nemutauro wako unotenderana nazvo. <b>71</b> Akatanga kutuka nekupika, achiti: Handimuzivi munhu uyu wamunoreva. <b>72</b> Zvino jongwe rikarira rwechipiri. Petro ndokurangarira shoko Jesu raakange areva kwaari rekuti: Jongwe risati rarira kaviri, uchandiramba katatu. Zvino wakati achifunga izvozvo akachema. 
<b>Marcos 15</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino pakarepo mangwanani-ngwanani vapristi vakuru vakaita zano nevakuru nevanyori nedare remakurukota rese, vakasunga Jesu, vakamutakura, vakamukumikidza kuna Pirato. <b>2</b> Pirato ndokumubvunza achiti: Iwe uri Mambo weVaJudha here? Akapindura, akati kwaari: Unoreva iwe. <b>3</b> Vapristi vakuru vakamupomera zvinhu zvizhinji, asi iye haana kupindura chinhu. <b>4</b> Pirato akamubvunzazve achiti: Haupinduri chinhu here? Tarira, zvinhu zvingani zvavanokupupurira! 
<b>5</b> Asi Jesu haana kuzopindurazve chinhu, zvekuti Pirato wakashamisika. <b>6</b> Zvino pamabiko waivasunungurira musungwa umwe, chero upi wavanenge vakumbira. <b>7</b> Zvino kwakange kune wainzi Bharabhasi, akasungwa pamwe nevakange vamutsa mhirizhonga naye, vakange vaita umhondi pamhirizhonga iyoyo. <b>8</b> Zvino chaunga chakadanidzira, vakatanga kukumbira kuti aite sezvaaigarovaitira. <b>9</b> Asi Pirato akavapindura, achiti: Munoda kuti ndikusunungurirei Mambo weVaJudha here? 
<b>10</b> Nokuti wakange achiziva kuti vapristi vakuru vakange vamutengesa negodo. <b>11</b> Asi vapristi vakuru vakamutsa chaunga, kuti zviri nani avasunungurire Bharabhasi. <b>12</b> Pirato akapindura akatizve kwavari: Naizvozvo munoda kuti ndimuitirei, wamunoti ndiMambo weVaJudha? <b>13</b> Vakadanidzirazve vachiti: Murovere pamuchinjikwa! <b>14</b> Asi Pirato akati kwavari: Nemhaka yei? Wakaita chakaipa chei? Vakanyanyisa kudanidzira vachiti: Muroverere pamuchinjikwa! 
<b>15</b> Zvino Pirato, achida kuti agutse chaunga, akavasunungurira Bharabhasi; ndokukumikidza Jesu kwavari, amurova netyava, kuti arovererwe pamuchinjikwa. Jesu anodadirwa nemauto <b>16</b> Zvino mauto akamuisa mukati mechivanze, chinova imba yemutungamiriri, akadanira hondo yese pamwe; <b>17</b> vakamupfekedza zvine ruvara rwehute, vakaruka korona yeminzwa, vakaisa paari. <b>18</b> Vakatanga kumukwazisa, vachiti: Hekanhi, Mambo weVaJudha! <b>19</b> Vakamurova musoro nerutsanga, vakamupfira, vakafugama mabvi, vakamunamata. 
<b>20</b> Zvino vakati vamuseka, vakamubvisa zveruvara rwehute, ndokumupfekedza nguvo dzake pachake, vakabuda naye kunomuroverera pamuchinjikwa. Jesu anorovererwa pamuchinjikwa <b>21</b> Vakamanikidza umwe waipfuura wainzi Simoni muKurini, achibva kuruwa, baba vaArekizandiro naRufo, kuti atakure muchinjikwa wake. <b>22</b> Vakamuisa kunzvimbo yainzi Gorogota, ndokuti kana zvichishandurwa: Nzvimbo yedehenya. <b>23</b> Vakamupa waini yakavhenganiswa nemura kuti anwe, asi haana kugamuchira. 
<b>24</b> Zvino vakati vamuroverera pamuchinjikwa, vakagovana nguvo dzake, vachikanda mujenya pamusoro padzo, kuti umwe neumwe achatorei. <b>25</b> Rakange rava awa rechitatu, vakabva vamuroverera pamuchinjikwa. <b>26</b> Nerugwaro rwemhosva yake rwakange rwakanyorwa pamusoro, rwuchiti: MAMBO WEVAJUDHA. <b>27</b> Pamwe naye vakaroverera pamuchinjikwa makororo maviri, umwe kuruoko rwerudyi, neumwe kuruboshwe rwake. <b>28</b> Rugwaro rukazadzisika, rwunoti: Wakaverengwa pamwe nevasina murairo. 
<b>29</b> Nevaipfuura vakamutuka, vachidzungudza misoro yavo, vachiti: Ehe-e, iwe unoputsa tembere, nekuivaka nemazuva matatu, <b>30</b> zviponese, uburuke pamuchinjikwa! <b>31</b> Saizvozvo vapristiwo vakuru vakamuseka pamwe nevanyori pakati pavo, vachiti: Wakaponesa vamwe, haagoni kuzviponesa. <b>32</b> Kristu Mambo waIsraeri ngaaburuke ikozvino pamuchinjikwa, kuti tione titende. Nevakange varovererwa pamuchinjikwa pamwe naye vakamunyomba. <b>33</b> Zvino awa rechitanhatu rakati rasvika, rima rikavapo panyika yese kusvikira paawa repfumbamwe. 
<b>34</b> Zvino neawa repfumbamwe, Jesu akadana nenzwi guru, achiti: "Eroi, Eroi, rama sabhakitani!" Ndokuti, kana zvichishandurwa: Mwari wangu, Mwari wangu, mandisiirei? <b>35</b> Vamwe vevakange vamirepo vakati vachizvinzwa, vakati: Tarirai, anodana Eria. <b>36</b> Ipapo umwe wakamhanya, akazadza chipanje nevhiniga, akaisa parutsanga, akamupa kuti anwe, achiti: Regai, tione kana Eria achiuya kumuburusa. <b>37</b> Ipapo Jesu akadanidzira nenzwi guru akapa mweya. <b>38</b> Vheiri retembere rikabvaruka kuita mapandi maviri, kubva kumusoro kusvikira pasi. 
<b>39</b> Zvino mukuru wezana wakange amirepo pakatarisana naye, wakati achiona kuti wadanidzira zvakadaro akapa mweya, akati: Zvirokwazvo, munhu uyu wanga ari Mwanakomana waMwari. <b>40</b> Uye kwakange kune vakadziwo vakatarira vari kure, pakati pavo pakange pana Maria Magidhariniwo, naMaria mai vaJakobho mudiki naJose, naSarome, <b>41</b> (avowo vaimutevera achiri muGarirea, vachimushandira), nevamwe vakadzi vazhinji vakange vakwira kuJerusarema naye. Kuvigwa kwaJesu 
<b>42</b> Zvino atova manheru, zvarakange riri gadziriro, ndokuti zuva rinotangira sabata, <b>43</b> Josefa waiva weArimatia, nhengo yedare yairemekedzwa, wakange akamirirawo pachake ushe hwaMwari; wakauya akapinda kuna Pirato akatsunga, akakumbira chitunha chaJesu. <b>44</b> Pirato akashamisika nokuti wakange atofa; akadanira kwaari mukuru wezana, akamubvunza kana akange ava nenguva afa. <b>45</b> Zvino wakati azvinzwa nemukuru wezana, akapa Josefa chitunha. 
<b>46</b> Akatenga mucheka wakaisvonaka, akamuburusa, akamuputira nemucheka wakapfava, ndokumuradzika muguva rakange rakacherwa muruware, ndokukungurusira ibwe pamukova weguva. <b>47</b> Zvino Maria Magidharini, naMaria mai vaJose, vakaona paakaradzikwa. 
<b>Marcos 16</b><br><b>1</b> Sabata rakati rapfuura, Maria Magidharini, naMaria mai vaJakobho, naSarome vakatenga zvinonhuhwira, kuti vauye kunomuzodza. <b>2</b> Mangwanani-ngwanani kwazvo nomusi wekutanga wevhiki vakauya kuguva, zuva robuda. <b>3</b> Vakati pakati pavo: Ndiani achatikungurusira ibwe kubva pamukova weguva? <b>4</b> Zvino vakati vachitarira, vakaona kuti ibwe rakange rakunguruswa, nokuti raiva guru-guru. <b>5</b> Zvino vakati vachipinda muguva, vakaona jaya rakange rigere kurudyi, rakapfeka nguvo refu chena; vakavhunduka; 
<b>6</b> asi rakati kwavari: Musavhunduka; munotsvaka Jesu muNazareta wakarovererwa pamuchinjikwa; wamuka, haasi pano; tarirai panzvimbo pavakange vamuradzika. <b>7</b> Asi endai, munoudza vadzidzi vake naPetro kuti anokutungamirirai kuGarirea, muchanomuonako sezvaakakuudzai. <b>8</b> Zvino vakabuda nekukurumidza vakatiza paguva; uyezve kudedera nekuvhunduka kwakavabata; vakasataura chinhu kuna ani zvake, nokuti vakange vachitya. 
<b>9</b> Zvino Jesu wakati amuka mangwanani-ngwanani newekutanga wevhiki, wakaonekwa pakutanga kuna Maria Magidharini, waakabudisa kwaari madhimoni manomwe. <b>10</b> Uyo akaenda akaudza avo vaiva naye, pavaichema vachirira. <b>11</b> Neava vanzwa kuti mupenyu, uye kuti wakange aonekwa naye, havana kutenda. <b>12</b> Shure kweizvozvo wakazviratidza nechimwe chimiro kuvaviri vavo vachifamba, vachienda muruwa. <b>13</b> Zvino avawo vakaenda vakaudza vakange vasara; naavo vakasavatenda. 
<b>14</b> Pashure wakazviratidza kune vanegumi neumwe vagere pakudya, akatsiura kusatenda kwavo neukukutu hwemoyo, nokuti havana kutenda vaya vakange vamuona amuka. <b>15</b> Akati kwavari: Endai munyika yese muparidze evhangeri kuchisikwa chega-chega. <b>16</b> Uyo anotenda akabhabhatidzwa achaponeswa; asi asingatendi achapiwa mhosva. <b>17</b> Zviratidzo izvi zvichavatevera vanotenda: Muzita rangu vachabudisa madhimoni, vachataura nendimi itsva; 
<b>18</b> vachanonga nyoka; kunyange vakanwa chero chinhu chinouraya, hachingatongovakuvadzi; vachaisa maoko pavarwere, vakapora. Kukwira kwaJesu <b>19</b> Naizvozvo Ishe shure kwekunge ataura kwavari, akagamuchirwa kumusoro kudenga, akagara kuruoko rwerudyi rwaMwari. <b>20</b> Avo vakabuda, vakaparidza kwese-kwese, Ishe achibata navo, achisimbisa shoko nezviratidzo zvaitevera.
