2 Timothy|4|0,5,11,15
<b>2 Timothy 1</b><br><b>1</b> Pauro, muapositori waJesu Kristu kubudikidza nechido chaMwari, zvichienderana nechivimbiso cheupenyu huri muna Kristu Jesu, <b>2</b> kuna Timotio mwana wangu unodikanwa: Nyasha, tsitsi, nerugare zvinobva kuna Mwari Baba naKristu Jesu Ishe wedu. Timotio anorairwa kuti asimbe pabasa rake <b>3</b> Ndinovonga Mwari, wandinoshumira kubva kumadziteteguru muhana yakachena, kuti ndisingamiri ndinokurangarira paminyengetero yangu siku nesikati, <b>4</b> ndichishuva kukuona, ndichirangarira misodzi yako, kuti ndizadzwe nemufaro, 
<b>5</b> ndichiisa mumurangariro rutendo rusinganyepedzeri rwuri mauri, rwaimbogara muna mbuya vako Roisi namai vako Yunisi, uye ndinovimba kuti rwuri mauriwo. <b>6</b> Nemhaka yeizvi ndinokuyeudzira kuti ukutidzire chipo chaMwari, chiri mauri nekuiswa kwemaoko angu. <b>7</b> Nokuti Mwari haana kutipa mweya wekutya asi wesimba, newerudo, newekuzvidzora. <b>8</b> Naizvozvo usanyadziswa neuchapupu hwaIshe wedu, kana hwangu musungwa wake; asi utambudzikire evhangeri pamwe neni zvichienderana nesimba raMwari, 
<b>9</b> wakatiponesa, akatidana nekudana kutsvene, kwete zvichienderana nemabasa edu, asi zvichienderana nezano rake pachake, nenyasha dzakapiwa kwatiri muna Kristu Jesu nhambo dzenguva dzisati dzavapo, <b>10</b> asi ikozvino kunoratidzwa nekuonekwa kweMuponesi wedu Jesu Kristu, wakaparadza rufu, akabudisa pachena upenyu nekusafa kubudikidza neevhangeri, <b>11</b> yandakagadzirwa ini kuva muparidzi, nemuapositori, nemudzidzisi wevahedheni. 
<b>12</b> Nemhaka yeizvi neni ndinotambudzika zvinhu izvi, zvakadaro handinyadziswi, nokuti ndinoziva wandakatenda, uye ndine chokwadi kuti anogona kuchengeta chandakaisa muruoko kusvikira zuva iroro. <b>13</b> Batisisa muenzaniso wemashoko mapenyu awakanzwa kwandiri murutendo nerudo rwuri muna Kristu Jesu. <b>14</b> Icho chinhu chakanaka chakaiswa mumaoko mako chengeta neMweya Mutsvene anogara matiri. <b>15</b> Unozviziva izvi, kuti vese vari paAsia vakandifuratira; pakati pavo pana Figero naHemogene. 
<b>16</b> Ishe ngaape tsitsi kuimba yaOnesiforo; nokuti kazhinji wakandivandudza, uye haana kunyadziswa neketani yangu, <b>17</b> asi asvika Roma, wakanditsvakisisa, akandiwana. <b>18</b> Ishe ngaamuitire kuti awane tsitsi kuna Ishe nezuva iro; uye muzvinhu zvingani zvaakandibatira paEfeso, iwe unozivisisa. 
<b>2 Timothy 2</b><br><b>1</b> Naizvozvo iwe, mwana wangu, simba munyasha dziri muna Kristu Jesu. <b>2</b> Nezvinhu zvawakanzwa kwandiri pakati pezvapupu zvizhinji, uzvikumikidze izvozvo kuvanhu vakatendeka vachagonawo kudzidzisa vamwe. <b>3</b> Naizvozvo iwe utambudzike zvakaoma, semurwi wakanaka waJesu Kristu. <b>4</b> Hakuna munhu anobata semurwi anozvipinganidza nezviitiko zveupenyu, kuti afadze uyo wakamupinza basa. <b>5</b> Uye kana munhuwo achikwikwidza, haashongedzwi korona kana asina kukwikwidza zviri pamurairo. 
<b>6</b> Murimi anobata zvakasimba anofanira kutanga kugamuchira zvibereko. <b>7</b> Fungisisa zvandinoreva; uye Ishe ngaakupe kunzwisisa pazvinhu zvese. <b>8</b> Rangarira Jesu Kristu wakamutswa kubva kuvakafa, wembeu yaDhavhidhi, zvichienderana neevhangeri yangu; <b>9</b> yandinotambudzika mairi zvakaipa, kusvikira pazvisungo, semuiti wezvakaipa; asi shoko raMwari harina kusungwa. <b>10</b> Saka ndinotsungirira zvinhu zvese nekuda kwevakasanangurwa, kuti naivo vawane ruponeso rwuri muna Kristu Jesu, pamwe nekubwinya kusingaperi. 
<b>11</b> Shoko iri rakatendeka; nokuti kana takafa pamwe naye, tichararamawo pamwe naye. <b>12</b> Kana tikatsungirira, tichatongawo pamwe naye; kana tikamuramba, naiye achatiramba. <b>13</b> Kana tisingatendeki, iye anoramba akatendeka; iye haagoni kuzviramba. Tinofanira kuita sei kune vakarasika padzidzo <b>14</b> Uvarangaridze zvinhu izvi, uchirairisa pamberi paIshe kusapikisa pamashoko kusingabatsiri chinhu asi kutsausa vanonzwa. 
<b>15</b> Shingaira kuti uzviratidze kuna Mwari uri wakatendeka, mushandi asingafaniri kunyadziswa, anonyatsoruramisa shoko rechokwadi. <b>16</b> Asi nzvenga kutaura kwetsvina kusina maturo; nokuti vacharamba vachingonyanya pakusatya Mwari, <b>17</b> neshoko ravo richanyenga segomarara; pakati pavo pana Himenayosi naFiretosi; <b>18</b> ivo vakatsauka pachokwadi, vachiti kumuka kwatovapo, vachipidigura rutendo rwevamwe. 
<b>19</b> Kunyange zvakadaro nheyo yaMwari inomira yakasimba, ine mucherechedzo uyu: Ishe anoziva vari vake, uye kuti: Umwe neumwe anoreva zita raKristu ngaabve pakusarurama. <b>20</b> Asi muimba huru hamuna midziyo yegoridhe neyesirivheri chete, asiwo yematanda neyevhu, uye imwe ndeyekukudzwa asi imwe ndeyekusakudzwa. <b>21</b> Naizvozvo kana munhu achizvinatsa pazvinhu izvi, achava mudziyo wekukudzwa, wakaitwa mutsvene, wakakwanira basa raTenzi, wakagadzirirwa basa rese rakanaka. 
<b>22</b> Tizawo kuchiva kweudiki, asi uteverere kururama, rutendo, rudo, rugare, pamwe nevanodana kuna Ishe zvichibva pamoyo wakachena. <b>23</b> Asi mibvunzo yeupenzi neyekusadzidza urambe, uchiziva kuti zvinomutsa kurwa. <b>24</b> Zvino muranda waIshe haafaniri kurwa, asi ave munyoro kune vese, anoziva kudzidzisa, ane moyo murefu, <b>25</b> anoraira neunyoro avo vanozvipikisa, kuti zvimwe Mwari avape kutendeukira kuruzivo rwechokwadi, <b>26</b> uye vapengenuke kubva pamusungo wadhiabhorosi, wavakange vakabatwa naye pakuda kwake. 
<b>2 Timothy 3</b><br><b>1</b> Asi uzive ichi, kuti mumazuva ekupedzisira nguva dzekutambudzika dzichasvika. <b>2</b> Nokuti vanhu vachava vanozvida, vanoda mari, vanozvirumbidza, vanozvikudza, vatuki, vasingateereri vabereki, vasingavongi, vasiri vatsvene, <b>3</b> vasina rudo rwechisikirwo, vasingaregereri, vacheri, vasingazvidzori, vane hasha, vasingadi zvakanaka, <b>4</b> vatengesi, vanomhanyirira, vane manyawi, vanoda mafaro kupfuura kuda Mwari, <b>5</b> vane mufananidzo wekunamata Mwari, asi vachiramba simba rawo; ubve pane vakadaiwo. 
<b>6</b> Nokuti kwavari kunobva vanoverevedza mudzimba, vachitapa vakadzi nzenza, vakaremedzwa nezvivi, vachitungamirirwa nekuchiva kwakasiyana-siyana, <b>7</b> vanongogara vachidzidza, asi havatongogoni kusvika paruzivo rwechokwadi. <b>8</b> Sezvowo Jane naJambere vakapikisa Mozisi, saizvozvowo ava vanoramba chokwadi, vanhu vendangariro dzakaodzwa, vakaraswa maererano nerutendo. <b>9</b> Asi havachapfuuriri mberi; nokuti upenzi hwavo huchaonekwa kune vese, sezvakazoita hwavowo. 
<b>10</b> Asi iwe wakanyatsoteverera dzidziso yangu, mufambiro, chinangwa, rutendo, moyo murefu, rudo, kutsungirira, <b>11</b> kushushwa, nenhamo, zvakandiwira paAndiyokiya, paIkonio, nepaRistra, kushushwa kwakadini kwandakatsungirira makuri; uye Mwari wakandinunura kubva maari ese. <b>12</b> Nevesewo vanoda kurarama mukunamata Mwari muna Kristu Jesu vachashushwa; <b>13</b> asi vanhu vakaipa nevanyengeri vachaenderera mberi pakuipa vachitsausa nekutsauswa. 
<b>14</b> Asi iwe rambira pazvinhu zvawakadzidza uye zvawakatendeswa, uchiziva kuti wakazvidzidza kuna ani, <b>15</b> uye kuti kubvira paucheche wakaziva magwaro matsvene, anogona kukuchenjedza kusvikira pakuponeswa kubudikidza nerutendo rwuri muna Kristu Jesu. <b>16</b> Rugwaro rwese rwakafemerwa naMwari uye rwunobatsira padzidziso, pakutsiura, pakururamisa, nepakuraira kuri mukururama; <b>17</b> kuti munhu waMwari ave wakakwana zvizere, agadzirirwa basa rese rakanaka. 
<b>2 Timothy 4</b><br><b>1</b> Naizvozvo ndinorairisa pamberi paMwari, naIshe Jesu Kristu, iye achatonga vapenyu nevakafa, pakuonekwa kwake neushe hwake: <b>2</b> Paridza shoko; iva wakagadzirira nenguva yakafanira, panguva isakafanira, dzora, tsiura, kurudzira nemoyo murefu wese nedzidziso. <b>3</b> Nokuti nguva ichavapo yavacharamba kutendera dzidziso mhenyu, asi zvichienderana nekuchiva kwavo pachavo vachazvivunganidzira vadzidzisi, vachirumwa nzeve; <b>4</b> uye vachafuratidza nzeve dzavo pachokwadi, vachitendeukira kungano. 
<b>5</b> Asi iwe svinura pazvinhu zvese, utsungirire pamatambudziko, uite basa remuevhangeri, uzadzise basa rako. <b>6</b> Nokuti ini ndatodururwa sechipo chinodururwa, nenguva yekubva kwangu yasvika. <b>7</b> Ndarwa kurwa kwakanaka, ndapedza nhangemutange, ndachengeta rutendo; <b>8</b> kubva ikozvino, ndakaisirwa korona yekururama Ishe yavachandipa, mutongi wakarurama nezuva iroro; kwetewo kwandiri ndega, asi nekune vese vanoda kuonekwa kwake. Mashoko ekupedzisira <b>9</b> Shingairira kukurumidza kuuya kwandiri. 
<b>10</b> Nokuti Dhemasi wakandisiya, ada nyika yaikozvino, akaenda kuTesaronika; Kresike kuGaratia, Tito kuDharimatia. <b>11</b> Ruka ega aneni. Tora Mariko uuye naye nokuti anondibatsira pakushumira. <b>12</b> Asi Tikiko ndakamutuma kuEfeso. <b>13</b> Nguvo yandakasiya paTroasi kuna Kapo, kana wouya uuye nayo, nemabhuku, zvikuru magwaro ematehwe. <b>14</b> Arekisandiro mupfuri wendarira wakandiitira zvakaipa zvizhinji; Ishe achamutsiva zvinoenderana nemabasa ake; <b>15</b> iwewo umuchenjerere, nokuti wakapikisa zvikurusa mashoko edu. 
<b>16</b> Pakuzvidavirira kwangu kwekutanga hapana wakamira neni, asi vese vakandisiya. Dai zvarega kuverengerwa kwavari. <b>17</b> Asi Ishe wakamira neni, akandisimbisa, kuti neni kuparidza kukwaniswe, nevahedheni vese vanzwe; uye ndakarwirwa kubva pamuromo weshumba. <b>18</b> Uye Ishe achandirwira pabasa rese rakaipa, uye achandichengetera muushe hwake hwekudenga; kubwinya ngakuve kwaari rinhi narinhi. Ameni. <b>19</b> Kwazisa Priska naAkwira, neimba yaOnesiforo. <b>20</b> Erasto wakasara paKorinde; asi Trofimo ndakamusiya paMireto achirwara. 
<b>21</b> Shingairira kuuya chando chisati chasvika. Yubhuro anokukwazisa, naPudheni, naRinosi, naKraudhia, nehama dzese. <b>22</b> Ishe Jesu Kristu ngaave nemweya wako. Nyasha ngadzive nemwi. Ameni.
