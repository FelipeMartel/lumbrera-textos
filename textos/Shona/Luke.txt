<b>5</b> Kwaivapo nemazuva aHerodhe mambo weJudhiya umwe mupristi wainzi Zakaria, weboka raAbhiya; nemukadzi wake waibva kuvanasikana vaAroni, nezita rake riri Erizabheti. <b>6</b> Uye vari vaviri vakange vakarurama pamberi paMwari, vachifamba nemirairo yese nezviga zvaIshe, vasina chavangapomerwa. <b>7</b> Uye vakange vasina mwana, nokuti Erizabheti wakange ari mhanje, uye vakange vaenda pamakore vari vaviri. <b>8</b> Zvino zvakaitika kuti wakati achiita ushumiri hweupristi pamberi paMwari, pachidanho cheboka rake,

<b>13</b> Asi mutumwa wakati kwaari: Usatya Zakaria; nokuti munyengetero wako wanzwikwa, nemukadzi wako Erizabheti achakuberekera mwanakomana, ugotumidza zita rake Johwani. <b>14</b> Uye uchava nemufaro nekupembera, nevazhinji vachafarira kuberekwa kwake. <b>15</b> Nokuti achava mukuru pamberi paIshe, haangatongonwi waini kana zvinodhaka; uye achazadzwa neMweya Mutsvene kunyange kubva padumbu ramai vake. <b>16</b> Nevazhinji vevana vaIsraeri achatendeusira kuna Ishe Mwari wavo; 
<b>17</b> uye iye achafamba pamberi pake mumweya nesimba raEria, kutendeusira moyo yemadzibaba kuvana, nevasingateereri kuuchenjeri hwevakarurama, kugadzirira Ishe vanhu vakagadzirwa. <b>18</b> Zakaria akati kumutumwa: Izvozvi ndichazviziva nei? Nokuti ini ndava mukweguru, nemukadzi wangu waenda pamazuva ake. <b>19</b> Mutumwa achipindura akati kwaari: Ini ndini Gabhirieri, anomira pamberi paMwari; ndatumwa kuti nditaure kwauri, ndikuparidzire mashoko awa akanaka.

Rwiyo rwaMaria rwekurumbidza <b>46</b> Maria akati: Moyo wangu unokudza Ishe, <b>47</b> nemweya wangu unofara muna Mwari Muponesi wangu; 
<b>48</b> nokuti wakatarira kuninipiswa kwemurandakadzi wake; nokuti tarira, kubva zvino mazera ese achati ndakaropafadzwa. <b>49</b> Nokuti ane masimba wakandiitira zvinhu zvikuru; nezita rake idzvene. <b>50</b> Netsitsi dzake dziri kuna avo vanomutya kubva kumazera kusvika kumazera. <b>51</b> Wakaita zvine simba neruoko rwake; wakaparadzira vanozvikudza mundangariro dzemoyo yavo. <b>52</b> Wakaburusa vane masimba pazvigaro zvoushe, akasimudza vakaderera. <b>53</b> Vane nzara wakavagutsa nezvinhu zvakanaka, asi vafumi wakavaendesa vasina chinhu. 
<b>54</b> Wakabatsira Israeri muranda wake kuti arangarire tsitsi, <b>55</b> sezvaakataura kumadzibaba edu, kuna Abhurahama nekumbeu yake kusvikira rinhi narinhi. <b>56</b> Maria akagara naye mwedzi inenge mitatu, akadzokera kumba kwake. Kuporofita kwaZakaria <b>67</b> Zvino Zakaria baba vake vakazadzwa neMweya Mutsvene, vakaporofita, vachiti: <b>68</b> Ngaarumbidzwe Ishe Mwari waIsraeri, nokuti wakashanyira nekuita rudzikunuro kuvanhu vake; <b>69</b> akatisimudzira runyanga rweruponeso mumba maDhavhidhi muranda wake;

<b>76</b> Newe mucheche, uchanzi muporofita weWekumusoro-soro, nokuti uchatungamira pamberi pechiso chaIshe, kugadzirira nzira dzake; <b>77</b> kupa ruzivo rweruponeso kuvanhu vake mukanganwiro yezvivi zvavo; <b>78</b> netsitsi dzemoyo dzaMwari wedu, mauri mambakwedza anobva kumusoro anotishanyira; <b>79</b> kuvhenekera vagere murima nemumvuri worufu, nekururamisa tsoka dzedu munzira yerugare. <b>80</b> Mucheche akakura, akasimba mumweya, akava mumarenje, kusvikira zuva rekuratidzwa kwake kuna Israeri. 
<b>Luke 2</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino zvakaitika nemazuva iwayo, chisungo chakabuda kubva kuna Kesari Agasto, kuti nyika yese inyorwe. <b>2</b> Kunyorwa uku kwaiva kwekutanga kwakaitwa Kuriniosi ari mutungamiriri weSiriya. <b>3</b> Vese ndokuenda kunonyorwa, umwe neumwe kuguta rekwake. <b>4</b> NaJosefawo akakwira achibva Garirea muguta Nazareta, akaenda Judhiya kuguta raDhavhidhi, rinonzi Bhetirehemu, nokuti waiva weimba nedzinza raDhavhidhi, <b>5</b> kunonyorwa naMaria mukadzi wakange atsidzira kuwanikwa naye, ava nemimba. 
<b>6</b> Zvakaitika kuti vachipo, mazuva ekusununguka kwake akazadzisika; <b>7</b> ndokuzvara mwanakomana wake wedangwe, akamuputira nemicheka, akamuvanzarika muchidyiro, nokuti makange musina nzvimbo yavo muimba yevaeni. Vafudzi veBhetirehemu <b>8</b> Zvino kwakange kune vafudzi munyika iyoyo vaigara kuuragu vachichengetedza nguva dzekurinda boka ravo usiku. <b>9</b> Zvino tarira, mutumwa waIshe akamira pedo navo, nekubwinya kwaIshe kukapenya kwakavakomba; vakatya nekutya kukuru. 
<b>10</b> Zvino mutumwa akati kwavari: Musatya; nokuti tarirai, ndinokuparidzirai mashoko akanaka emufaro mukuru, uchava kuvanhu vese; <b>11</b> nokuti mazvarirwa nhasi Muponesi, anova Kristu Ishe, muguta raDhavhidhi. <b>12</b> Zvino ichi chiratidzo kwamuri: Muchawana mucheche akaputirwa nemicheka, arere muchidyiro. <b>13</b> Uye pakarepo kukavapo pamwe nemutumwa chaunga chehondo yekudenga vachirumbidza Mwari, vachiti: <b>14</b> Rumbidzo kuna Mwari kumusoro-soro, nerugare panyika; chishuwo chakanaka kuvanhu. 
<b>15</b> Zvino kwakaitika kuti vatumwa vabva kwavari vaenda kudenga, vanhu vafudzi vakataurirana, vachiti: Ngatiende ikozvino Bhetirehemu, tinoona chinhu ichi chakaitika, Ishe chaatizivisa. <b>16</b> Zvino vakakurumidza kusvika, vakawana vese Maria naJosefa, nemucheche arere muchidyiro. <b>17</b> Vakati vaona, vakazivisa kwese shoko ravakange vaudzwa maererano nemucheche uyu. <b>18</b> Zvino vese vakanzwa vakashamisika nezvinhu zvakange zvataurwa kwavari nevafudzi. <b>19</b> Asi Maria wakachengeta zvinhu izvi zvese, achifungisisa mumoyo make. 
<b>20</b> Zvino vafudzi vakadzokera, vachikudza nekurumbidza Mwari pamusoro pezvinhu zvese zvavakange vanzwa nekuona, sezvakange zvataurwa kwavari. <b>Luke 3</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino negore regumi nemashanu rekutonga kwaTibherio Kesari, Pondiyo Pirato ari mutungamiriri weJudhiya, Herodhe ari umwe wevatongi vana weGarirea, Firipi munin'ina wake ari umwe wevatongi vana weIturea nedunhu reTrakoniti, Risanio ari umwe wevatongi vana weAbhirene, <b>2</b> Anasi naKayafasi vari vapristi vakuru, shoko raMwari rakasvika kuna Johwani mwanakomana waZakaria murenje. <b>3</b> Akasvika munyika yese yakapoteredza yaJoridhani, achiparidza rubhabhatidzo rwekutendeuka kuve kukanganwirwa kwezvivi;

<b>47</b> Ani nani anouya kwandiri akanzwa mashoko angu akaaita, ndichakuratidzai kuti wakafanana nani; <b>48</b> wakafanana nemunhu wakavaka imba, akachera akadzikisa, akateya nheyo paruware; zvino mafashame akati auya, rukova rukarova zvine simba paimba iyo, rukasagona kuizunungusa; nokuti yakange yakateyiwa paruware. <b>49</b> Asi anonzwa asingaiti, wakafanana nemunhu wakavaka imba pavhu isina nheyo; ipo rukova rwakarova nesimba, pakarepo ikawa, kuwa kweimba iyo kukava kukuru. 
<b>Luke 7</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakati apedza mashoko ake ese munzeve dzevanhu, akapinda muKapenaume. <b>2</b> Zvino muranda weumwe mukuru wezana, waiva akakosha kwaari, wakange achirwara, achitandadza. <b>3</b> Zvino wakati anzwa zvaJesu, akatuma kwaari vakuru veVaJudha, achikumbira kuti auye aporese muranda wake. <b>4</b> Zvino ivo vakati vachisvika kuna Jesu, vakamukumbirisa zvikuru, vachiti: Wakafanira kuti mumuitire izvozvi; <b>5</b> nokuti anoda rudzi rwedu, uye ndiye wakativakira sinagoge. 
<b>6</b> Zvino Jesu akaenda navo. Zvino apo akange asisiri kure neimba, mukuru wezana akatuma shamwari kwaari, achiti kwaari: Ishe, musazvitambudza; nokuti handina kufanira kuti mupinde pasi pedenga reimba yangu; <b>7</b> naizvozvo handina kutongofunga ini pachangu ndakafanira kuuya kwamuri; asi taurai neshoko, muranda wangu achaporeswa. <b>8</b> Nokuti iniwo ndiri munhu wakaiswa pasi pesimba, ndine mauto pasi pangu; ndinoti kune uyu: Enda, anoenda; uye kune umwe: Uya, anouya; nekumuranda wangu: Ita ichi, achiita.

Jesu anomutsa mwanakomana wechirikadzi paNaini <b>11</b> Zvino zvakaitika zuva raitevera, kuti wakaenda kuguta rainzi Naini; zvino vazhinji vevadzidzi vake nechaunga chikuru vakaenda naye.

Johwani Mubhabhatidzi anotuma vadzidzi kunobvunza Jesu <b>18</b> Vadzidzi vaJohwani ndokumupira zvezvinhu izvozvi zvese. <b>19</b> Johwani akadanira kwaari vamwe vaviri vevadzidzi vake, akavatuma kuna Jesu achiti: Imwi ndimwi anouya here, kana tichakataririra umwe?

<b>23</b> Uye wakaropafadzwa ani nani asingagumbuswi neni. <b>24</b> Zvino vatumwa vaJohwani vakati vabva, wakatanga kutaura kuzvaunga pamusoro paJohwani, achiti: Makabudira kurenje kunoonei? Rutsanga rwunozununguswa nemhepo here? <b>25</b> Asi makange mabuda kunoonei? Munhu wakapfeka nguvo dzakapfava here? Tarirai, vari munguvo dzinobwinya, vachirarama zvakanaka, vari mudzimba dzemadzimambo. <b>26</b> Asi makange mabuda kunoonei? Muporofita here? Hongu, ndinoti kwamuri: Kunyange anopfuura zvikuru muporofita. 
<b>27</b> Uyu ndiye wakanyorwa pamusoro pake, zvichinzi: Tarira, ini ndinotuma mutumwa wangu pamberi pechiso chako, achagadzira nzira yako pamberi pako. <b>28</b> Nokuti ndinoti kwamuri: Pakati pevakaberekwa nevakadzi, hakuna muporofita mukuru kuna Johwani Mubhabhatidzi; asi mudikisa muushe hwaMwari mukuru kwaari. <b>29</b> Zvino vanhu vese nevateresi vakati vachizvinzwa vakati Mwari wakarurama, zvavakange vabhabhatidzwa nerubhabhatidzo rwaJohwani. <b>30</b> Asi VaFarisi nenyanzvi dzemutemo vakaramba zano raMwari vachizvipikisa, vakasabhabhatidzwa naye.

Vakafanana nei? <b>32</b> Vakafanana nevana vadiki vagere pamisika vachidanidzirana vachiti: Takakuridzirai nyere asi hamuna kutamba, takakuririrai asi hamuna kuchema. <b>33</b> Nokuti Johwani Mubhabhatidzi akauya asingadyi chingwa kana kunwa waini, zvino moti: Ane dhimoni; <b>34</b> Mwanakomana wemunhu wauya achidya, achinwa, zvino moti: Tarirai munhu wemakaro nemunwi wewaini, shamwari yevateresi nevatadzi. 
<b>35</b> Asi uchenjeri hunonzi hwakarurama nevana vahwo vese. Mukadzi mutadzi anozodza makumbo aJesu <b>36</b> Zvino umwe weVaFarisi wakamukumbira kuti adye naye; akapinda mumba memuFarisi, akagara pakudya. <b>37</b> Zvino tarira, mukadzi muguta iro, waiva mutadzi, wakati aziva kuti agere pakudya mumba memuFarisi, akauya nechinu chearibhasiteri* chechizoro. <b>38</b> Akamira patsoka dzake mushure make achichema, akatanga kunyorovesa tsoka dzake nemisodzi, akapukuta nevhudzi remusoro wake, ndokutsvoda tsoka dzake ndokudzizodza nechizoro. 
<b>39</b> Zvino muFarisi wakange amudana wakati achizviona, akataura mukati make, achiti: Uyu dai aiva muporofita, angadai aiziva kuti ndiani, uye ndewakadini mukadzi anomubata, nokuti mutadzi. <b>40</b> Jesu ndokupindura akati kwaari: Simoni, ndine chekutaura kwauri. Iye ndokuti: Mudzidzisi, revai. <b>41</b> Kwakange kune vakwereti vaviri kune umwe mukweretesi; umwe wakange ane chikwereti chamadhenario* mazana mashanu, uye umwe makumi mashanu. 
<b>42</b> Vakati vasina chekuripisa, akavakanganwira vari vaviri. Naizvozvo ndeupi wavo, taura, achamuda kupfuura umwe? <b>43</b> Simoni akapindura akati: Ndinofunga ndouyo waakakanganwira kupfuura umwe. Akati kwaari: Watonga zvakarurama. <b>44</b> Zvino akatendeukira kumukadzi, akati kuna Simoni: Unoona mukadzi uyu here? Ndapinda mumba mako, mvura yetsoka dzangu hauna kundipa; asi uyu wanyorevesa tsoka dzangu nemisodzi, akapusika nevhudzi remusoro wake. <b>45</b> Hauna kunditsvoda, asi iye chinguri chandapinda, haana kumira kutsvoda tsoka dzangu. 
<b>46</b> Nemafuta musoro wangu hauna kuuzodza; asi iye wazodza tsoka dzangu nechizoro. <b>47</b> Naizvozvo, ndinoti kwauri: Zvivi zvake zvizhinji zvakanganwirwa, nokuti wada zvikuru; asi anokanganwirwa zvishoma, anoda zvishoma. <b>48</b> Zvino akati kwaari: Zvivi zvako zvakanganwirwa. <b>49</b> Zvino avo vakange vagere pakudya naye vakatanga kureva mukati mavo, vachiti: Ndiani uyu anokanganwirawo zvivi? <b>50</b> Akati kumukadzi: Rutendo rwako rwakuponesa; enda nerugare. 
<b>Luke 8</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino zvakaitika shure kweizvozvo, kuti iye wakagura nemuguta nemusha umwe neumwe, achidanidzira nekuparidza mashoko akanaka eushe hwaMwari; nevanegumi nevaviri vanaye, <b>2</b> nevamwe vakadzi vakange vaporeswa pamweya yakaipa neurwere, Maria anonzi Magidharini, maari makabuda madhimoni manomwe, <b>3</b> naJohwana mukadzi waKuza mutariri waHerodhe, naSusana, nevamwe vazhinji, vaimubatsira nenhumbi dzavo. Mufananidzo wemukushi 
<b>4</b> Zvino chaunga chikuru chakati chaungana, uye vaibva kuguta ripi neripi vachiuya kwaari, akataura nemufananidzo akati: <b>5</b> Mukushi wakabuda kunokusha mbeu yake; zvino pakukusha kwake, imwe yakawira kurutivi rwenzira, ndokutsikwa netsoka, shiri dzekudenga dzikaidya dzikaipedza. <b>6</b> Uye imwe ikawira paruware; zvino yakamera, ikawoma, nokuti yakashaiwa unyoro. <b>7</b> Neimwe ikawira pakati peminzwa, minzwa ikakura nayo, ikaivhunga.

Wakati ataura zvinhu izvi, akadanidzira achiti: Ane nzeve dzekunzwa, ngaanzwe. <b>9</b> Vadzidzi vake ndokumubvunza vachiti: Mufananidzo uyu ungavei? <b>10</b> Ndokubva ati: Kwamuri kwakapiwa kuziva zvakavanzika zveushe hwaMwari; asi kune vamwe zviri mumifananidzo, kuti vachiona vasaona; uye vachinzwa vasanzwisisa. <b>11</b> Zvino mufananidzo ndiwoyu: Mbeu ishoko raMwari. 
<b>12</b> Avo vaparutivi rwenzira ndivo vanonzwa, ipapo dhiabhorosi anouya ndokubvisa shoko pamoyo wavo, kuti varege kutenda, vaponeswe. <b>13</b> Nevaparuware ndevaya, vanoti kana vanzwa, vanogamuchira shoko nemufaro; asi ava havana mudzi, vanotenda kwenguva, asi nenguva yemuedzo vanowa. <b>14</b> Neyakawira paminzwa, ndevaya kana vanzwa, zvino vanoenda ndokuvhungwa nekufunganya nefuma nemafaro eupenyu, vakasasvitsa zvibereko. 
<b>15</b> Asi iri muvhu rakanaka, ndevaya vanoti vanzwa shoko, vanorichengeta mumoyo wakarurama nowakanaka, vachibereka zvibereko nekutsungirira. Mufananidzo wemwenje <b>16</b> Hakuna munhu anoti kana atungidza mwenje, anoufukidzira nemudziyo, kana kuisa pasi pemubhedha; asi anouisa pachigadziko chemwenje, kuti avo vanopinda vaone chiedza. <b>17</b> Nokuti hakuna chakavanzika, chisingazovi pachena, kana chakavigwa, chisingazozikanwi nekubuda pachena. 
<b>18</b> Naizvozvo chenjerai, kuti munonzwa sei; nokuti ani nani anazvo, achapiwa; asi ani nani asina, achatorerwa kunyange icho chaanofunga kuti anacho. Ukama naJesu <b>19</b> Zvino kwakauya kwaari mai nevanin'ina vake, vakasagona kusvika kwaari nekuda kwechaunga. <b>20</b> Akaudzwa nevamwe vakati: Mai venyu nevanin'ina venyu vamire panze, vanoda kukuonai. <b>21</b> Asi wakapindura akati kwavari: Mai vangu nevanin'ina vangu ndeava vanonzwa shoko raMwari vachiriita. Jesu anonyaradza dutu remhepo 
<b>22</b> Zvino zvakaitika nerimwe remazuva kuti wakapinda muchikepe iye nevadzidzi vake, akati kwavari: Ngatiyambukire kune rumwe rutivi rwegungwa; vakasimuka nechikepe. <b>23</b> Zvino vakati vachienda nechikepe, akarara hope; kukauya dutu guru remhepo padziva; vakazarirwa nemvura, vakava munjodzi. <b>24</b> Vakaswedera vakamumutsa, vachiti: Tenzi, tenzi, toparara! Jesu anobudisa mweya yetsvina panyika yeVaGadharini <b>26</b> Zvino vakaenda nechikepe kunyika yeVaGadharini, yakatarisana neGarirea. <b>27</b> Wakati achibudira panyika, akamuchingamidza umwe murume achibva muguta, wakange ane madhimoni kwenguva refu, uye asingapfeki nguvo, asingagari mumba, asi kumarinda.

Ndinokukumbirisai, musanditambudza. <b>29</b> Nokuti wakange araira mweya wetsvina kuti ubude kumunhu; nokuti wakange wamubata nguva zhinji uye waichengetwa akasungwa nemaketani nemabote; asi akadambura zvisungo, achitinhirwa kumarenje nedhimoni. <b>30</b> Jesu ndokumubvunza achiti: Zita rako ndiani? Akamatendera. <b>33</b> Zvino madhimoni akabuda kumunhu, akapinda munguruve; boka ndokupitirikira kumawere richienda kudziva, rikabitirirwa. <b>34</b> Zvino vaidzifudza vachiona zvaitika, vakatiza, vakaenda kunotaura muguta nemumaruwa. 
<b>35</b> Zvino vakabuda kunoona zvaitika; vakasvika kuna Jesu, vakawana munhu, wakange abuda madhimoni maari agere patsoka dzaJesu, akapfeka uye akakwana mupfungwa; vakatya. <b>36</b> Vakange vazvionawo vakavaudza kuti akaponeswa sei aiva nemadhimoni. <b>37</b> Zvino chaunga chese chedunhu rakapoteredza VaGadharini chakakumbirisa kwaari kuti abve kwavari, nokuti vabatwa nekutya kukuru; akapinda muchikepe, akadzokera. <b>38</b> Asi murume wakange abuda madhimoni maari, wakakumbirisa kwaari kuti ave naye. Asi Jesu wakamurega achienda, achiti:

Jesu anoporesa mukadzi waiva nekubuda ropa, uye anomutsa mukunda waJairosi <b>40</b> Zvino zvakaitika kuti pakudzoka kwaJesu, chaunga chakamugamuchira; nokuti vese vakange vakamurindira. <b>41</b> Zvino tarira, kwakasvika murume, zita rake rainzi Jairosi, iye wakange ari mukuru wesinagoge, ndokuwira patsoka dzaJesu, akamukumbirisa kuti apinde mumba make;

<b>Luke 9</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakadanira pamwe vadzidzi vake gumi nevaviri, akavapa simba nechikuriri pamusoro pemadhimoni ese, nekuporesa zvirwere. <b>2</b> Akavatuma kuparidza ushe hwaMwari nekuporesa varwere. <b>3</b> Zvino akati kwavari: Musatakura chinhu cherwendo, kana mudonzvo, kana nhava, kana chingwa, kana mari; uye musava nenguvo refu mbiri umwe neumwe. <b>4</b> Uye imba ipi neipi yamunopinda mairi, garaimo, mugobvamo.

Petro anopupura kuti Jesu ndiye Kristu <b>18</b> Zvino zvakaitika ari ega achinyengetera, vadzidzi vakange vanaye; akavabvunza, achiti: Zvaunga zvinoti ndini ani?

Ndokuenda kune umwe musha. Kutevera Jesu <b>57</b> Zvino zvakaitika vachifamba munzira, umwe wakati kwaari: Ishe, ndichakuteverai kupi zvako kwamunoenda.

<b>Luke 10</b><br><b>1</b> Shure kwezvinhu izvi, Ishe akagadza vamwe makumi manomwewo, akavatuma vaviri vaviri pamberi pechiso chake muguta rimwe nerimwe nenzvimbo imwe neimwe iye kwaakange achizoenda. <b>2</b> Naizvozvo wakati kwavari: Goho zvirokwazvo iguru, asi vashandi vashoma. Naizvozvo kumbirai Ishe wegoho kuti abudisire vashandi pagoho rake. <b>3</b> Endai; tarirai, ini ndinokutumai semakwayana pakati pemapumhi. <b>4</b> Musatakura chikwama, kana nhava, kana manyatera; uye musakwazisa munhu panzira.

Kudzoka kwevanhu makumi manomwe <b>17</b> Vanemakumi manomwe vakadzoka nemufaro, vachiti: Ishe, nemadhimoni anozviisa pasi pedu muzita renyu. <b>18</b> Zvino akati kwavari: Ndakaona Satani semheni achiwa achibva kudenga.

Jesu anovonga Baba <b>21</b> Neawa iro Jesu wakafara mumweya, akati: Ndinokuvongai, Baba, Ishe wekudenga nenyika, kuti makavanza zvinhu izvi kune vakachenjera nevanonzwisisa, mukazvizarurira vacheche; hongu, Baba, nokuti saizvozvo zvakava zvakanaka pamberi penyu.

<b>21</b> Zvino mwanakomana akati kwavari: Baba, ndakatadzira kudenga nepamberi penyu, uye handichafaniri kunzi mwanakomana wenyu. <b>22</b> Asi baba vakati kuvaranda vavo: Budai nenguvo refu yakanakisisa mumupfekedze, muise mhete paruoko rwake, nemanyatera patsoka; <b>23</b> muuye nemhuru yakakodzwa, mubaye, uye ngatidye tifare; <b>24</b> nokuti uyu mwanakomana wangu wakange afa, zvino wararamazve; wakange arasika, asi wawanikwa; zvino vakatanga kufara. 
<b>25</b> Zvino mwanakomana wake mukuru wakange ari kumunda; zvino wakati achisvika, oswedera paimba, akanzwa mumhanzi nekutamba. <b>26</b> Zvino wakadanira kwaari umwe wevaranda, akabvunza kuti izvi zvinorevei. <b>27</b> Zvino akati kwaari: Munin'ina wako wasvika; zvino baba vako vabaya mhuru yakakodzwa, nokuti vamugamuchira ari mutano. <b>28</b> Asi wakatsamwa, akasada kupinda; naizvozvo baba vake vakabuda, vakamukumbirisa. 
<b>29</b> Asi iye wakapindura akati kuna baba: Tarirai, makore mazhinji akadai ndakakubatirai, handina kutongodarika murairo wenyu, asi hamuna kutongondipa mbudzana, kuti ndifare neshamwari dzangu. <b>30</b> Asi mangoti mwanakomana wenyu uyu wakaparadza nhumbi dzenyu nezvifeve achisvika, mamubaira mhuru yakakodzwa. <b>31</b> Vakati kwaari: Mwana, iwe uneni nguva dzese, uye zvese zvangu ndezvako. <b>32</b> Asi kwaifanira kuti kupemberwe nekufarwa, nokuti munin'ina wako uyu wakange afa, wararamazve; wakange arasika, wawanikwa. 
<b>Luke 16</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakataurawo kuvadzidzi vake achiti: Kwakange kune umwe munhu mufumi, waiva nemutariri; iye wakapomerwa kwaari seanoparadza nhumbi dzake. <b>2</b> Zvino wakamudana akati kwaari: Chii ichi chandinonzwa nezvako? Rondedzera nezveutariri hwako; nokuti hauchazogoni kuva mutariri. <b>3</b> Zvino mutariri wakati mukati make: Ndichaitei, sezvo ishe wangu wonditorera utariri? Handigoni kuchera, kupemha ndinonyara. <b>4</b> Ndaziva zvandichaita, kuti kana ndabudiswa pautariri, vandigamuchire mudzimba dzavo. 
<b>5</b> Zvino wakadanira kwaari umwe neumwe wavakange vane chikwereti kuna ishe wake, akati kune wekutanga: Une chikwereti chakadini kuna ishe wangu? <b>6</b> Iye ndokuti: Zviyero zana zvemafuta. Ndokuti kwaari: Tora rugwaro rwako, ugare pasi nekukurumidza unyore makumi mashanu. <b>7</b> Ndokuti kune umwe: Newe, une chikwereti chakadini? Iye ndokuti: Zviyero zana zvezviyo. Akati kwaari: Tora rugwaro rwako, unyore makumi masere. 
<b>8</b> Zvino ishe wakarumbidza mutariri asakarurama, nokuti wakange aita nekuchenjera; nokuti vana venyika ino vakachenjera pazera ravo kukunda vana vechiedza. <b>9</b> Neni ndinoti kwamuri: Muzviitire shamwari nafuma wekusakarurama, kuti kana musingachagoni, vakugamuchirei mudzimba dzekusingaperi. <b>10</b> Wakatendeka pachidikisa wakatendekawo pachikuru, neusakarurama pachidikisa haana kururamawo pachikuru. <b>11</b> Naizvozvo kana musina kutendeka pana fuma usakarurama, ndiani achakubatisai chinhu chechokwadi? 
<b>12</b> Uye kana musina kutendeka pane cheumwe, ndiani achakupai chenyu? <b>13</b> Hakuna muranda anogona kubatira madzitenzi maviri; nokuti zvimwe achavenga umwe, ndokuda umwe; kana kuti achanamatira kune umwe, agozvidza umwe. Hamugoni kubatira Mwari nafuma. Jesu unomhura VaFarisi <b>14</b> Zvino neVaFarisi vakange vari vadi vemari, vakanzwa zvinhu izvi zvese, vakamuseka. <b>15</b> Zvino wakati kwavari: Imwi ndimwi vanozviruramisa pamberi pevanhu, asi Mwari anoziva moyo yenyu; nokuti icho chinokwiridzirwa pakati pevanhu, chinyangadzo pamberi paMwari. 
<b>16</b> Murairo nevaporofita zvaivapo kusvikira kuna Johwani; kubva ipapo ushe hwaMwari hunoparidzwa, uye umwe neumwe anosenerera mahuri. <b>17</b> Asi zvakareruka kuti denga nenyika zvipfuure, pakuti chidodzi chimwe chemurairo chiwe. <b>18</b> Ani nani anoramba mukadzi wake akawana umwe anoita upombwe; uye ani nani anowana wakarambwa kumurume anoita upombwe. Mufananidzo wemufumi naRazaro <b>19</b> Kwakange kune umwe munhu mufumi, waipfeka zvine ruvara rwehute nemicheka yakaisvonaka, nekufara paumbozha mazuva ese. 
<b>20</b> Zvino kwakange kune umwe mupemhi wainzi Razaro, wakange akaiswa pasuwo rake azere maronda, <b>21</b> uye waishuva kugutswa nezvimedu zvaiwa patafura yemufumi; asi kunyange imbwa dzaiuya dzichinanzva maronda ake. <b>22</b> Zvino zvakaitika kuti mupemhi wakafa, akatakurirwa nevatumwa kuchifuva chaAbhurahama; mufumi akafawo, akavigwa. <b>23</b> Zvino ari muhadhesi* wakasimudza meso ake, ari pamarwadzo, akaona Abhurahama ari kure, naRazaro ari pachifuva chake. 
<b>24</b> Iye ndokudanidzira akati: Baba Abhurahama, ndinzwirei tsitsi, mutume Razaro, kuti anyike muromo wemunwe wake mumvura, atonhodze rurimi rwangu; nokuti ndinorwadziswa mumurazvo uyu. <b>25</b> Asi Abhurahama wakati: Mwana, rangarira kuti iwe muupenyu hwako wakagamuchira zvakanaka zvako, saizvozvo naRazaro zvakaipa; asi zvino iye anonyaradzwa, uye iwe unorwadziwa. <b>26</b> Uye pamusoro peizvi zvese, pakati pedu nemwi pane mukaha mukuru wakaiswa, kuti vanoda kuyambuka kubva kuno vachiuya kwamuri vakonewe, neveko varege kuyambukira kwatiri. 
<b>27</b> Iye ndokuti: Naizvozvo ndinokukumbirisai, baba, kuti mumutumire kuimba yababa vangu, <b>28</b> nokuti ndine vanakomana vamai vangu vashanu, kuti anyatsovapupurira, zvimwe ivowo vangauya panzvimbo ino yemarwadzo. <b>29</b> Abhurahama akati kwaari: Mozisi nevaporofita vanavo; ngavanzwe ivo. <b>30</b> Iye ndokuti: Kwete, baba Abhurahama; asi kana umwe akaenda kwavari kubva kuvakafa, vachatendeuka. <b>31</b> Asi wakati kwaari: Kana vasingateereri Mozisi nevaporofita, havangatendiswi kunyange kana umwe akamuka kubva kuvakafa. 
<b>Luke 17</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakati kuvadzidzi: Hazvigoneki kuti zvigumbuso zvisauya; asi ane nhamo wazvinouya kubudikidza naye. <b>2</b> Zvaiva nani kwaari kuti guyo rinorema risungirirwe kupoteredza mutsipa wake, ndokukandirwa mugungwa, pakuti agumbuse umwe wevadiki ava. <b>3</b> Muzvichenjerere imwi. Zvino kana umwe wako akakutadzira, mutsiure; uye kana akatendeuka, mukanganwire. <b>4</b> Uye kana akutadzira kanomwe pazuva, akadzoka kwauri kanomwe pazuva, achiti: Ndatendeuka; uchamukanganwira. Simba rekutenda; varanda pasina 
<b>5</b> Zvino vaapositori vakati kuna Ishe: Wedzerai kwatiri rutendo. <b>6</b> Ishe ndokuti: Kana maiva nerutendo rwakaita setsanga yemasitadha, maiti kumuhabhurosi uyu: Dzurwa, usimwe mugungwa; uye waikuteererai. <b>7</b> Asi ndeupi kwamuri ane muranda anorima kana kufudza, achati pakarepo achipinda achibva kumunda: Swedera ugare pakudya? <b>8</b> Asi angarega here kuti kwaari: Gadzira zvandingaraira, uye uzvisunge chiuno, undishandire, kusvikira ndadya, ndikanwa; zvino mushure meizvozvi iwe udye nekunwa? 
<b>9</b> Anotenda muranda uyo nokuti wakaita zvarairwa here? Handifungi. <b>10</b> Saizvozvo nemwi, kana maita zvese zvamakarairwa, muti: Tiri varanda pasina, nokuti taita zvataifanira kuita. Jesu anoporesa vane maperembudzi gumi <b>11</b> Zvino zvakaitika pakuenda kwake kuJerusarema, kuti iye wakapfuura nepakati peSamaria neGarirea. <b>12</b> Zvino wakati achipinda mune umwe musha, varume gumi vaiva nemaperembudzi vakasangana naye, vamire kure. <b>13</b> Zvino ivo vakasimudza inzwi, vachiti: Jesu, Tenzi, tinzwirei tsitsi! 
<b>14</b> Zvino wakati achivaona, akati kwavari: Endai munozviratidza kuvapristi. Zvino zvakaitika kuti vachienda, vakanatswa. <b>15</b> Zvino umwe wavo paakaona kuti aporeswa, wakadzoka achikudza Mwari nenzwi guru; <b>16</b> ndokuwira pasi nechiso patsoka dzake, achimuvonga; zvino iye wakange ari muSamaria. <b>17</b> Asi Jesu wakapindura akati: Vanga vasiri gumi here vanatswa? Ko vapfumbamwe varipi? <b>18</b> Hapana kuwanikwa vadzokera kuzopa Mwari rukudzo, kunze kweuyu mutorwa. 
<b>19</b> Zvino akati kwaari: Simuka, enda; rutendo rwako rwakuponesa. Ushe hwaMwari nekudzoka kwaIshe <b>20</b> Zvino wakati achibvunzwa neVaFarisi, kuti ushe hwaMwari hunosvika rinhi, akavapindura akati: Ushe hwaMwari hahuuyi nekuonekwa; <b>21</b> uye havangati: Tarirai pano; kana: Tarira uko. Nokuti tarirai, ushe hwaMwari huri mukati menyu. <b>22</b> Zvino wakati kuvadzidzi: Mazuva achasvika amuchashuva kuona rimwe ramazuva eMwanakomana wemunhu, asi hamungarioni. 
<b>23</b> Zvino vachati kwamuri: Tarirai pano; kana: Tarirai uko; regai kuenda, kana kutevera. <b>24</b> Nokuti semheni inopenya ichibva kune rumwe rutivi pasi pedenga ichivhenekera kusvikira kune rumwe rutivi pasi pedenga, saizvozvo zvichaitawo Mwanakomana wemunhu nezuva rake. <b>25</b> Asi kutanga anofanira kutambudzika zvinhu zvizhinji, nekurambwa nezera iri. <b>26</b> Uye sezvazvakange zvakaita mumazuva aNowa, ndizvo zvazvichavawo mumazuva eMwanakomana wemunhu. 
<b>27</b> Vaidya, vainwa, vaiwana, vachiwaniswa, kusvikira zuva iro Nowa raakapinda naro muareka, mafashame akasvika, ndokuvaparadza vese. <b>28</b> Saizvozvowo sezvazvakange zvakaita mumazuva aRoti; vaidya, vainwa, vaitenga, vaitengesa, vaidzvara, vaivaka; <b>29</b> asi nemusi Roti waakabuda muSodhoma, kwakanaya moto nesarufa zvichibva kudenga, zvikaparadza vese. <b>30</b> Zvichava seizvi nezuva Mwanakomana wemunhu raachabudiswa pachena naro. 
<b>31</b> Muzuva iro, uyo achava pamusoro pedenga reimba, nenhumbi dzake dziri mumba, ngaarege kuburuka kundodzitora; neari kumunda saizvozvo ngaarege kudzokera zvinhu zviri shure. <b>32</b> Yeukai mukadzi waRoti. <b>33</b> Ani nani anotsvaka kuponesa upenyu hwake acharasikirwa nahwo, uye ani nani anorasikirwa nahwo achahuraramisa. <b>34</b> Ndinoti kwamuri: Muusiku uhwo kuchava nevaviri pamubhedha umwe; umwe achatorwa, uye umwe achasiiwa. <b>35</b> Vaviri vachava vachikuya pamwe, umwe achatorwa, uye umwe achasiiwa. 
<b>36</b> Vaviri vachava mumunda; umwe achatorwa, uye umwe asiiwe. <b>37</b> Zvino vakapindura vakati kwaari: Kupi Ishe? Ndokuti kwavari: Pane mutumbi, ndipo pachaunganidzwa magora. 
<b>Luke 18</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakavaudzawo mufananidzo kuti vanofanira kunyengetera nguva dzese, uye vasaora moyo, <b>2</b> achiti: Kwaiva mune rimwe guta neumwe mutongi, wakange asingatyi Mwari, uye asingaremekedzi munhu; <b>3</b> zvino kwakange kune chirikadzi muguta iro, yakange ichiuya kwaari, ichiti: Ndiruramisirei kumuvengi wangu. <b>4</b> Asi wakange asingadi kwenguva; asi pashure pezvinhu izvi wakati mukati make: Kunyange ndisingatyi Mwari, uye ndisingaremekedzi munhu; 
<b>5</b> asi nokuti chirikadzi iyi inonditambudza, ndichairuramisira, zvimwe pakupedzisira ingasvika pakunditsamwisa kwazvo. <b>6</b> Ishe ndokuti: Inzwai mutongi asakarurama zvaanoreva. <b>7</b> Ko Mwari haangaruramisiri here vakasanangurwa vake, vanodana kwaari masikati neusiku, kunyange achivanonokera? <b>8</b> Ndinoti kwamuri: Achakurumidza kuvaruramisira. Jesu anogamuchira vanana <b>15</b> Zvino vakauisa kwaari vachechewo, kuti avabate; asi vadzidzi vakati vachizviona, vakavatsiura.

<b>Luke 19</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino Jesu wakapinda, akagura muJeriko. <b>2</b> Zvino tarira, murume wainzi Zakewu nezita; iye wakange ari mutungamiriri wevateresi, uye iye waiva mufumi; <b>3</b> uye wakatsvaka kuona Jesu kuti ndiani; asi wakange asingagoni nekuda kwechaunga, nokuti wakange ane chimiro chifupi. <b>4</b> Zvino wakamhanyira mberi, akakwira mumuonde wemusikamori kuti amuone; nokuti wakange achizopfuura nenzira iyo. 
<b>5</b> Zvino Jesu wakati achisvika panzvimbo iyo, wakatarira kumusoro, akamuona, akati kwaari: Zakewu, kurumidza uburuke; nokuti nhasi ndinofanira kugara mumba mako. <b>6</b> Zvino wakakurumidza kuburuka, akamugamuchira achifara. <b>7</b> Zvino vakati vazviona vakan'un'una vese vachiti: Wapinda kunogara nemurume mutadzi. <b>8</b> Asi Zakewu wakamira, akati kuna Ishe: Tarirai, hafu yefuma yangu, Ishe, ndinopa kuvarombo, uye kana ndakatora chinhu kune umwe munhu nekunyengera, ndinodzosera runa. 
<b>9</b> Jesu akati kwaari: Nhasi ruponeso rwasvika mumba muno; nokuti iyewo mwanakomana waAbhurahama. <b>10</b> Nokuti Mwanakomana wemunhu wakauya kuzotsvaka nekuponesa chakarasika. Mufananidzo wepondo <b>11</b> Vakati vanzwa zvinhu izvi, wakawedzera akataura mufananidzo, nokuti wakange ava pedo neJerusarema, uye nekuti vaifunga kuti ushe hwaMwari hwobva hwodoonekereswa. <b>12</b> Naizvozvo wakati: Umwe munhu ane mbiri wakaenda kunyika iri kure, kunozvigamuchirira ushe agodzoka. 
<b>13</b> Zvino wakadana varanda vake gumi, ndokuvapa pondo gumi, ndokuti kwavari: Bhindaukai nayo kusvikira ndichiuya. <b>14</b> Asi vanhu venyika yake vakange vachimuvenga, vakatuma nhume shure kwake, vachiti: Hatidi uyu kuti atitonge. <b>15</b> Zvino zvakaitika kuti adzoka agamuchira ushe, wakaraira kuti vaya varanda vaakange apa mari, vadanirwe kwaari, kuti anzwe kuti umwe neumwe wakawana zvakadini nekutengeserana. <b>16</b> Wekutanga ndokusvika, achiti: Ishe, pondo yenyu yawana pondo gumi. 
<b>17</b> Zvino akati kwaari: Zvakanaka, muranda wakanaka; nokuti wakange wakatendeka pachidiki-diki, uve nesimba pamusoro pemaguta gumi. <b>18</b> Newechipiri wakasvika, achiti: Ishe, pondo yenyu yawana pondo shanu. <b>19</b> Akati kwaariwo: Newe, uve pamusoro pemaguta mashanu. <b>20</b> Zvino umwe wakasvika, achiti: Ishe, tarirai pondo yenyu, yandakange ndakachengeta mumucheka wekumeso; <b>21</b> nokuti ndakange ndichikutyai, nokuti muri munhu wakaoma; munotora zvamusina kuisa, muchikohwa izvo zvamusina kudzvara. 
<b>22</b> Zvino wakati kwaari: Ndichakutonga nezvinobuda mumuromo mako, muranda wakaipa; wakange uchiziva kuti ini ndiri munhu wakaoma, ndichitora zvandisakaisa, nekukohwa izvo zvandisina kudzvara; <b>23</b> naizvozvo sei usina kuisa mari yangu mubhanga, kuti kana ndouya ndigozoitora pamwe nezvibereko. <b>24</b> Ndokuti kune vakange vamirepo: Mutorerei pondo mupe kune ane pondo gumi. <b>25</b> Asi vakati kwaari: Ishe, ane pondo gumi. <b>26</b> Nokuti ndinoti kwamuri: Anazvo achapiwa; uye asina, kwaari kuchatorwa nezvaanazvo. 
<b>27</b> Asi vavengi vangu avo, vakange vasingadi kuti nditonge pamusoro pavo, uisai pano, muvauraye pamberi pangu. <b>28</b> Zvino wakati areva zvinhu izvi, akapfuurira mberi, achikwira kuJerusarema. Jesu anogamuchirwa paJerusarema <b>29</b> Zvino zvakaitika oswedera kuBhetifage neBhetaniya pagomo rinonzi reMiorivhi, wakatuma vaviri vevadzidzi vake, <b>30</b> achiti: Fambai muende mumusha wakatarisana nemwi; kana muchipinda mauri, muchawana dhongwana rakasungirwa, risina munhu wakamborigara; murisunungure, mugoriuisa. 
<b>31</b> Zvino kana chero munhu akakubvunzai achiti: Munorisunungurirei? Muchataura kwaari kudai: Ishe anorida. <b>32</b> Avo vakatumwa vakaenda vakanowana sezvaakange ataura kwavari. <b>33</b> Zvino vakati vachisunungura dhongwana, varidzi varo vakati kwavari: Munosunungurirei dhongwana? <b>34</b> Ivo ndokuti: Ishe anorida. <b>35</b> Zvino vakariuisa kuna Jesu; ndokukanda nguvo dzavo pamusoro pedhongwana, vakatasvisa Jesu pariri. <b>36</b> Zvino achifamba, vakawarira nguvo dzavo munzira. 
<b>37</b> Zvino wakati oswedera ikozvino pamateru egomo reMiorivhi, chaunga chese chevadzidzi chikatanga kufara chichirumbidza Mwari nenzwi guru pamusoro pamabasa ese esimba avakange vaona, <b>38</b> vachiti: Wakaropafadzwa mambo anouya muzita raIshe; rugare kudenga, nekubwinya kumusoro-soro. <b>39</b> Uye vamwe veVaFarisi vari pakati pechaunga vakati kwaari: Mudzidzisi, tsiurai vadzidzi venyu. <b>40</b> Zvino wakapindura akati kwavari: Ndinokuudzai kuti kana ava vakanyarara, mabwe achadanidzira. Jesu anochema pamusoro peJerusarema 
<b>41</b> Zvino wakati aswedera, wakaona guta, akachema pamusoro paro, <b>42</b> achiti: Dai newe waiziva, kunyange pazuva rino rako zvinhu zverugare rwako; asi zvino zvakavanzirwa meso ako. <b>43</b> Nokuti mazuva achasvika pamusoro pako, apo vavengi vako vachakukandira muchinjiziri wakakupoteredza, vachakukomba, nekukudzvinyidzira pamativi ese, <b>44</b> uye vachakuputsira pasi iwe nevana vako mukati mako, havangasii mukati mako ibwe pamusoro pebwe; nokuti hauna kuziva nguva yekushanyirwa kwako. Jesu anonatsa tembere 
<b>45</b> Zvino wakapinda mutembere, akatanga kudzingira panze vakange vachitengesamo nevaitenga, <b>46</b> achiti kwavari: Kwakanyorwa kuti: Imba yangu imba yemunyengetero; asi imwi maiita bako remakororo. <b>47</b> Zvino waidzidzisa zuva rimwe nerimwe mutembere; asi vapristi vakuru nevanyori nevakuru vevanhu vakatsvaka kumuparadza. <b>48</b> Asi havana kuwana zvavangaita, nokuti vanhu vese vakamunamatira vachimunzwa. 
<b>Luke 20</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino zvakaitika parimwe remazuva iwayo, achidzidzisa vanhu mutembere nekuparidza evhangeri, vapristi vakuru nevanyori vakasvika nevakuru, <b>2</b> ndokutaura kwaari vachiti: Tiudzei, munoita zvinhu izvi nesimba ripi, kana ndiani iye wakakupai simba iri? <b>3</b> Zvino wakapindura akati kwavari: Ini ndichakubvunzaiwo shoko rimwe; uye mundiudze: <b>4</b> Rubhabhatidzo rwaJohwani rwakabva kudenga, kana kuvanhu? <b>5</b> Zvino vakarangana, vachiti: Kana tikati: Kudenga; achati: Naizvozvo makaregerei kumutenda? 
<b>6</b> Asi kana tikati: Kuvanhu; vanhu vese vachatitaka nemabwe; nokuti vaiva nechokwadi kuti Johwani waiva muporofita. <b>7</b> Vakapindura, vachiti havazivi kwarwakabva. <b>8</b> Jesu ndokuti kwavari: Neni handikuudzii kuti ndinoita zvinhu izvi nesimba ripi. Mufananidzo wevarimi vakaipa <b>9</b> Zvino wakatanga kuudza vanhu mufananidzo uyu: Umwe munhu wakasima munda wemizambiringa, akauhaisa kuvarimi, akaenda parwendo nguva refu; 
<b>10</b> zvino nenguva yakafanira wakatuma muranda kuvarimi, kuti vamupe zvechibereko chemunda wemizambiringa; asi varimi vakamurova, vakamuendesa asina chinhu. <b>11</b> Zvino wakatumazve umwe muranda; naiye vakamurovawo vakamunyadzisa, vakamuendesa asina chinhu. <b>12</b> Zvino wakatumazve wechitatu; uyuwo ndokumukuvadza, vakamukandira kunze. <b>13</b> Zvino mwene wemunda wemizambiringa wakati: Ndichaitei? Ndichatuma mwanakomana wangu anodikanwa, zvimwe vakaona iye vachamukudza. 
<b>14</b> Asi varimi vakati vachimuona vakarangana, vachiti: Uyu ndiye mugari wenhaka; uyai, ngatimuuraye, kuti nhaka igova yedu. <b>15</b> Vakamukandira kunze kwemunda wemizambiringa, vakamuuraya. Naizvozvo mwene wemunda wemizambiringa achavaitirei? <b>16</b> Achauya, ndokuparadza varimi ivava, agopa munda wemizambiringa vamwe. Zvino vachinzwa, vakati: Zvisadaro! <b>17</b> Asi wakavatarisa, akati: Naizvozvo chii ichi chakanyorwa, chinoti: Ibwe vavaki ravakaramba, ndiro rava musoro wekona? 
<b>18</b> Wese anowira pamusoro pebwe iro, achavhuniwa-vhuniwa; asi ani nani warinowira pamusoro richamukuya. Kuripira Kesari mutero <b>19</b> Zvino vapristi vakuru nevanyori neawa iroro vakatsvaka kuisa maoko paari, asi vakatya vanhu; nokuti vakaziva kuti wakareva mufananidzo uyu akanangana navo. <b>20</b> Vakamucherekedza, vakatumira vashori vakanyepedzera kuva vakarurama, kuti vamubate pakutaura, kuti vamukumikidze kuushe nesimba remutungamiriri. 
<b>21</b> Zvino vakamubvunza, vachiti: Mudzidzisi, tinoziva kuti munotaura nekudzidzisa zvakarurama, uye hamugamuchiri chiso, asi munodzidzisa nzira yaMwari muchokwadi. <b>22</b> Zviri pamutemo here kwatiri kupa mutero kuna Kesari, kana kwete? <b>23</b> Asi wakaziva unyengeri hwavo, akati kwavari: Munondiidzirei? <b>24</b> Ndiratidzei dhenario*; rine mufananidzo nerunyoro rwaani? Vakapindura vakati: ZvaKesari. <b>25</b> Ndokuti kwavari: Naizvozvo dzorerai kuna Kesari zvinhu zvaKesari, nekuna Mwari zvinhu zvaMwari. 
<b>26</b> Vakakoniwa kumubata neshoko rake pamberi pevanhu; vakashamisika nemhinduro yake; vakanyarara. Vanobvunza Jesu pamusoro pekumuka kwevakafa <b>27</b> Zvino kwakaswedera vamwe veVaSadhusi, vanoramba kuti kune kumuka, ndokumubvunza, <b>28</b> vachiti: Mudzidzisi, Mozisi wakatinyorera kuti: Kana mukoma wemunhu akafa ane mukadzi, asi iye akafa asina vana, munin'ina wake atore mukadzi, amutsire mukoma wake mbeu. <b>29</b> Naizvozvo kwakange kune vanakomana vemunhu umwe vanomwe; wekutanga ndokutora mukadzi, akafa asina mwana; 
<b>30</b> newechipiri akatora mukadzi uyo, neuyu akafa asina mwana; <b>31</b> newechitatu akamutora. Zvikadarowo nevanomwe, uye vakasasiya vana, vakafa. <b>32</b> Pakupedzisira kwavo vese mukadzi wakafawo. <b>33</b> Naizvozvo pakumuka, achava mukadzi waani kwavari? Nokuti vanomwe vakava naye semukadzi. <b>34</b> Zvino Jesu wakapindura akati kwavari: Vana venyika ino vanowana, vanowaniswa; <b>35</b> asi avo vachanzi vakafanira kuwana nyika iyo nekumuka kubva kuvakafa havawani kana kuwaniswa; 
<b>36</b> nokuti havagoni kufazve; nokuti vakaita sevatumwa, uye vanakomana vaMwari, vari vanakomana vekumuka. <b>37</b> Asi kuti vakafa vanomutswa, kunyange Mozisi wakaratidza pagwenzi reminzwa, paakaidza Ishe Mwari waAbhurahama, naMwari waIsaka, naMwari waJakobho. <b>38</b> Nokuti haasi Mwari wevakafa, asi wevapenyu; nokuti vese vanoraramira iye. <b>39</b> Zvino vamwe vevanyori vakapindura vakati: Mudzidzisi, mataura zvakanaka. <b>40</b> Uye havana kuzoshinga kumubvunzazve chinhu. Kristu Mwanakomana waDhavhidhi 
<b>41</b> Zvino wakati kwavari: Vanoreva sei kuti Kristu Mwanakomana waDavhidhi? <b>42</b> Nokuti Dhavhidhi pachake wakareva mubhuku reMapisarema achiti: Ishe wakati kuna Ishe wangu: Gara kuruoko rwangu rwerudyi, <b>43</b> kusvikira ndaita vavengi vako chitsiko chetsoka dzako. <b>44</b> Naizvozvo Dhavhidhi anomuidza Ishe, ko Mwanakomana wake sei? Jesu anonyevera vadzidzi vake <b>45</b> Zvino vanhu vese vachakateerera, wakati kuvadzidzi vake: 
<b>46</b> Chenjerai vanyori, vanoda kufamba nenguvo refu, uye vanoda kwaziso pamisika, nezvigaro zvepamusorosa mumasinagoge, nezvigaro zvemberi pazvirairo; <b>47</b> vanodya dzimba dzechirikadzi, vanonyepedzera nekuita minyengetero mirefu; ivavo vachagamuchira mutongo mukuru. 
<b>Luke 21</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakati atarira kumusoro akaona vafumi vachikanda zvipo zvavo muchivigiro chezvipo. <b>2</b> Uye wakaonawo imwe chirikadzi murombo ichikandamo tumari twemhangura tuviri. <b>3</b> Ndokuti: Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Chirikadzi iyi murombo yakanda kupfuura vese; <b>4</b> nokuti avo vese vakanda muzvipo zvaMwari pamuraudziro wavo, asi iye, paurombo hwake wakanda chese chaaiva nacho chaairarama nacho. Jesu anoporofita kuputswa kweJerusarema nekudzoka kwake 
<b>5</b> Zvino vamwe vachiri kutaura pamusoro petembere, kuti yakange yakashongedzwa nemabwe akanaka nezvipo, wakati: <b>6</b> Zvinhu izvi zvamunoona, mazuva achasvika okuti ibwe harizosiiwi pamusoro pebwe, risingazoputsirwi pasi. <b>7</b> Zvino vakamubvunza, vachiti: Mudzidzisi, ko zvinhu izvi zvichava rinhi? Nechiratidzo chii, kana izvozvi zvoitika? <b>8</b> Iye ndokuti: Chenjerai kuti musatsauswa; nokuti vazhinji vachauya muzita rangu, vachiti: Ndini Kristu; uye: Nguva yaswedera. Naizvozvo musavatevera. 
<b>9</b> Zvino kana muchinzwa zvehondo nenyonga-nyonga, musavhunduswa; nokuti zvinhu izvi zvinofanira kutanga kuitika; asi kuguma hakuvipo pakarepo. <b>10</b> Ipapo wakati kwavari: Rudzi ruchamukira rudzi, neushe huchamukira ushe. <b>11</b> Uye kudengenyeka kwenyika kukuru kuchava kunzvimbo dzakasiyana-siyana, nenzara nematenda, uye zvinotyisa nezviratidzo zvikuru zvichabva kudenga zvichavapo. 
<b>12</b> Asi mberi kwaizvozvi zvese vachaisa maoko avo pamuri, nekushusha, vachikukumikidzai kumasinagoge nemumatirongo, muchiiswa pamberi pemadzimambo nevabati, nekuda kwezita rangu. <b>13</b> Uye izvi zvichashandukira kwamuri kuva uchapupu. <b>14</b> Naizvozvo zviisei mumoyo menyu, kuti murege kufanofunganya zvamuchazvidavirira. <b>15</b> Nokuti ini ndichakupai muromo nenjere, vavengi venyu vese zvavachakonewa kupikisa kana kuramba. 
<b>16</b> Zvino muchatengeswa kunyange nevabereki, nevanakomana vamai venyu, nehama, neshamwari, uye vachauraya vamwe venyu. <b>17</b> Uye muchavengwa nevese nekuda kwezita rangu. <b>18</b> Asi hakungatongoparari ruvhudzi rwemusoro wenyu. <b>19</b> Mukutsungirira kwenyu wanai mweya yenyu. <b>20</b> Zvino kana muchiona Jerusarema rakombwa nemauto, zivai ipapo kuti kuparadzwa kwaro kwaswedera. <b>21</b> Panguva iyo vari muJudhiya ngavatizire kumakomo; nevari mukati maro ngavabude; nevari kumaruwa ngavarege kupinda mariri. 
<b>22</b> Nokuti awa mazuva etsividzo, kuti zvinhu zvese zvakanyorwa zvizadziswe. <b>23</b> Asi vane nhamo vane mimba nevanonwisa mumazuva iwayo! Nokuti kuchavapo kutambudzika kukuru munyika, nehasha pamusoro perudzi urwu. <b>24</b> Uye vachawa nemuromo wemunondo, nekutapirwa kumarudzi ese; uye Jerusarema richatsikirwa pasi nevechirudzi, kusvikira nguva dzevechirudzi dzazadziswa. <b>25</b> Zvino zviratidzo zvichavapo pazuva nepamwedzi nepanyeredzi; nepanyika kutambudzika kwemarudzi nekukanganiswa; gungwa nemafungu zvichitinhira, 
<b>26</b> vanhu vachapera simba nekutya nekutarisira zvinhu izvo zvinouya panyika; nokuti masimba ekumatenga achazungunuswa. <b>27</b> Zvino ipapo vachaona Mwanakomana wemunhu achiuya mugore ane simba nekubwinya kukuru. <b>28</b> Zvino kana zvinhu izvi zvichitanga kuitika, itii tasa, uye musimudze misoro yenyu, nokuti rudzikunuro rwenyu rwaswedera. <b>29</b> Zvino wakavataurira mufananidzo achiti: Tarirai muonde nemiti yese; <b>30</b> kana ikozvino yotunga, munozvionera nekuziva kuti zhizha ikozvino rava pedo. 
<b>31</b> Saizvozvo imwiwo, kana moona zvinhu izvozvi zvichiitika, zivai kuti ushe hwaMwari hwava pedo. <b>32</b> Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Zera iri haringatongopfuuri, kusvikira zvese zvaitika. <b>33</b> Denga nenyika zvichapfuura, asi mashoko angu haangatongopfuuri. <b>34</b> Zvino zvichenjererei, kuti moyo yenyu irege kuremedzwa nekudyisa nekuraradza nekufunganya zveupenyu huno, zuva iro rikusvikirei musingafungiri. <b>35</b> Nokuti seriva richasvika pamusoro pevese vagere pachiso chenyika yese. 
<b>36</b> Naizvozvo rindai panguva yese munyengetere kuti muverengwe makafanira kupukunyuka zvinhu izvi zvese zvichaitika, nekumira pamberi peMwanakomana wemunhu. <b>37</b> Zvino waidzidzisa masikati mutembere; asi waibuda achipedzera usiku mugomo rinonzi reMiorivhi. <b>38</b> Vanhu vese ndokuuya kwaari mangwanani-ngwanani mutembere kuti vamunzwe. 
<b>Luke 22</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino mabiko ezvingwa zvisina mbiriso akaswedera, anonzi pasika. <b>2</b> Vapristi vakuru nevanyori ndokutsvaka kuti vangamuuraya sei; nokuti vaitya vanhu. Judhasi Isikariyoti anotsvaka kutengesa Jesu <b>3</b> Zvino wakapinda Satani kuna Judhasi anotumidzwa Isikariyoti, waiverengwa pavanegumi nevaviri. <b>4</b> Akaenda, akanotaura nevapristi vakuru nevatungamiriri, kuti angamutengesa sei kwavari. <b>5</b> Zvino vakafara, vakatenderana kumupa mari. 
<b>6</b> Zvino wakatenda, akatsvaka mukana wakafanira wekumutengesa kwavari pasina chaunga. Pasika nechirairo chaIshe <b>7</b> Zvino zuva rezvingwa zvisina mbiriso rakasvika, paifanira kubaiwa pasika. <b>8</b> Zvino wakatuma Petro naJohwani, achiti: Endai munotigadzirira pasika, kuti tidye. <b>9</b> Zvino vakati kwaari: Ndekupi kwamunoda kuti tigadzirire? <b>10</b> Zvino wakati kwavari: Tarirai, kana mapinda muguta, munhu achasangana nemwi akatakura chirongo chemvura; mumutevere mumba maanopinda. 
<b>11</b> Uye muti kumwene weimba: Mudzidzisi anoti kwauri: Imba yevaeni iripi, mandingadyira pasika nevadzidzi vangu? <b>12</b> Uye iye achakuratidzai imba huru yekumusoro yakarongedzwa; gadzirirai ipapo. <b>13</b> Zvino vakaenda vakanowana sezvaakange avaudza; ndokugadzirira pasika. <b>14</b> Zvino awa rakati rasvika, akagara pakudya, nevaapositori gumi nevaviri vanaye. <b>15</b> Ndokuti kwavari: Nechishuwo ndakashuva kudya pasika iyi nemwi ndisati ndatambudzika; 
<b>16</b> nokuti ndinoti kwamuri: Handichazodyizve pairi, kusvikira yazadziswa muushe hwaMwari. <b>17</b> Zvino wakatora mukombe, avonga akati: Torai ichi, mugogovana. <b>18</b> Nokuti ndinoti kwamuri: Handichatongonwi zvechibereko chemuzambiringa, kusvikira ushe hwaMwari hwasvika. <b>19</b> Zvino wakatora chingwa, akavonga, akamedura, ndokuvapa, achiti: Uyu muviri wangu, unopiwa pachinzvimbo chenyu; izvi itai kundirangarira. <b>20</b> Saizvozvowo mukombe shure kwekurayira, achiti: Mukombe uyu isungano itsva muropa rangu, rinoteurirwa imwi.
