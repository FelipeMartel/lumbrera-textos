<b>Mateo 1</b><br><b>1</b> Bhuku redzinza raJesu Kristu, Mwanakomana waDhavhidhi, mwanakomana waAbhurahama. <b>2</b> Abhurahama wakabereka Isaka; Isaka ndokubereka Jakobho; Jakobho ndokubereka Judha nevanakomana vababa vake; <b>3</b> Judha ndokubereka Faresi naZara kuna Tamari; Faresi ndokubereka Esiromi; Esiromi ndokubereka Arami; <b>4</b> Arami ndokubereka Aminadhabhi; Aminadhabhi ndokubereka Naashoni; Naashoni ndokubereka Sarimoni; <b>5</b> Sarimoni ndokubereka Bhowazi kuna Rahabhi; Bhowazi ndokubereka Obhedhi kuna Rute; Obhedhi ndokubereka Jese; 
<b>6</b> Jese ndokubereka Dhavhidhi mambo; Dhavhidhi mambo ndokubereka Soromoni kumukadziwaiva waUria; <b>7</b> Soromoni ndokubereka Robhoami; Robhoami ndokubereka Abhiya; Abhiya ndokubereka Asa; <b>8</b> Asa ndokubereka Josafati; Josafati ndokubereka Joramu; Joramu ndokubereka Oziasi; <b>9</b> Oziasi ndokubereka Jotamu; Jotamu ndokubereka Akazi; Akazi ndokubereka Hezekia; <b>10</b> Hezekia ndokubereka Manase; Manase ndokubereka Amoni; Amoni ndokubereka Josiyasi; 
<b>11</b> Josiyasi ndokubereka Jekoniasi nevanakomana vamai vake nenguva yekutapirwa kuBhabhironi. <b>12</b> Zvino shure kwekutapirwa kuBhabhironi, Jekoniasi wakabereka Saratieri; Saratieri ndokubereka Zerubhabheri; <b>13</b> Zerubhabheri ndokubereka Abhiudhi; Abhiudhi ndokubereka Eriakimi; Eriakimi ndokubereka Azori; <b>14</b> Azori ndokubereka Sadhoki; Sadhoki ndokubereka Akimi; Akimi ndokubereka Eriyudhi; <b>15</b> Eriyudhi ndokubereka Eriazari; Eriazari ndokubereka Matani; Matani ndokubereka Jakobho; 
<b>16</b> Jakobho ndokubereka Josefa murume waMaria, kwakazvarwa kwaari Jesu, anonzi Kristu. <b>17</b> Naizvozvo mazera ese kubva kuna Abhurahama kusvikira kuna Dhavhidhi mazera gumi nemana; nekubva kuna Dhavhidhi kusvikira pakutapirwa kuBhabhironi mazera gumi nemana; nekubva pakutapirwa kuBhabhironi kusvikira kuna Kristu mazera gumi nemana. Kuberekwa kwaJesu Kristu <b>18</b> Zvino kuberekwa kwaJesu Kristu kwaiva kwakadai: Nokuti mai vake Maria vakati vatsidzirwa kuroorwa naJosefa, vasati vasangana vakaonekwa vava nemimba kubva kuMweya Mutsvene. 
<b>19</b> Zvino Josefa murume wavo zvaakange ari wakarurama, asingadi kuvafumura, waida kuvaramba muchivande. <b>20</b> Asi zvino wakati acharangarira zvinhu izvozvi, tarira, mutumwa waIshe wakaonekwa kwaari muchiroto achiti: Josefa, mwanakomana waDhavhidhi, usatya kuzvitorera Maria mukadzi wako; nokuti icho chakaumbwa maari ndecheMweya Mutsvene. <b>21</b> Uye achabereka Mwanakomana, uye uchatumidza zita rake Jesu, nokuti iye achaponesa vanhu vake pazvivi zvavo. 
<b>22</b> Zvino zvese izvozvi zvakaitwa kuti zvizadziswe zvakarehwa naIshe kubudikidza nemuporofita achiti: <b>23</b> Tarira, mhandara ichava nemimba, igobereka Mwanakomana, uye vachatumidza zita rake Emanueri, ndokuti kana zvichishandurwa, Mwari anesu. <b>24</b> Zvino Josefa amuka pahope akaita sezvakaraira mutumwa waIshe kwaari, akazvitorera mukadzi wake; <b>25</b> uye haana kumuziva kusvikira azvara Mwanakomana wake wedangwe; akatumidza zita rake JESU. 
<b>Mateo 2</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino Jesu wakati aberekwa muBhetirehemu reJudhiya, pamazuva aHerodhe mambo, tarira, kwakauya vachenjeri vachibva kumabvazuva kuJerusarema, <b>2</b> vachiti: Uripi iye wakazvarwa Mambo weVaJudha? Nokuti taona nyeredzi yake kumabvazuva, tauya kuzomunamata. <b>3</b> Zvino Herodhe mambo wakati anzwa, akashushikana, neJerusarema rese naye. <b>4</b> Zvino wakati aunganidza vapristi vakuru vese nevanyori vevanhu, akabvunza kwavari kuti Kristu uchazvarirwa kupi. 
<b>5</b> Zvino vakati kwaari: MuBhetirehemu reJudhiya; nokuti zvakanyorwa saizvozvi nemuporofita zvichinzi: <b>6</b> Newe Bhetirehemu nyika yaJudha, hausi mudikisa kuvatongi vaJudha; nokuti kwauri kuchabuda mutungamiriri, achafudza vanhu vangu Israeri. <b>7</b> Zvino Herodhe, wakati adana vachenjeri muchivande, akabvunzisisa kwavari nguva yakaonekwa nyeredzi. <b>8</b> Akavatuma kuBhetirehemu, akati: Endai munobvunzisisa zvemucheche; kana mamuwana mundisume, kuti ini ndiuyewo ndimunamate. 
<b>9</b> Vakati vanzwa mambo, vakabva; zvino tarira, nyeredzi yavakaona kumabvazuva yakavatungamirira, kusvikira yasvika ikamira pamusoro pepakange pane mucheche. <b>10</b> Vakati vaona nyeredzi vakafara nemufaro mukuru kwazvo. <b>11</b> Vakati vapinda mumba, vakaona mucheche naMaria mai vake, vakawira pasi vakamunamata; zvino vakati vazarura fuma yavo, vakamupemberera nezvipo, goridhe, nezvipfungaidzo zvinonhuhwira, nemura*. 
<b>12</b> Vakati vanyeverwa naMwari muchiroto, kuti varege kudzokera kuna Herodhe, vakabva vakaenda kunyika yavo neimwe nzira. Kutizira kuEgipita <b>13</b> Vakati vaenda, tarira, mutumwa waIshe anoonekwa kuna Josefa muchiroto, achiti: Muka utore mucheche namai vake, utizire kuEgipita, uveko kusvikira ndichikuudza; nokuti Herodhe achatsvaka mucheche kuti amuparadze. <b>14</b> Zvino amuka wakatora mucheche namai vake usiku, akabva akaenda kuEgipita, 
<b>15</b> uye wakavako kusvikira pakufa kwaHerodhe; kuti zvizadziswe zvakarehwa naIshe nemuporofita, achiti: Ndakadana mwanakomana wangu abude muEgipita. Kuurawa kwevana naHerodhe <b>16</b> Zvino Herodhe, wakati aona kuti anyengerwa nevachenjeri, akatsamwa zvikuru, akatuma, akauraya vana vese vaiva muBhetirehemu nemumigumo yaro yese, kubvira pamakore maviri neari pasi, zvichienderana nenguva yaakange abvunzisisa kuvachenjeri. <b>17</b> Zvino zvakazadziswa zvakarehwa naJeremiya muporofita achiti: 
<b>18</b> MuRama rakanzwika inzwi, kuungudza nekuchema nekurira kukuru, Rakeri achichema vana vake asingadi kunyaradzwa, nokuti havachipo. Kudzokera kunyika yaIsraeri <b>19</b> Asi Herodhe wakati afa, tarira, mutumwa waIshe wakaonekwa muchiroto kuna Josefa muEgipita, <b>20</b> achiti: Muka, utore mucheche namai vake, uende kunyika yaIsraeri; nokuti vakafa avo vaitsvaka mweya wemucheche. <b>21</b> Akasimuka ndokutora mucheche namai vake akasvika kunyika yaIsraeri. 
<b>22</b> Asi wakati achinzwa kuti Arikerawo anotonga muJudhiya panzvimbo yababa vake Herodhe, akatya kuendako; asi wakati anyeverwa naMwari muchiroto, akabva akaenda kumativi eGarirea. <b>23</b> Akasvika akagara muguta rinonzi Nazareta; kuti zvizadziswe zvakarehwa nevaporofita, kuti: Achanzi muNazareta. 
<b>Mateo 3</b><br><b>1</b> Pamazuva iwayo kwakauya Johwani Mubhabhatidzi, achiparidza murenje reJudhiya, <b>2</b> uye achiti: Tendeukai; nokuti ushe hwematenga hwaswedera. <b>3</b> Nokuti uyu ndiye wakarehwa nezvake naIsaya muporofita, achiti: Inzwi reanodanidzira murenje: Gadzirirai mugwagwa waIshe, ruramisai nzira dzake. <b>4</b> Uyuwo Johwani waiva nechipfeko chake chemakushe ekamera nebhanhire rechikumba rakapoteredza chiuno chake; nekudya kwake kwaiva mhashu neuchi hwedondo. 
<b>5</b> Zvino kwakabudira kwaari Jerusarema neJudhiya rese nedunhu rese rakapoteredza Joridhani; <b>6</b> ndokubhabhatidzwa naye muna Joridhani vachireurura zvivi zvavo. <b>7</b> Asi wakati achiona vazhinji veVaFarisi neVaSadhusi vachiuya kurubhabhatidzo rwake akati kwavari: Zvizvarwa zvenyoka, ndiani wakakuratidzirai kuti mutize hasha dzinouya? <b>8</b> Naizvozvo berekai zvibereko zvakafanira kutendeuka. 
<b>9</b> Uye musafunga kutaura mukati menyu muchiti: Tina Abhurahama anova baba; nokuti ndinoti kwamuri: Mwari anokwanisa kubva pamabwe aya kumutsira Abhurahama vana. <b>10</b> Uye ikozvinowo sanhu ratoiswa pamudzi wemiti, naizvozvo muti umwe neumwe usingaiti chibereko chakanaka unotemwa ugokandirwa mumoto. <b>11</b> Ini zvirokwazvo ndinokubhabhatidzai nemvura mupinde mukutendeuka; asi uyo anouya shure kwangu ane simba kupfuura ini, wandisina kufanira kutakura shangu dzake; iye achakubhabhatidzai neMweya Mutsvene nemoto. 
<b>12</b> Ane rusero rwuri muruoko rwake, uye achachenesa buriro rake kwazvo, ndokuunganidzira zviyo zvake mudura, asi hundi achaipisa nemoto usingadzimiki. Kubhabhatidzwa kwaJesu <b>13</b> Ipapo Jesu wakabva Garirea akasvika paJoridhani kuna Johwani, kuti abhabhatidzwe naye. <b>14</b> Asi Johwani wakamurambidza achiti: Ini kunodikanwa kuti ndibhabhatidzwe nemwi, asi imwi mouya kwandiri? <b>15</b> Asi Jesu achipindura wakati kwaari: Tenda ikozvino, nokuti saizvozvo zvakatifanira kuti tizadzise kururama kwese. Ipapo akamutendera. 
<b>16</b> Asi Jesu, wakati abhabhatidzwa, akakwira pakarepo achibva mumvura; zvino tarira, matenga akamuzarukira, ndokuona Mweya waMwari achiburuka senjiva, achiuya pamusoro pake. <b>17</b> Zvino tarira, inzwi richibva kumatenga richiti: Uyu ndiye Mwanakomana wangu anodikanwa, wandinofara maari kwazvo. 
<b>Mateo 4</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino Jesu wakatungamirirwa neMweya kurenje kuti anoidzwa nadhiabhorosi. <b>2</b> Zvino wakati atsanya mazuva makumi mana neusiku makumi mana, akazonzwa nzara. <b>3</b> Zvino muidzi wakati auya kwaari, akati: Kana uri Mwanakomana waMwari, raira kuti mabwe awa aite zvingwa. <b>4</b> Asi iye wakapindura akati: Kwakanyorwa kuchinzi: Munhu haangararami nechingwa chega, asi neshoko rimwe nerimwe rinobuda mumuromo maMwari. <b>5</b> Zvino dhiabhorosi wakamutora akamuisa muguta dzvene, akamumisa pachiruvu chetembere, 
<b>6</b> ndokuti kwaari: Kana uri Mwanakomana waMwari, zvikandire pasi; nokuti kwakanyorwa kuti: Iye acharaira vatumwa vake pamusoro pako, uye pamaoko vachakugamha, zvimwe ungagumbusa rutsoka rwako pabwe. <b>7</b> Jesu akati kwaari: Kwakanyorwa zvekare: Usaidza Ishe Mwari wako. <b>8</b> Dhiabhorosi akamuisazve pagomo refusa, akamuratidza ushe hwese hwenyika nekubwinya kwahwo. <b>9</b> Akati kwaari: Zvese zvinhu izvi ndichapa kwauri kana ukawira pasi ukandinamata. 
<b>10</b> Ipapo Jesu akati kwaari: Ibva, Satani, nokuti kwakanyorwa kuchinzi: Uchanamata Ishe Mwari wako, uchashumira iye oga. <b>11</b> Ipapo dhiabhorosi akamusiya; zvino tarira, vatumwa vakauya vakamushandira. Jesu anotanga basa paGarirea <b>12</b> Zvino Jesu wakati anzwa kuti Johwani wakumikidzwa mutirongo, akabva akaenda Garirea. <b>13</b> Zvino wakabva Nazareta, akauya akagara muKapenaume riri pagungwa, mumiganhu yeZebhuruni neNafitarimi; <b>14</b> kuti zvizadziswe zvakarehwa naIsaya muporofita, achiti: 
<b>15</b> Nyika yeZebhuruni nenyika yeNefitarimi, panzira yegungwa, mhiri kwaJoridhani, Garirea revahedheni; <b>16</b> vanhu vakange vagere murima vakaona chiedza chikuru; nevakange vagere panharaunda nemumumvuri werufu, chiedza chakabudira kwavari. Jesu anotanga kuparidza <b>17</b> Kubvira panguva iyo Jesu wakatanga kuparidza nokuti: Tendeukai, nokuti ushe hwekumatenga hwaswedera. Kudanwa kwevadzidzi vekutanga 
<b>18</b> Zvino Jesu wakati achifamba-famba parutivi rwegungwa reGarirea akaona vanakomana vemunhu umwe vaviri, Simoni anonzi Petro, naAndiriya munin'ina wake, vachikandira mumbure mugungwa; nokuti vakange vari varedzi. <b>19</b> Akati kwavari: Nditeverei, ndigokuitai varedzi vevanhu. <b>20</b> Pakarepo vakasiya mimbure vakamutevera. <b>21</b> Zvino wakati apfuurira kubva ipapo akaona vamwe vaviri vanakomana vemunhu umwe, Jakobho waZebhedhi naJohwani munin'ina wake muchikepe pamwe naZebhedhi baba vavo, vachigadzira mimbure yavo, akavadana. 
<b>22</b> Pakarepo vakasiya chikepe nababa vavo vakamutevera. Jesu anoshanda munyika yeGarirea <b>23</b> Jesu akapota neGarirea rese achidzidzisa mumasinagoge avo, nekuparidza evhangeri yeushe, achiporesa denda rega-rega neurwere hwega-hwega pavanhu. <b>24</b> Shoko rake rikabudira kuSiriya yese; vakauisa kwaari varwere vese, vakabatwa nehosha dzakasiyana-siyana, nemarwadzo, nevakange vane madhimoni, nevaiva nezvipusha, nevakange vakafa mitezo; akavaporesa. 
<b>25</b> Zvino zvaunga zvikuru zvakamutevera, vaibva Garirea, neDhekapori, neJerusarema, neJudhiya, nemhiri kwaJoridhani. 
<b>Mateo 5</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakati achiona zvaunga akakwira mugomo, zvino wakati agara pasi vadzidzi vake vakauya kwaari; <b>2</b> akashamisa muromo wake, akavadzidzisa achiti: <b>3</b> Vakaropafadzwa varombo pamweya; nokuti ushe hwekumatenga ndehwavo. <b>4</b> Vakaropafadzwa vanochema; nokuti ivo vachanyaradzwa. <b>5</b> Vakaropafadzwa vanyoro; nokuti ivo vachagara nhaka yenyika. <b>6</b> Vakaropafadzwa vane nzara nenyota yekururama; nokuti ivo vachagutiswa. <b>7</b> Vakaropafadzwa vane tsitsi; nokuti ivo vachaitirwa tsitsi. 
<b>8</b> Vakaropafadzwa vakachena pamoyo; nokuti ivo vachaona Mwari. <b>9</b> Vakaropafadzwa vanoyananisa; nokuti ivo vachanzi vana vaMwari. <b>10</b> Vakaropafadzwa vanoshushwa nekuda kwekururama; nokuti ushe hwekumatenga ndehwavo. <b>11</b> Makaropafadzwa imwi, kana vachikutukai, vachikushushai, vachikutaurirai zvakaipa zvese vachireva nhema nekuda kwangu. <b>12</b> Farai mupembere kwazvo, nokuti mubairo wenyu mukuru mumatenga; nokuti vakashusha saizvozvo vaporofita vakakutangirai. Munyu nechiedza chenyika 
<b>13</b> Imwi muri munyu wenyika; asi kana munyu warasa kuvava, ucharungwa nei? Hauchabatsiri chinhu, asi kurasirwa kunze, utsikwe-tsikwe nevanhu. <b>14</b> Imwi muri chiedza chenyika; guta rakamiswa pamusoro pegomo haringavanziki; <b>15</b> uye mwenje havautungidzi vakauisa pasi pedengu, asi pachigadziko chemwenje, uvhenekere vese vari mumba. <b>16</b> Saizvozvo chiedza chenyu ngachivhenekere pamberi pevanhu, kuti vaone mabasa enyu akanaka, vagokudza Baba venyu vari kumatenga. Murairo wakare nemurairo waJesu Kristu 
<b>17</b> Musafunga kuti ndauya kuzoparadza murairo kana vaporofita; handina kuuya kuzoparadza, asi kuzozadzisa. <b>18</b> Nokuti zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Kusvikira denga nenyika zvichipfuura, vara diki rimwe kana chidodzi chimwe kubva pamurairo hazvingatongopfuuri, kusvikira zvese zvaitika. <b>19</b> Naizvozvo ani nani anotyora umwe wemirairo midikisa iyi, akadzidzisa vanhu saizvozvo, achanzi mudikisa muushe hwekumatenga; asi ani nani anoita akaidzidzisa, iye achanzi mukuru muushe hwekumatenga. 
<b>20</b> Nokuti ndinoti kwamuri: Kunze kwekuti kururama kwenyu kukapfuura kwevanyori nekweVaFarisi, hamungatongopindi muushe hwekumatenga. <b>21</b> Makanzwa kuti zvakanzi kune vekare: Usauraya; uye ani nani anouraya achava nemhosva yemutongo; <b>22</b> asi ini ndinoti kwamuri: Ani nani anotsamwira umwe wake pasina chikonzero, achava nemhosva yemutongo; neanoti kune umwe wake: Dununu! achava nemhosva yedare remakurukota; uye anoti: Benzi! achava nemhosva yegehena remoto. 
<b>23</b> Naizvozvo kana uchiuisa chipo chako kuaritari, ipapo ukarangarira kuti umwe wako ane chekupikisana newe, <b>24</b> siya ipapo chipo chako pamberi pearitari, uende umbonotanga wayanana neumwe wako, ugouya wopira chipo chako. <b>25</b> Tenderana nemudzivisi wako nekukurumidza, uchiri munzira naye, zvimwe mudzivisi angakukumikidza kumutongi, mutongi akukumikidze kumupurisa, ukakandirwa mutirongo. <b>26</b> Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwauri: Haungatongobudimo, kusvikira waripa kamari kekupedzisira. 
<b>27</b> Makanzwa kuti zvakanzi kune vekare: Usaita upombwe; <b>28</b> asi ini ndinoti kwamuri: Wese anotarisa mukadzi kuti amuchive, watoita upombwe naye mumoyo make. <b>29</b> Uye kana ziso rako rerudyi richikugumbusa, uridzure urase kubva kwauri; nokuti zviri nani kwauri kuti umwe wemitezo yako uparare, kwete kuti muviri wako wese ukandirwe mugehena. <b>30</b> Zvino kana ruoko rwako rwerudyi ruchikugumbusa, urugure urasire kure newe; nokuti zviri nani kwauri kuti umwe wemitezo yako uparare, kwete kuti muviri wako wese ukandirwe mugehena. 
<b>31</b> Zvakanziwo: Ani nani anoramba mukadzi wake, ngaamupe rugwaro rwekurambana; <b>32</b> asi ini ndinoti kwamuri: Ani nani anoramba mukadzi wake, kunze kwemhosva yeupombwe, anomuitisa upombwe; uye ani nani anowana wakarambwa anoita upombwe. <b>33</b> Zvekare makanzwa kuti zvakanzi kune vekare: Usapika nhema, asi uripire Ishe mhiko dzako; <b>34</b> asi ini ndinoti kwamuri: Musatongopika; kana nedenga, nokuti chigaro chaMwari cheushe; <b>35</b> kana nenyika, nokuti chitsiko chetsoka dzake; kana neJerusarema, nokuti iguta raMambo mukuru; 
<b>36</b> uye usapika nemusoro wako, nokuti haugoni kuita ruvhudzi rumwe ruchena kana rutema. <b>37</b> Asi shoko renyu ngarive: Hongu, hongu; kwete, kwete; chinopfuura izvi chinobva kune wakaipa. Kuda vavengi <b>38</b> Makanzwa kuti zvakanzi: Ziso neziso, uye zino nezino; <b>39</b> asi ini ndinoti kwamuri: Musapikisa wakaipa; asi ani nani anokurova padama rako rerudyi, umutendeusire rimwewo. <b>40</b> Neanoda kukusungisa, nekutora shati yako, umutendere nguvo yakowo. <b>41</b> Ani nani anokumanikidza kuenda maira imwe uende naye mbiri. 
<b>42</b> Ipa anokumbira kwauri, usafuratira anoda kukwereta kwauri. <b>43</b> Makanzwa kuti zvakanzi: Ude umwe wako, uvenge muvengi wako. <b>44</b> Asi ini ndinoti kwamuri: Idai vavengi venyu, ropafadzai vanokutukai, batai zvakanaka vanokuvengai, munyengeterere vanokubatai zvakaipa vachikushushai. <b>45</b> Kuti muve vana vaBaba venyu vari kumatenga, nokuti vanobudisira zuva ravo kune vakaipa nevakanaka, nemvura vanoinaisira vakarurama nevasakarurama. <b>46</b> Nokuti kana muchida vanokudai, mune mubairo wei? Ko, vateresiwo havaiti izvozvo here? 
<b>47</b> Uye kana muchikwazisa vamwe venyu chete, munoita zvikuru kupfuura vamwe nei? Ko, vateresiwo havaiti saizvozvo here? <b>48</b> Naizvozvo imwi muve vakakwana, saBaba venyu vari kumatenga vakakwana. 
<b>Mateo 6</b><br><b>1</b> Chenjerai kuti basa renyu rerudo musariita pamberi pevanhu kuti muonekwe navo; kana zvisakadaro hamuna mubairo kuna Baba venyu vari kumatenga. <b>2</b> Naizvozvo kana uchiita basa rerudo, rega kuridza hwamanda pamberi pako sezvinoita vanyepedzeri mumasinagoge nemunzira dzemumaguta kuti vakudzwe nevanhu. Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Vagamuchira mubairo wavo. <b>3</b> Asi iwe kana uchiita basa rerudo, ruboshwe rwako ngarurege kuziva zvinoita rudyi rwako. 
<b>4</b> Kuti kuita kwako basa rerudo kuve pakavanda; uye Baba vako vanoona pakavanda, ivo vachakuripira pachena. Kunyengetera <b>5</b> Zvino kana uchinyengetera, usava sevanyepedzeri; nokuti vanoda kunyengetera vamire mumasinagoge nepamharadzano dzenzira dzemumaguta kuti vaonekwe nevanhu. Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Vagamuchira mubairo wavo. <b>6</b> Asi iwe kana uchinyengetera, pinda muimba yako yemukati, ugovhara mukova wako, unyengetere kuna Baba vako vari pakavanda; zvino Baba vako vanoona pakavanda vachakuripira pachena. 
<b>7</b> Asi kana muchinyengetera, musapamhidzira-pamhidzira zvisina maturo sevahedheni; nokuti vanofunga kuti vachanzwikwa nekutaura kwavo kuzhinji. <b>8</b> Naizvozvo musafanana navo; nokuti Baba venyu vanoziva chamunoshaiwa musati mavakumbira. Jesu anodzidzisa kunyengetera <b>9</b> Naizvozvo imwi nyengeterai seizvi: Baba vedu vari kumatenga, zita renyu ngariitwe dzvene; <b>10</b> ushe hwenyu ngahuuye; chido chenyu ngachiitwe panyikawo sezvachinoitwa kudenga; <b>11</b> mutipei nhasi kudya kwedu kwemisi yese; 
<b>12</b> uye mutikanganwire mangava edu, isu sezvatinokanganwirawo vane ngava nesu; <b>13</b> uye musatipinza pakuedzwa, asi mutisunungure pakuipa. Nokuti ushe ndehwenyu nesimba nekubwinya kusvikira rinhi narinhi. Ameni. <b>14</b> Nokuti kana muchikanganwira vanhu kudarika kwavo, Baba venyu vari kudenga vachakukanganwiraiwo. <b>15</b> Asi kana musingakanganwiri vanhu kudarika kwavo, Baba venyu havangakanganwiriwo kudarika kwenyu. Kutsanya 
<b>16</b> Uyezve kana muchitsanya, musava nezviso zvinopunyaira sevanyepedzeri; nokuti vanounyanisa zviso zvavo, kuti vaonekwe nevanhu kuti vanotsanya. Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Vagamuchira mubairo wavo. <b>17</b> Asi iwe kana uchitsanya, zodza musoro wako, ugeze chiso chako, <b>18</b> kuti urege kuonekwa kuvanhu kuti unotsanya, asi kuna Baba vako vari pakavanda; zvino Baba vako vanoona pakavanda vachakuripira pachena. Fuma yenyika neyekudenga 
<b>19</b> Regai kuzviunganidzira fuma panyika, pane chifusi nengura zvinoodza, nepane mbavha dzinopaza dzichiba; <b>20</b> asi muzviunganidzire fuma kudenga, uko kusina chifusi kana ngura zvinoodza, naapo mbavha padzisingapazi kana kuba. <b>21</b> Nokuti pane fuma yenyu, ndipo pachavawo nemoyo yenyu. <b>22</b> Mwenje wemuviri iziso; naizvozvo kana ziso rako riri benyu, muviri wako wese uchazara nechiedza. <b>23</b> Asi kana ziso rako rakaipa, muviri wako wese uchazara nerima. Naizvozvo kana chiedza chiri mauri chiri rima, rima iro iguru sei! 
<b>24</b> Hapana angashumira vatenzi vaviri; nokuti achavenga umwe, akada umwe; kana achanamatira umwe, akazvidza umwe. Hamungashumiri Mwari naMamona*. Kutambudzika nekuvimba naMwari <b>25</b> Naizvozvo ndinoti kwamuri: Musafunganya pamusoro peupenyu hwenyu, zvamuchadya kana zvamuchanwa, kana pamusoro pemuviri wenyu, zvamuchapfeka. Upenyu hahupfuuri kudya here, nemuviri chipfeko? <b>26</b> Tarirai shiri dzedenga, nokuti hadzidzvari kana kukohwa kana kuunganidza mumatura, asi Baba venyu vekudenga vanodzirurira. Imwi hamudzipfuuri zvikuru here? 
<b>27</b> Ndiani kwamuri nekufunganya angawedzera mbimbi imwe paurefu hwake? <b>28</b> Nepamusoro pezvipfeko munofunganyirei? Nyatsorangarirai midovatova yenyika, inokura sei; haishingairi, hairuki; <b>29</b> asi ndinoti kwamuri: Kunyange Soromoni pakubwinya kwake kwese haana kupfekedzwa seimwe yeiyi. <b>30</b> Zvino kana Mwari achifukidza saizvozvo uswa hwenyika, huripo nhasi, hunokandirwa muchoto mangwana, haangakufukidziyi nekupfuurisa here imwi verutendo rudiki? <b>31</b> Naizvozvo musafunganya, muchiti: Tichadyei? Kana: Tichanwei? Kana: Tichafukei? 
<b>32</b> Nokuti izvozvi zvese vahedheni vanozvitsvaka, nokuti Baba venyu vekudenga vanoziva kuti munoshaiwa izvozvi zvese. <b>33</b> Asi tangai kutsvaka ushe hwaMwari, nekururama kwake, zvino izvozvi zvese zvichawedzerwa kwamuri. <b>34</b> Naizvozvo musafunganyira mangwana, nokuti mangwana achazvifunganyira zvinhu zvawo. Zuva rakakwanirwa nezvakaipa zvaro. 
<b>Mateo 7</b><br><b>1</b> Musatonga, kuti murege kutongwa, <b>2</b> nokuti muchatongwa nekutonga kwamunotonga nako; uye muchayerwa zvekare nechiyero chamunoyera nacho. <b>3</b> Unotarisirei rubanzu rwuri muziso reumwe wako, asi usingananzereri danda riri muziso rako? <b>4</b> Kana ungataura sei kune umwe wako uchiti: Rega ndivhomore rubanzu muziso rako, asi tarira, danda riri muziso rako? <b>5</b> Munyepedzeri, tanga kuvhomora danda muziso rako, ugoona kwazvo kuvhomora rubanzu muziso reumwe wako. 
<b>6</b> Musapa chitsvene kuimbwa, kana kukandira maparera* enyu pamberi penguruve, zvimwe dzingaatsika netsoka dzadzo, dzikatendeuka dzikakubvamburai. Kutsunga pakunyengetera <b>7</b> Kumbirai, uye muchapiwa; tsvakai, uye muchawana; gogodzai, uye muchazarurirwa. <b>8</b> Nokuti umwe neumwe anokumbira anogamuchira, neanotsvaka anowana, neanogogodza anozarurirwa. <b>9</b> Kana ndeupi munhu kwamuri, iye kana mwanakomana wake akakumbira chingwa, achamupa ibwe? <b>10</b> Kana kuti akakumbira hove, achamupa nyoka? 
<b>11</b> Zvino kana imwi makaipa muchiziva kupa vana venyu zvipo zvakanaka, Baba venyu vari kumatenga vachapfuurisa sei kupa zvinhu zvakanaka ivo vanovakumbira? <b>12</b> Naizvozvo zvinhu zvese zvamunoda kuti vanhu vakuitirei, imwi muvaitirewo saizvozvo; nokuti uyu ndiwo murairo nevaporofita. Nzira mbiri <b>13</b> Pindai nesuwo rakamanikana; nokuti suwo rakafara nenzira yakapamhamha inoenda kukuparadzwa, uye vazhinji varipo vanopinda naro. <b>14</b> Nokuti suwo rakamanikana nenzira nhete inoenda kuupenyu, uye vashoma vanoiwana. Vadzidzisi venhema 
<b>15</b> Asi chenjerai vaporofita venhema, vanouya kwamuri nezvipfeko zvemakwai, asi mukati mapumhi anoparadza. <b>16</b> Muchavaziva nezvibereko zvavo; vanhu vanotanha mazambiringa paminzwa here, kana maonde parubayambeva? <b>17</b> Saizvozvo muti umwe neumwe wakanaka unobereka zvibereko zvakanaka, asi muti wakaipa unobereka zvibereko zvakaipa. <b>18</b> Muti wakanaka haugoni kubereka zvibereko zvakaipa, nemuti wakaipa haubereki zvibereko zvakanaka. <b>19</b> Muti umwe neumwe usingaiti chibereko chakanaka unotemwa wokandirwa mumoto. 
<b>20</b> Naizvozvo kubva pazvibereko zvavo muchavaziva. Kunzwa nekuita <b>21</b> Haasi wese anoti kwandiri: Ishe, Ishe, achapinda muushe hwekumatenga; asi anoita kuda kwaBaba vangu vari kumatenga. <b>22</b> Vazhinji vachati kwandiri pazuva iro: Ishe, Ishe, hatina kuporofita muzita renyu here, tikabudisa madhimoni muzita renyu, tikaita mabasa esimba mazhinji muzita renyu? <b>23</b> Zvino ipapo ndichareva kwavari pachena ndichiti: Handina kukuzivai chero nguva; ibvai kwandiri imwi vaiti vezvakaipa. Mufananidzo wedzimba mbiri 
<b>24</b> Naizvozvo umwe neumwe anonzwa mashoko angu awa uye akaaita, ndichamufananidza nemurume wakachenjera, wakavaka imba yake paruware. <b>25</b> Zvino mvura ikaturuka, mafashame akauya, mitutu ikavhuvhuta, ikarova paimba iyo, ikasawa; nokuti yakange yakateyiwa paruware. <b>26</b> Zvino umwe neumwe anonzwa mashoko angu awa, akasaaita, uchafananidzwa nemurume benzi, wakavaka imba yake pajecha; <b>27</b> zvino mvura ikaturuka, mafashame ndokuuya, mitutu ndokuvhuvhuta, ndokurova paimba iyo, ndokuwa; uye kuwa kwayo kukava kukuru. 
<b>28</b> Zvino zvakaitika kuti Jesu apedza mashoko iwawa, zvaunga zvakashamiswa nedzidziso yake. <b>29</b> Nokuti wakavadzidzisa seune simba, kwete sevanyori. 
<b>Mateo 8</b><br><b>1</b> Wakati aburuka mugomo, zvaunga zvikuru zvakamutevera. <b>2</b> Zvino tarira, kwakauya ane maperembudzi, akamunamata achiti: Ishe, kana muchida, munogona kundinatsa. <b>3</b> Jesu akatandavadza ruoko, akamubata, achiti: Ndinoda, natswa. Pakarepo maperembudzi ake akanatswa. <b>4</b> Zvino Jesu akati kwaari: Ona kuti usaudza munhu, asi enda unozviratidza kumupristi, ubaire chipo Mozisi chaakaraira, chive uchapupu kwavari. Jesu anoporesa muranda wemukuru wezana 
<b>5</b> Zvino Jesu wakati apinda muKapenaume, kwakauya kwaari mukuru wezana, achimukumbirisa, <b>6</b> achiti: Ishe, muranda wangu arere kumba wakafa mitezo, anotambudzika kwazvo. <b>7</b> Zvino Jesu akati kwaari: Ini ndichauya ndimuporese. <b>8</b> Mukuru wezana akapindura akati: Ishe, handina kufanira kuti mupinde pasi pedenga rangu remba; asi taurai shoko chete, zvino muranda wangu achaporeswa. 
<b>9</b> Nokuti neniwo ndiri munhu ari pasi pesimba, ndine mauto pasi pangu; zvino ndinoti kune uyu: Enda; anoenda; nekune umwe: Uya; anouya; nekumuranda wangu: Ita ichi; anoita. <b>10</b> Jesu wakati achizvinzwa akashamisika, akati kune vaimutevera: Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Handina kuwana rutendo rukuru rwakadai kunyange pakati paIsraeri. <b>11</b> Zvino ndinoti kwamuri: Vazhinji vachauya vachibva kumabvazuva nemavirira, vachagara pakudya naAbhurahama, naIsaka, naJakobho, muushe hwekumatenga; 
<b>12</b> asi vana veushe vachakandirwa kunze kurima rekunze kwekupedzisira; ikoko kuchava nekuchema nekugeda-geda kwemeno. <b>13</b> Zvino Jesu akati kumukuru wezana: Enda, zviitirwe kwauri sezvawakatenda. Muranda wake akapora nenguva iyoyo. Jesu anoporesa mai vemukadzi waPetro <b>14</b> Jesu wakati apinda mumba maPetro, akaona mai vemukadzi wake vakaradzikwa vane fivhiri. <b>15</b> Akabata ruoko rwavo, fivhiri ikabva kwavari; vakasimuka vakavashandira. 
<b>16</b> Zvino ava madekwani vakauisa kwaari vazhinji vaiva vakagarwa nemadhimoni; akabudisa mweya neshoko, akaporesa vese vairwara; <b>17</b> kuti zvizadziswe zvakataurwa naIsaya muporofita achiti: Iye wakatora utera hwedu, akatakura matenda edu. Kutevera Jesu <b>18</b> Jesu wakati achiona zvaunga zvikuru zvakamukomba, akaraira kuti vabve vaende mhiri. <b>19</b> Umwe munyori ndokuswedera akati kwaari: Mudzidzisi, ndichakuteverai kwese kwamunoenda. 
<b>20</b> Zvino Jesu akati kwaari: Makava ane mwena, neshiri dzedenga matendere; asi Mwanakomana wemunhu haana paangatsamhidza musoro. <b>21</b> Umwe wevadzidzi vake akati kwaari: Ishe, ndiregei nditange ndaenda ndinoviga baba vangu. <b>22</b> Asi Jesu wakati kwaari: Nditevere, urege vakafa vavige vakafa vavo. Jesu anonyaradza dutu remhepo <b>23</b> Zvino wakati apinda muchikepe, vadzidzi vake vakamutevera. <b>24</b> Zvino tarira, dutu guru remhepo rakamuka mugungwa, zvekuti igwa rakange rofukidzwa nemafungu; asi iye wakange arere. 
<b>25</b> Zvino vadzidzi vake vakaswedera vakamumutsa vachiti: Ishe, tiponesei, toparara! <b>26</b> Zvino akati kwavari: Munotyirei, imwi verutendo rudiki? Ndokusimuka, akatsiura mitutu negungwa; kudzikama kukuru kukavapo. <b>27</b> Asi vanhu vakashamisika vachiti: Uyu munhu rudzii kuti kunyange mitutu negungwa zvinomuteerera? Jesu anobudisa madhimoni panyika yevaGerigesi 
<b>28</b> Wakati asvika kune rimwe divi kunyika yeVaGerigesi, vakamuchingamidza vaviri vaiva vakagarwa nemadhimoni, vachibuda kumarinda, vane hasha kwazvo-kwazvo, zvekuti kwakange kusina munhu waigona kupfuura nenzira iyo. <b>29</b> Zvino tarira, vakadanidzira vachiti: Tinei nemwi, Jesu, Mwanakomana waMwari? Mauya pano kuzotirwadzisa nguva isati yasvika here? <b>30</b> Zvino kure navo kwakange kune boka renguruve zhinji richifura. <b>31</b> Madhimoni akamugombetedza achiti: Kana muchitibudisa, titenderei kuti tiende tinopinda muboka renguruve. 
<b>32</b> Zvino akati kwaari: Endai. Zvino akati abuda, akapinda muboka renguruve; zvino tarira, boka rese renguruve rakapitirikira kumateru kugungwa, dzikafira mumvura. <b>33</b> Zvino vaidzifudza vakatiza, vakaenda kuguta, vakapira zvese uye zvevakange vane madhimoni. <b>34</b> Zvino tarira, guta rese rakabuda kunochingamidza Jesu; zvino vakati vamuona, vakagombetedza kuti abve pamiganhu yavo. 
<b>Mateo 9</b><br><b>1</b> Akabva apinda mugwa, akayambuka ndokusvika muguta rake pachake. <b>2</b> Zvino tarira, vakauisa kwaari wakafa mitezo, arere pahwanyanza; zvino Jesu achiona rutendo rwavo akati kune wakafa mitezo: Tsunga moyo, mwana, wakanganwirwa zvivi zvako. <b>3</b> Zvino tarira, vamwe vevanyori vakati mukati mavo: Uyu unonyomba. <b>4</b> Asi Jesu achiona mifungo yavo wakati: Imwi munofungirei zvakaipa mumoyo yenyu? <b>5</b> Nokuti ndechipi chakareruka, kuti: Wakanganwirwa zvivi; kana kuti: Simuka ufambe? 
<b>6</b> Asi kuti muzive kuti Mwanakomana wemunhu ane simba panyika rekukanganwira zvivi (wakabva ati kune wakange akafa mitezo): Simuka, tora hwanyanza hwako, uende kumba kwako. <b>7</b> Akasimuka, akaenda kumba kwake. <b>8</b> Zvaunga zvakati zvichizviona zvikashamisika, zvikarumbidza Mwari wakapa simba rakadai kuvanhu. Kudanwa kwaMatewu <b>9</b> Zvino Jesu wakati achipfuurapo akaona munhu anonzi Matewu agere pahofisi yemutero, akati kwaari: Nditevere! Ndokusimuka, akamutevera. 
<b>10</b> Uye zvakaitika agere pakudya mumba, zvino tarira, vateresi vazhinji nevatadzi vakauya vakagara pakudya naJesu nevadzidzi vake. <b>11</b> Zvino VaFarisi vakati vachizviona, vakati kuvadzidzi vake: Nemhaka yei Mudzidzisi wenyu achidya nevateresi nevatadzi? <b>12</b> Asi Jesu wakati anzwa, akati kwavari: Vatano havadi murapi asi vanorwara. <b>13</b> Asi endai mudzidze kuti zvinorevei kuti: Ndinoda tsitsi uye kwete chibairo; nokuti handina kuuya kuzodana vakarurama, asi vatadzi vauye mukutendeuka. Kutsanya 
<b>14</b> Ipapo kwakasvika kwaari vadzidzi vaJohwani, vachiti: Nemhaka yei isu neVaFarisi tichitsanya kazhinji, asi vadzidzi venyu vasingatsanyi? <b>15</b> Jesu ndokuti kwavari: Vana veimba yemuchato vanogona kuchema here apo muwani anavo? Asi mazuva achasvika pachabviswa muwani kwavari, ipapo vachatsanya. <b>16</b> Hakuna anoisa chigamba chemucheka usakaunyana panguvo sharu, nokuti icho chinonamirwa pairi chinobvarura panguvo, buri rikanyanya. 
<b>17</b> Uye havadiri waini itsva muhombodo tsaru; zvikasadaro hombodo dzinoputika, waini ikateuka, nehombodo dzikaparara; asi vanodira waini itsva muhombodo itsva, zvichengeteke zviri zviviri. Jesu anoporesa mukadzi waiva nekubuda ropa uye anomutsa mukunda wemutungamiriri kuvakafa <b>18</b> Wakati achitaura zvinhu izvozvi kwavari, tarira, kwakasvika umwe mutungamiriri akamunamata, akati: Mukunda wangu achangofa; asi uyai muise ruoko rwenyu pamusoro pake, uye achararama. <b>19</b> Jesu ndokusimuka akamutevera, nevadzidzi vake. 
<b>20</b> Zvino, tarira, mukadzi waiva nekubuda ropa makore gumi nemaviri, wakaswedera shure kwake, akabata mupendero wenguvo yake. <b>21</b> Nokuti wakati mukati make: Kana ndikangobata nguvo yake, ndichaporeswa. <b>22</b> Asi Jesu wakatendeuka akati amuona, akati: Mukunda, tsunga moyo; rutendo rwako rwakuponesa. Mukadzi ndokuponeswa kubva panguva iyo. <b>23</b> Jesu ndokusvika mumba memutungamiriri akaona varidzi venyere nechaunga chichiita mhere-mhere, 
<b>24</b> akati kwavari: Ibvai; nokuti musikana haana kufa, asi arere. Ndokumuseka vachimusvavaidza. <b>25</b> Asi chaunga chakati chabudiswa, akapinda, ndokubata ruoko rwake, musikana ndokumuka. <b>26</b> Mukurumbira uyu ukabudira kunyika iyo yese. Jesu anoporesa mapofu maviri nechimumumu <b>27</b> Zvino Jesu wakati opfuurapo, mapofu maviri akamutevera, achidanidzira achiti: Tinzwirei tsitsi, Mwanakomana waDhavhidhi! 
<b>28</b> Zvino wakati apinda mumba, mapofu akauya kwaari; Jesu akati kwaari: Munotenda kuti ndinogona kuita izvozvi here? Akati kwaari: Hongu, Ishe. <b>29</b> Ipapo wakabata meso avo achiti: Ngazviitike kwamuri maererano nerutendo rwenyu. <b>30</b> Zvino meso avo akasvinudzwa; Jesu akavarairisa achiti: Onai kuti hakuna anozviziva. <b>31</b> Asi vakati vabuda, vakaparidzira mukurumbira wake munyika iyo yese. <b>32</b> Zvino vakati vachibuda, tarira, vakauisa kwaari munhu chimumumu waiva akagarwa nedhimoni. 
<b>33</b> Zvino dhimoni rakati rabudiswa, chimumumu chikataura; zvaunga zvikashamisika zvikati: Hakuna kumboonekwa zvakadai muna Israeri. <b>34</b> Asi VaFarisi vakati: Anobudisa madhimoni nemukuru wemadhimoni. <b>35</b> Zvino Jesu wakapota nemaguta ese nemisha, achidzidzisa mumasinagoge avo, nekuparidza evhangeri yeushe, nekuporesa denda rese neurwere hwese pakati pevanhu. <b>36</b> Asi wakati achiona zvaunga, akavanzwira tsitsi, nokuti vakange vaneta, vakapararira semakwai asina mufudzi. 
<b>37</b> Ipapo akati kuvadzidzi vake: Zvirokwazvo goho iguru, asi vashandi vashoma; <b>38</b> naizvozvo kumbirisai kuna Ishe wegoho, kuti atumire vashandi pagoho rake. 
<b>Mateo 10</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino wakadanira kwaari vadzidzi vake gumi nevaviri, akavapa simba pamusoro pemweya yetsvina, kuti vaibudise nekuporesa denda rese neurwere hwese. <b>2</b> Zvino mazita evaapositori gumi nevaviri ndiwawa: Wekutanga, Simoni, anonzi Petro, naAndiriya munin'ina wake; Jakobho waZebhedhi, naJohwani munin'ina wake; <b>3</b> Firipi, naBhatoromeo; Tomasi, naMatewu muteresi; Jakobho waArifiyo, naRebheu wainziwo Tadhiyo; <b>4</b> Simoni muKenani, naJudhasi Isikariyoti iyewo wakamutengesa. 
<b>5</b> Ava gumi nevaviri Jesu akavatuma, akavaraira, achiti: Musaenda munzira yevahedheni, musapinda muguta reVaSamaria; <b>6</b> asi zvikuru muende kumakwai akarasika eimba yaIsraeri. <b>7</b> Zvino pakufamba, muparidze muchiti: Ushe hwekumatenga hwaswedera. <b>8</b> Poresai vanorwara, natsai vane maperembudzi, mutsai vakafa, budisai madhimoni. Makagamuchira pachena, ipai pachena. <b>9</b> Musawana goridhe, kana sirivheri, kana ndarira mumabhanhire enyu, 
<b>10</b> kana homwe yerwendo, kana nguvo refu mbiri, kana manyatera, kana tsvimbo; nokuti mushandi wakafanira kudya kwake. <b>11</b> Muguta ripi neripi kana mumusha wamunopinda, bvunzai kuti ndiani mauri wakafanira, mugarepo, kusvikira muchibva. <b>12</b> Zvino kana muchipinda muimba, muikwazise. <b>13</b> Uye kana imba yakafanira, rugare rwenyu ngaruuye pamusoro payo; asi kana isingafaniri, rugare rwenyu ngarudzokere kwamuri.

<b>18</b> zvino muchauiswawo pamberi pevatungamiriri nemadzimambo nekuda kwangu, huve uchapupu kwavari nekuvahedheni. <b>19</b> Asi kana vachikukumikidzai, musafunganya kuti muchataura sei kana chii; nokuti muchapiwa nenguva iyo chamuchataura. <b>20</b> Nokuti hamusi imwi munotaura, asi Mweya waBaba venyu anotaura mamuri. <b>21</b> Mwanakomana wamai achakumikidza mwanakomana wamai kurufu, nababa mwana; uye vana vachamukira vabereki nekuvauraya. <b>22</b> Uye muchavengwa nevese nekuda kwezita rangu; asi anotsungirira kusvika pakuguma, ndiye achaponeswa. 
<b>23</b> Asi kana vachikushushai muguta iri, tizirai kune rimwe; nokuti zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Hamungatongopedzi maguta aIsraeri, kusvikira Mwanakomana wemunhu asvika. <b>24</b> Mudzidzi haasi pamusoro pemudzidzisi, kana muranda pamusoro paishe wake. <b>25</b> Zvakaringanira mudzidzi kuti ave semudzidzisi wake, nemuranda saishe wake. Kana vaidza Ishe weimba kuti Bheerizebhuri, vachadaro zvikuru sei veimba yake! Jesu anoyambira vadzidzi vake 
<b>26</b> Naizvozvo musavatya; nokuti hakuna chakafukidzwa chisingazobvundunurwi, kana chakavanzika chisingazozikanwi. <b>27</b> Zvandinokuudzai murima, taurai pachiedza, nezvamunonzwa munzeve, paridzirai pamusoro pematenga edzimba. <b>28</b> Uye musatya vanouraya muviri, asi havagoni kuuraya mweya; asi mutye zvikuru uyo anogona kuparadza zvese mweya nemuviri mugehena. <b>29</b> Ko tushiri tuviri hatutengeswi nekobiri here? Asi kamwe kadzo hakangawiri pasi kunze kwekuda kwaBaba venyu. <b>30</b> Asi nevhudzi remusoro wenyu rakaverengwa rese. 
<b>31</b> Naizvozvo musatya, imwi munopfuura tushiri tuzhinji. <b>32</b> Naizvozvo umwe neumwe achandibvuma pamberi pevanhu, ini ndichamubvumawo pamberi paBaba vangu vari kumatenga. <b>33</b> Asi ani nani anondiramba pamberi pevanhu, neni ndichamuramba pamberi paBaba vangu vari kumatenga. Zviratidzo zvenguva <b>34</b> Musafunga kuti ndakauya kuzoisa rugare panyika; handina kuuya kuzoisa rugare, asi munondo. <b>35</b> Nokuti ndakauya kuzopesanisa munhu nababa vake, nemukunda namai vake, nemuroora navamwene vake; 
<b>36</b> nevavengi vemunhu ndeveimba yake. <b>37</b> Anoda baba kana mai kupfuura ini haana kufanira ini; neanoda mwanakomana kana mukunda kupfuura ini haana kufanira ini; <b>38</b> uye asingatori muchinjikwa wake akanditevera, haana kufanira ini. <b>39</b> Anowana mweya wake acharasikirwa nawo; asi anorasikirwa nemweya wake nekuda kwangu achauwana. <b>40</b> Uyo anogamuchira imwi anogamuchira ini; neanogamuchira ini anogamuchira iye wakandituma. 
<b>41</b> Anogamuchira muporofita muzita remuporofita achawana mubairo wemuporofita; neanogamuchira wakarurama muzita rewakarurama achawana mubairo wewakarurama. <b>42</b> Uye ani nani anonwisa umwe wevadiki ava mukombe chete wemvura inotonhorera muzita remudzidzi, zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Haangatongorasikirwi nemubairo wake. 
<b>Mateo 11</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino zvakaitika apo Jesu apedza kuraira vadzidzi vake gumi nevaviri, akabvira ipapo kudzidzisa nekuparidza pamaguta avo. Johwani Mubhabhatidzi anotuma vadzidzi kunobvunza Jesu <b>2</b> Zvino Johwani wakati anzwa ari mutirongo mabasa aKristu, akatuma vaviri vevadzidzi vake, <b>3</b> akati kwaari: Imwi ndimwi anouya here, kana titarisire umwe? <b>4</b> Jesu akapindura akati kwavari: Endai munoudza Johwani zvinhu zvamunonzwa nezvamunoona: 
<b>5</b> Mapofu anoonazve, uye mhetamakumbo dzinofamba, vane maperembudzi vanonatswa, nevematsi vanonzwa, vakafa vanomutswa, nevarombo vanoparidzirwa evhangeri; <b>6</b> wakaropafadzwa ani nani asingagumbuswi neni. <b>7</b> Ava vakati vachienda, Jesu akatanga kuudza zvaunga pamusoro paJohwani akati: Makange mabuda kurenje kunoonei? Rutsanga rwunozungunuswa nemhepo here? <b>8</b> Asi makange mabuda kunoonei? Munhu wakapfeka nguvo dzakapfava here? Tarirai, vanopfeka zvakapfava vari mudzimba dzemadzimambo. 
<b>9</b> Asi makange mabuda kunoonei? Muporofita here? Hongu, ndinoti kwamuri: Kunyange anopfuura kwazvo muporofita; <b>10</b> nokuti uyu ndiye wakanyorwa pamusoro pake zvichinzi: Tarira, ini ndinotuma mutumwa wangu pamberi pechiso chako, achagadzira nzira yako pamberi pako. <b>11</b> Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Hakuna kumuka pakati pevakaberekwa nevakadzi mukuru kuna Johwani Mubhabhatidzi. Asi mudikisa muushe hwekumatenga mukuru kwaari. 
<b>12</b> Uye kubva pamazuva aJohwani Mubhabhatidzi kusvikira zvino, ushe hwekumatenga hunopambiwa, nevapambi vanohutora nesimba. <b>13</b> Nokuti vaporofita vese nemurairo vakaporofita kusvikira kuna Johwani; <b>14</b> zvino kana muchida kuzvigamuchira, uyu ndiye Eria wakange achizouya. <b>15</b> Une nzeve dzekunzwa, ngaanzwe. <b>16</b> Asi ndicharifananidza nei zera iri? Rakafanana nevana vagere pamisika, vachidanidzira kushamwari dzavo, <b>17</b> vachiti: Takakuridzirai nyere, asi hamuna kutamba; takakuririrai, asi hamuna kuchema. 
<b>18</b> Nokuti Johwani wakauya asingadyi kana kunwa, asi vanoti: Ane dhimoni. <b>19</b> Mwanakomana wemunhu wakauya achidya, achinwa, zvino vanoti: Tarirai munhu anokara uye munwi wewaini, shamwari yevateresi nevatadzi. Asi njere dzinonzi dzakarurama nevana vadzo. Jesu anotsiura maguta matatu akamuramba <b>20</b> Ipapo akatanga kutsiura maguta makange maitwa mabasa ake esimba mazhinji, nokuti haana kutendeuka, akati: 
<b>21</b> Une nhamo, Korazini! Une nhamo, Bhetisaidha! Nokuti dai akaitwa muTire neSidhoni mabasa esimba akaitwa mamuri, vangadai vakatendeuka kare vakapfeka masaga nedota. <b>22</b> Asi ndinoti kwamuri: Zvichava nani kuTire neSidhoni nezuva rekutongwa kupfuura imwi. <b>23</b> Newe Kapenaume, wakasimudzirwa kudenga, uchawisirwa pasi muhadhesi; nokuti dai akaitwa muSodhoma mabasa esimba akaitwa mauri, vangadai vachipo kusvikira zuva rino. <b>24</b> Asi ndinoti kwamuri: Zvichava nani kunyika yeSodhoma nezuva rekutongwa kupfuura iwe. Jesu anorumbidza Baba 
<b>25</b> Nenguva iyo Jesu akapindura akati: Ndinokutendai, Baba, Ishe wedenga nenyika, nokuti makavanzira zvinhu izvi vakachenjera nevakangwara, mukazvizarurira vacheche. <b>26</b> Hongu Baba, nokuti zvakadaro zvakava zvakanaka pamberi penyu. <b>27</b> Zvinhu zvese zvakakumikidzwa kwandiri naBaba vangu; uye hakuna anoziva Mwanakomana kunze kwaBaba; hakunazve anoziva Baba kunze kweMwanakomana, uye ani nani Mwanakomana waanenge achida kuzarurira. Jesu anokoka vatadzi <b>28</b> Uyai kwandiri mese makaneta makaremerwa, uye ini ndichakupai zororo. 
<b>29</b> Torai joko rangu muise pamusoro penyu, mudzidze kwandiri, nokuti ndiri munyoro, ndakaderera mumoyo; uye muchawana zororo pamweya yenyu; <b>30</b> nokuti joko rangu rakanakisa, nemutoro wangu wakareruka. 
<b>Mateo 12</b><br><b>1</b> Nenguva iyo Jesu wakagura nemumunda wezviyo nesabata; zvino vadzidzi vake vakange vane nzara, vakatanga kutanha hura ndokudya. <b>2</b> Asi VaFarisi vakati vachiona vakati kwaari: Tarira, vadzidzi vako vanoita zvisingatenderwi kuitwa nesabata. <b>3</b> Asi wakati kwavari: Hamuna kuverenga here Dhavhidhi zvaakaita paakange ava nenzara, iye nevakange vanaye; <b>4</b> kuti wakapinda sei mumba maMwari, akadya zvingwa zvekuratidza, zvaakange asingatenderwi kuzvidya iye kunyange nevaiva naye, kunze kwevapristi chete? 
<b>5</b> Kana hamuna kuverenga here pamurairo, kuti nemasabata vapristi vanosvibisa masabata mutembere asi vasina chavangapomerwa? <b>6</b> Asi ndinoti kwamuri: Pano pane mukuru kutembere. <b>7</b> Asi dai maiziva kuti zvinorevei kuti: Ndinoda tsitsi kwete chibairo, mungadai musina kupa mhosva vasina mhosva. <b>8</b> Nokuti Mwanakomana wemunhu ndiIshe kunyange wesabata. Jesu anoporesa munhu aiva neruoko rwakaonyana nesabata <b>9</b> Zvino wakati abvapo akapinda musinagoge ravo. 
<b>10</b> Zvino tarira, kwakange kune munhu akange ane ruoko rwakawonyana. Vakamubvunza vachiti: Zvakatenderwa kuporesa nemasabata here? kuti vamupe mhosva. <b>11</b> Asi wakati kwavari: Ndeupi munhu pakati penyu achava negwai rimwe, iro kana rikawira mugomba nemasabata, angarega kuribata akasimudza? <b>12</b> Naizvozvo munhu anopfuura gwai zvikuru sei! Naizvozvo zvakatenderwa kuita zvakanaka nemasabata. <b>13</b> Zvino akati kumunhu: Tandavadza ruoko rwako. Akarutandavadza, rwukaporeswa rwukagwinya serumwe. Muranda wakasanangurwa 
<b>14</b> Zvino VaFarisi vakabuda, vakaita dare pamusoro pake kuti vangamuparadza sei. <b>15</b> Asi Jesu wakati aziva, akabvapo; zvaunga zvikuru zvikamutevera, akavaporesa vese. <b>16</b> Akavarairisa kuti varege kumubudisa pachena. <b>17</b> Kuti zvizadziswe zvakarehwa naIsaya muporofita, achiti: <b>18</b> Tarira muranda wangu wandakasanangura, mudikanwi wangu, mweya wangu maunofara maari kwazvo. Ndichaisa Mweya wangu pamusoro pake, uye achazivisa vahedheni kutonga. 
<b>19</b> Haangarwisi kana kudanidzira, uye hakuna achanzwa inzwi rake munzira dzemumaguta. <b>20</b> Rutsanga rwakapwanyika haangaruvhuni, netambo yemwenje inopfungaira haangadzimi; kusvikira achituma kutonga mukukunda. <b>21</b> Uye vahedheni vachavimba muzita rake. Jesu anobudisa dhimoni ndokudadirwa neVaFarisi <b>22</b> Zvino kwakauyiswa kwaari wakagarwa nedhimoni, bofu uye mbeveve; akamuporesa, zvekuti bofu mbeveve rakati kutaura rikati kuona. <b>23</b> Zvaunga zvese zvikashamisika zvikati: Ko uyu haasi Mwanakomana waDhavhidhi here? 
<b>24</b> Asi VaFarisi vakati vachinzwa vakati: Uyu haabudisi madhimoni, kunze naBheerizebhuri mukuru wemadhimoni. <b>25</b> Zvino Jesu wakaziva ndangariro dzavo, akati kwavari: Ushe humwe nehumwe hwakapesana huchizvipikisa hunoparadzwa, neguta rimwe nerimwe kana imba yakapesana ichizvipikisa haingamiri. <b>26</b> Uye kana Satani achibudisa Satani, anopesana achizvipikisa; zvino ushe hwake huchamira sei? <b>27</b> Uye kana ini ndichibudisa madhimoni naBheerizebhuri, vanakomana venyu vanoabudisa nani? Naizvozvo ivo vachava vatongi venyu. 
<b>28</b> Asi kana ini ndichibudisa madhimoni neMweya waMwari, saka ushe hwaMwari hwasvika kwamuri. <b>29</b> Kana munhu angapinda sei mumba mechikakarara akapamba nhumbi dzacho kunze kwekuti atanga kusunga chikakarara? Ipapo achapamba imba yake. <b>30</b> Uyo asina ini, anopikisana neni, neasingaunganidzi neni, anoparadzira. <b>31</b> Naizvozvo ndinoti kwamuri: Vanhu vachakanganwirwa chivi chese nekunyomba, asi kunyomba Mweya hakungakanganwirwi vanhu. 
<b>32</b> Uye ani nani anotaura shoko rinopikisa Mwanakomana wemunhu achakanganwirwa; asi ani nani anotaura achipikisa Mweya Mutsvene haangakanganwirwi, kana panguva ino kana kune inouya. <b>33</b> Kana itai muti uve wakanaka nechibereko chawo chakanaka, kana itai muti uve wakaipa nechibereko chawo chakaipa, nokuti muti unozikanwa nechibereko. <b>34</b> Vana venyoka, mungagona sei kutaura zvinhu zvakanaka makaipa? Nokuti muromo unotaura kubva pazvizere mumoyo. 
<b>35</b> Munhu wakanaka anobudisa zvinhu zvakanaka pafuma yakanaka yemoyo; nemunhu wakaipa anobudisa zvinhu zvakaipa pafuma yakaipa. <b>36</b> Asi ndinoti kwamuri: Shoko rimwe nerimwe risina maturo vanhu ravanoreva, vachazvidavirira pamusoro paro nezuva rekutongwa. <b>37</b> Nokuti nemashoko ako uchanzi wakarurama, uye nemashoko ako uchapiwa mhosva. Vanokumbira chiratidzo <b>38</b> Zvino vamwe vevanyori neveVaFarisi vakapindura vakati: Mudzidzisi, tinoda kuona chiratidzo kubva kwamuri. 
<b>39</b> Asi wakapindura akati kwavari: Zera rakaipa rinofeva rinotsvaka chiratidzo; asi hakuna chiratidzo chingapiwa kwariri, kunze kwechiratidzo chaJona muporofita. <b>40</b> Nokuti Jona sezvaakava mudumbu rehove huru mazuva matatu neusiku utatu, saizvozvo Mwanakomana wemunhu achava mumoyo wenyika mazuva matatu neusiku utatu. <b>41</b> Varume veNinivhi vachasimuka mukutongwa nezera iri, vacharipa mhosva; nokuti vakatendeuka nemharidzo yaJona; zvino tarirai, mukuru kuna Jona ari pano. 
<b>42</b> Mambokadzi wechamhembe achasimuka mukutongwa nezera iri, agoripa mhosva; nokuti wakabva pamagumisiro enyika kuzonzwa uchenjeri hwaSoromoni; zvino tarirai, mukuru kuna Soromoni ari pano. Kutendeuka kusina kukwana <b>43</b> Zvino kana mweya wetsvina wabuda kumunhu, unofamba uchigura nzvimbo dzakawoma, uchitsvaka zororo, asi uchishaiwa. <b>44</b> Zvino unoti: Ndichadzokera kumba kwangu kwandakabuda; uye uchisvika unoiwana isina chinhu, yakatsvairwa yakarongedzwa. 
<b>45</b> Ipapo unoenda ndokutora pamwe naye mimwe mweya minomwe yakaipa kumupfuura pachake, ndokupinda ikagaramo; kugumawo kwemunhu uyu kwakaipa kupfuura kutanga. Zvichava saizvozvowo kuzera iri rakaipa. Hama dzaJesu dzechokwadi <b>46</b> Wakati achataura nezvaunga, tarira, mai nevanin'ina vake vakamira panze, vachitsvaka kutaura naye. <b>47</b> Umwe ndokuti kwaari: Tarirai, mai venyu nevanin'ina venyu vamire panze, vachitsvaka kutaura nemwi. <b>48</b> Asi wakapindura akati kuna iye wakamuudza: Mai vangu ndiani? Nevanin'ina vangu ndivana ani? 
<b>49</b> Zvino wakatandavadzira ruoko rwake kuvadzidzi vake, akati: Tarirai, mai vangu nevanin'ina vangu. <b>50</b> Nokuti ani nani unoita kuda kwaBaba vangu vari kumatenga, ndiye munin'ina wangu nehanzvadzi namai. 
<b>Mateo 13</b><br><b>1</b> Nezuva iro, Jesu wakabuda mumba akagara pasi parutivi rwegungwa. <b>2</b> Uye zvaunga zvikuru zvakaungana kwaari, kusvikira apinda muchikepe akagara pasi; nechaunga chese chakamira pamahombekombe. <b>3</b> Ndokutaura zvinhu zvizhinji kwavari nemifananidzo, achiti: Tarirai, mukushi wakabuda kunokusha. <b>4</b> Zvino pakukusha kwake, imwe mbeu yakawira kurutivi rwenzira; shiri dzikasvika dzikaidya. <b>5</b> Imwe ikawira panzvimbo dzine mabwe, payakange isina ivhu zhinji; ikamera pakarepo, nokuti pakange pasina udziku hwevhu. 
<b>6</b> Kuzoti zuva rabuda, yakapiswa, uye nokuti yakange isina mudzi, yakasvava. <b>7</b> Asi imwe yakawira paminzwa; minzwa ikakura, ikaivhunga. <b>8</b> Asi imwe yakawira muvhu rakanaka, ikabereka chibereko, imwe zana, neimwe makumi matanhatu, neimwe makumi matatu. <b>9</b> Ane nzeve dzekunzwa, ngaanzwe. Mabasa emifananidzo <b>10</b> Zvino vadzidzi vakasvika, vakati kwaari: Munotaurirei kwavari nemifananidzo? <b>11</b> Akapindura akati kwavari: Nokuti kwakapiwa kwamuri kuziva zvakavanzika zveushe hwekumatenga, asi kwavari hakuna kupiwa. 
<b>12</b> Nokuti ani nani anazvo achapiwa, uye achava nezvakawanda; asi ani nani asina achatorerwa kunyange nezvaanazvo. <b>13</b> Naizvozvo ndinotaura kwavari nemifananidzo, kuti vachiona havaoni; uye vachinzwa havanzwi, uye havanzwisisi. <b>14</b> Uye kwavari kwakazadziswa chiporofita chaIsaya chinoti: Nekunzwa muchanzwa, asi hamungatongonzwisisi; nekuona muchaona, asi hamungatongoonesesi. 
<b>15</b> Nokuti moyo wevanhu ava wava mukukutu, uye vanonzwa nenzeve nemutsutsururu, nemeso avo vakaatsinzina; zvimwe varege kuona nemeso, uye vanzwe nenzeve, uye vanzwisise nemoyo, vatendeukewo, ndigovaporesa. <b>16</b> Asi akaropafadzwa meso enyu, nokuti anoona; nenzeve dzenyu, nokuti dzinonzwa. <b>17</b> Nokuti zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Vaporofita nevakarurama vazhinji vakange vachishuva kuona zvamunoona, asi havana kuzviona; nekunzwa zvamunonzwa, asi havana kunzwa. Kududzirwa kwemufananidzo wemukushi 
<b>18</b> Naizvozvo imwi inzwai mufananidzo wemukushi. <b>19</b> Umwe neumwe anonzwa shoko reushe asi asinganzwisisi, wakaipa anosvika ndokubvuta chakakushwa mumoyo make. Uyu ndiye wakakushwa kurutivi rwenzira. <b>20</b> Asi wakakushwa panzvimbo dzine mabwe, ndiye anonzwa shoko ndokurigamuchira pakarepo nemufaro; <b>21</b> asi haana mudzi maari, asi anogara kwenguva, uye kana dambudziko kana kushushwa zvichiuya nekuda kweshoko, pakarepo anogumburwa. 
<b>22</b> Uye wakakushwa paminzwa, ndiye anonzwa shoko, asi kufunganya kwenyika ino nekunyengera kwefuma zvinovhunga shoko, rova risingabereki. <b>23</b> Asi wakakushwa muvhu rakanaka, ndiye anonzwa shoko akanzwisisa, anoberekawo zvibereko, achiita, umwe zana, neumwe makumi matanhatu, neumwe makumi matatu. Mufananidzo wemashawi nezviyo <b>24</b> Wakaisa pamberi pavo umwe mufananidzo, achiti: Ushe hwekumatenga hunofananidzwa nemunhu wakakusha mbeu yakanaka mumunda make. 
<b>25</b> Asi vanhu vakati varere, muvengi wake wakauya, akakusha mashawi pakati pezviyo, akaenda. <b>26</b> Asi chipande chakati chamera nekubereka chibereko, zvino mashawi akaonekwawo. <b>27</b> Zvino varanda vemwene weimba vakaswedera vakati kwaari: Ishe, hamuna kukusha mbeu yakanaka mumunda wenyu here? Zvino wawanepi mashawi? <b>28</b> Akati kwavari: Muvengi waita izvi. Varanda vakati kwaari: Saka munoda kuti tinoaunganidza here? <b>29</b> Asi wakati: Kwete, zvimwe kana muchiunganidza mashawi, mungadzura zviyo nawo. 
<b>30</b> Regai zvikure pamwe zviri zviviri kusvikira pakukohwa; zvino nenguva yekukohwa ndichati kuvakohwi: Unganidzai pakutanga mashawi, muasunge mwanda kuti muapise; asi muunganidze zviyo mudura rangu. Mufananidzo wetsanga yemasitadha newembiriso <b>31</b> Wakaisa pamberi pavo umwe mufananidzo achiti: Ushe hwekumatenga hwakafanana netsanga yemasitadha*, munhu yaanotora akaidzvara mumunda make. <b>32</b> Iyo idiki zvirokwazvo pambeu dzese, asi kana yakura ihuru pamiriwo, inova muti, zvekuti shiri dzekudenga dzinouya dzichivaka matendere pamatavi awo. 
<b>33</b> Akataura umwe mufananidzo kwavari akati: Ushe hwekumatenga hwakafanana nembiriso, mukadzi yaakatora, akaiisa muzviyero zvitatu zveupfu kusvikira hwese hwaviriswa. <b>34</b> Izvi zvinhu zvese Jesu wakaudza zvaunga nemifananidzo, uye pasina mufananidzo haana kutaura kwavari; <b>35</b> kuti chizadziswe chakarehwa nemuporofita, achiti: Ndichazarura muromo wangu nemifananidzo; ndichataura zvinhu zvakange zvakavanzika kubva pamavambo enyika. Kududzirwa kwemufananidzo wemashawi nezviyo 
<b>36</b> Zvino Jesu wakati arega zvaunga zvichienda, akapinda mumba; vadzidzi vake vakaswedera kwaari, vachiti: Tidudzirei mufananidzo wemashawi emunda. <b>37</b> Akapindura akati kwavari: Anokusha mbeu yakanaka Mwanakomana wemunhu; <b>38</b> uye munda inyika; nembeu yakanaka ndivo vana veushe; asi mashawi vana vewakaipa. <b>39</b> Uye muvengi wakaakusha ndidhiabhorosi; uye kukohwa kuguma kwenyika; nevakohwi vatumwa. <b>40</b> Naizvozvo mashawi sezvaanounganidzwa achipiswa mumoto, zvichava saizvozvo pakuguma kwenyika ino. 
<b>41</b> Mwanakomana wemunhu achatuma vatumwa vake, vagounganidza kubva muushe hwake zvigumbuso zvese nevanoita zvakaipa; <b>42</b> zvino vachavakandira muvira remoto; ipapo pachava nekuchema nekugeda-geda kwemeno. <b>43</b> Ipapo vakarurama vachapenya sezuva muushe hwaBaba vavo. Ane nzeve dzekunzwa, ngaanzwe. Mufananidzo wefuma yakavanzwa <b>44</b> Zvekare ushe hwekumatenga hwakafanana nefuma yakavigwa mumunda, inoti kana munhu aiwana, anoiviga; zvino nemufaro wake anoenda akanotengesa zvese zvaanazvo akatenga munda uyo. Mufananidzo wemaparera 
<b>45</b> Zvekare ushe hwekumatenga hwakafanana nemushambadzi anotsvaka maparera* akanaka; <b>46</b> akati awana parera rimwe rine mutengo mukuru, akaenda akanotengesa zvese zvaakange anazvo, akaritenga. Mufananidzo wemumbure <b>47</b> Zvekare ushe hwekumatenga hwakafanana nemumbure wakakandirwa mugungwa, ukaunganidza marudzi ese ehove. <b>48</b> Iwo kana wazara, vanoukwevera pamahombekombe, ndokugara pasi, vakaunganidzira dzakanaka mumidziyo, asi dzakaipa vakadzirasira kunze. 
<b>49</b> Zvichava saizvozvo pakuguma kwenguva; vatumwa vachabuda, vachaparadzanisa vakaipa kubva pakati pevakarurama, <b>50</b> uye vachavakandira muvira remoto; ipapo pachava nekuchema nekugeda-geda kwemeno. <b>51</b> Jesu akati kwavari: Manzwisisa izvi zvese here? Vakati kwaari: Hongu Ishe. <b>52</b> Zvino akati kwavari: Naizvozvo munyori umwe neumwe wakadzidziswa zveushe hwekumatenga wakafanana nemunhu, mwene weimba, anobudisa zvinhu zvitsva nezvisharu pafuma yake. Jesu unoshovorwa paNazareta 
<b>53</b> Zvino zvakaitika kuti Jesu apedza mifananidzo iyi wakabvapo. <b>54</b> Uye wakati asvika kunyika yekwake akavadzidzisa musinagoge ravo, kusvikira vashamisika vakati: Kunobvepi kune uyu kuchenjera uku nemabasa esimba? <b>55</b> Uyu haasi mwanakomana wemuvezi here? Ko mai vake havanzi Maria here, nevanin'ina vake Jakobho naJose naSimoni naJudhasi here? <b>56</b> Nehanzvadzi dzake hadzisi nesu dzese here? Ko zvinobvepi kune uyu zvinhu izvozvi zvese? 
<b>57</b> Vakagumbuswa naye. Asi Jesu akati kwavari: Muporofita haavi asina kukudzwa kunze kwemunyika yekwake nemumba make. <b>58</b> Uye haana kuita mabasa esimba mazhinji ipapo nekuda kwekusatenda kwavo. 
<b>Mateo 14</b><br><b>1</b> Panguva iyo Herodhe umwe wevatongi vana wakanzwa mukurumbira waJesu, <b>2</b> akati kuvaranda vake: Uyu ndiJohwani Mubhabhatidzi; iye wakamuka kuvakafa, naizvozvowo mabasa esimba awa anobata maari. <b>3</b> Nokuti Herodhe wakange abata Johwani akamusunga akamuisa mutirongo nekuda kwaHerodhiasi mukadzi waFiripi munin'ina wake. <b>4</b> Nokuti Johwani wakange ati kwaari: Hazvitenderwi kwauri kuti uve naye. <b>5</b> Zvino achida kumuuraya, akatya chaunga, nokuti vaimutora semuporofita. 
<b>6</b> Asi kwakati kuchichengetedzwa zuva rekuberekwa kwaHerodhe, mukunda waHerodhiasi akatamba pakati pavo, akafadza Herodhe. <b>7</b> Naizvozvo wakavimbisa nemhiko kuti achamupa chipi nechipi chaaizokumbira. <b>8</b> Zvino iye, wakati amborairwa namai vake, akati: Ndipei pano mundiro musoro waJohwani Mubhabhatidzi. <b>9</b> Mambo akashungurudzika, asi nekuda kwemhiko, uye kwevakange vagere naye pakudya, wakaraira kuti apiwe. <b>10</b> Akatuma vanhu, akagura Johwani musoro mutirongo. 
<b>11</b> Musoro wake ukauyiswa mundiro, ukapiwa kumusikana, akauisa kuna mai vake. <b>12</b> Vadzidzi vake vakauya vakatora mutumbi vakauviga, vakaenda ndokuudza Jesu. Zvuru zvishanu zvevanhu zvinopiwa kudya <b>13</b> Jesu wakati azvinzwa, akabvapo nechikepe, akaenda kunzvimbo yerenje ari ega; asi zvaunga zvakati zvichizvinzwa zvikamutevera netsoka zvichibva mumaguta. <b>14</b> Zvino Jesu wakati abuda akaona chaunga chikuru, akavanzwira tsitsi, akaporesa varwere vavo. 
<b>15</b> Zvino ava madekwani, vadzidzi vake vakauya kwaari vachiti: Iyi inzvimbo yerenje, uye ikozvino nguva dzaenda; regai zvaunga zviende, kuti vaende kumisha vanozvitengera kudya. <b>16</b> Asi Jesu wakati kwavari: Havafaniri kuenda; vapei imwi kuti vadye. <b>17</b> Asi vakati kwaari: Hatina pano kunze kwezvingwa zvishanu nehove mbiri. <b>18</b> Iye akati: Uyai nazvo pano pandiri. 
<b>19</b> Akaraira zvaunga kuti zvigare pasi pauswa, zvino akatora zvingwa zvishanu nehove mbiri, akatarira kumusoro kudenga, akazviropafadza, ndokumedura akapa zvingwa kuvadzidzi, vadzidzi ndokupa kuzvaunga. <b>20</b> Vakadya vese, vakaguta; vakanonga zvimedu zvakasara, matengu gumi nemaviri azere. <b>21</b> Uye vakange vadya vaiva varume vanenge zvuru zvishanu pasina vakadzi nevana. Jesu anofamba pamusoro pegungwa <b>22</b> Pakarepo Jesu wakamanikidza vadzidzi vake kupinda muchikepe, vamutungamirire mhiri, iye acharega zvaunga zvichienda. 
<b>23</b> Zvino wakati arega zvaunga zvichienda, akakwira mugomo ari ega kunonyengetera; akati ava madekwani, wakange ari ega ipapo. <b>24</b> Asi chikepe zvino chakange chava pakati pegungwa chichitambudzwa nemafungu; nokuti mhepo yakange ichipikisa. <b>25</b> Nenguva yechina yemurindiro yeusiku Jesu akaenda kwavari achifamba pamusoro pegungwa. <b>26</b> Zvino vadzidzi vakati vachimuona achifamba pamusoro pegungwa, vakavhunduka vachiti: Chipoko; ndokudanidzira nekutya. <b>27</b> Asi pakarepo Jesu wakataura navo akati: Tsungai moyo; ndini; musatya. 
<b>28</b> Petro ndokumupindura akati: Ishe, kana muri imwi, ndirairei ndiuye kwamuri pamusoro pemvura. <b>29</b> Ndokubva ati: Uya. Petro ndokuburuka muchikepe akafamba pamusoro pemvura, kuenda kuna Jesu. <b>30</b> Asi wakati achiona mhepo ine simba, akatya; zvino otanga kunyura, akadanidzira, achiti: Ishe, ndiponesei! <b>31</b> Pakarepo Jesu wakatandavadza ruoko, akamubata, akati kwaari: Iwe werutendo rudiki, unokononei? <b>32</b> Vakati vapinda muchikepe, mhepo ikanyarara. 
<b>33</b> Vakange vari muchikepe ndokuuya vakamunamata vachiti: Zvirokwazvo muri Mwanakomana waMwari. Jesu anoporesa paGenesareti <b>34</b> Vakati vayambuka, vakasvika panyika yeGenesareti. <b>35</b> Varume venzvimbo iyo vakati vamuziva vakatuma shoko panyika yese iyo yakapoteredza, vakauisa kwaari vese vairwara. <b>36</b> Vakakumbirisa kwaari kuti vabate chete mupendero wenguvo yake; uye vese vakabata vakaporeswa. 
<b>Mateo 15</b><br><b>1</b> Ipapo kwakauya kuna Jesu vanyori neVaFarisi vachibva kuJerusarema vachiti: <b>2</b> Sei vadzidzi venyu vachidarika tsika yevakuru? Nokuti havashambi maoko avo kana vachidya chingwa. <b>3</b> Asi akapindura akati kwavari: Nemhaka yei imwiwo muchidarika murairo waMwari nekuda kwetsika yenyu? <b>4</b> Nokuti Mwari wakaraira achiti: Kudza baba namai vako; uye kuti: Anotuka baba kana mai, anofanira kufa rufu. <b>5</b> Asi imwi munoti: Ani nani anoti kuna baba kana kuna mai: Chipi nechipi chaungabatsirwa neni chibairo, 
<b>6</b> naizvozvo haatongokudzi baba vake kana mai vake; naizvozvo mashayisa murairo waMwari maturo netsika yenyu. <b>7</b> Vanyepedzeri, Isaya wakaporofita zvakanaka pamusoro penyu achiti: <b>8</b> Vanhu ava vanoswedera kwandiri nemuromo wavo, vachindikudza nemiromo; asi moyo wavo uri kure neni. <b>9</b> Asi vanondinamata pasina, vachidzidzisa dzidziso dziri mirairo yevanhu. <b>10</b> Zvino akadanira chaunga kwaari, akati kwavari: Teererai munzwisise. 
<b>11</b> Hazvisi zvinopinda mumuromo zvinosvibisa munhu; asi izvo zvinobuda mumuromo, ndizvo zvinosvibisa munhu. <b>12</b> Zvino vadzidzi vake vakaswedera vakati kwaari: Munoziva kuti VaFarisi vakati vachinzwa shoko iri vakagumbuka here? <b>13</b> Asi wakapindura akati: Chinomera chimwe nechimwe Baba vangu vekudenga chavasina kudzvara chichadzurwa. <b>14</b> Varegei; vatungamiriri vakapofumara vemapofu. Zvino kana bofu richitungamirira bofu, achawira mugomba ari maviri. <b>15</b> Petro akapindura akati kwaari: Tidudzirei mufananidzo uyu. 
<b>16</b> Zvino Jesu akati: Imwiwo muchiri vasinganzwisisi nhai? <b>17</b> Nazvino hamunzwisisi here, kuti zvese zvinopinda mumuromo zvinoenda mudumbu, uye zvinoraswa kunze? <b>18</b> Asi zvinhu zvinobuda mumuromo zvinobva mumoyo; izvo zvinosvibisa munhu. <b>19</b> Nokuti mumoyo munobuda ndangariro dzakaipa, umhondi, ufeve, upombwe, umbavha, uchapupu hwenhema, kunyomba. <b>20</b> Izvi ndizvo zvinosvibisa munhu; asi kudya nemaoko asina kushambwa hazvisvibisi munhu. Jesu anoporesa mukunda wemukadzi weKenani 
<b>21</b> Zvino Jesu wakabvapo akaenda kumiganhu yeTire neSidhoni. <b>22</b> Zvino tarira, mukadzi muKenani wakabva kumiganhu iyo akadanidzira kwaari achiti: Ndinzwirei tsitsi, Ishe, Mwanakomana waDhavhidhi; mukunda wangu wakabatwa zvakaipa nedhimoni. <b>23</b> Asi haana kumupindura shoko. Vadzidzi vake ndokuuya kwaari vakamukumbirisa vachiti: Muindisei, nokuti anodanidzira shure kwedu. <b>24</b> Asi wakapindura akati: Handina kutumwa zvimwe, kunze kwekumakwai akarasika eimba yaIsraeri. <b>25</b> Zvino akauya akamunamata achiti: Ishe, ndibatsirei. 
<b>26</b> Asi wakapindura akati: Hazvina kunaka kutora chingwa chevana ndokukandira imbwanana. <b>27</b> Asi iye akati: Hongu, Ishe, asi nembwanana dzinodya zvezvimedu zvinowa patafura yavatenzi vadzo. <b>28</b> Zvino Jesu akapindura akati kwaari: Haiwa mukadzi, rutendo rwako rukuru! Ngazvive kwauri sezvaunoda. Mukunda wake akaporeswa kubva panguva iyo. Jesu anoporesa vazhinji <b>29</b> Jesu ndokubva ipapo akauya parutivi rwegungwa reGarirea; akakwira mugomo, akagara ipapo. 
<b>30</b> Zvino zvaunga zvikuru zvakauya kwaari zvine mhetamakumbo, mapofu, zvimumumu, zvirema, nevamwe vazhinji, vakavakanda patsoka dzaJesu; akavaporesa; <b>31</b> zvekuti zvaunga zvakashamisika zvichiona zvimumumu zvichitaura, zvirema zvakagwinya, vanokamhina vachifamba, nemapofu achiona; vakarumbidza Mwari waIsraeri. Vanodarika zvuru zvina vanopiwa kudya naJesu 
<b>32</b> Jesu ndokudanira vadzidzi vake kwaari akati: Ndinonzwa tsitsi nechaunga, nokuti vakagara neni zvino mazuva matatu vasina chinhu chekudya, uye handidi kuvaindisa vasina kudya, zvimwe vangaziya panzira. <b>33</b> Vadzidzi vake ndokuti kwaari: Tichawanepi zvingwa zvakawanda zvakadaro murenje, kuti tigutise chaunga chikuru chakadai? <b>34</b> Jesu ndokuti kwavari: Mune zvingwa zvingani? Vakati: Zvinomwe, nehove diki shomanene. <b>35</b> Ndokuraira zvaunga kuti zvigare pasi pavhu. 
<b>36</b> Akatora zvingwa zvinomwe nehove, akavonga, akamedura, ndokupa vadzidzi vake, vadzidzi vakapa chaunga. <b>37</b> Vakadya vese vakaguta; vakanonga zvimedu zvakasara, matengu manomwe azere. <b>38</b> Zvino avo vakadya vakange vari varume zvuru zvina pasina vakadzi nevana. <b>39</b> Zvino wakati aregera zvaunga zvichienda, akapinda muchikepe, akasvika kumiganhu yeMagidhara. 
<b>Mateo 16</b><br><b>1</b> VaFarisi neVaSadhusi ndokuuya vachimuidza, vakamukumbira kuti avaratidze chiratidzo chinobva kudenga. <b>2</b> Asi wakapindura akati kwavari: Kana ava madekwani munoti: Kunze kwakanaka; nokuti denga rakatsvuka. <b>3</b> Uye mangwanani munoti: Nhasi kunze kwakaipa; nokuti denga rakatsvuka richikwidibira. Vanyepedzeri, munoziva kuparadzanisa mamiriro edenga, asi hamugoni kuparadzanisa zviratidzo zvenguva here? 
<b>4</b> Zera rakaipa uye rinofeva richitsvaka chiratidzo; asi haripiwi chiratidzo, kunze kwechiratidzo chaJona muporofita. Ndokuvasiya, akaenda. Jesu anodudzira shoko rembiriso yeVaFarisi neVaSadhusi <b>5</b> Zvino vadzidzi vakati vasvika mhiri, vakakanganwa kutora zvingwa. <b>6</b> Zvino Jesu akati kwavari: Onai mungwarire mbiriso yeVaFarisi neVaSadhusi. <b>7</b> Vakarangana pakati pavo vachiti: Unodaro nokuti hatina kutora zvingwa. 
<b>8</b> Zvino Jesu azviziva akati kwavari: Imwi verutendo rudiki, nemhaka yei muchirangana pakati penyu kuti hamuna kutora zvingwa? <b>9</b> Muchigere kunzwisisa here, kana kurangarira zvingwa zvishanu zvevanezvuru zvishanu, uye matengu mangani amakanonga? <b>10</b> Kana zvingwa zvinomwe zvevanezvuru zvina, uye matengu mangani amakanonga? <b>11</b> Hamunzwisisi sei, kuti handina kutaura kwamuri pamusoro pezvingwa kuti mungwarire mbiriso yeVaFarisi neVaSadhusi? 
<b>12</b> Zvino vakanzwisisa kuti haana kutaura kuti vachenjere mbiriso yechingwa, asi dzidziso yeVaFarisi neVaSadhusi. Petro anopupura kuti Jesu ndiye Kristu <b>13</b> Jesu wakati asvika kumativi eKesariya yeFiripi, akabvunza vadzidzi vake, achiti: Vanhu vanoti ini Mwanakomana wemunhu ndini ani? <b>14</b> Ndokubva vati: Vamwe vanoti Johwani Mubhabhatidzi, uye vamwe Eria, vamwewo Jeremiya, kana umwe wevaporofita. <b>15</b> Akati kwavari: Ko imwi munoti ndini ani? <b>16</b> Zvino Simoni Petro akapindura akati: Ndimwi Kristu, Mwanakomana waMwari mupenyu. 
<b>17</b> Jesu akapindura akati kwaari: Wakaropafadzwa, Simoni mwanakomana waJona, nokuti nyama neropa hazvina kukuzarurira izvozvo, asi Baba vangu vari kumatenga. <b>18</b> Neni ndinoti kwauri: Ndiwe Petro, uye pamusoro peruware urwu ndichavaka kereke yangu, nemasuwo egehena* haangaikundi. <b>19</b> Uye ndichapa kwauri kiyi dzeushe hwekumatenga; uye chipi nechipi chauchasunga panyika, chichasungwa kumatenga; nechipi nechipi chauchasunungura panyika, chichasunungurwa kumatenga. 
<b>20</b> Ipapo akarairisa vadzidzi vake kuti varege kuudza munhu kuti ndiye Jesu iye Kristu. Jesu anovaudza zvekutambura kwake nerufu rwake <b>21</b> Kubva panguva iyo Jesu wakatanga kuratidza kuvadzidzi vake kuti anofanira kuenda kuJerusarema, uye kutambudzika zvizhinji zvichibva kuvakuru nevapristi vakuru nevanyori, nekuurawa, nekumutswa nezuva retatu. <b>22</b> Petro akamutora parutivi, akatanga kumutsiura akati: Ngazvirege kutongoitika kwamuri, Ishe; izvi hazvingatongoitiki kwamuri. 
<b>23</b> Asi iye wakatendeuka akati kuna Petro: Ibva shure kwangu, Satani! Uri chigumbuso kwandiri; nokuti haurangariri zvinhu zvaMwari, asi zvinhu zvevanhu. Kutevera Jesu <b>24</b> Zvino Jesu akati kuvadzidzi vake: Kana ani nani achida kuuya shure kwangu, ngaazvirambe, atakure muchinjikwa wake, anditevere. <b>25</b> Nokuti ani nani anoda kuponesa upenyu hwake acharasikirwa nahwo; asi ani nani anorasikirwa neupenyu hwake nekuda kwangu achahuwana. 
<b>26</b> Nokuti munhu anobatsirwei kana akawana nyika yese, akarasikirwa nemweya wake? Kana munhu achapei chive muripo wemweya wake? <b>27</b> Nokuti Mwanakomana wemunhu achauya mukubwinya kwaBaba vake nevatumwa vake, ipapo achapa mubairo umwe neumwe zvichienderana nebasa rake. <b>28</b> Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Vamwe varipo kune vamire pano, vasingatongoraviri rufu kusvikira vaona Mwanakomana wemunhu achiuya muushe hwake. 
<b>Mateo 17</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino shure kwemazuva matanhatu Jesu wakatora Petro naJakobho naJohwani munin'ina wake, akavakwidza mugomo refu vari vega. <b>2</b> Zvino akashandurwa chimiro pamberi pavo, uye chiso chake chikapenya sezuva, nenguvo dzake dzikachena sechiedza. <b>3</b> Zvino tarira, kwakaonekwa kwavari Mozisi naEria vachitaura naye. <b>4</b> Zvino Petro akapindura akati kuna Jesu: Ishe, zvakatinakira kuva pano; kana muchida, ngatiite pano matumba matatu, rimwe renyu, nerimwe raMozisi, nerimwe raEria. 
<b>5</b> Wakati achataura, tarira, gore rinopenya rakavadzikatira; uye tarira, inzwi rikabva mugore, richiti: Uyu Mwanakomana wangu anodikanwa, wandinofara maari kwazvo; inzwai iye. <b>6</b> Vadzidzi vakati vachizvinzwa, vakawa nechiso chavo, vakatya zvikuru. <b>7</b> Asi Jesu akaswedera akavabata, akati: Simukai, musatya. <b>8</b> Vakati vachisimudza meso avo, havana kuona munhu, kunze kwaJesu chete. <b>9</b> Zvino vakati vachiburuka mugomo, Jesu akavaraira, achiti: Musaudza munhu chiratidzo ichi, kusvikira Mwanakomana wemunhu amuka kubva kuvakafa. 
<b>10</b> Vadzidzi vake vakamubvunza vachiti: Vanyori vanogotaurirei vachiti Eria anofanira kutanga kuuya? <b>11</b> Jesu akapindura akati kwavari: Eria achatanga kusvika zvirokwazvo, achavandudza zvinhu zvese. <b>12</b> Asi ndinotaura kwamuri kuti Eria wakatosvika, asi havana kumuziva, asi vakamuitira zvese zvavakada; saizvozvowo Mwanakomana wemunhu achatambura navo. <b>13</b> Zvino vadzidzi vakanzwisisa kuti wakataura kwavari zvaJohwani Mubhabhatidzi. Jesu anoporesa mukomana ane zvipusha 
<b>14</b> Vakati vasvika kuchaunga, kwakauya kwaari munhu, akamufugamira, achiti: <b>15</b> Ishe, donzwirai mwanakomana wangu tsitsi, nokuti ane zvipusha, anotambudzika kwazvo; nokuti kazhinji anowira mumoto, kazhinji mumvura. <b>16</b> Uye ndakamuuisa kuvadzidzi venyu, asi havana kugona kumuporesa. <b>17</b> Jesu akapindura akati: Haiwa zera risina rutendo nerakakombama, ndichava nemwi kusvikira rinhi? Ndichakuitirai moyo murefu kusvikira rinhi? Muuisei pano pandiri. 
<b>18</b> Zvino Jesu akaritsiura, dhimoni rikabuda kwaari, mwana akaporeswa kubva panguva iyo. <b>19</b> Zvino vadzidzi vakauya kuna Jesu vari vega, vakati: Isu takange tisingagoni nei kuribudisa? <b>20</b> Jesu akati kwavari: Nemhaka yekusatenda kwenyu. Nokuti zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Kana mune rutendo rwakaita setsanga yemasitadha, mungati kugomo iri: Ibva pano, enda koko; uye richabva; uye hakuna chinhu chingakukonai. <b>21</b> Asi zera iri haribudi kunze chete nekunyengetera nekutsanya. Jesu anoripa mari yetembere <b>24</b> Zvino vakati vachisvika Kapenaume, vaigamuchira mutero vakauya kuna Petro, vakati: Mudzidzisi wenyu haateri here? <b>25</b> Akati: Hongu. <b>Mateo 18</b><br><b>1</b> Nenguva iyo vadzidzi vakauya kuna Jesu vachiti: Ko ndiani mukurusa paushe hwekumatenga? <b>2</b> Jesu akadanira kwaari mucheche akamumisa pakati pavo. <b>3</b> Akati: Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Kana musingashandurwi mukava sevacheche, hamungatongopindi muushe hwekumatenga. <b>4</b> Naizvozvo ani nani anozvininipisa semucheche uyu ndiye mukurusa muushe hwekumatenga. <b>5</b> Zvino ani nani anogamuchira mucheche umwe wakadai muzita rangu anogamuchira ini. 
<b>6</b> Asi ani nani anogumbusa umwe wevacheche ava vanotenda kwandiri, zvaiva nani kwaari kuti guyo guru risungirirwe kupoteredza pamutsipa wake, anyudzwe mukudzika kwegungwa. <b>7</b> Ine nhamo nyika nemhaka yezvigumbuso! Nokuti kunodikanwa kuti zvigumbuso zviuye; asi une nhamo munhu uyo chigumbuso wachinouya naye! 
<b>8</b> Zvino kana ruoko rwako kana rutsoka rwako zvichikugumbusa, uzvigure urase kubva kwauri; zviri nani kwauri kuti upinde muupenyu uri mhetamakumbo kana uri chirema pakuti une maoko maviri kana tsoka mbiri ukandirwe mumoto wekusingaperi. <b>9</b> Kana ziso rako richikugumbusa, uridzure urase kubva kwauri; zviri nani kwauri kuti upinde muupenyu une ziso rimwe pakuti une meso maviri ukandirwe mugehena remoto. 
<b>10</b> Chenjerai musazvidza umwe wevadiki ava, nokuti ndinoti kwamuri: Kumatenga vatumwa vavo vanogaroona chiso chaBaba vangu vari kumatenga. <b>11</b> Nokuti Mwanakomana wemunhu wakauya kuzoponesa chakange chakarasika. Gwai rakarasika <b>12</b> Munofunga sei? Kana umwe munhu ane makwai zana, rimwe rawo rikarasika, haangasii here makumi mapfumbamwe nemapfumbamwe, akaenda kumakomo kunotsvaka rakarasika? <b>13</b> Kana zvikaitika kuti ariwana, zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Anorifarira kupfuura makumi mapfumbamwe nemapfumbamwe asina kurasika. 
<b>14</b> Saizvozvo hakusi kuda kwaBaba venyu vari kumatenga, kuti umwe wevadiki ava aparare. Kutsiura vadariki <b>15</b> Asi kana umwe wako akutadzira, enda umudzore pakati pako naye mega; kana akakunzwa, wawana umwe wako. <b>16</b> Asi kana asinganzwi, tora umwe kana vamwe vaviri newe, kuti nemuromo wezvapupu zviviri kana zvitatu shoko rese risimbiswe. <b>17</b> Kana asina hanya nekuvanzwa, uudze kereke; asi kana asina hanya nekunzwa kerekewo, ngaave kwauri semuhedheni nemuteresi. 
<b>18</b> Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Chipi nechipi chamuchasunga panyika, chichasungwa kudenga; uye chipi nechipi chamunosunungura panyika, chichasunungurwa kudenga. Kunyengetera pamwe <b>19</b> Zvekare ndinoti kwamuri: Kana vaviri venyu vakatenderana panyika pamusoro pechinhu chipi nechipi chavanokumbira, chichaitwa kwavari naBaba vangu vari kumatenga. <b>20</b> Nokuti apo vaviri kana vatatu vakaungana muzita rangu, ipapo ndiripo pakati pavo. Mufananidzo wemuranda asina tsitsi 
<b>21</b> Zvino Petro wakauya kwaari akati: Ishe, umwe wangu uchanditadzira kangani, ndichimukanganwira? Kusvikira kanomwe here? <b>22</b> Jesu akati kwaari: Handiti kwauri: Kusvikira kanomwe; asi: Kusvikira makumi manomwe akapamhidzirwa kanomwe. <b>23</b> Naizvozvo ushe hwekumatenga hunofananidzwa neumwe mambo, wakange achida kugadzirisa zvemari nevaranda vake. <b>24</b> Zvino wakati achitanga kugadzirisa zvemari, umwe akauyiswa kwaari wakange ane chikwereti chematarenda* gumi rezvuru. 
<b>25</b> Asi zvaakange asina chaangaripa nacho, ishe wake akaraira kuti atengeswe pamwe nemukadzi wake nevana, nezvese zvaakange anazvo, kuti muripo uitwe. <b>26</b> Naizvozvo muranda wakawira pasi, akamunamata, achiti: Ishe, ivai nemoyo murefu neni, ndichakuripirai zvese. <b>27</b> Zvino ishe wemuranda uyo akamunzwira tsitsi, akamusunungura, akamukanganwira chikwereti. <b>28</b> Asi muranda uyo wakati achibuda, akawana umwe wevaranda vaibata pamwe naye, wakange ane ngava kwaari remadhenario* zana; akamubata, akamudzipa, akati: Ndiripe zvaunofanira. 
<b>29</b> Zvino muranda waibata pamwe naye akawira pasi patsoka dzake akamugombetedza achiti: Uve nemoyo murefu neni, ndichakuripira zvese. <b>30</b> Asi iye haana kuda, asi wakaenda, akamukanda mutirongo, kusvikira aripa chikwereti. <b>31</b> Zvino varanda vaibata pamwe naye vakati vachiona zvakange zvaitika, vakashungurudzika kwazvo, vakaenda vakaudza ishe wavo zvese zvakaitika. <b>32</b> Zvino ishe wake wakati amudanira kwaari akati kwaari: Iwe muranda wakaipa, ndakakukanganwira chikwereti icho chose, nokuti wakandigombetedza. 
<b>33</b> Ko newe hausaifanira kunzwira tsitsi here umwe muranda unobata pamwe newe, seniwo ndakakunzwira tsitsi? <b>34</b> Zvino ishe wake akatsamwa, akamukumikidza kuvarwadzisi, kusvikira aripa zvese zvakafanira kwaari. <b>35</b> Saizvozvo Baba vangu vari kudenga vachakuitiraiwo, kana musingakanganwiri umwe neumwe umwe wake zvitadzo zvavo nemoyo yenyu yese. 
<b>Mateo 19</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino zvakaitika apo Jesu apedza mashoko awa, akabva kuGarirea, akasvika kumiganhu yeJudhiya mhiri kweJoridhani. <b>2</b> Zvaunga zvikuru zvikamutevera, akazviporesapo. <b>3</b> VaFarisi vakauyawo kwaari, vachimuidza vachiti kwaari: Zvinotenderwa here kumunhu kuramba mukadzi wake nemhaka ipi neipi? <b>4</b> Akapindura, akati kwavari: Hamuna kurava here, kuti iye wakavaita kubva pakutanga wakavaita murume nemukadzi? 
<b>5</b> Akati: Nekuda kwaizvozvi munhu achasiya baba namai, uye achanamatira kumukadzi wake; avo vaviri vachava nyama imwe. <b>6</b> Naizvozvo havachisiri vaviri, asi nyama imwe. Naizvozvo izvo Mwari zvaakabatanidza munhu ngaarege kuparadzanisa. <b>7</b> Vakati kwaari: Mozisi wakagorairei kupa rugwaro rwekurambana, nekumuramba? <b>8</b> Akati kwavari: Nekuda kweukukutu hwemoyo yenyu Mozisi wakakutenderai kuramba vakadzi venyu; asi kubva pakutanga zvakange zvisina kudaro. 
<b>9</b> Asi ndinoti kwamuri: Ani nani anoramba mukadzi wake, kunze kwemhaka yeupombwe, akawana umwe, anopomba; neanowana wakarambwa anopomba. <b>10</b> Vadzidzi vake vakati kwaari: Kana nyaya yemunhu nemukadzi yakadaro, hazvibatsiri kuwana. <b>11</b> Asi akati kwavari: Vese havagamuchiri shoko iri, kunze kwevakaripiwa. 
<b>12</b> Nokuti kune vatenwa, vakaberekwa vakadaro kubva mudumbu ramai; uye kune vatenwa, vakaitwa vatenwa nevanhu; uye kune vatenwa vakazviita vatenwa nekuda kweushe hwekumatenga. Anogona kugamuchira izvi, ngaagamuchire. Jesu anoropafadza vanana <b>13</b> Zvino kwakauyiswa kwaari vana vadiki, kuti aise maoko pamusoro pavo, anyengetere; asi vadzidzi vakavatsiura. <b>14</b> Asi Jesu wakati: Tenderai vana vadiki, musavadzivisa kuuya kwandiri; nokuti ushe hwekumatenga ndehwevakadai. <b>15</b> Akaisa maoko pamusoro pavo, akabvapo. Jaya mufumi 
<b>16</b> Zvino tarira, kwakaswedera umwe akati kwaari: Mudzidzisi wakanaka, ndingaita chinhu chakanaka chipi kuti ndive neupenyu husingaperi? <b>17</b> Akati kwaari: Unondiidzirei wakanaka? Hakuna wakanaka, kunze kweumwe, ndiye Mwari. Asi kana uchida kupinda muupenyu, chengeta mirairo. <b>18</b> Akati kwaari: Ipi? Jesu ndokuti: Usauraya, usapomba, usaba, usapupura nhema, <b>19</b> kudza baba vako namai, uye, ude umwe wako sezvaunozvida iwe. <b>20</b> Jaya rikati kwaari: Izvozvi zvese ndakazvichengeta kubva paudiki hwangu; ndichiri kushaiwei? 
<b>21</b> Jesu akati kwariri: Kana uchida kuva wakaperera, enda utengese zvaunazvo upe varombo, uye uchava nefuma kudenga; ugouya pano unditevere. <b>22</b> Asi jaya rakati ranzwa shoko iro rikaenda richishungurudzika; nokuti rakange rine nhumbi zhinji. <b>23</b> Jesu akati kuvadzidzi vake: Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Mufumi ucharemerwa kupinda muushe hwekumatenga. <b>24</b> Ndinotizve kwamuri: Zvakarerukira kamera kudarika nemuburi retsono, kumufumi kupinda muushe hwaMwari. 
<b>25</b> Vadzidzi vake vakati vazvinzwa, vakashamisika kwazvo, vachiti: Saka ndiani angagona kuponeswa? <b>26</b> Asi Jesu wakatarira akati kwavari: Kuvanhu izvi hazvibviri, asi kuna Mwari zvese zvinobvira. <b>27</b> Ipapo Petro akapindura, akati kwaari: Tarirai, isu takasiya zvese, tikakuteverai; zvino tichava nei? 
<b>28</b> Jesu akati kwavari: Zvirokwazvo ndinoti kwamuri: Imwi makanditevera, pakuberekwa kutsva kana Mwanakomana wemunhu agara pachigaro cheushe chekubwinya kwake, imwiwo muchagara pazvigaro zveushe gumi nezviviri, muchitonga marudzi gumi nemaviri aIsraeri. <b>29</b> Uye umwe neumwe wakasiya dzimba, kana vanin'ina nemadzikoma, kana hanzvadzi, kana baba, kana mai, kana mukadzi, kana vana, kana minda, nekuda kwezita rangu, uchagamuchira zvine zana, akagara nhaka yeupenyu husingaperi. 
<b>30</b> Asi vazhinji vekutanga vachava vekupedzisira, nevekupedzisira vekutanga. 
<b>Mateo 20</b><br><b>1</b> Nokuti ushe hwekumatenga hwakafanana nemunhu, mwene weimba, wakabuda mangwanani-ngwanani kunopinza basa vashandi mumunda wake wemizambiringa. <b>2</b> Wakati atenderana nevabati padhenario* pazuva, akavatuma kumunda wake wemizambiringa. <b>3</b> Akabuda neawa rinenge rechitatu, akaona vamwe vamire pamusika, vasina chekuita; <b>4</b> akati kwavari: Endai imwiwo mumunda wemizambiringa, ndigokupai chero zvakafanira. Akati kwavari: Endai nemwiwo mumunda wemizambiringa, muchagamuchira chero zvakafanira. <b>8</b> Kuzoti ava madekwani, mwene wemunda wemizambiringa wakati kumutariri wake: Dana vabati, uvape mubairo, uchitanga kune vekupedzisira, kusvikira kune vekutanga. 
<b>9</b> Zvino vakati vachiuya veawa rinenge regumi nerimwe, vakagamuchira umwe neumwe dhenario. <b>10</b> Asi vekutanga vakati vachiuya, vakafunga kuti vachagamuchira zvakapfuura; asi ivowo vakagamuchira umwe neumwe dhenario. <b>11</b> Vakati vagamuchira vakagunun'unira mwene weimba, <b>12</b> vachiti: Ava vekupedzisira vakabata awa rimwe chete, asi mavaenzanisa nesu takatakura mutoro wezuva nekupisa. <b>13</b> Asi wakapindura akati kune umwe wavo: Shamwari, handikuitiri zvisakarurama; hauna kutenderana here neni padhenario?

<b>Mateo 28</b><br><b>1</b> Zvino pakupera kwesabata kotanga kuedza, koenda kumusi wekutanga wevhiki, kwakasvika Maria Magidharini neumwe Maria kuzoona guva. <b>2</b> Zvino tarira, kwakaitika kudengenyeka kwenyika kukuru, nokuti mutumwa waIshe wakaburuka kubva kudenga, akaswedera, akakungurusa ibwe kubva pamukova, akagara pamusoro paro. <b>3</b> Kuonekwa kwake kwakange kwakaita semheni, nechipfeko chake chaiva chichena semagada echando. <b>4</b> Zvino varindi vakadedera nekumutya, vakava sevakafa. 
<b>5</b> Asi mutumwa akapindura akati kuvakadzi: Musatya imwi; nokuti ndinoziva kuti munotsvaka Jesu wakange arovererwa pamuchinjikwa. <b>6</b> Haasi pano; nokuti wamuka sezvaakareva. Uyai muone nzvimbo Ishe paakange arere. <b>7</b> Kurumidzai kuenda munoudza vadzidzi vake kuti wamuka kuvakafa; uye tarirai anokutungamirirai kuGarirea, ikoko muchanomuona; tarirai, ndakuudzai. <b>8</b> Ndokubva vakakurumidza kubuda paguva vane kutya nemufaro mukuru, vakamhanyira kunoudza vadzidzi vake. 
<b>9</b> Zvino vakati vachienda kunoudza vadzidzi vake, tarira, Jesu wakavachingamidza, achiti: Hekanhi! Vakaswedera vakabata tsoka dzake vakamunamata. <b>10</b> Zvino Jesu akati kwavari: Musatya; endai, munoudza hama dzangu kuti vaende kuGarirea, uye vachanondionako. <b>11</b> Vakati vachifamba, tarira, vamwe vevarindi vakaenda muguta vakapira vapristi vakuru zvese zvakaitika. <b>12</b> Vakati vaungana nevakuru, vakarangana, vakapa mari zhinji kumauto, <b>13</b> vachiti: Itii: Vadzidzi vake vakauya usiku vakamuba isu tirere. 
<b>14</b> Kana izvi zvikazikanwa kumutungamiriri, isu tichamunyengerera, tikuitei musava mukushungurudzika. <b>15</b> Ndokubva vatora mari vakaita sezvavakadzidziswa. Shoko iri rikaparadzirwa pakati peVaJudha kusvikira nhasi. <b>16</b> Zvino vadzidzi gumi neumwe vakaenda kuGarirea, kugomo Jesu raakavaraira. <b>17</b> Vakati vachimuona, vakamunamata; asi vamwe vakakonona. <b>18</b> Jesu ndokuswedera akataura kwavari, achiti: Simba rese rakapiwa kwandiri kudenga nepanyika. 
<b>19</b> Endai naizvozvo muite marudzi ese vadzidzi, muchivabhabhatidza muzita raBaba nereMwanakomana nereMweya Mutsvene; <b>20</b> muchivadzidzisa kuchengeta zvese zvandakakurairai; zvino tarirai, ini ndinemwi mazuva ese kusvikira pakuguma kwenyika. Ameni.
