<b>Lucas 1</b><br><b>1</b> Forsothe for manye men enforceden to ordeyne the tellyng of thingis, whiche ben fillid in vs, <b>2</b> as thei that seyn atte the bigynnyng, and weren ministris of the word, <b>3</b> bitaken, it is seen also to me, hauynge alle thingis diligentli bi ordre, to write to thee, <b>4</b> thou best Theofile, that thou knowe the treuthe of tho wordis, of whiche thou art lerned. 
<b>5</b> In the daies of Eroude, kyng of Judee, ther was a prest, Sakarie bi name, of the sorte of Abia, and his wijf was of the douytris of Aaron, and hir name was Elizabeth. <b>6</b> And bothe weren iust bifor God, goynge in alle the maundementis and iustifiyngis of the Lord, withouten pleynt. <b>7</b> And thei hadden no child, for Elizabeth was bareyn, and bothe weren of grete age in her daies. <b>8</b> And it bifel, that whanne Zacarie schulde do the office of preesthod, in the ordre of his cours tofor God, 
<b>9</b> aftir the custome of the preesthod, he wente forth bi lot, and entride in to the temple, to encense. <b>10</b> And al the multitude of the puple was with outforth, and preiede in the our of encensyng. <b>11</b> And an aungel of the Lord apperide to hym, and stood on the riythalf of the auter of encense. <b>12</b> And Zacarie seynge was afraied, and drede fel vpon hym. <b>13</b> And the aungel seide to hym, Zacarie, drede thou not; for thi preyer is herd, and Elizabeth, thi wijf, schal bere to thee a sone, and his name schal be clepid Joon. 
<b>14</b> And ioye and gladyng schal be to thee; and many schulen `haue ioye in his natyuyte. <b>15</b> For he schal be greet bifor the Lord, and he schal not drynke wyn and sidir, and he schal be fulfillid with the Hooli Goost yit of his modir wombe. <b>16</b> And he schal conuerte many of the children of Israel to her Lord God; <b>17</b> and he schal go bifor hym in the spirit and the vertu of Helie; and he schal turne the hertis of the fadris in to the sones, and men out of bileue to the prudence of iust men, to make redi a perfit puple to the Lord. 
<b>18</b> And Zacarie seide to the aungel, Wherof schal Y wite this? for Y am eld, and my wijf hath gon fer in to hir daies. <b>19</b> And the aungel answeride, and seide to hym, For Y am Gabriel, that stonde niy bifor God; and Y am sent to thee to speke, and to euangelize to thee these thingis. <b>20</b> And lo! thou schalt be doumbe, and thou schalt not mow speke til in to the dai, in which these thingis schulen be don; for thou hast not bileued to my wordis, whiche schulen be fulfillid in her tyme. 
<b>21</b> And the puple was abidynge Zacarie, and thei wondriden, that he tariede in the temple. <b>22</b> And he yede out, and myyte not speke to hem, and thei knewen that he hadde seyn a visioun in the temple. And he bikenyde to hem, and he dwellide stille doumbe. <b>23</b> And it was don, whanne the daies of his office weren fulfillid, he wente in to his hous. <b>24</b> And aftir these daies Elizabeth, his wijf, conseyuede, and hidde hir fyue monethis, and seide, 
<b>25</b> For so the Lord dide to me in the daies, in whiche he bihelde, to take awei my repreef among men. <b>26</b> But in the sixte moneth the aungel Gabriel was sent fro God in to a citee of Galilee, whos name was Nazareth, <b>27</b> to a maidyn, weddid to a man, whos name was Joseph, of the hous of Dauid; and the name of the maidun was Marie. <b>28</b> And the aungel entride to hir, and seide, Heil, ful of grace; the Lord be with thee; blessid be thou among wymmen. 
<b>29</b> And whanne sche hadde herd, sche was troublid in his word, and thouyte what maner salutacioun this was. <b>30</b> And the aungel seide to hir, Ne drede thou not, Marie, for thou hast foundun grace anentis God. <b>31</b> Lo! thou schalt conceyue in wombe, and schalt bere a sone, and thou schalt clepe his name Jhesus. <b>32</b> This schal be greet, and he schal be clepid the sone of the Hiyeste; and the Lord God schal yeue to hym the seete of Dauid, his fadir, and he schal regne in the hous of Jacob with outen ende, 
<b>33</b> and of his rewme schal be noon ende. <b>34</b> And Marie seide to the aungel, On what maner schal this thing be doon, for Y knowe not man? <b>35</b> And the aungel answeride, and seide to hir, The Hooly Goost schal come fro aboue in to thee, and the vertu of the Hiyeste schal ouerschadewe thee; and therfor that hooli thing that schal be borun of thee, schal be clepid the sone of God. <b>36</b> And lo! Elizabeth, thi cosyn, and sche also hath conceyued a sone in hir eelde, and this moneth is the sixte to hir that is clepid bareyn; 
<b>37</b> for euery word schal not be inpossible anentis God. <b>38</b> And Marie seide, Lo! the handmaydyn of the Lord; be it don to me aftir thi word. And the aungel departide fro hir. <b>39</b> And Marie roos vp in tho daies, and wente with haaste in to the mounteyns, in to a citee of Judee. <b>40</b> And sche entride in to the hous of Zacarie, and grette Elizabeth. <b>41</b> And it was don, as Elizabeth herde the salutacioun of Marie, the yong child in hir wombe gladide. And Elizabeth was fulfillid with the Hooli Goost, 
<b>42</b> and criede with a greet vois, and seide, Blessid be thou among wymmen, and blessid be the fruyt of thi wombe. <b>43</b> And whereof is this thing to me, that the modir of my Lord come to me? <b>44</b> For lo! as the voice of thi salutacioun was maad in myn eeris, the yong child gladide in ioye in my wombe. <b>45</b> And blessid be thou, that hast bileued, for thilke thingis that ben seid of the Lord to thee, schulen be parfitli don. <b>46</b> And Marie seide, Mi soule magnyfieth the Lord, <b>47</b> and my spirit hath gladid in God, myn helthe. 
<b>48</b> For he hath biholdun the mekenesse of his handmaidun. <b>49</b> For lo! of this alle generaciouns schulen seie that Y am blessid. For he that is myyti hath don to me grete thingis, and his name is hooli. <b>50</b> And his mercy is fro kynrede in to kynredes, to men that dreden hym. <b>51</b> He made myyt in his arme, he scaterede proude men with the thouyte of his herte. <b>52</b> He sette doun myyti men fro sete, and enhaunside meke men. <b>53</b> He hath fulfillid hungri men with goodis, and he hath left riche men voide. 
<b>54</b> He, hauynge mynde of his mercy, took Israel, his child; <b>55</b> as he hath spokun to oure fadris, to Abraham and to his seed, in to worldis. <b>56</b> And Marie dwellide with hir, as it were thre monethis, and turnede ayen in to hir hous. <b>57</b> But the tyme of beryng child was fulfillid to Elizabeth, and sche bare a sone. <b>58</b> And the neiyboris and cosyns of hir herden, that the Lord hadde magnyfied his mercy with hir; and thei thankiden hym. 
<b>59</b> And it was don in the eiyte dai, thei camen to circumcide the child; and thei clepiden hym Zacarie, bi the name of his fadir. <b>60</b> And his moder answeride, and seide, Nay, but he schal be clepid Joon. <b>61</b> And thei seiden to hir, For no man is in thi kynrede, that is clepid this name. <b>62</b> And thei bikeneden to his fadir, what he wolde that he were clepid. <b>63</b> And he axynge a poyntil, wroot, seiynge, Joon is his name. 
<b>64</b> And alle men wondriden. And anoon his mouth was openyd, and his tunge, and he spak, and blesside God. <b>65</b> And drede was maad on alle her neiyboris, and alle these wordis weren pupplischid on alle the mounteyns of Judee. <b>66</b> And alle men that herden puttiden in her herte, and seiden, What maner child schal this be? For the hoond of the Lord was with hym. <b>67</b> And Zacarie, his fadir, was fulfillid with the Hooli Goost, and prophesiede, 
<b>68</b> and seide, Blessid be the Lord God of Israel, for he hath visitid, and maad redempcioun of his puple. <b>69</b> And he hath rerid to vs an horn of heelthe in the hous of Dauid, his child. <b>70</b> As he spak bi the mouth of hise hooli prophetis, that weren fro the world. <b>71</b> Helthe fro oure enemyes, and fro the hoond of alle men that hatiden vs. <b>72</b> To do merci with oure fadris, and to haue mynde of his hooli testament. <b>73</b> The greet ooth that he swoor to Abraham, oure fadir, to yyue hym silf to vs. 
<b>74</b> That we with out drede delyuered fro the hoond of oure enemyes, <b>75</b> serue to hym, in hoolynesse and riytwisnesse bifor hym in alle oure daies. <b>76</b> And thou, child, schalt be clepid the prophete of the Hiyest; for thou schalt go bifor the face of the Lord, to make redi hise weies. <b>77</b> To yyue scyence of helthe to his puple, in to remyssioun of her synnes; <b>78</b> bi the inwardnesse of the merci of oure God, in the whiche he spryngynge vp fro an hiy hath visitid vs. 
<b>79</b> To yyue liyt to hem that sitten in derknessis and in schadewe of deeth; to dresse oure feet in to the weie of pees. <b>80</b> And the child wexide, and was coumfortid in spirit, and was in desert placis `til to the dai of his schewing to Israel. 
<b>Lucas 2</b><br><b>1</b> And it was don in tho daies, a maundement wente out fro the emperour August, that al the world schulde be discryued. <b>2</b> This firste discryuyng was maad of Cyryn, iustice of Sirie. <b>3</b> And alle men wenten to make professioun, ech in to his owne citee. <b>4</b> And Joseph wente vp fro Galilee, fro the citee Nazareth, in to Judee, in to a citee of Dauid, that is clepid Bethleem, for that he was of the hous and of the meyne of Dauid, 
<b>5</b> that he schulde knouleche with Marie, his wijf, that was weddid to hym, and was greet with child. <b>6</b> And it was don, while thei weren there, the daies weren fulfillid, that sche schulde bere child. <b>7</b> And sche bare hir first borun sone, and wlappide hym in clothis, and leide hym in a cratche, for ther was no place to hym in no chaumbir. <b>8</b> And scheepherdis weren in the same cuntre, wakynge and kepynge the watchis of the nyyt on her flok. 
<b>9</b> And lo! the aungel of the Lord stood bisidis hem, and the cleernesse of God schinede aboute hem; and thei dredden with greet drede. <b>10</b> And the aungel seide to hem, Nyle ye drede; for lo! Y preche to you a greet ioye, that schal be to al puple. <b>11</b> For a sauyoure is borun to dai to you, that is Crist the Lord, in the citee of Dauid. <b>12</b> And this is a tokene to you; ye schulen fynde a yong child wlappid in clothis, and leid in a cratche. 
<b>13</b> And sudenli ther was maad with the aungel a multitude of heuenli knyythod, heriynge God, <b>14</b> and seiynge, Glorie be in the hiyeste thingis to God, and in erthe pees be to men of good wille. <b>15</b> And it was don, as the `aungelis passiden awei fro hem in to heuene, the scheephirdis spaken togider, and seiden, Go we ouer to Bethleem, and se we this word that is maad, which the Lord hath `maad, and schewide to vs. <b>16</b> And thei hiyynge camen, and founden Marie and Joseph, and the yong child leid in a cratche. 
<b>17</b> And thei seynge, knewen of the word that was seid to hem of this child. <b>18</b> And alle men that herden wondriden, and of these thingis that weren seid to hem of the scheephirdis. <b>19</b> But Marie kepte alle these wordis, berynge togider in hir herte. <b>20</b> And the scheepherdis turneden ayen, glorifyinge and heriynge God in alle thingis that thei hadden herd and seyn, as it was seid to hem. 
<b>21</b> And aftir that the eiyte daies weren endid, that the child schulde be circumcided, his name was clepid Jhesus, which was clepid of the aungel, bifor that he was conceyued in the wombe. <b>22</b> And aftir that the daies of the purgacioun of Marie weren fulfillid, aftir Moyses lawe, thei token hym into Jerusalem, to offre hym to the Lord, as it is writun in the lawe of the Lord, <b>23</b> For euery male kynde openynge the wombe, schal be clepid holi to the Lord; and that thei schulen yyue an offryng, 
<b>24</b> aftir that it is seid in the lawe of the Lord, A peire of turturis, or twei culuer briddis. <b>25</b> And lo! a man was in Jerusalem, whos name was Symeon; and this man was iust and vertuous, and aboode the coumfort of Israel; and the Hooli Goost was in hym. <b>26</b> And he hadde takun an answere of the Hooli Goost, that he schulde not se deeth, but he sawy first the Crist of the Lord. <b>27</b> And he cam in spirit into the temple. And whanne his fadir and modir ledden the child Jhesu to do aftir the custom of the lawe for hym, 
<b>28</b> he took hym in to hise armes, and he blesside God, <b>29</b> and seide, Lord, now thou leuyst thi seruaunt aftir thi word in pees; <b>30</b> for myn iyen han seyn thin helthe, <b>31</b> which thou hast maad redi bifor the face of alle puplis; <b>32</b> liyt to the schewyng of hethene men, and glorie of thi puple Israel. <b>33</b> And his fadir and his modir weren wondrynge on these thingis, that weren seid of hym. 
<b>34</b> And Symeon blesside hem, and seide to Marie, his modir, Lo! this is set in to the fallyng doun and in to the risyng ayen of many men in Israel, and in to a tokene, to whom it schal be ayenseid. <b>35</b> And a swerd schal passe thorou thin owne soule, that the thouytis ben schewid of many hertis. <b>36</b> And Anna was a prophetesse, the douytir of Fanuel, of the lynage of Aser. And sche hadde goon forth in many daies, and hadde lyued with hir hosebonde seuene yeer fro hir maydynhode. 
<b>37</b> And this was a widewe to foure scoor yeer and foure; and sche departide not fro the temple, but seruyde to God nyyt and dai in fastyngis and preieris. <b>38</b> And this cam vpon hem in thilk our, and knoulechide to the Lord, and spak of hym to alle that abiden the redempcioun of Israel. <b>39</b> And as thei hadden ful don alle thingis, aftir the lawe of the Lord, thei turneden ayen in to Galilee, in to her citee Nazareth. <b>40</b> And the child wexe, and was coumfortid, ful of wisdom; and the grace of God was in hym. 
<b>41</b> And his fadir and modir wenten ech yeer in to Jerusalem, in the solempne dai of pask. <b>42</b> And whanne Jhesus was twelue yeer oold, thei wenten vp to Jerusalem, aftir the custom of the feeste dai. <b>43</b> And whanne the daies weren don, thei turneden ayen; and the child abood in Jerusalem, and his fadir and modir knewen it not. <b>44</b> For thei gessynge that he hadde be in the felowschip, camen a daies iourney, and souyten hym among hise cosyns and hise knouleche. 
<b>45</b> And whanne thei founden hym not, thei turneden ayen in to Jerusalem, and souyten hym. <b>46</b> And it bifelle, that aftir the thridde dai thei founden hym in the temple, sittynge in the myddil of the doctours, herynge hem and axynge hem. <b>47</b> And alle men that herden hym, wondriden on the prudence and the answeris of hym. <b>48</b> And thei seyn, and wondriden. And his modir seide to hym, Sone, what hast thou do to vs thus? Lo! thi fadir and Y sorewynge han souyte thee. 
<b>49</b> And he seide to hem, What is it that ye souyten me? wisten ye not that in tho thingis that ben of my fadir, it behoueth me to be? <b>50</b> And thei vndurstoden not the word, which he spak to hem. <b>51</b> And he cam doun with hem, and cam to Nazareth, and was suget to hem. And his moder kepte togidir alle these wordis, and bare hem in hir herte. <b>52</b> And Jhesus profitide in wisdom, age, and grace, anentis God and men. 
<b>Lucas 3</b><br><b>1</b> In the fiftenthe yeer of the empire of Tiberie, the emperoure, whanne Pilat of Pounce gouernede Judee, and Eroude was prince of Galilee, and Filip, his brothir, was prince of Iturye, and of the cuntre of Tracon, and Lisanye was prince of Abilyn, <b>2</b> vndir the princis of prestis Annas and Caifas, the word of the Lord was maad on Joon, the sone of Zacarie, in desert. <b>3</b> And he cam in to al the cuntre of Jordan, and prechide baptym of penaunce in to remyssioun of synnes. 
<b>4</b> As it is wrytun in the book of the wordis of Isaye, the prophete, The voice of a crier in desert, Make ye redi the weie of the Lord, make ye hise pathis riyt. <b>5</b> Ech valey schal be fulfillid, and euery hil and litil hil schal be maad lowe; and schrewid thingis schulen ben in to dressid thingis, and scharp thingis in to pleyn weies; <b>6</b> and euery fleisch schal se the heelthe of God. 
<b>7</b> Therfor he seid to the puple, which wente out to be baptisid of hym, Kyndlyngis of eddris, who schewide to you to fle fro the wraththe to comynge? <b>8</b> Therfor do ye worthi fruytis of penaunce, and bigynne ye not to seie, We han a fadir Abraham; for Y seie to you, that God is myyti to reise of these stoonys the sones of Abraham. <b>9</b> And now an axe is sett to the roote of the tree; and therfor euery tre that makith no good fruyt, schal be kit doun, and schal be cast in to the fier. 
<b>10</b> And the puple axide hym, and seiden, What thanne schulen we do? <b>11</b> He answeride, and seide to hem, He that hath twei cootis, yyue to hym that hath noon; and he that hath metis, do in lijk maner. <b>12</b> And pupplicans camen to be baptisid; and thei seiden to hym, Maister, what schulen we do? <b>13</b> And he seide to hem, Do ye no thing more, than that that is ordeyned to you. 
<b>14</b> And knyytis axiden hym, and seiden, What schulen also we do? And he seide to hem, Smyte ye wrongfuli no man, nethir make ye fals chalenge, and be ye apayed with youre sowdis. <b>15</b> Whanne al the puple gesside, and alle men thouyten in her hertis of Joon, lest perauenture he were Crist, <b>16</b> Joon answeride, and seide to alle men, Y baptize you in watir; but a stronger than Y schal come aftir me, of whom Y am not worthi to vnbynde the lace of his schoon; he schal baptize you in the Hooli Goost and fier. 
<b>17</b> Whos `wynewyng tool in his hond, and he schal purge his floor of corn, and schal gadere the whete in to his berne; but the chaffis he schal brenne with fier vnquenchable. <b>18</b> And many othere thingis also he spak, and prechide to the puple. But Eroude tetrark, whanne he was blamed of Joon for Erodias, <b>19</b> the wijf of his brother, and for alle the yuelis that Eroude dide, <b>20</b> encreside this ouer alle, and schitte Joon in prisoun. 
<b>21</b> And it was don, whanne al the puple was baptised, and whanne Jhesu was baptised, and preiede, heuene was openyd. <b>22</b> And the Hooli Goost cam doun in bodili licnesse, as a dowue on hym; and a vois was maad fro heuene, Thou art my derworth sone, in thee it hath plesid to me. <b>23</b> And Jhesu hym silf was bigynninge as of thritti yeer, that he was gessid the sone of Joseph, which was of Heli, <b>24</b> which was of Mathath, which was of Leuy, which was of Melchi, that was of Jamne,

<b>30</b> that was of Leuy, that was of Symeon, that was of Juda, that was of Joseph, that was of Jona, that was of Eliachym, <b>31</b> that was of Melca, that was of Menna, that of Mathatha, that was of Nathan, <b>32</b> that was of Dauid, that was of Jesse, that was of Obeth, that was of Boz, that was of Salmon, that was of Nason, <b>33</b> that was of Amynadab, that was of Aram, that was of Esrom, that was of Fares, <b>34</b> that was of Judas, that was of Jacob, that was of Isaac, that was of Abraham, that was of Tare, that was of Nachor,

<b>Lucas 4</b><br><b>1</b> And Jhesus ful of the Hooli Goost turnede ayen fro Jordan, and was led bi the spirit into desert fourti daies, <b>2</b> and was temptid of the deuel, and eet nothing in tho daies; and whanne tho daies weren endid, he hungride. <b>3</b> And the deuel seide to him, If thou art Goddis sone, seie to this stoon, that it be maad breed. <b>4</b> And Jhesus answeride to hym, It is writun, That a man lyueth not in breed aloone, but in euery word of God. 
<b>5</b> And the deuel ladde hym in to an hiy hil, and schewide to hym alle the rewmes of the world in a moment of tyme; <b>6</b> and seide to hym, Y schal yyue to thee al this power, and the glorie of hem, for to me thei ben youun, and to whom Y wole, Y yyue hem; <b>7</b> therfor if thou falle doun, and worschipe bifore me, alle thingis schulen be thine. <b>8</b> And Jhesus answeride, and seide to hym, It is writun, Thou schalt worschipe thi Lord God, and to hym aloone thou schalt serue. 
<b>9</b> And he ledde hym in to Jerusalem, and sette hym on the pynacle of the temple, and seide to hym, If thou art Goddis sone, sende thi silf fro hennes doun; <b>10</b> for it is writun, For he hath comaundide to hise aungels of thee, that thei kepe thee in alle thi weies, <b>11</b> and that thei schulen take thee in hondis, lest perauenture thou hirte thi foote at a stoon. <b>12</b> And Jhesus answeride, and seide to him, It is seid, Thou schalt not tempte thi Lord God.

And anoon yit while he spak, the cok crewe. <b>61</b> And the Lord turnede ayen, and bihelde Petre; and Petre hadde mynde on the word of Jhesu, as he hadde seid, For bifor that the cok crowe, thries thou schalt denye me. <b>62</b> And Petre yede out, and wepte bittirli. <b>63</b> And the men that helden hym scorneden hym, and smyten hym.

<b>68</b> And he seide to hem, If Y seie to you, ye schulen not bileue to me; and if Y axe, ye schulen not answere to me, nethir ye schulen delyuere me. <b>69</b> But aftir this tyme mannys sone schal be sittynge on the riyt half of the vertu of God. <b>70</b> Therfor alle seiden, Thanne art thou the sone of God? And he seide, Ye seien that Y am. <b>71</b> And thei seiden, What yit desiren we witnessyng? for we vs silf han herd of his mouth. 
<b>Lucas 23</b><br><b>1</b> And al the multitude of hem arysen, and ledden hym to Pilat. <b>2</b> And thei bigunnen to accuse hym, and seiden, We han foundun this turnynge vpsodoun oure folk, and forbedynge tributis to be youun to the emperour, and seiynge that hym silf is Crist and kyng. <b>3</b> And Pilat axide hym, and seide, Art thou kyng of Jewis? And he answeride, and seide, Thou seist. <b>4</b> And Pilat seide to the princis of prestis, and to the puple, Y fynde no thing of cause in this man. 
<b>5</b> And thei woxen stronger, and seiden, He moueth the puple, techynge thorou al Judee, bigynnynge fro Galile til hidir. <b>6</b> And Pilat herynge Galile axide, if he were a man of Galile. <b>7</b> And whanne he knewe that he was of the powere of Eroude, he sente hym to Eroude; which was at Jerusalem in tho daies. <b>8</b> And whanne Eroude siy Jhesu, he ioyede ful myche; for long tyme he coueitide to se hym, for he herde many thingis of hym, and hopide to see sum tokene `to be don of hym. 
<b>9</b> And he axide hym in many wordis; and he answeride no thing to hym. <b>10</b> And the princis of preestis and the scribis stoden, stidfastli accusynge hym. <b>11</b> But Eroude with his oost dispiside hym, and scornede hym, and clothide with a white cloth, and sente hym ayen to Pilat. <b>12</b> And Eroude and Pilat weren maad freendis fro that dai; for bifor thei weren enemyes togidre. <b>13</b> And Pilat clepide togider the princis of prestis and the maiestratis of the puple, and seide to hem,

Y fynde no cause of deeth in hym; therfor Y schal chastise hym, and Y schal delyuer. <b>23</b> And thei contynueden with greet voicis axynge, that he schulde be crucified; and the voicis of hem woxen stronge. 
<b>24</b> And Pilat demyde her axyng to be don. <b>25</b> And he delyueride to hem hym, that for mansleyng and sedicioun was sent in to prisoun, whom thei axiden; but he bitook Jhesu to her wille. <b>26</b> And whanne thei ledden hym, thei token a man, Symon of Syrenen, comynge fro the toun, and thei leiden on hym the cross to bere aftir Jhesu. <b>27</b> And there suede hym myche puple, and wymmen that weiliden, and bymorneden hym. 
<b>28</b> And Jhesus turnede to hem, and seide, Douytris of Jerusalem, nyle ye wepe on me, but wepe ye on youre silf and on youre sones. <b>29</b> For lo! daies schulen come, in whiche it schal be seid, Blessid be bareyn wymmen, and wombis that han not borun children, and the tetis that han not youun souke. <b>30</b> Thanne thei schulen bigynne to seie to mounteyns, Falle ye doun on vs, and to smale hillis, Keuere ye vs. <b>31</b> For if in a greene tre thei don these thingis, what schal be don in a drie?

<b>44</b> And it was almest the sixte our, and derknessis weren maad in al the erthe `in to the nynthe our. <b>45</b> And the sun was maad derk, and the veile of the temple was to-rent atwo. <b>46</b> And Jhesus criynge with a greet vois, seide, Fadir, in to thin hoondis Y bitake my spirit. <b>48</b> And al the puple of hem that weren there togidir at this spectacle, and sayn tho thingis that weren don, smyten her brestis, and turneden ayen. <b>49</b> But alle his knowun stoden afer, and wymmen that sueden hym fro Galile, seynge these thingis. <b>50</b> And lo! a man, Joseph bi name, of Aramathie, a cite of Judee, that was a decurien, a good man and a iust, <b>51</b> this man concentide not to the counseil and to the dedis of hem; and he abood the kyngdom of God. <b>52</b> This Joseph cam to Pilat, and axide the bodi of Jhesu,
