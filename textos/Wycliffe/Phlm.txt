<b>Filemón</b><br><b>1</b> Poul, the boundun of Crist Jhesu, and Timothe, brother, to Filemon, bilouyd, and oure helpere, and to Appia, <b>2</b> most dere sister, and to Archip, oure euene kniyt, and to the chirche that is in thin hous, <b>3</b> grace be to you, and pees of God oure fader, and of the Lord Jhesu Crist. <b>4</b> I do thankingis to my God, euere more makinge mynde of thee in my preieris, <b>5</b> heringe thi charite and feith, that thou hast in the Lord Jhesu, and to alle hooli men, 
<b>6</b> that the comynyng of thi feith be maad opyn, in knowing of al good thing in Crist Jhesu. <b>7</b> And Y hadde greet ioye and coumfort in thi charite, for the entrailis of hooli men restiden bi thee, brother. <b>8</b> For which thing Y hauynge myche trist in Crist Jhesu, to comaunde to thee that that perteyneth to profit; <b>9</b> but Y biseche more for charite, sithen thou art siche as the elde Poul, and now the boundun of Jhesu Crist. <b>10</b> Y biseche thee for my sone Onesyme, whom Y in boondis bigat, 
<b>11</b> which sumtyme was vnprofitable to thee, but now profitable bothe to thee and to me; whom Y sente ayen to thee. <b>12</b> And resseyue thou hym as myn entrailis; <b>13</b> whom Y wolde withholde with me, that he schulde serue for thee to me in boondis of the gospel; <b>14</b> but with out thi counseil Y wolde not do ony thing, that thi good schulde not be as of nede, but wilful. <b>15</b> For perauenture therfor he departide fro thee for a tyme, that thou schuldist resseyue hym with outen ende; 
<b>16</b> now not as a seruaunt, but for a seruaunt a most dere brother, most to me; and how myche more to thee, bothe in fleisch and in the Lord? <b>17</b> Therfor if thou hast me a felowe, resseyue hym as me; for if he hath ony thing anoied thee, <b>18</b> ethir owith, arette thou this thing to me. <b>19</b> Y Poul wroot with myn hoond, Y schal yelde; that Y seie not to thee, that also thou owist to me thi silf. <b>20</b> So, brothir, Y schal vse thee in the Lord; fille thou myn entrails in Crist. 
<b>21</b> Y tristnynge of thin obedience wroot to thee, witynge that thou schalt do ouer that that Y seie. <b>22</b> Also make thou redi to me an hous to dwelle in; for Y hope that bi youre preyeris Y schal be youun to you. <b>23</b> Epafras, prisoner with me in Crist Jhesu, <b>24</b> greetith thee wel, and Mark, Aristark, Demas, Lucas, myn helperis. <b>25</b> The grace of oure Lord Jhesu Crist be with youre spirit. Amen.
