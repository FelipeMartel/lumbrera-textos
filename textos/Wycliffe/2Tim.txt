<b>2 Timothy 1</b><br><b>1</b> Poul, apostle of Jhesu Crist, bi the wille of God, bi the biheest of lijf that is in Crist Jhesu, <b>2</b> to Tymothe, his moost dereworthe sone, grace, merci, and pees of God the fadir, and of Jhesu Crist, oure Lord. <b>3</b> I do thankyngis to my God, to whom Y serue fro my progenytouris in clene conscience, that with outen ceessyng Y haue mynde of thee in my preyeris, <b>4</b> niyt and dai, desirynge to se thee; hauynge mynde of thi teeris, that Y be fillid with ioye. 
<b>5</b> And Y bithenke of that feith, that is in thee not feyned, which also dwellide firste in thin aunte Loide, and in thi modir Eunyce. And Y am certeyn, that also in thee. <b>6</b> For which cause Y moneste thee, that thou reise ayen the grace of God, that is in thee bi the settyng on of myn hondis. <b>7</b> For whi God yaf not to vs the spirit of drede, but of vertu, and of loue, and of sobrenesse. 
<b>8</b> Therfor nyl thou schame the witnessyng of oure Lord Jhesu Crist, nether me, his prisoner; but trauele thou togidere in the gospel bi the vertu of God; <b>9</b> that delyueride vs, and clepide with his hooli clepyng, not after oure werkis, but bi his purpos and grace, that is youun in Crist Jhesu bifore worldli tymes; <b>10</b> but now it is opyn bi the liytnyng of oure sauyour Jhesu Crist, which destriede deth, and liytnede lijf and vncorrupcioun bi the gospel. <b>11</b> In which Y am set a prechour and apostle, and maistir of hethene men. 
<b>12</b> For which cause also Y suffre these thingis; but Y am not confoundid. For Y woot to whom Y haue bileuyd, and Y am certeyne that he is miyti for to kepe that is take to my keping in to that dai. <b>13</b> Haue thou the fourme of hoolsum wordis, whiche thou herdist of me in feith and loue in Crist Jhesu. <b>14</b> Kepe thou the good takun to thi kepyng bi the Hooli Goost, that dwellith in vs. <b>15</b> Thou wost this, that alle that ben in Asie ben turnyd awey fro me, of whiche is Figelus and Ermogenes. 
<b>16</b> The Lord yyue merci to the hous of Onesyforus, for ofte he refreischide me, and schamyde not my chayne. <b>17</b> But whanne he cam to Rome, he souyte me bisili, and foond. <b>18</b> The Lord yyue to hym to fynde merci of God in that dai. And hou grete thingis he mynystride to me at Effesi, thou knowist betere. 
<b>2 Timothy 2</b><br><b>1</b> Therfor thou, my sone, be coumfortid in grace that is in Crist Jhesu. <b>2</b> And what thingis thou hast herd of me bi many witnessis, bitake thou these to feithful men, whiche schulen `be also able to teche othere men. <b>3</b> Trauele thou as a good knyyt of Crist Jhesu. <b>4</b> No man holdinge knyythod to God, wlappith hym silf with worldli nedis, that he plese to hym, to whom he hath preuyd hym silf. <b>5</b> For he that fiytith in a batel, schal not be corowned, but he fiyte lawfuli. 
<b>6</b> It bihoueth an erthetiliere to resseyue first of the fruytis. <b>7</b> Vndurstonde thou what thingis Y seie. For the Lord schal yyue to thee vndurstonding in alle thingis. <b>8</b> Be thou myndeful that the Lord Jhesu Crist of the seed of Dauid hath rise ayen fro deth, <b>9</b> aftir my gospel, in which Y trauele `til to boondis, as worching yuele, but the word of God is not boundun. <b>10</b> Therfor Y suffre alle thingis for the chosun, that also thei gete the heelthe, that is in Crist Jhesu, with heuenli glorie. 
<b>11</b> A trewe word, that if we ben deed togidere, also we schulen liue togidere; <b>12</b> if we suffren, we schulen regne togidere; if we denyen, he schal denye vs; <b>13</b> if we bileuen not, he dwellith feithful, he mai not denye hym silf. <b>14</b> Teche thou these thingis, witnessinge bifore God. Nyle thou stryue in wordis; for to no thing it is profitable, but to the subuerting of men that heren. <b>15</b> Bisili kepe to yyue thi silf a preued preisable werkman to God, with oute schame, riytli tretinge the word of treuthe. 
<b>16</b> But eschewe thou vnhooli and veyn spechis, for whi tho profiten myche to vnfeithfulnesse, <b>17</b> and the word of hem crepith as a canker. Of whiche Filete is, and Ymeneus, <b>18</b> whiche felden doun fro the treuthe, seiynge that the rising ayen is now doon, and thei subuertiden the feith of summen. <b>19</b> But the sad foundement of God stondith, hauynge this marke, The Lord knowith whiche ben hise, and, Ech man that nameth the name of the Lord, departith fro wickidnesse. 
<b>20</b> But in a greet hous ben not oneli vessels of gold and of siluer, but also of tree and of erthe; and so summen ben in to onour, and summe in to dispit. <b>21</b> Therfor if ony man clensith hym silf fro these, he schal be a vessel halewid in to onour, and profitable to the Lord, redi to al good werk. <b>22</b> And fle thou desiris of yongthe, but sue thou riytwisnesse, feith, charite, pees, with hem that inwardli clepen the Lord of a clene herte. 
<b>23</b> And eschewe thou foltische questiouns, and without kunnyng, wytynge that tho gendren chidyngis. <b>24</b> But it bihoueth the seruaunt of the Lord to chide not; but to be mylde to alle men, able to teche, <b>25</b> paciente, with temperaunce repreuynge hem that ayenstonden the treuthe, that sum tyme God yyue to hem forthenkyng, that thei knowen the treuthe, <b>26</b> and that thei rise ayen fro the snares of the deuel, of whom thei ben holdun prisoneris at his wille. 
<b>2 Timothy 3</b><br><b>1</b> But wite thou this thing, that in the laste daies perelouse tymes schulen neiye, and men schulen be louynge hem silf, <b>2</b> coueitouse, hiy of bering, proude, blasfemeris, not obedient to fadir and modir, vnkynde, <b>3</b> cursid, with outen affeccioun, with out pees, false blameris, vncontynent, vnmylde, <b>4</b> with out benygnyte, traitouris, ouerthwert, bollun with proude thouytis, blynde, loueris of lustis more than of God, 
<b>5</b> hauynge the licknesse of pitee, but denyynge the vertu of it. And eschewe thou these men. <b>6</b> Of these thei ben that persen housis, and leden wymmen caitifs chargid with synnes, whiche ben led with dyuerse desiris, euere more lernynge, <b>7</b> and neuere perfitli comynge to the science of treuthe. <b>8</b> And as Jannes and Mambres ayenstoden Moises, so these ayenstonden treuthe, men corrupt in vndirstonding, repreuyd aboute the feith. 
<b>9</b> But ferthere thei schulen not profite, for the vnwisdom of hem schal be knowun to alle men, as hern was. <b>10</b> But thou hast getun my teching, ordinaunce, purposing, feith, long abiding, loue, <b>11</b> pacience, persecuciouns, passiouns, whiche weren maad to me at Antioche, at Ycony, at Listris, what maner persecucyouns Y suffride, and the Lord hath delyuered me of alle. <b>12</b> And alle men that wolen lyue feithfuli in Crist Jhesu, schulen suffre persecucioun. 
<b>13</b> But yuele men and disseyueris schulen encreese in to worse, errynge, and sendinge in to errour. <b>14</b> But dwelle thou in these thingis that thou hast lerud, and that ben bitakun to thee, witinge of whom thou hast lerud; <b>15</b> for thou hast knowun hooli lettris fro thi youthe, whiche moun lerne thee to heelthe, bi feith that is in Crist Jhesu. <b>16</b> For al scripture inspirid of God is profitable to teche, to repreue, to chastice, to lerne in riytwisnes, that the man of God be parfit, lerud to al good werk. 
<b>2 Timothy 4</b><br><b>1</b> I witnesse bifore God and Crist Jhesu, that schal deme the quike and the deed, and bi the comyng of hym, and the kyngdom of hym, <b>2</b> preche the word, be thou bisi couenabli with outen rest, repreue thou, biseche thou, blame thou in al pacience and doctryn. <b>3</b> For tyme schal be, whanne men schulen not suffre hoolsum teching, but at her desiris thei schulen gadere `togidere to hem silf maistris yitchinge to the eeris. 
<b>4</b> And treuli thei schulen turne awei the heryng fro treuthe, but to fablis thei schulen turne. <b>5</b> But wake thou, in alle thingis traueile thou, do the werk of an euangelist, fulfille thi seruyce, be thou sobre. <b>6</b> For Y am sacrifisid now, and the tyme of my departyng is nyy. <b>7</b> Y haue stryuun a good strijf, Y haue endid the cours, Y haue kept the feith. 
<b>8</b> In `the tothir tyme a coroun of riytwisnesse is kept to me, which the Lord, a iust domesman, schal yelde to me in that dai; and not oneli to me, but also to these that louen his comyng. <b>9</b> Hyye thou to come to me soone. For Demas, louynge this world, hath forsakun me, and wente to Tessalonyk, <b>10</b> Crescens in to Galathi, Tite in to Dalmacie; <b>11</b> Luk aloone is with me. Take thou Mark, and brynge with thee; for he is profitable to me in to seruyce. <b>12</b> Forsothe Y sente Titicus to Effesi. 
<b>13</b> The cloth which Y lefte at Troade at Carpe, whanne thou comest, bringe with thee, and the bookis, but moost parchemyne. <b>14</b> Alisaundre, the tresorer, schewide to me myche yuele; `the Lord schal yelde to hym aftir his werkis. <b>15</b> Whom also thou eschewe; for he ayenstood ful greetli oure wordis. <b>16</b> In my firste defence no man helpide me, but alle forsoken me; be it not arettid to hem. 
<b>17</b> But the Lord helpide me, and coumfortide me, that the preching be fillid bi me, and that alle folkis here, that Y am delyueride fro the mouth of the lioun. <b>18</b> And the Lord delyueride me fro al yuel werk, and schal make me saaf in to his heuenly kingdom, to whom be glorie in to worldis of worldis. <b>19</b> Amen. Grete wel Prisca, and Aquila, and the hous of Oneseforus. <b>20</b> Erastus lefte at Corynthi, and Y lefte Trofymus sijk at Mylete. 
<b>21</b> Hiye thou to come bifore wyntir. Eubolus, and Prudent, and Lynus, and Claudia, and alle britheren, greten thee wel. <b>22</b> Oure Lord Jhesu Crist be with thi spirit. The grace of God be with you. Amen.
